Patna also has every receipt and payment voucher (2020-21 to 2026-27) in its reports, and a separate [voucher report](patna_vouchers/report.md). Voucher lists for other districts can be collected from India with `python scripts/run_vouchers.py --district <code> --fy <FY>`; the reports then pick them up.

Rebuild everything: `python scripts/make_district_report.py --district all --fy all` then `python scripts/make_district_index.py --district all`.
