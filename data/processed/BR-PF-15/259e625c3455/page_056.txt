2.2.4 Tax: GSDP Ratio
The Tax: GSDP ratio of Bihar, which represents the state's own tax revenues as a percentage of its
Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP), has fluctuated over the years. It declined from 6.9% in 2015-16
to a low of 4.9% in 2017-18, primarily due to the impact of imposing prohibition in the state which
caused the state exchequer significant reduction from excise and sales tax on alcohol of about Rs 4500
crore in 2016-17 (about Rs 3100 crore on excise). However, it has gradually improved and reached
5.9% in 2022-23, well short of its peak of 6.9%.
