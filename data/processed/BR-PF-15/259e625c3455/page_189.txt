6.6.4 Expenditure Analysis of Urban Local Bodies in Bihar (2015-2020)
Table 6.11 shows the Expenditure of All ULBs in Bihar during the period 2015-20. Over this five-year period,
the total expenditure of all ULBs in Bihar amounted to Rs. 14,894 crore, with establishment costs and civic
services receiving relatively smaller allocations (20% and 10% of total allocations respectively). Scheme
expenditure dominated the ULBs' financial activities, with Rs. 4,058 crore spent on Centrally Sponsored
Schemes (23 percent of total) and Rs. 6,328 crore spent on State Specified Schemes (47% of total) during
this period. Together, these scheme-based expenditures accounted for 70% of the total spending.
