7. Land and Asset Management
Non-functioning SPSEs in Bihar might still hold significant land and building assets that are
currently underutilized. These assets can be monetised trough an SPV, with the proceeds used to
settle liabilities and fund restructuring initiatives.
By adopting these measures, Bihar can still revitalize its SPSEs, transforming them into efficient and
financially viable entities that contribute positively to the state's economy.
