5.6 Maturity Profile of Outstanding Debt
Table 5.8 A, B and C as well as Chart 5.6 show the maturity profile of the State’s outstanding debt as
at the end of 2022-23. Before the award period of the 16th Finance Commission, the State would have
discharged 30% of its outstanding debt as on 31st March 2023, but 48% of its debt will get discharged
during the award period of the 16th Finance Commission (2026-31), amounting to Rs 90022 crore, as
of now. Any debt sustainability roadmap of the State must factor in this maturity profile of the
outstanding debt.
