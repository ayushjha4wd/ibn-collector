Chapter 1: Annexure
Annexure 1.4A
MINISTRY OF FINANCE
(Department of Economic Affairs)
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 10th October, 2025
S.O. 4640(E).—The following order made by the President is to be published for general
information:-
ORDER
In pursuance of powers under clause (1) of article 280 of the Constitution, read with the
provisions contained in the Finance Commission (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951 (33 of
1951), the President is pleased to make the following amendments in the Order number S.O. 5533
(E), dated the 31st December, 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section
3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 31st December, 2023, namely:-
In the said order:-
(a) in paragraph 3, for the figures, letters and words “31st day of October, 2025”, the figures,
letters and words, “the 30th day of November, 2025” shall be substituted;
(b) in paragraph 5, for the figures, letters and words “31st day of October, 2025”, the figures,
letters and words “the 30th day of November, 2025” shall be substituted.
Sd/-
DROUPADI MURMU
President of India
Camp: Sasangir, Gujarat
Dated the 10th October, 2025
