Annexure 1.3
MINISTRY OF FINANCE
(Department of Economic Affairs)
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 5th April, 2024
S.O. 1625(E).—The following order made by the President is to be published for general
information: —
ORDER
In pursuance of clause (1) of article 280 of the Constitution read with the provisions of the Finance
Commission (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951 (33 of 1951), the President is pleased to appoint the
following as Member of the Sixteenth Finance commission, namely:-
Dr. Manoj Panda, former Director, Institute of Economic Growth Full-time Member
2. The above member of the Commission shall hold office from the date of assuming office up to the
date of submission of Report or 31st day of October, 2025, whichever is earlier.
3. This order is in continuation of the Notification No. S.O.5533 (E) dated 31.12.2023 and Notification
No. S.O.369 (E) dated 30.01.2024 with respect to constitution of Sixteenth Finance Commission.
Sd/-
New Delhi, DROUPADI MURMU,
Dated 5th April, 2024 President of India
