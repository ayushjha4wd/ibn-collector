MINISTRY OF FINANCE
(Department of Economic Affairs)
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 30th January, 2024
S.O. 369(E).—The following order made by the President is to be published for general information: —
Order
In pursuance of clause (1) of article 280 of the Constitution read with the provisions of the Finance
Commission (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951 (33 of 1951), the President is pleased to appoint
following as Members of the Sixteenth Finance Commission, namely:-
1. Shri. Ajay Narayan Jha, former Member, 15th Finance Full time Member
Commission and former Secretary, Department of Expenditure
2. Smt. Annie George Mathew, former Special Secretary, Full time Member
Department of Expenditure
3. Dr. Niranjan Rajadhyaksha, Executive Director, Artha Full time Member
Global
4. Dr. Soumya Kanti Ghosh, Group Chief Economic Advisor, Part time Member
State Bank of India
2. The Chairman and other members of the Commission shall hold office from the date on which they
respectively assume office up to the date of submission of Report or 31st day of October, 2025, whichever
is earlier.
3. This order is in continuation of the Notification No. S.O.5533 (E) dated 31.12.2023 with respect to
constitution of Sixteenth Finance Commission.
Sd/-
New Delhi, DROUPADI MURMU
Dated the 30th January, 2024 President of India
