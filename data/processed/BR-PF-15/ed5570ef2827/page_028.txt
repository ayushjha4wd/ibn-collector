OFFICE MEMORANDUM
Subject: Constitution of an Advisory Council - Sixteenth Finance Commission
The Sixteenth Finance Commission hereby constitutes an Advisory Council with the following
members:
i. Dr. D.K. Srivastava
ii. Mr. Neelkanth Mishra
iii. Dr. Poonam Gupta
iv. Ms. Pranjul Bhandari
v. Mr. Rahul Bajoria
2. Dr. Poonam Gupta shall be the Convenor of the Advisory Council.
3. The role and function of the Advisory Council will be:
i. To advise the Commission on any of the ToR or related subjects that may be of relevance.
ii. To assist in the preparation of papers or research studies and to monitor or assess studies
commissioned by the Finance Commission, thereby enhancing the Commission's
understanding of the issues in its ToR.
iii. To help broaden the Commission's ambit and understanding by seeking the best national and
international practices on matters pertaining to fiscal devolution and improving the quality,
reach, and enforcement of its recommendations.
4. The Commission may hold meetings in person/virtually as per the requirements of the Commission
and availability of the Council members.
5. The Commission would bear the cost of travel wherever required and facilitate other logistic
arrangements as admissible.
6. This issues with the approval of the Competent Authority
