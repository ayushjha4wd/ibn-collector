Sixteenth Finance Commission
Notes: –
1) Assam suggested no floor on area, but an assured minimum amount for all States and ceiling of 5% in area.
2) Chhattisgarh and Tamil Nadu suggested two floors of 1% and 2% in area.
3) Madhya Pradesh suggested multiple floors of 0.5%, 1%, 1.5% and 2% in area, excluding the States of Goa, Haryana, Kerala and
Punjab.
4) Rajasthan suggested floor of 1.5% in area.
5) Uttarakhand suggested using 3-dimensional (3-D) area of States instead of 2-dimesnaional geographical area.
6) Goa, Meghalaya and Sikkim suggested floor of 1% on population.
7) Rajasthan suggested using projected population of 2024, instead of population from Census 2011.
8) Tamil Nadu suggested using population from Census 1971.
9) Chhattisgarh suggested using low population density based on Census 2011.
10) Kerala and Odisha suggested using high population density based on Census 2011.
11) Chhattisgarh and Meghalaya suggested to include percentage of ST in State's population as per Census 2011.
12) Himachal Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh suggested to include percentage of SC and ST in State's population as per Census 2011.
13) Odisha suggested including modified population that includes - SC, ST, Old-aged (80+) and widowed population.
14) Punjab suggested to give 5% weight to percentage of ST population and 2.5% weight to old- age population (aged 60+), based on
Census 2011.
15) West Bengal suggested using an index of State’s population, with higher weightage to State’s SC & ST population as per Census
2011.
16) Gujarat and West Bengal suggested to use the share of urban population in total urban population of all States.
17) Maharashtra suggested to give 10% weight to share of urban population in total urban population of all States and 10% weight to
share of rural population in total rural population of all States.
18) Meghalaya suggested using rural dispersion gap defined as rural population per village.
19) Tamil Nadu suggested using share of urban population in total population of a State.
20) Arunachal Pradesh suggested to include demographic change based on preservation of tribal people, culture and heritage.
21) Assam suggested to use a floor value of 2.1 on Total Fertility Rate (TFR) under demographic performance criterion.
22) Goa suggested floor value of 1 on TFR.
23) Himachal Pradesh suggested using upper and lower bound on TFR scaled by population as per Census 1971.
24) Madhya Pradesh suggested using TFR based on NFHS-5 scaled with population as per Census 2011.
25) Punjab suggested using inverse of TFR in demographic performance criterion, without multiplying it with population.
26) Rajasthan suggested to give 5% weight to TFR based on NFHS-5 multiplied with population as per 1971 Census and 7.5% weight
to change in TFR based on NFHS-4 and NFHS-5.
27) Goa suggested taking income distance from the State with the highest per capita income (without removing outliers).
28) Karnataka suggested taking square root of per capita income distance before multiplying it with population, and per capita income
distance should be calculated from average per capita income of top 3 States.
29) Maharashtra suggested excluding district domestic products of outlier districts from State’s GSDP for the purpose of calculating per
capita income distance.
30) Rajasthan suggested to include State’s share of SECC deprived households with 5% weight by reducing income distance’s weight,
upon FC’s discretion.
31) Tamil Nadu suggested using real income of States calculated using CPI-AL as price deflator under income distance criterion.
32) Andhra Pradesh suggested to give 5% weight to share of State’s dense forest cover (very dense & moderately dense forest) in total
dense forest cover of all States and 15% weight to change in dense forest cover over 10 years.
33) Assam and Madhya Pradesh suggested to include Open Forest (OF) along-with Very Dense Forest (VDF) and Moderately Dense
Forest (MDF).
34) Goa suggested to introduce floor of 1% in forest cover.
35) Himachal Pradesh suggested to give 2.5% weight to dense forest cover, 5% weight to initiatives taken for Green Credit Programme,
and 5% weight to forest cover as proportion of total geographical area under this criterion.
36) Jharkhand and Manipur suggested to use total forest cover (VDF, MDF and OF) as proportion of total geographical area of State.
37) Nagaland suggested to use forest cover with 3% ceiling value excluding the States- Chhattisgarh and Arunachal Pradesh.
38) Telangana suggested to use change in forest cover over 5 years.
39) Uttar Pradesh suggested to use total forest cover including - VDF, MDF, OF and Tree cover.
40) Uttarakhand suggested to use modified forest cover criterion viz. State’s dense forest cover as proportion of State’s geographical
area wherein very dense forest cover is given twice the weight of moderately dense forest while calculating State’s Forest cover (VDF
+ MDF).
41) Arunachal Pradesh suggested to use a forest and ecology index based on State-wise data on carbon sink and forest cover.
42) Rajasthan suggested to use forest and ecology composite indicator based on State-wise data on wasteland area, water scarcity and
forest cover.
43) Tamil Nadu suggested forest and ecology index based on State-wise data on wetlands, coastline and renewable energy’s installed
capacity.
