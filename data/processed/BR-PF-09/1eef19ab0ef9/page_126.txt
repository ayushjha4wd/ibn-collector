CAPACITY BUILDING
5.52 UDHD has reported that the training programme under capacity building have been
undertaken, but detailed information has not been provided. It appears to this
Commission that a comprehensive action plan for capacity building has not been
drafted. On account of low capacity of ULBs, Bihar has not been able to take advantage
of the central funds as has been brought out in Paragraph 5.42 above. In fact, Bihar’s
performance in implementing Smart Cities, AMRUT, JNNURM etc. has been far from
satisfactory.
5.53 ULBs in Bihar suffer capacity constraints in terms of skilled manpower, IT facilities,
equipment, office space, etc leading to meagre utilization of available funds, inability
to avail central resources (CSSs/ACAs), poor project implementation as well as less
than satisfactory delivery of services.
