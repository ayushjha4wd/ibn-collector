ACCOUNTABILITY
5.57 The ULBs, being units of local governance, must be accountable to the people. SFC5
had recommended that accountability of ULBs may be ensured through Ombudsman,
ward sabhas and social audit.
1. Ombudsman
(i) One of the main recommendations of the UFC13 was that State
Governments should put in place a system of independent Ombudsmen for
local bodies who will look into complaints of corruption and
maladministration against the functionaries, both elected and official, and
recommend suitable action. 2nd Administrative Reforms Commission of the
Central Government had made similar suggestion and 5SFC too
recommended the same.
