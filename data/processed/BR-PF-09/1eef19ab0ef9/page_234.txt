8.55 To resolve this mismatch, the Commission recommends that for FY 2021-22 alone, the
amount of shortfall (Rs 370 crore) should be transferred from Development Fund to
General Fund. With this modification, the amounts available in Development Fund,
Maintenance Fund and General Fund in FY 2021-22 will become Rs. 802 crore, Rs.
781 crore and Rs. 2,323 crore. This is recommended as a one-time measure and no
diversion of amounts from Development Fund shall be done in subsequent years.
It is clarified that the recommended transfer from Development Fund to General Fund
shall be done only if the amount of Total SFC Transfer to General Fund, computed in
accordance with Paragraphs 8.36 to 8.40 on the basis of actuals of CFS and Divisible
Pool on t-1 basis, falls short of the projected requirement of Rs 2,322.86 crore indicated
in the last row of Table 8.17.
