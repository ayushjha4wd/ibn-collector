8.49 In case of ULBs, UDHD issues release orders and the respective ULBs draw money
from the Government Treasury. Commission suggests that UDHD should explore
the possibility of remitting funds to ULBs’ bank accounts directly by electronic
bank transfer through CFMS.
TIMELINES FOR SFC TRANSFERS
8.50 In FY 2021-22, release orders for each item under Development Fund, Maintenance
Fund and General Fund should be issued in a single instalment by August 31, 2021.
8.51 In subsequent years, funds for each item under Development Fund, Maintenance Fund
and General Fund should be released in two instalments – first instalment of 50 percent
by June 30 and second instalment of remaining 50 percent by October 31.
8.52 If there is any delay in release of funds vis-à-vis the deadlines mentioned above,
release should be made to the LBs along with a Delay Compensation. The Delay
Compensation shall be equal to interest for the period of delay beyond the
specified deadline calculated as per effective rate of interest on market
borrowings/ State Development Loans (SDLs) for the previous year.
