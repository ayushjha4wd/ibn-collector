Chapter 6
PRI FINANCES
REVIEW AND FORECAST OF REVENUE AND EXPENDITURE
6.1 The primary objective of this Chapter is to project the Expenditure-Resources Gap of the
Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs) for the award period of the SFC6 (2021-22 to 2024-
25) (four years). On the basis of information furnished by the Panchayati Raj Department
(PRD), the Commission has reviewed the finances of the PRIs of Bihar for the period
2015-16 to 2019-20, when the recommendations of the SFC5 were under implementation,
and thereafter made forecast of Revenue and Expenditure for the period 2021-22 to
2024-25.
