4.38 Swachh Bharat Mission
(i) To accelerate the efforts to achieve universal sanitation coverage and to put focus
on sanitation, Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM) was launched in 2014. Under the
mission, all villages, Gram Panchayats, Districts, States and Union Territories
have declared themselves Open-Defecation Free (ODF) by October 2, 2019 by
constructing over 100 million toilets in rural India.
