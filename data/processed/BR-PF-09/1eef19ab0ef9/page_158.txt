2020-25 is Rs 64,217.51 crore, which is almost one and a half times the projected
expenditure of Rs 44,643.11 crore in Table 6.6. Compared to actual revenue of all PRIs
during five year period 2015-20 amounting to Rs 43,444.52 crore too, PRD’s revenue

amounts expected to be received by way of Finance Commission Transfers.
