3. Majority of the posts in City Managers Cadre as well as the Engineering Cadre
are lying vacant. Almost 25 percent of Executive Officers’ posts are also vacant.
Progress of recruitment is very slow and needs to be speeded up. There are critical
gaps in staffing, particularly for finance and accounting, sanitation and town
planning functions.

for engaging agencies/ individuals on outsourcing basis for various kinds of
services.
