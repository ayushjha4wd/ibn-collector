2. Ward Committee
(i) Article 243S of the Constitution of India provides for constituting Wards
Committees in a Municipality having a population of three lakhs or more
and empowers the State Legislature to enact law in this regard. Accordingly,
BMA 2007 has provisions for setting up Wards Committee in Sections 30
and 31. As per Section 30 “Every Municipal Corporation having a
population of three lakhs or more may, at its first meeting after the election
of Councillors thereto or as soon as may be thereafter, group the wards of
the Corporation in such manner that each group consists of not less than
three wards, and constitute a wards committee for each such group”.
