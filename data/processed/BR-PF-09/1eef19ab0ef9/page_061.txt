2.72 At the GP level, positions of Office Assistant (one per GP), Junior Engineer (one for every
four GPs) and Accountant (one for every four GPs) have been created and they are
appointed on contract basis. Funds for payment of remuneration of Office Assistant are
allotted to the District Panchayat Officer out of the Grant funds, who releases the requisite
amount to the GPs.

components of Saat Nishchay schemes, the balance 50 percent is allowed to be used for
payment of arrears of pensionary benefits of retired employees, payment of salaries (of
existing / new employees) and electricity bills and meeting expenditure over solid waste
