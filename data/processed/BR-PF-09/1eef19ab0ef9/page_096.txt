5.6 Urbanization Scenario in Bihar: Urbanization in Bihar is far behind all-India average
and today Bihar is one of the least urbanized states in the country. Bihar’s urbanisation
has increased from lowly figure of 9.59 percent in 1981 to just 11.29 percent in 2011,
as against 22.89 percent and 31.16 percent respectively for all India. Relevant
Urbanisation data is shown in Table 5.1.
5.7 The SFC5 in its report had noted that “Seventy-two new towns, i.e., settlements that
became urban for the first time in 2011, added around 35 percent to the urban
population. Emergence of census towns is a recent development in Bihar and needs to
be continuously monitored. …. The role of rural - urban migration in the process of
Bihar’s urbanization is relatively modest, though rural– urban migration usually is
central to urbanization.”
