3. Finance Commission Transfers: ULBs are recipients of fiscal transfers from
Central and State Governments based on the recommendations of Union and State
Finance Commissions. Such transfers comprise (i) Grant from Central
Government and (ii) Devolution and Grant from Sate Government (Details in
Chapter 8).
4. Scheme Transfers: Funds also flow to the ULBs under State and Central schemes
for specific functions/ purposes, but they are extremely tied in nature. ULBs act as
agency to implement the schemes specified by the respective Government.
7.4 Historically, major part of ULBs’ resources comes through FC transfers and Scheme
transfers. Own revenue of the ULBs of Bihar is low.
