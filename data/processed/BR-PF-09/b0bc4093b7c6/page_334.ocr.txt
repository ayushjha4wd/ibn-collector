e) Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM)-This is one of the flagship projects launched by the
central government in 2014 to improve the sanitation standards in the country. Bihar facing an
acute problem of open defecation and overall sanitation, the state government has joined hands
with the central government in eliminating this problem. Bihar has set a target for the
construction of 3,83,079 toilets in the urban areas. The estimated expenditure is Rs. 759.60 crore
(GOI share + GOB share), to be spread over four years.
