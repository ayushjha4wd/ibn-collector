Status of National Urban Sanitation Policy —

The Urban Development and Housing Department is to facilitate construction of 5.81
lakh Individual Household Latrines (IHHL) by beneficiaries themselves and construction of
66,361 Community Sanitation Centre (CSC) seats for urban families who do not possess own
land and to stop Open Defecation in all 3377 urban wards of 143 Urban Local Bodies. All the
households without toilets, living in urban areas will benefit under this scheme without any
discrimination. Families having adequate land will be given individual toilets and families not
having adequate land for the construction of toilets will be covered through community toilets.
99 urban local bodies have been declared Open Defecation Free (ODF), as of now.
