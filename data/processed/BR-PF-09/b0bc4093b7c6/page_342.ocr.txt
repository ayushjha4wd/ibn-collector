SLPMU appointed and started work from 11 July, 2019. The Current status of implementation of

DEAS are as follow:-

i. Training to Accounting Personnel
FLIAs are regularly organising training at ULB Level and submitting training report in their
monthly report. In addition, FLIAs are also providing handholding support in maintaining
accounts,

ii. Preparation of Budget as Bihar Municipal budget Manual 2014
Most of the Municipalities are now preparing budget as per Bihar Municipal Budget Manual,

2014. Budgets are checked by UD&HD and uploaded on department website
http://udhd. bihar.gov.in/Budget/

iii. Adherence to Bihar Municipal Accounting Manual
Bihar Municipal Accounting Manual was notified in 2014 and being implemented through

DEAS and Internal Audit filed level agencies.
