Annexure-8.1
Methodology for Projection of
Consolidated Fund of State (CFS) and Divisible Pool
1. State's Own Tax Revenue:
a. In the first step, actuals for the period 2015-20 was obtained from the Finance
Department and CAGR for each item was computed (Table-1). Projection for each
year of 2020-26 was made using the growth rates calculated in Table-1. The
projected values are in Table-2.
b. In the second step, in view of the impact of lockdown due to Covid19, the
projected figures were reduced by 30 percent for FY 2020-21, by 20 percent for FY
2021-22, and by 10 percent for FY 2022-23. For the remaining years, original
projections were left unaltered. Projection after adjusting for Covid impact is in
Table-3.
2. State's Own Revenue (Non-Tax Revenue) :
a. Here again, projection was made for the years 2020-26, using its growth rate
during the last 5 years (17.3 percent) (Table 1). Projected values are in Table-2.
b. In view of Covid19 lockdown, the projected figures for only FY 2020-21 was
reduced by 30 percent, the remaining projections being left unaltered. Projection
after adjusting for Covid impact is in Table-3.
3. Share in Central Taxes : The 15 FC has projected the size of Divisible Pool of taxes for the
years 2021-26. Applying the share of Bihar (10.058 percent) to this sum, the share in
central tax for Bihar was projected for each year from 2020-21 to 2025-26 (Table-4).
