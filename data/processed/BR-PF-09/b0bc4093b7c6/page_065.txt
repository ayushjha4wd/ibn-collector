Recommendation for Devolution and Grants in 2020-21
17. In order to make recommendation as to the extent of devolution and grants during
the award period 2020-25, the Commission would need to proceed as follows:
a. Review the receipts and expenditure of the PRIs and ULBs during 2015-20,
b. Make a projection of expenditure during the award period 2020-25 based
on (i) expenditure trends of 2015-20, and (ii) services to be rendered and
schemes and activities planned to be taken up during 2020-25
c. Make an estimate of the resources of the local bodies during award period
taking into account, inter alia, their own revenue and grants
recommended by the 15th Finance Commission.
d. Compute the resource gap and recommend devolution and grants
required to bridge the gap.
