15. PRD would need to initiate concerted action to ensure that the bouquet of
softwares is deployed swiftly across the State and for this to happen, following
would need to be done:
a. Identify an existing organisation or create a new organisation to
develop, own, operate, maintain and propagate a comprehensive IT
system for PRIs. The organisation should have IT professionals with
requisite skills and may be designated as the Local Body IT Agency
(LBITA). PRD can take a cue from the Kerala IT Mission which is an IT
organisation created by the Govt of Kerala exclusively to develop,
manage and operate IT system for PRIs and ULBs of that State.
b. The IT system should have at least the following modules:
i. Plan formulation, approval and monitoring
ii. Online repository of Acts, Rules, Govt orders/ circulars/
guidelines pertaining to local bodies
iii. Financial Management System
iv. Double entry accrual based accounting system
v. Revenue and License System
vi. Payroll and HR System
vii. Module for recording agenda, minutes and follow-up actions of
meetings
viii. Disbursement of social welfare pensions
