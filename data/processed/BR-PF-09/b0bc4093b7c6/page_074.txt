efficiency and rendering citizen-centric services. The following is suggested in
this context:
a. UDHD should ensure that the e-Municipality is developed as a
comprehensive IT system to provide services to citizens as well as to
facilitate office and personnel management, financial management,
supervision and monitoring of schemes in all ULBs. The coverage should
be universal and Nagar Panchayats should not be left out.
b. This system should have, mutatis mutandis, all modules listed in
paragraph 14 (b).
