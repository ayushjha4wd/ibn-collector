20. Devolution: Interaction of the Commission with the elected representatives of the
PRIs and ULBs at all levels have revealed that the local bodies are facing a resource
crunch. This is primarily on two accounts – (a) actual transfer by way of devolution
during 2015-20 was merely 57% of the amount recommended by 5th FC, and (b)
no untied funds were available for the local bodies to cater to local needs.
21. In this context, it may be noted that after analysing State’s data, 5th SFC had come
to the conclusion that “Bihar can easily devolve 12% of the taxes/duties without
strain on its finances. However, in order to make the increases gradual, 8.5%
devolution is recommended for the year 2015-16 and 9% for 2016-17 to 2019-20”.
The State Government, however, adopted the rate of 8.5% for devolution during
each of the five years from 2015-16. As per calculations of 5th SFC, the devolution
required in 2019-20 was 9% and going by their suggestion of gradual increase, this
Commission is of the view that the devolution in 2019-20 should be pegged at 10%
of SOTR.
