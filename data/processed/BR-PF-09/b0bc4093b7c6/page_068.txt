Chapter 3
Other Recommendations
1. Apart from the financial issues, the Commission would like to make
observations on several other aspects of the working of the PRIs and ULBs. It
is not possible to cover all those issues in this interim report. Nevertheless, we
have decided to comment on some issues that need urgent attention, and
these are recorded in the following paragraphs.
Transfer of Functions, Functionaries and Funds
2. Memorandum of the State Government to the 15th Finance Commission in
Chapter VII on Grants to Local Bodies states that “As prescribed in the Eleventh
Schedule of the Constitution, the PRIs at all the three levels have been given
the right of self-governance in respect of 29 subjects of 20 Departments, after
duly completing the activity mapping. The PRIs have been entrusted with 621
types of responsibilities by various departments of the state government from
time to time ...”.
3. The Commission during visits to Gram Panchayats, Panchayat samitis and Zila
Parishads found that they were indeed performing a few functions assigned
by other departments. For example, RTPS Centre located in Panchayat Sarkar
Bhawan is receiving RTPS applications (for birth/ death/ marriage certificates,
caste certificate, income certificate, etc) and filing them online. GPs/ PSs/ ZPs
have been given powers to appoint teachers of schools, supervisors of MDMS
and Anganvadi Sevikas of ICDS but have no role in supervision and monitoring
of schools/ MDMS/ ICDS. The Commission did not find evidence of
performance of as many as 621 functions by PRIs and it appears that even
though activity mapping may have been done, the same have not been acted
upon in most cases.
