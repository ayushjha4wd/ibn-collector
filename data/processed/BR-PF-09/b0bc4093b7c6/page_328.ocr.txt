the proposals of the Development Plan and acting as a link between the Development Plan and
the layout plan in respect of a zone. “Zoning Regulations” mean, in respect of a zone, regulations
for regulating and controlling of land use and enforcing of layout plan and prescribing regulatory
principles for buildings such as permissible Floor Area Ratio, Floor Space Index, height of the
