Terms of Reference
3. The terms of reference of the Commission mandated the following:
a. The Finance Commission shall review the financial position of the
Panchayats ( Zila Parishad, Panchayat Samiti, and Gram Panchayat) and
the Municipalities ( Nagar Nigam, Nagar parishad and Nagar Panchayat )
and make recommendations as to –
a. the principles which should govern—
i. the distribution between the State and the Panchayats of the net
proceeds of the taxes, duties, tolls and fees leviable by the State,
which may be divided between them and the allocation between
the Panchayats at all levels of their respective shares of such
proceeds;
ii. the determination of the taxes, duties, tolls and fees which may
be assigned to, or appropriated by, the Panchayats;
