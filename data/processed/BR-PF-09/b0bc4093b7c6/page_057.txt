16. Recommendations of 15th FC with regard to grants-in-aid to the local bodies will
have a bearing on the availability of resources for their efficient and effective
functioning. The 6th SFC will need to take into account the fund flow from the
Central Government to the PRIs and ULBs while estimating their balance fund
requirements. Also, the recommendations of the 15th FC on devolution of
central taxes would impact the size of the consolidated fund of the State which
this Commission will need to take into account while recommending grants out
of the consolidated fund to the local bodies.
17. As per the TOR, the 15th FC was to submit its report by 30th October 2019, but
its term has been extended by a year and the Commission has submitted an
interim report. The contents of the interim report are not made public yet.
Hence, at this point of time, we do not have any indication of the interim
recommendations as to the grants-in-aid to the local bodies. On the other hand,
the award period of the 5th SFC will come to an end on 31st March 2020. The
State Govt’s budget is likely to be presented in the last week of February 2020.
Therefore, in order to enable the Government to make necessary provisions in
the Budget of FY 2020-21 for devolution of revenues and grants-in-aid to the
PRIs and ULBs, the 6th SFC has resolved to submit this interim report to the State
Government.
