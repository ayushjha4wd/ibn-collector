15. Government of Bihar has submitted a Memorandum to the 15th FC wherein
Chapter VII gives an overview of the working of local bodies in Bihar. At the end
of this Chapter, the State Government has made the following suggestions:
a. While recommending grants for the local bodies, the present Finance
Commission should remember that, unless the capacities of the local
bodies are substantially enhanced, they will not be able to play their
desired roles. For such capacity enhancement, the local bodies need far
more resources than what the previous Finance Commission had
recommended. The present Finance Commission should enhance those
resources substantially.
b. Deviating from the earlier practice, XIVFC had made grants for only Gram
Panchayats, not for other two tiers of PRI — Panchayat Samiti and Zilla
Parishad. The present Finance Commission may restore that earlier
practice of providing adequate grants to all the three tiers of PRI and
help them function effectively.
c. XIVFC had split the total grant for local bodies in two parts — basic grant
and performance grant. The poorer states like Bihar also suffer from
inadequate state capacity and are, therefore, unable to obtain any grant
under the performance head. To help such states, the present
Commission may do away with any performance grant and treat the
entire grant as basic (unconditional) grant. Even if it is essential to retain
the performance grant, it should be ensured that it is only based on
