Recommendations of Central Finance Commissions
5. Article 280(3)(bb) and 280(3)(c) of the Constitution mandate the Central Finance
Commission to recommend measures to augment the Consolidated Fund of a
state government to supplement the resource of PRIs and ULBs.
6. The 13th FC recommended 2.28% of the divisible pool of central taxes to be given
as grants-in-aid to the local bodies in the shape of Basic Grants and Performance
Grants. Both PRIs and ULBs were entitled to receive these grants, their shares
being fixed on the basis of population at 73.18% and 26.82% respectively. Criteria
listed in table 2.8 was used for computing share of each state for grants to PRIs
and ULBs and release of the performance grant was subject to fulfilment of 9
conditionalities.

7. The state government received an allotment of Rs 4810.74 crore for the period
2010-15, out of which the PRIs could utilise only Rs 2676.41 crore (56 percent),
leaving an unspent balance of Rs 2134.33 crore. Performance Grant amounting to
only Rs 167.75 crore was released by the central government on 31 March 2015
for the period 2014-15.

(i) Basic Grant and (ii) Performance Grant, but it made two significant departures

a. The entire PRI grant was given to the GPs since they are directly
