8. During 2015-20, a total sum of Rs 38,210.79 cr was transferred to the local
bodies by the State and Central Governments through devolution and grants.
On an average, a GP received Rs 65 lakh per annum during this period. In
addition, money came for some state and central schemes as well. The fund
flows are likely to double during 2020-25. In this scenario, if the desired result
of expenditure over State’s development through local bodies is to be
achieved, it is absolutely necessary to develop a comprehensive IT system that
would capture and make available in real time financial figures, scheme data,
manpower reports, etc from the local bodies to the government and also act
as a vehicle for dissemination of Government orders, policies and fund
allocations.
9. Some beginnings have been made in this direction. Under the Mission Mode
Project ‘e-Panchayat’ of Govt of India, NIC has developed 12 software
applications known as Panchayat Enterprise Suite (PES). PRD has started
working on the following applications of PES through Bihar Gram Swaraj
Yojana Society (BGSYS) under the World Bank funded Bihar Panchayat
Strengthening Project (BPSP):
a. Local Govt Directory: Captures details of local govt and maps GPs with
Assembly and Parliamentary Constituencies
b. Area Profiler: Captures geographic, demographic, infrastructural,
natural resources and socio-economic data
c. Plan Plus: For preparation of perspective, annual and action plans
