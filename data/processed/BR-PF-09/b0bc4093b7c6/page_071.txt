10. PriaSoft Module of PES is meant to capture expenditure data of schemes
implemented by the three levels of PRIs. Use of this module was abandoned
in 2015-16 and a separate software called Gram Panchayat Management
System (GPMS) was commissioned by BGSYS. This software is an integrated
solution covering accounting, audit and budgeting for GPs. The pilot phase of
software deployment has been completed in 330 GPs and the software is
currently being rolled out in 3,186 GPs of 12 districts.
11. For online monitoring of the Panchayat Sarkar Bhawans under construction,
e-PMS software is being used.
12. NIC has developed the Panchayat NishchaySoft software which is being used
to monitor physical and financial progress of the two Nishchay schemes under
implementation by local bodies, namely “Har Ghar Nal Ka Jal” and “Ghar Tak
Pakki Gali Naaliyan”.

circulars, disbursement of pensions and scholarships, etc.
