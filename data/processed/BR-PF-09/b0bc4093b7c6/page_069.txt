5. It appears that the main reason for activity mapping of most functions
remaining on paper in Bihar is that the respective departments have not
transferred the operational control of their district and block level offices to
the PRIs nor have they given funds for the activities to be carried out by these
offices to the PRIs. Unless this is done, the PRIs will not be able to take up
activities as per mapping.
6. There is an urgent need to operationalize the activity mapping by issuing
orders for operational control of respective PRI over the office and
functionaries of the transferred functions and allotting funds for their
activities to the PRIs. These funds would be over and above the devolution and
grants recommended in Chapter 2. To start with, this should be done for at
least the education, health, agriculture and veterinary sectors. As a first step,
the block level offices of these departments and their functionaries may be
brought under operational control of the Panchayat Samitis. Today, the
Panchayat Samitis have little work at their disposal and they keep trying to
take up functions assigned to the GP. In our view, they are well placed to work
with the transferred offices and their staff. Once this is done successfully,
transfer of District and Panchayat level offices could be taken up.
