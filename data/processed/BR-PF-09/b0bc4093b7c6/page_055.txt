Impact of the Recommendations of the Central Finance Commission on deliberations
of 6th SFC
12. 14th Central Finance Commission had recommended grants from the Central
Government to PRIs and ULBs for supporting their primary functions to deliver
basic services. Accordingly, for the award period of 2015-20, a sum of Rs
23,694.87 cr was allocated for the local bodies of Bihar. Of this, grant of Rs
21,017.84 cr was earmarked for Gram Panchayats and Rs 2,677.03 cr for
Municipal Corporations, Municipalities and Nagar Panchayats. 90% of the grant
was meant to be used for basic services and 10% was performance grant. To
date, actual releases from the Central Govt have been Rs 18,916.062123 cr for
Gram Panchayats and Rs 2227.66 cr for ULBs.
13. Availability of Central Grants for the local bodies for meeting their expenses over
basic services enabled the State Govt to earmark 90% / 50% of the funds
devolved from State’s revenues and grants given out of State’s consolidated
fund to PRIs/ ULBs for implementation of two schemes of Saat Nishchay viz ‘Har
Ghar Nal Ka Jal’ and ‘Ghar Tak Pakki Gali-Naali’.
