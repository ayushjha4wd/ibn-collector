➢ Himachal Pradesh : One IDCC was held under the chairmanship of Secretary,
Health, during the year 2023. DLCC meetings were conducted at Districts of Solan and
Kinnaur.
➢ Karnataka: One IDCC meeting was conducted under the chairmanship of
Secretary to Government, Planning, Program Monitoring and Statistics Department
during the year 2023. 46 District level and 424 Taluk level Coordination Committee
meetings were convened during the year 2023.
➢ Kerala: During the year 2023, one state level meeting on vital statistics was
held which was chaired by the Principal Secretary of local self Government Department,
Kerala.
➢ Madhya Pradesh: 7 DLCC meetings were held in the year 2023.
➢ Maharashtra: No IDCC was conducted during the year 2023. However,
reporting and registration activities were discussed in periodical meeting held via video
conferencing conducted by higher officials.
➢ Manipur: a meeting was conducted regarding 100 percentage digitization of
birth and death registration system in the state in presence of Director of Health
services, Manipur, Joint Director (MAHUD), Deputy Secretary (Home), Deputy Director
(RD & PR), Deputy Municipal Commissioner and all the District Registrar (CMOs) of 16
districts. Laptops and printers were distributed in all the registration functionaries.
➢ Meghalaya: During the year 2023, one IDCC meeting was conducted. Also,
DLCC meeting was held in one district namely, West Jaintia Hills.
➢ Mizoram: The meeting of IDCC was convened at Aizawl under the
Chairmanship of Chief Registrar of Births & Deaths during the year. Also, two DLCC
meetings were convened in the districts, Champhai and Mamit during the year 2023.
➢ Odisha: One IDCC meeting was conducted during the year 2023. Also, 12 DLCC
meetings were conducted at district and block levels.
➢ Punjab: One IDCC meeting was held under the chairpersonship of Secretary,
Health and Family Welfare in the year 2023. District Level Coordination Committees
(DLCC) was formed in the year 2021 under the Chairpersonship of respective Deputy
Commissioner of the District for smooth functioning of CRS at district level. 14 districts
i.e. Barnala, Faridkot, Fatehgarh Sahib, Ferozepur, Fazilka, Gurdaspur, Jalandhar,
Kapurthala, Ludhiana, Moga, Patiala, SBS Nagar, Sangrur, and Tarn Taran have
conducted their DLCC meetings during the year 2023. Instructions have been issued to
all the District Registrars to conduct this DLCC meeting once in a year.
