Still Birth Registration:
• The number of registered still births has decreased from 1.15 lakhs in 2022 to 1.01 lakhs in
2023 (Statement 22, page 58)
• Share of registered still births in rural and urban area is 35.7% and 64.3% respectively
(Statement 22, page 58).
Death Registration:
• The number of registered deaths has increased slightly from 86.5 lakhs in 2022 to 86.6 lakhs
in 2023. (Statement 9, page 41; Chart 2, page 42)
• Out of the total registered deaths, the share of male and female is 60.6% and 39.4%
respectively. (Statement 16, page 51; Chart 7, page 51).
• The share of deaths registered in rural and urban is 57.7% and 42.3% respectively. (Statement
18, page 53; Chart 11 page 54).
• The share of Institutional deaths in total registered deaths is 24.0%. However, this does not
include information from Sikkim (Chart 15, page 57).
• The level of registration of death is 97.2% for the year 2023. (Statement 24, page 63; Chart 16
page 59).
