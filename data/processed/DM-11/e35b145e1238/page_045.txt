2.5. Training of Civil Registration Functionaries- Civil Registration functionaries
involved in registration of births and deaths at various levels in most of the States/UTs
belong to different departments. Almost all of these functionaries perform registration
work as an additional work along with other work assigned to them by their respective
department. In addition, due to frequent transfer of registration functionaries, there is
a need to impart training at regular intervals on RBD Act, 1969 (Amended in 2023) and
registration rules, procedures and practices being followed in the State/UT. As per the
information furnished by the States, the details of training programs organized in
various States during the year 2023 are given below:
➢ Andhra Pradesh: Virtual trainings were conducted for newly recruited/
promoted / transferred registrars and assisting staff in the entire state.
➢ Arunachal Pradesh: CRS /MCCD Training Programme was organized at
Directorate of Economics & Statistics (DES) - HQ for officials during 2023.
➢ Assam: Due to absence of fund in the State budget for implementation of RBD
Act, trainings could not be arranged during the year. However, with the support of
Director Census Operations, Assam and the concerned Joint Director of Health Services
trainings were conducted in a few Districts.
➢ Bihar: 12 Training programmes of officers and officials were organized at
district. Also, 106 trainings were conducted at block levels during the year, 2023 for
capacity building.
➢ Chhattisgarh: 128 training programmes on provisions of RBD Act and State
rules framed thereunder were organized for Civil Registration functionaries at rural and
urban areas and Registrar of institutional registration units.
➢ Goa: Although no formal trainings as such were conducted during the year
2023, regular interactions are continuously held with the Civil Registration
functionaries at the Village Panchayat and Municipal level along with the special birth
and death registration unit at Goa Medical College, Bambolim, in order to solve any
problem/difficulty faced by them in carrying out their day-to-day activities.
