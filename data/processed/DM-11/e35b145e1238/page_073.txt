3.13. Rural-Urban Composition of Registered Births and Deaths in Major States
3.13.1 The Rural-Urban composition of Registered Births and Deaths in major states is
presented in Statement 19. The data on Rural-Urban distribution of birth and death
registrations reveals that the share of rural registration is higher in Assam, Bihar, Jharkhand,
and Uttar Pradesh than the share of Births registered in urban areas. In the remaining 15 Major
States, the share of births registration is higher in case of urban areas. On the other hand, in the
case of Death Registration, the share of Rural Registration is higher in almost all major States
except Haryana, Maharashtra, Uttarakhand, and Telangana.
