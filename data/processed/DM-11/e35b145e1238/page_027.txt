There is a wide variation in the registration machinery across the country,
details of which are given below: -
➢ The Chief Registrars of births and deaths in 17 States (Andhra Pradesh, Assam,
Gujarat, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Odisha, Punjab,
Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand and West Bengal)
and 4 Union Territories (A& N Islands, Chandigarh, Ladakh and Lakshadweep) are from
the Health Department, in 10 States (Arunachal Pradesh, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Goa,
Jharkhand, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Mizoram, Nagaland and Rajasthan) and 2
Union Territories (Delhi and Jammu & Kashmir) are from Planning, Economics &
Statistics Department, in 1 State (Kerala) from Local Self Government Department, in
UT of Dadra & Nagar Haveli & Daman & Diu, Finance Secretary/Secretary (Planning &
Statistics) and in remaining 1 UT (Puducherry) it is with Local Administration
Department.
➢ The district level authorities in the States/UTs such as District Collectors/
District Magistrate, Additional Collector, Chief Medical Officers/ District Medical and
Health, Civil Surgeon, District Statistical/ Planning Officers, Deputy Commissioner,
Deputy Director (Panchayat) and Dy. Director (local administration) are appointed as
District Registrar.
➢ In rural areas, the registration work is entrusted to the officials of Panchayat
Department, Health Department, Revenue Department and Education Department.
