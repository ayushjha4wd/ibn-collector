The following States have also furnished the information regarding inspection: -
➢ Andhra Pradesh: During the year 2023, 1001 Registration Units were
inspected. Besides this, Officers from DCO and CRBD also visited some of the
registration units.
➢ Arunachal Pradesh: 20 Urban and 68 rural registration units were inspected
in the jurisdiction of 18 districts namely Lower Subansiri, Changlang, Namsai, Lower
Dibang Valley, Upper-Siang, Lohit, Tirap, West Siang, East Siang, Kurung Kumey,
Papumpare, KraDaadi, Longding, East Kameng, Siang, Upper Subansiri, Anjaw and
Dibang Valley to ensure qualitative and quantitative improvement of registration work.
Besides this, 41 ADC HQ were also inspected.
➢ Assam: During the year inspection of registration units were initiated in
association with the Directorate of Census Operations, (DCO), Assam.
➢ Bihar: During the year 2023, 1976 Inspections of registration units were made
in 38 districts. Maximum numbers of units were inspected in Darbhanga (312) followed
by Gaya (310) district of Bihar. In addition to above, 2673 Inspections were conducted
in Birth & Death Registration Units related to Health Institutions during special
inspection drive.
➢ Chhattisgarh: 1035 Registration Units covering rural/urban/institutional
units were inspected intensively by officials from the office of the Chief Registrar and
District Registrars (births and deaths) during the year 2023.
➢ Goa: In order to improve the quality and functioning of the Births and Deaths
Registration System in the State, regular inspections are conducted by the Office of the
Chief Registrar of Births and Deaths for checking the physical as well as computerized
records maintained therein.
➢ Gujarat: During the year 2023, 6770 Inspections were carried out in the State.
➢ Himachal Pradesh: 3 Inspections were carried out in the District Kinnaur
during the year 2023.
➢ Jharkhand: During the year 2023, 219 inspections were carried out in the
State.
➢ Karnataka: Besides all routine inspections by State level as well as by District
Registrar and Additional District Registrar, registration centers were also inspected by
the officials of Revenue Department. 1989 inspections of the registration units were
done by the District Statistical Officers of the Directorate and Officers/Officials of the
Revenue Department for the year 2023.
