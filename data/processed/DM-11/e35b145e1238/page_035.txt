➢ Jharkhand: The registration of births and deaths is being done using ORGI
uniform CRS portal (www.crsorgi.gov.in) during 2023. “974211” births and “152750”
deaths were registered online during 2023 in the State.
➢ Karnataka: Registration of birth and death events is being carried out online
in the State through uniform web-based application namely e-JanMa developed by State
NIC since 2018.
➢ Kerala:
• Civil Registration has been computerized in all the registration units by using
the softwares developed by Information Kerala Mission. To register a birth or death in
Grama Panchayats, the Integrated Local Governance Management Software system
and in urban units ‘Sevana’ Civil Registration Software is being used. The Civil
Registration records prior to the date of computerization have been digitized. The
certificates in respect of the registers maintained under the traditional system are also
issued using the software in computerized units.
• Under the Hospital Kiosk Project, online registration of birth and death events
occurring in hospitals has been implemented in 6 Corporations, 70 Municipalities and
243 Gram Panchayats up to the end of 2023.
• Under this Project, the report of births and deaths are sent to the Registration
Unit as electronic data through telephone line from the kiosks, which are functioning
in the hospital. The electronic report received at the Registration Unit is registered
after verification and the certificate under Section 12 is issued through the hospital.
Efforts are made to issue the birth certificate before the mother and baby leaves the
hospital.
➢ Madhya Pradesh: Online registration of births and deaths has been initiated
from 01-01-2015 in the State of Madhya Pradesh using ORGI uniform CRS portal
(www.crsorgi.gov.in). During 2023, 1988575 and 520765 Births and deaths were
registered respectively.
➢ Maharashtra: In the urban and rural areas of the State, the ORGI uniform CRS
portal (www.crsorgi.gov.in) is being used for registration of birth and death events from
2016 onwards.
➢ Manipur: Chief registrar issued several instructions/memorandum to all the
district registrar, registrar and medical officers for 100 percentage digitization of birth
& death in the state during the year 2023.
