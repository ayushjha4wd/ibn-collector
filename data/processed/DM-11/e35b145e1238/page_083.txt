3.17.9 Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil
Nadu, Telangana and West Bengal, are such major 10 States that are recording more than 90 percent of
registered births as well as deaths in 2023. Six Major States Bihar, Kerala, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha,
Chhattisgarh and Assam were placed in category 80-90% for Level of Birth Registration. None of the
Major States found to be having level of registration of births lower than 80 percent. In case of Level of
registration of deaths, 2 States namely Madhya Pradesh and Uttarakhand are in the category 80 to 90
percent while 4 States namely Uttar Pradesh, Assam, Jharkhand and Bihar are placed in the category
50-80 percent. No Major State is found to be having level of Death Registration below the 50 percent.
