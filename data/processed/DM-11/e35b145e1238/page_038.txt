➢ Chandigarh: Registration units of UT are working on the CRS portal since
October 2015. The birth and death data from 1996 onwards are already available on the
portal. Additionally, the legacy data from 1958 to 1995 has been fully scanned and
digitized, and is ready to be uploaded to the CRS portal.
➢ Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu: Registration of birth and deaths in
all the registration Centers in Rural as well as Urban area have been computerized and
online registration is being carried out.
➢ Delhi: All local bodies i.e. New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC), Municipal
Corporation of Delhi (MCD) and Delhi Cantonment Board (DCB)have computerized the
birth & deaths registration system. All hospital are reporting the events online to the
concerned registrar (Births & Deaths). NIC has developed new software for Birth and
Death registration process, addition of name of child, correction in birth/ death records
in Municipal Corporation of Delhi. This has enabled a complete online system to
facilitate citizen to apply Birth and death registration/certificates from their home.
Birth/death certificates can also be downloaded by the Citizens from the websites of
concerned local bodies. In NDMC & DCB, almost all hospital are reporting the vital
events online. Soft copy of the CRS data is supplies along with MCCD to chief Registrar’s
office by the local bodies for preparation of annual report.
➢ Jammu & Kashmir: Registration of Births & Deaths has been reported to carry
through CRS portal across all registration units in UT of J&K except Cantonment Boards
of Satwari (Jammu) and Badami Bagh (Srinagar).
➢ Ladakh: The online Registration system CRS started in UT – Ladakh from
December, 2022 onwards.
➢ Lakshadweep: All the registration units of Hospital/CHC/PHC are
computerized with support of UT of Lakshadweep Administration from 2008 onwards.
Now all Births, Deaths & still births are registered online by using the CRS uniform
software provided by the ORGI and therefore, online certificates are issued on time.
➢ Puducherry: All local bodies are issuing computerized birth and death
certificates for the event registered under the Act. In order to cater the needs of the
public who requires birth and death certificates of their ancestors, computerization of
birth and death record has been taken up for the period prior to 1979 in Puducherry
and Oulgaret Municipalities. The computerized birth records from 1930 and death
records from 1954 are available in Puducherry Municipality. In Oulgaret Municipality
birth records from 1920 and death records from 1979 are available.
