3.18 Sex Ratio at Birth
3.18.1 The Sex Ratio (Number of females per thousand males) at birth of registered events is an
important indicator to map the sex differential of the population at the beginning of their life.
The sex ratio at birth has been calculated after deducting the delayed registration of more than
1 year for the year 2023. Statement 26 shows Sex Ratio at Birth (SRB) based on registered events
for the years 2017-2023. Sex Ratio at Birth based on registered events for the year 2023 for All
India (excluding figures of Sikkim) stood at 928 females per thousand males.
