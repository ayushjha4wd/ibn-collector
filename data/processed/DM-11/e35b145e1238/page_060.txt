3.6 The number of registered births has decreased from 254.4 lakhs in 2022 to 252.1 lakhs
in 2023 – a decrease of about 0.9%. The birth registration has increased in the year 2023 in
States/UTs of Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, West Bengal, Haryana, Gujarat,
Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Andhra Pradesh, Manipur, Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, Mizoram,
D& N Haveli & Daman & Diu, Ladakh, Sikkim, A& N Islands and Chandigarh compared to 2022,
while in ten major states, namely, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Maharashtra, Assam, Telangana, Kerala,
Uttarakhand, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, and Karnataka, birth registration has decreased in the year
2023 compared to 2022.
