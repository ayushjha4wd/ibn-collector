➢ Manipur: 5000 copies of leaflets on importance of births and deaths
registration were distributed in all the 16 districts in 2023.
➢ Mizoram: Adequate publicity has been made on the importance of Civil
Registration. Advertisements were often displayed in the daily newspapers and
periodicals in local language. Young Mizo Association (YMA), an NGO, and local
council/village council took up the responsibility of informing the public about the
importance of reporting vital events to the Registrar of births and deaths.
➢ Nagaland: Pamphlets, flyers, and posters were printed highlighting the
importance of online and timely registration of Births and Deaths and disseminated to
AHODs/Deputy Commissioner Offices/Medical Institutions/Municipal Councils,
Churches and Local Bodies in the State and it has also been shared in the local dailies
newspaper in Nagaland Post and Morung Express and also advertised in the digital
media Hornbill TV channel, Department of Information & Public Relations(DIPR) portal
for wide publicity.
➢ Odisha: Adequate publicity through Electronic media, All India Radio, Display
of Posters, Distribution of leaflets, screening of the films etc., has been done in the State
during the year on registration of births and deaths to popularize the Civil Registration
within community.
➢ Punjab: The following publicity measures were taken by the State: -
• The District Registrar and Local Registrars have been instructed to display
boards or wall painting on the various provisions of the Act and procedure at important
places in their offices for public awareness.
• Instructions issued to display publicity material in the various Exhibitions
organized by the department at block /village level.
• Regular announcements on the importance of registration of births and deaths
have been made through public address system of Village Gurudwaras. Paramedical
staff also spread awareness in immunization camps during their routine visits.
• Instructions were issued to District and Local Registrars to use CRS logo and
message of birth and death registration in regular correspondence.
➢ Rajasthan: Publicity on registration of birth and death were made through
Radio Jingles and FM Bands, through advertisements made in Newspapers, awareness
though Chaupal Programme of Doordarshan, through appeal made awareness on Births
and Deaths Registration by District administration during the year 2023.
