• With IPA funding, Training of Trainers (2 CRS functionaries from each district)
on Civil Registration System (CRS) was conducted during 2023.
• One-day training and testing of the Revamped CRS (ORGI) Portal was
conducted by the Office of Census Operations, Meghalaya for two Master Trainers.
• With IPA funding, Hands-On Training was conducted by the two Master

➢ Mizoram: During the year 2023, annual training of RBD, Act and Rules was
convened at all 11 district headquarters and at the same time general guidelines and
registration procedure including frequently asked questions (FAQ) in Mizo (local)
dialect were issued by office of the Additional Chief Registrar and the same was
distributed to all Registration Units.
➢ Nagaland: A one day Training of Trainers on Online Civil Registration System
was conducted in the DES to train the district staff as resource persons for imparting
training to Registrars of Births & Deaths in their respective districts.
➢ Punjab: The training of new officials and re-orientation courses for
registration functionaries are arranged at State headquarters to keep the functionaries
well versed with the latest clarifications and provisions of the Act. The participants are
also attending various courses at the Regional Health Statistics Training Centre SAS
Nagar and Health and Family Welfare Training Centre, Amritsar. The State Institute of
Health and Family Welfare Training, Mohali has also imparting trainings on the subject
of registration of births and deaths. Apart from this, Department of Governance Reforms
is also providing training to all the registration functionaries involved in the process of
various birth and death related services under E-Sewa regarding usage of this
application
➢ Rajasthan: 1 State level, 12 District level and 56 block level training
programmes were organized in the State for Civil Registration functionaries during the
year 2023. In all, 69 Training programmes were conducted in which 5145 participants
were trained.
➢ Tamil Nadu: Frequent orientation sessions were conducted at district and
state levels, including updates on the latest executive instructions and amendments to
the Registration of Births and Deaths Act(amendment), 2023. These sessions were held
via Zoom. Joint District Births and Deaths Registrars, Statistical Assistants, and other
CRS functionaries were trained on the highlights of the amended Act, with presentations
shared across districts.
