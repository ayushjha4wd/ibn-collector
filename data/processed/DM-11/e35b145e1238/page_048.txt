➢ Uttarakhand: Training programme related to birth and death registration
were held from time to time at Primary Health Centre, Community Health Centre, Chief
Medical Officers office in all the district of state. Online training programme was also
conducted by the directorate of census operations for providing portal related
information and online registration to the registrar in the district. Trainees were trained
on online registration and issuance of certificate through CRS portal.
➢ Uttar Pradesh: On the special request of the Director General of UP academy
of administration and management, Birth and Death Registration Act has been included
as a mandatory component in institutional curriculum of IAS and PCS officers in the
state Academy. Separate three days online programmes were conducted for these
officers and one day offline training session was organized for the registrar of the urban
areas. Apart from this, 8 training sessions were organized at State Institute of Health
and Family Welfare (SIHFW) for medical officers and officers/employees working in
registration work.
➢ West Bengal: Re-orientation training programmes are being conducted by the
Dy. Chief Registrar of Births & Deaths in the State. 24 training programmes were
organized at District level during the year 2023.
➢ Chandigarh: One training programme was conducted for Civil Registration
Functionaries during the year 2023.
➢ Delhi: Municipal Corporation of Delhi organized 15 Training programmes for
the Civil Registration functionaries. Besides this, a half day training programme was
also arranged on MCCD.
➢ Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu: Hands-on training was provided to
concerned registration functionaries during the regular visits/inspections.
➢ Jammu & Kashmir: 46 training programmes were organized at district
level/Regional level for all registration functionaries during the year 2023
➢ Puducherry: Training Programme on the migration of data in Birth and Death
Information System (BDIS) of State Portal to ORGI’S Civil Registration System (CRS)
software was conducted for the Civil Registration functionaries during the year 2023
for 2 days in Puducherry and Karaikal regions respectively and 1 day each in Mahe and
Yanam regions.
➢ No Training: 11 States/UTs namely, Madhya Pradesh, Sikkim, Telangana,
Tripura, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, and Ladakh, did not organize any training
