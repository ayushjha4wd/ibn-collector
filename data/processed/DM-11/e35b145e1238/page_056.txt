➢ Haryana: Births & Deaths Amended Act 2023 was implemented in the State.
Lack of Budget on Training for Civil Registration functionaries like Registrars need to
be trained regarding the new CRS Portal and the Amended Act.
➢ Himachal Pradesh: Local Registrars cum Panchayats Secretaries in concerned
Panchayats are not posting vital events online regularly. There is shortage of manpower
in some of the registration units across the State. Moreover, shortage of manpower and
budget in most of the health institutions across State and District Headquarter is also
affecting the routine working of online CRS.
➢ Jharkhand: Some difficulties are encountered in implementing the provisions
of RBD Act and Rules in respect of delayed registration. Difficulties in timely submission
of reports and supervision due to lack of Block Statistical Supervisor at block level.
Difficulties under uniform CRS software in obtaining affidavit by general public for
reporting events after 30 days of occurrence.
➢ Karnataka: Inadequacy of staff at all levels. Lack of administrative authority of
the Chief Registrar over registration functionaries.
➢ Kerala: Pre-occupation of Registrars and other functionaries with other
duties. Damage caused to very old registration records. Non-registration of large chunk
of infant and child deaths particularly in rural areas. Inclusion of details without care in
registration by the public and request for correction of particulars in the registers as
entered in other documents pertaining to the individuals. Lack of proper training are
some of the difficulties faced in the implementation of the Act.
➢ Madhya Pradesh: Vacant posts of Assistant Statistical Officer, Statistical Clerks
and Clerks in various Municipal Corporations and Municipalities; Lack of control over
Notifiers in the rural areas.
➢ Maharashtra: In rural area yet to perceive, the real need of the registration of
births and deaths. The Gram Sevaks who is Ex-officio Registrar of births and deaths, has
to cover many villages due to vacant posts. The situation may improve with increase by
appointing Gram sevak where posts are vacant as well as if possible appointing assistant
for day to day birth and death registration work.
➢ Mizoram: The geographic location of the State has affected the registration
process. Gap in communication and transportation facilities hampers timely reports
making it difficult to consistently maintain 100% level of reporting.
➢ Nagaland: Inadequate postal, transportation and communication facilities.
