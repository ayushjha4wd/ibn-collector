Maharashtra, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Odisha, Punjab, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Tripura, West Bengal, A& N

followed by the Telangana, Himachal Pradesh, Rajasthan, Lakshadweep, and Chhattisgarh registering
more than 90 percent level of registration of deaths. 21 States/UTs have recorded their level of
registration of deaths higher than the National level of 97.2% while remaining 15 States/ UTs have their
level of registration of deaths below the national level. 8 major States namely Bihar, Jharkhand, Assam,
Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Rajasthan features among States that
have level of death registration below the National Level.
