➢ Chhattisgarh: Publicity has been made through Pamphlets / Poster (4 Types
of A3 size posters) for birth and death registration. Video circulated on youtube (Hindi
and local language) for Birth and death registration.
➢ Goa: Information regarding various services provided by the Births and Deaths
Registration Units, fees charged for these services, timeline for delivery of services, etc.
have been displayed in all the offices of the Civil Registration functionaries for creating
awareness among the citizens.
➢ Gujarat: Registrars were sensitized for the early registration of birth and death
event during Monthly meetings held at the Taluka level.
➢ Himachal Pradesh: Posters for awareness on birth and death registration
were provided to district registrar to distribute among all the health and development
blocks during the year 2023.
➢ Karnataka: During the year 2023, wide publicity regarding importance of
registration of births & deaths were made through Newspapers and all publications
published by Directorate of Economics and Statistics. A full-fledged help desk with toll
free number 1800-425-6578 has been established for providing information on birth
and death registration in e-JanMa software and to resolve the grievances for the public.
➢ Kerala: In creating awareness among the public regarding the importance and
necessity of registration of vital events, various publicity measures were taken in the
year 2023 such as: -
• Arranged publicity measures through Doordarshan and All India radio.
• Permanent Notice Boards were exhibited in front of all registration units.
• Social media like YouTube videos WhatsApp and departmental training
programs are helping to popularize the importance of Civil Registration.
• The details of services rendered, name of designated officer, time limit for
registering births/deaths and the duties of the public under Civil Registration
System are included in the ‘Citizen’s Charter’ published by the Gram Panchayats
and Municipalities under the Kerala Panchayat Raj Act 1994 and Kerala
Municipality Act 1994.
➢ Maharashtra: Official correspondence was made to all Registrars for proper
publicity in their jurisdiction to create awareness in general public. An article narrating
importance of registration and procedure of registration of births & deaths has also
been published in ‘Arogya Patrika’ of Information, Education & Communication (IEC)
Bureau of Public Health Department.
