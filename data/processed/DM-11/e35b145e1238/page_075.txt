3.14 Medical attention received at the time of birth and death
3.14.1 The complete information in respect of birth by type of medical attention at the time of
delivery and medical attention received by the deceased at the time of death during the year
2023 has been received from 35 States/UTs. As partial information was submitted by Sikkim,
its data has not been considered. In the case of Punjab, the relevant information excludes cases
registered more than 1 year. The distribution of medical attention received at the time of birth
for these States/UTs has been illustrated in Chart 14. The data shows that the majority of births
have occurred in institutions, either in government or private ones. Institutional births
occurred in about 74.7% of cases, while 6.2% of the cases were attended by a
Physicians/Nurse/midwife. However, about 9.6% of the events were attended by an untrained
midwife/ Dai, and another 9.5% by others, including Not stated/Not Available.
3.14.2 In the case of Medical Attention received by deceased at the time of death, as evident
from Chart 15, 24.0% of the total registered deaths have occurred in institutions; about 12.9%
of registered deaths were reported under the category of medical attention other than
institutions. About 2.6% of the total registered deaths have received medical attention from
qualified medical practitioners, and 53.4% of deceased did not receive any medical attention at
the time of death.
