➢ Rajasthan: Besides one IDCC meeting at State level, 14 DLCCs meetings were
held during the year 2023.
➢ Tamil Nadu: One IDCC meeting under the chairmanship of Additional Chief
Secretary Health and Family Welfare Department and 69 DLCC meetings were
conducted in all districts under Chairmanship of District Collectors and District
Revenue Officers and District Registrars of births and deaths during 2023. Also, The
Government of Tamil Nadu have included the status of Civil Registration System and
Medical certification of cause of death as one of the agendas in collectors conference.
Revenue Department also conduct review meeting to all District Registrar on
performance of District with the subject Birth and Death Registration. At the District
level.
➢ Uttarakhand: In the IDCC meeting held at the end of 2022 under the
Chairmanship of Chief Secretary, complete migration to CRS portal from 1 January 2023
was directed and during the year 2023, user ID and others details for online registration
were exchanged. Meetings to remove the difficulties faced by registration functionaries
meeting were conducted during the year 2023.
➢ Uttar Pradesh: During the 2023, IDCC and DLCC were reconstituted at state
and district levels respectively. In 2023, 2 IDCCs, 5 Mandal IDCCs and 147 DLCCs were
conducted. These committees play an important role in effective monitoring of birth
and death registration and implementing innovative measures. The major innovatives
included an order by government directing government hospital to provide birth
certificate before the mother is discharged, linking of birth and death registration with
various beneficiaries schemes through aadhaar based authentication, integrating
ManTra new born tracking application with CRS.
➢ West Bengal: One IDCC meeting at State level and 5 DLCC meetings were
arranged during 2023.
➢ Andaman & Nicobar Islands: The registration of Births and Deaths in the
Island is solely carried out by the Health Department except one Defence Hospital which
is solely monitored by the O/o the CRBD which ensure better co-ordination and
effective functioning among Civil Registration functionaries. No IDCC meeting was held
during 2023.
➢ Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu: Due to Administrative reasons IDCC
