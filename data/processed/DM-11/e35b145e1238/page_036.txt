➢ Meghalaya: Online registration process was implemented in 46 Registration
Units. Computerization of old Civil Registration Records has not been possible due to
lack of fund.
➢ Mizoram: Digitization of all the Civil Registration Records in Mizoram was
completed during 2019. These digitized records contain some minor errors and the
same are being rectified. Online Registration is not yet done at the Registration Unit
level. However, online submission to ORGI’s CRS portal of all the reports of live Births,
still births and deaths are done at the respective ADR Offices. Moreover, all the records
are also kept in digitized form in the State specific software 'CRS Mizoram’.
➢ Nagaland: Births & Deaths data is compiled through computerization in every
district headquarter. Online registration of births and deaths through CRS ORGI portal
was launched in the state on 19th April 2022. Currently the Registration units in the
village/block level are functioning manually only but efforts are being made through
training for digitization of CRS in the rural areas. Online registration at district level not
functional due to connectivity issues. The State is taking concrete steps to upgrade the
ICT capability of the district headquarters for implementation of the ORGI uniform CRS
portal (www.crsorgi.gov.in) for online registration.
➢ Odisha: Online registration of births and deaths in 116 urban registration units
and all 314 Rural registration units started from 01.01.2021 in OBDRS portal, developed
by the State, and is continuing till date.
➢ Punjab: The e-Sewa application was introduced by the Department of
Governance Reforms (DoGR), Punjab in July, 2019 after revamping the earlier e-District
application. The services of issuance of birth and death certificate, addition of name of
child, correction in record and delayed registration of birth and death have been
included in the e-Sewa application and 535 Sewa Kendra’s were established in the State.
All the birth and death certificates are now being issued electronically through eSewa
portal. If a citizen applies for a birth or death certificate and the relevant registration
record is still maintained in manual form then the related entry is digitized through
eSewa application and then a computer-generated certificate is issued.
➢ Rajasthan: The online registration of births and deaths through the web portal
‘Pehchan’ is being done since 01.01.2014 in the State. 100 percent computerization and
online registration is going on in the State.
