➢ Tamil Nadu: The common CRS software (www.crstn.org) is implemented
uniformly throughout the State from 2018 onwards for registration of birth and death
and to issue/download the birth and death certificates. The digitization of legacy
records of registered births and deaths are carried out in the phased manner and the
data entry of about 3 crore data have been done and the process of transferring it to the
TNCRS portal is under process.
➢ Telangana: Civil registration records are fully digitalized in the State.
➢ Tripura: The work of online registration of births & deaths and reporting of
each event has been started through ORGI uniform CRS software (www.crsorgi.gov.in)
all over the State from 8th April 2016 onwards.
➢ Uttarakhand: The online registration of births & deaths is done in complete
State through ORGI uniform CRS software (www.crsorgi.gov.in). Computerization of old
registration record was done partially some districts due to shortage manpower in
others birth and death registration work.
➢ Uttar Pradesh: The registration of births and deaths is being done using ORGI
uniform CRS portal (www.crsorgi.gov.in) and computerized birth and death certificates
are issued by all registration units. Tablets have been provided to health units for
implementation of aadhaar based birth registration process for birth registration of
new born. Apart from this, birth and death registration data is link with various
beneficiaries schemes such as shramik kalyan yojana, Mukhya Mantri Kanya sumangla
yojana and Parivar Labh yojana through API based authentication.
➢ West Bengal: The State’s own portal Janma Mrityu Tathya (JMT) has been used
since 5th May, 2022 and the use of ORGI Portal has been discontinued. CRS Portal & JMT
Portal have been integrated and almost all data registered in CRS Portal are available as
old legacy data in JMT Portal. During the year, “1427576” births and “662991” deaths
were registered using the State’s own Software.
➢ Andaman & Nicobar Islands: The ORGI uniform CRS online software
(www.crsorgi.gov.in) has been implemented in all the registration units w.e.f. 01-01-
2016 and all the old birth and death records have been uploaded in CRS portal w.e.f.
Aug 1982.
