15. The birth/death reported after 30 days but within one year of its occurrence can be
registered with the written permission of the District Registrar or such other authority and
on production of self-attested documents and on payment of such fee as prescribed.
16. Births and deaths reported after one year of its occurrence shall be registered only on an
order of the District Magistrate or Sub-Divisional Magistrate or by an Executive Magistrate
authorised by the District having jurisdiction over the area where birth or death has taken
place after verifying the correctness of birth and death and on payment of the prescribed
fee.
17. A birth can be registered without the name of the child.
18. Name of the child can be entered in the birth certificate within 12 months without any fee
and after one year but within 15 years of registration on payment of prescribed fee and by
approaching the concerned Registrar.
19. Error(s), if any, in the particulars of birth or death records can be corrected as per the
prescribed procedure under Section 15 of the Act.
20. Any entry in the birth or death records made fraudulently or improperly, can be cancelled
by the concerned Registrar.
21. Under Section 17 of the RBD Act, any person can cause a search in the birth or death
registers for any entry and obtain a certified extract. Such extracts shall be admissible as
evidence of birth or death to which the entry relates.
22. All Registrars are required to send a monthly return regarding entries of births and deaths
in the register kept by them. to the concerned Addl. District Registrar/District Registrar or
Chief Registrar.
23. It is mandatory for all medical institutions to provide a certificate as to the cause of death
to the Registrar and a copy of the same to the nearest relative
24. Appointment of Special "Sub-Registrars" in the event of disaster or epidemic for speedy
registration of deaths and issue of certificates.
25. The birth of an Indian Citizen, occurred outside the country, can also be registered in India
within sixty days of return of the parents at the place where they intend to settle. After this
period, it could be registered under delayed registration provisions of the Act.
26. There is no provision to register death of an Indian Citizen that has occurred outside the
country.
27. Non-reporting of births and deaths, giving false information for inclusion in the register,
refusing to put signature in the reporting form/register and non-registration of events
reported to the Registrar is punishable under the Act.
28. Registrars and Sub-Registrars are deemed to be the public servants.
