2.8. Difficulties encounter in implementation of the Act: Some of the States have
furnished the details of problems facing in implementing the provision of RBD Act and
State Rules in the State/UT. The brief points given by the State Governments in their
annual report is summarized below: -
➢ Andhra Pradesh: No fresh sanction of posts of Statistical staff was made in
2023. The lack of budget under CRS and MCCD towards trainings, Publicity, inspection
and printing of forms affects the successful implementation of RBD Act in the State.
There are about 1.8 crore legacy events in UBD Portal of State. The data porting from
UBD to CRS software need to be completed.
➢ Arunachal Pradesh: Inadequate infrastructure is the major problem for
implementation of the Act. Further, due to shortage of vital statistics staff, lack of proper
communication, illiteracy among the villagers, villages being scattered in far flung areas,
etc. caused the issue of under reporting.
➢ Assam: Non-availability of CRS funds in State budget for implementation of
RBD Act, 1969. Due to this Trainings and inspections could not be carried out
adequately.
➢ Bihar: Guidance/ directions were issued to regional offices to resolve the
difficulties faced by them in implementation of the RBD Act during the year 2023.
➢ Goa: All the Births & Deaths records from 1971 onwards have been
computerized; there is a possibility of damage to the physical Birth/Death records as
the Registers containing these records are worn out over the years. Therefore, this
Office is planning to digitize these physical records during the year 2024, so that the
same can be stored in the digital format, before getting fully damaged.
➢ Gujarat: Some post such as Research Officer, Research Assistant and Statistical
Assistant are lying Vacant this may delay administrative/technical work.
