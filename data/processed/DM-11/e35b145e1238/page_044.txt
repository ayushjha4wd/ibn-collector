➢ Tamil Nadu: Regular inspections of registration units were emphasized. Joint
District Registrars and Statistical Assistants were instructed to inspect major
registration units, such as hospitals, on a scheduled basis to ensure proper reporting
and online entries. Inspections also included cross-verification of accident deaths,
suicides, and homicides reported by police departments to ensure accurate data entry.
➢ Tripura: Inspections were performed by Data Processing Assistant from time
to time in year 2023.
➢ Uttarakhand: Regular Inspections of the Registration Units were made by the
appointed Registration authorities and suggestions and directions were given to
resolve the problem faced by Registrar in registering event. Inspections on up keep of
civil registration record were carried by officials of State appointed for Civil registration
work. Difficulties faced at registration unit level were removed in such inspection.
➢ Uttar Pradesh: During the year 2023, 592 Registration Units were inspected
by Officers from the office of the Chief Medical Officer. A well organized and logical form
was developed for inspections. These reports were sent to Districts and Additional
District Registrar for rectification of deficiencies mentioned in the reports.
➢ West Bengal: During the year 2023, 418 Rural and 42 Urban Registration units
were inspected.
➢ Andaman & Nicobar Islands: During the year 2023, 15 Registration Units
were inspected.
➢ Chandigarh: Inspections in 6 Government Registration units and 28 Private
Hospitals & Nursing homes were made in the year 2023.
➢ Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu: During the year 2023, regular visits
/ inspection on were carried out on Monthly basis in all three districts.
➢ Delhi: During the year 2023, MRD branch of Aruna Asaf Ali Hospital was jointly
inspected by officers of Directorate of Census Operations (DCO), Delhi and CRBD office.
DCO, Delhi and CRBD Office jointly inspected.
➢ Jammu & Kashmir: During the year 2023, eighty four (84) inspections were
carried out at district level in the UT.
➢ Lakshadweep: Inspection was carried out in two Islands during the year 2023.
➢ Puducherry: All the Civil Registration Centres in the Puducherry Union
Territory were inspected during the year 2023.
➢ No Inspections: No inspections of registration Units were carried out in the
States/UTs namely Haryana, Manipur, Meghalaya, Ladakh.
