2.3. Penalties: There is a provision for imposing penalties on defaulting
institutions, and the individuals for non-reporting and for providing false information
and also on Registrars for negligence or refusal to register any birth or death without
any reasonable cause under section 23 of the RBD Act, 1969. Despite the aforesaid
provision, most of the State Governments are not imposing penalties on the defaulters
It has been found that very few States have reported the details on imposing the
penalties. The details of imposing penalties and prosecutions were not provided by
majority of States. However, few States have initiated actions in this regard, the details
of which are given below: -
➢ Andhra Pradesh: During the year 2023, show-cause notices were issued to the
Registrars by the District Registrars, where Births and Deaths events were registered
without proper field verification.
➢ Arunachal Pradesh: No penalties were imposed under section 23 of the RBD
Act, 1969 during 2023 on defaulting institutions, Registrars, and individuals for non-
registration /non-reporting and for negligence to register any birth/death.
➢ Assam: No prosecutions of CRS functionaries were done during the year 2023,
but some of the officials were called for explanation for irregular practices and
reminded not to delay the process.
➢ Chhattisgarh: Only in two cases penalties were imposed in the Year 2023.
➢ Haryana: All medical institutions in the state have been registered in the CRS
portal and are reporting the events online in time. There was no such institution in
knowledge which did not reporting births / deaths events in CRS portal. Also, some of
the birth and death registration services have been covered under Haryana Right to
Service Act, 2014. Time frame for each and every service has been fixed under the RTS
Act and regular monitoring is being done at senior level. Hence no delay in action at the
level of Registrar was observed. Therefore, no penalty was imposed on institution
during year 2023.
➢ Karnataka: No prosecution was launched during the year. Penalization was
avoided and improvement was attempted through educative, publicity/propaganda
and persuasive measures.
