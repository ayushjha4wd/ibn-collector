➢ Haryana: A meeting of Statistical Assistants is organized at state HQ every
month. In these meetings, besides collecting the monthly returns, participants were
trained on day-to-day functioning of the system. Their problems were also sorted out in
these monthly meetings.
➢ Himachal Pradesh: During the year 2023, 3 training programmes were
organized in District Kinnaur.
➢ Jharkhand: 24 District level and 264 block level training programmes were
organized during the year 2023. These programmes were attended by 1440 and 10581
participants respectively at District and block level.
➢ Karnataka: 125 trainings were conducted during 2023. District Statistical
Officers conducted training programs in all the Taluks and in Bruhath Bangalore
Mahanagara Palike to improve the Civil Registration System. Detailed procedures of
RBD Act and State Rules, Clarifications and discrepancies in filling up of reporting forms
and use of e-JanMa software were also discussed during training.
➢ Kerala: CRS training to all local registrars was conducted under the leadership
of Kerala Institute of Local Administration using the funds of the state government
during the year 2023. The staff of the hospital kiosks have been trained online with the
help of software under the guidance of Chief Registrar and Additional Chief Registrar to
ensure accuracy of information in birth and death registrations.
➢ Maharashtra: An Online training has been conducted for Registrars, Births
and Deaths. Besides this, two training programmes were conducted on the subject CRS
for newly appointed medical officers and statistical investigators during 2023.
➢ Manipur: Two days training progammes on digitization of birth and deaths
registration system for Civil Registration functionaries was organized during 2023. This
training programme was attended by Registrars/Sub Registrars.
➢ Meghalaya: During the year 2023, Following efforts were made on training:
• Training was conducted by the Office of Census Operations, Meghalaya on
Online Registration of Births & Deaths using Office of the Registrar General, India
(ORGI) Portal, for CRS functionaries of 6 Registration Units.
• Virtual Training was conducted by the Office of Census Operations, Meghalaya
on Online Registration of Births & Deaths using Office of the Registrar General, India
(ORGI) CRS Portal.
