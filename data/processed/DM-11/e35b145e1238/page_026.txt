CHAPTER-II
ANNUAL REPORT FOR 2023 ON THE WORKING OF
THE REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS AND DEATHS (RBD) ACT, 1969
(AMENDED IN 2023)
2.1. Annual report on the working of the Registration of Births and Deaths
(RBD) Act, 1969(Amended in 2023): Under the provision of Section 4 (4) of the Act,
the Chief Registrars are required to submit an Annual Report on the working of the RBD
Act to the respective State Governments once approved, a copy of the same is sent to the
Office of Registrar General of India (ORGI). The ORGI compiles the reports received from
States/UTs and release the working of RBD Act report at National level. The aforesaid
Annual Report for the year 2023 has been received from all the States/UTs.
2.2. Registration Machinery: Section 3 to 7 of the Registration of Births and
Deaths (RBD)Act, 1969(amended in 2023) provides for the appointment of Civil
Registration System functionaries at all administrative and Jurisdictional levels viz.: -
(i) National level:- Registrar General of India; (Section 3 of the RBD Act)
(ii) State level:- Chief Registrar; (Section 4 of the Act)
(iii) District level: - District Registrar/Additional District Registrar (Section 6 of the
Act )
(iv) Local area level: - Registrar for a village, Panchayat, municipality, municipal
Corporation, or other local bodies; (Section 7(1) of the Act), and;
(v) Sub-Registrar for specified area within the area under the jurisdiction of a
Registrar (Section 7 (5) of the Act).
The Registrar General of India (RGI) at Central level takes steps to coordinate
and unify the activities of Chief Registrars of all States/Union Territories and issues
general directions regarding registration of births and deaths under section 3(3) of the
Act. The work relating to Civil Registration in the office of the Registrar General of India
is handled by the Vital Statistics Division.
The Chief Registrar, appointed by the State Government, is the chief executive
authority in the respective States/ UTs for implementing the provisions of RBD Act and
the Rules and orders made thereunder. The State Government also appoints District
