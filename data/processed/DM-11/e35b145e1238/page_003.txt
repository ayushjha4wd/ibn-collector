PREFACE
Birth and Deaths are the two most important vital events that define life of an individual. Not
only do they describe the legal existence of an individual, registration of these events is a primary
source of vital data of the population to which they belong. Civil Registration is a continuous,
permanent, compulsory recording of the occurrence and characteristics of vital events, like births,
deaths and still births. These vital statistics are invaluable for planning, monitoring and evaluating
various programmes related to primary health care, family planning, maternal and child health,
education etc. Information collected through the registration process about vital events provides
useful and important statistics.
2. This issue of the Report on ‘Vital Statistics of India based on the Civil Registration System’ for
the year 2023 provides an overview of the working of the Civil Registration System (CRS) in the
country and presents a compilation of data on registered births, deaths, infant deaths and still births
with rural-urban & sex-wise break-up and sex ratio at birth based on Civil Registration Records.
Based on the Annual Reports of the States/UTs, the report for the year 2023 containing key
information of events pertaining to the period 1st January 2023 to 31st December 2023 has been
brought out for the convenience of users.
3. The publication is divided into two parts. Part-I provides a brief review of the Civil Registration
System and contains a summary analysis of data on registered births, deaths, infant deaths and sex
ratio at birth based on registered births at the State level. Part-II consists of the main tables which
provide State and District-wise data on registered births, deaths, infant deaths and still births with
rural-urban & sex-wise break-up.
4. The data source for the report is the statutory Annual Statistical Report prepared by the Chief
Registrars of Births & Deaths of States/UTs. I take this opportunity to express my sincere gratitude
to all the Chief Registrars of Births & Deaths of various States/UTs for their kind cooperation in
providing the requisite information. I am thankful to each one of them for their invaluable and
continuing cooperation.
5. I would like to take this opportunity to mention that the Registration of Births & Deaths Act
(RBD Act), 1969 has been amended for the first time since its inception, through the Registration of
Births & Deaths Act, 1969(Amended in 2023) (No. 20 of 2023) with a view to accommodate changes
and new developments in the society during the last five decades. Various provisions of the Act have
been suitably amended to enhance the convenience of general public in getting the events registered.
I am hopeful that more dynamic implementation of the provisions of the RBD Act 1969(amended in
2023) shall help reduce the time gap and establish this as a reliable source of information on Birth
and Death registration in the country.
