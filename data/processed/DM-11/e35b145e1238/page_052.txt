• Various IEC (Information, Education, and Communication) modes were used to
create awareness about the importance of birth and death registration. Campaigns
were conducted in educational institutions, hospitals, and public places.
• Wide publicity was given to the extension of the time limit for name inclusion
in birth records until 31.12.2024. Advertisements were published in newspapers to
inform the public about the importance of registration and the extended time limit for
name inclusion.
• Sample IEC materials were shared with district authorities for display in
delivery wards and public gathering areas. Registrars were instructed to display name
boards with their working hours in government institutions to inform the public about
their availability.
➢ Uttarakhand: Importance of Births & Deaths registration was discussed in
departmental and other meetings at district and block level and emphasis was laid on
informing the general public about the importance of birth and death registration.
Special emphasis was laid on 100% registration by creating public awareness through
ANM/Aaganwadi/ ASHA worker of the area. In addition, awareness among school
children on registration of birth and death was spread during other programs organized
in schools.
➢ Uttar Pradesh: An amount of 2.44 crore was allocated to all districts by state
to publicize among general public through hoardings/wall paintings/FM radios at the
district levels. Besides this, a short video film was made on the automatic birth
registration process through ManTra new born tracking application in government
hospitals. Apart from this information boards displaying name of registrar visiting
hours, room no. etc. were installed in government institutions to make general public
aware.
➢ West Bengal: Normal publicity efforts were taken during the year 2023.
➢ Andaman & Nicobar Islands: Intensive efforts were made by the Health
department for strengthening the effective internal mechanism of CRS in the Island
during the year 2023.

government and private hospitals and nursing homes across Chandigarh. This has
significantly contributed to awareness and compliance.
