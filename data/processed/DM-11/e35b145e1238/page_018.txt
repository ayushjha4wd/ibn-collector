GLOSSARY OF TERMS
Birth: Live birth or still birth.
Live birth: Complete expulsion or extraction from its mother of a product of conception, irrespective of the
duration of pregnancy, which, after such expulsion or extraction, breathes or shows any other evidence of
life, and each product of such birth is considered live-born.
Still Birth: Foetal death where a product of conception has attained at least the prescribed period of
gestation.
Death: Permanent disappearance of all evidence of the life at any time after live birth has taken place.
Infant Death: Death of a child less than one year old.
Level of Registration: The level of registration, defined as the percentage of registered births/deaths to
the births/deaths estimated through SRS, determines the performance level of a State / Union territory
with regard to functioning of Civil Registration System.

Population, July. 2020)
