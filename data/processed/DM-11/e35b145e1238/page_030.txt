During 2023, such meetings were held in only 18 States/UTs namely Arunachal

Mizoram, Odisha, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Delhi,

Such Coordination Committees have also been set up at District and below
district level (i.e. at Mandal and Taluka level) in many States. However, the information
on convening the regular meeting of District Level Coordination Committees (DLCCs)
and below district level committee meetings has not been received from all the
States/UTs. The following information regarding district level and below district level
meetings during 2023 has been provided by the Chief Registrars in the respective
annual report of State/UT:-
➢ Andhra Pradesh: During the year 2023, no IDCC was held. However, 3
meetings were held at Chief Secretary level on CRS during the year with all other line
department officials. It gave a boost to CRS Programme in the state.
➢ Arunachal Pradesh: During the year 2023, one IDCC Meeting was conducted
by the Chief Registrar of Births & Deaths in the State Headquarter, Itanagar, while no
DLCC was conducted during the period.
➢ Assam: One IDCC Meeting was conducted during the year 2023.
➢ Bihar: One IDCC meeting for strengthening state level vital statistics was
held under the chairmanship of Development Commissioner Bihar Patna. The
meeting was attended by representatives of urban and housing department health

➢ Chhattisgarh: 10 DLCC meetings were conducted during the year 2023.
