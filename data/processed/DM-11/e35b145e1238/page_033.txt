2.2. Computerization of Civil registration record/ work: The detail of
information furnished by States/ UTs on computerization of civil registration records
and online registration is given below: -
➢ Andhra Pradesh: Uniform software developed by Office of Registrar General
India (ORGI), New Delhi (www.crsorgi.gov.in) is being implemented in all the Birth and
Death registration units i.e. Gram Panchayats, Govt. Health Facilities and Municipalities
from 01.11.2015, 01.05.2016 & 01.12.2016 respectively in all the Registration units.
➢ Arunachal Pradesh: All district headquarters have been equipped with
computers and initiated the procedure of computerization for record of births and
deaths. In the State, online registration facility is available in 20 District Headquarters
namely Bomdila, Yupia, Ziro, Aalo, Koloriang, Yingkiong, Pasighat, Boleng, Tezu, Roing,
Hawai, Seppa, Daporijo, Changlang, Longding, Tawang, Palin, Khonsa, Namsai and Anini
registration units and 41 ADCs (HQs) namely, Mio, Jairampur, Mechuka, Seijusa Gepen,
Yomcha Kamba, Nari, Kamba, Yomcha Rupa, Nafra, Singchung, Thrizino, Kalaktang,
Dirang, Tali, Pania, Nyapin, Patuk, Rumgong, Chongkam, Kanubari, Siyum, Taliha,
Dumporijo, Kimin, Sagalee, Balijan, Lumla, Jang, Mariyang, Tuting, Deomali, Yachuli,
Dambuk, Wakro, Mebo, Ruksin, Bameng, Chayangtajo, Jairampur, Bordumsa, Mio,
Bordumsa and Hayuliang registration units. Provision for registration facilities in other
registration units is offline.
➢ Assam: Prior to 2023, there were 678 registration units. These numbers were
reduced to 505 online Units in 2023 which are mapped by the surrounding areas. Now
the process of registration has been switched over with digitization completely.
Uniform software developed by Office of Registrar General India (ORGI), New Delhi
(www.crsorgi.gov.in) has been Implemented in urban units
➢ Bihar: The registration of births and deaths is being done through ORGI
uniform CRS software (www.crsorgi.gov.in). 29,38,819 births and 4,15,107 deaths were
registered online during 2023 in the State.
