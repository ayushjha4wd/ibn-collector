➢ Chhattisgarh: In the State, 17977 Registration Units are registering events
online through ORGI uniform CRS software (www.crsorgi.gov.in). The old records
before the year 2020 are yet to be digitized.
➢ Goa: All the available birth and death records from 01/01/1971 onwards,
maintained at the Rural (Village Panchayats) and Urban (Municipal
Council/Corporation) level, along with the records at the special births and deaths
registration unit at the Goa Medical College, Bambolim, are computerized through
‘Infogram’ and ‘MAS’ software respectively, developed by the National Informatics
Centre, Goa. The computerized birth and death certificates, digitally signed by the
concerned Registrar of Births and Deaths have been made available online on the web
portal (rbd.goa.gov.in). Till date, more than 13.59 lakh births and 5.59 lakh death events
have been registered since the implementation of the RBD Act, 1969 (Central Act No. 18
of 1969) in the State. During the year 2023, 17803 birth events and 15397 death events
were registered under the RBD Act, 1969 (amended in 2023).
➢ Gujarat: A web-based application namely “e-Olakh” developed by State NIC is
being used in the State for the purpose of registration of birth and death. All Gram
Panchayats, municipalities and 8 municipal corporations have been covered under “e-
Olakh” (GCRS). “e-Olakh” application is in operation.
➢ Haryana: In the State, the registration of births and deaths is being done only
online using ORGI uniform CRS portal (www.crsorgi.gov.in) and computerized birth and
death certificate are being issued by all the Registration Units. The Aadhaar linkage of
new born to the birth registration is also started in the State. For online registration and
Aadhaar linkage about 481 Laptop and 540 Tablets were provided to the Registration
Units. Efforts have been made to link the birth and death registration to ensure its
regular updation. Few services such as application for issuance of birth/death
certificates/Non-Availability Certificate (NAC), correction in records, inclusion of child
name and delayed registration are being delivered in online mode through Antyodaya
Saral Portal developed and maintained under the Chief Minister Good Governance
Programme (CMGGA).
➢ Himachal Pradesh: The registration of births and deaths is being done using
ORGI uniform CRS portal (www.crsorgi.gov.in). 94235 births, 49681 deaths & 539
Infant Deaths have been registered through CRS Portal during the year 2023.
