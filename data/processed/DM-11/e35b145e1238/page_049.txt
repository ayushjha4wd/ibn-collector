2.6. Publicity efforts for improving Registration of Births and Deaths: Lack of
awareness about the need and importance of registration among the general public and
lack of procedural knowledge among the registration functionaries are among
important reasons for low levels of registration in the country. In order to make the
public aware about the need and importance of registration, some of the State
Governments have undertaken various publicity measures through various means with
their own funds like printing of posters, cloth banners, pamphlets, leaflets on the
importance of registration of births and deaths. Radio talks were also arranged on the
importance of registration of births and deaths. In addition, advertisements were
published in leading newspapers. As per the details given by the States, following
publicity measures have been taken by them during the year: -
➢ Andhra Pradesh: Publicity on Civil Registration system has not been taken up
in the State due to lack of funds. However, the following steps were taken to create
awareness on registration of births and deaths during 2023: -
• District Registrars were requested to instruct Registrars of Registration Units
to ensure display of posters in all public places within the Registration Units.
• Commissioner, Women & Child Department, was also requested to issue
instructions to Project Directors to arrange for distribution and display of
posters at Anganwadi Centers and to ensure Anganwadi workers notify Births
/ Deaths regularly to Village Secretaries.
➢ Arunachal Pradesh: Publicity measures were taken to create awareness
among the general public about the need, importance and benefits of births and deaths
registration via distribution of pamphlets through the students of various schools and
public on 26th January & 15th August 2023 and during the “Sarkar Apke Dwar Camps’’
campaign through displaying Banners/Posters in the stall of Economics & Statistics
Department at General Ground, various district during the period 2023.
➢ Bihar: The Information and Public Relations department of State
Government carried out publicity on the importance of registration of births and deaths
by bringing out advertisements in daily newspapers from time to time.
