➢ Kerala: In all units of Kerala, the Internal Vigilance Officer under the concerned
Joint Director conducts quarterly inspections. The District Registrar forward the report
with specific remarks to the Chief Registrar. Efforts are made to issue instructions to
rectify the defects pointed out in the inspection reports.
➢ Madhya Pradesh: Regular inspections of registration units as per the
provisions of RBD Act were done. During the year 2023, 997 inspections of registration
units were made out of which 816 were in Rural and 181 in Urban areas. Maximum
number of inspections were done in Hoshangabad (151) followed by Ujjain (83)
districts of Madhya Pradesh.
➢ Maharashtra: During the year 2023, State level officers inspected 18
Registration Units namely RH Uran, Wagholi, Jejuri, Pandharpur, Talegaon, Lonavla , MC
Ahmednagar, Daund, Aurungabad, Dhule, Solapur, Zila Parishad, Aurungabad, Nagar
Parishad, Daryapur, Dhamangaav, Dondaicha, Shindkhed raja, P.C. Moshi and R.H
chimthana. In addition, inspections were carried out in 2 districts namely, Aurangabad
(3 RUs), and Ahmednagar (6 RUs) by the concerned District level officers.
➢ Mizoram: During the year 2023, 391 inspections were carried out of which 234
were in Rural areas and 157 in Urban areas .
➢ Nagaland: A three days field inspection was carried out in six rural registration
units in Kohima district, to check and review the implementation of the Registration of
Births & Deaths Act 1969 (amended in 2023) and corresponding Nagaland Births and
Deaths (Amendment) Rules 1999 in the district by the Registrars of Births and Deaths.
➢ Punjab: During the year 2023, number of inspections of registration centers
and their records were carried out in 8 Districts by the Officers/Staff of Chief Registrar
Office and Directorate of Census Operations Officers for both qualitative and
quantitative improvement of Civil Registration System.
➢ Rajasthan: In order to bring qualitative and quantitative improvement in the
registration work, in the Registration Units, inspection was carried out by the officers
of various level. DSOs and their staff inspected the rural and urban units and gave
necessary instructions and suggestions for improving the registration work during the
year 2023, 2685 registrar/sub-registrar units were inspected.
➢ Sikkim: Routine inspections of registration units and registration records for
improvement of registration were done by the officials from the Headquarter. During
the year 10 Registration units were inspected.
