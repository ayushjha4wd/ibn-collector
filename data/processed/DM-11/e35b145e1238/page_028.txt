2.3. Registration of Births and Deaths occurring to Indian Citizens abroad:
Under the provisions of Citizenship Act, 1955 the event of births and deaths to Indian
Citizens occurring abroad are registered by the respective Indian Missions/Consulates.
Under Rule 5 of Citizen’s (Registration at Indian Consulates) Rules 1956, Consulates are
statutorily required to send the annual returns of births and deaths of Indian Citizens
to the office of the RGI. It has been observed that requisite returns are not furnished by
all Indian Missions/Consulates; such returns for the year 2023 have been received from
196 missions located in 150 countries, covering 62182 births and 10531 deaths. As can
be seen from the Statement 3 below, out of the total registered births at the various
missions during 2023 Mission/Consulate of Dubai located in UAE registered 12770 i.e.
about 20.5 per cent of the total births registered abroad, followed by Mission/Consulate
of London which is at 10 per cent. In case of Deaths, Mission/Consulate of Dubai located
in UAE registered 1687 i.e. 16 per cent of the total deaths registered abroad followed by
Mission/Consulate of Riyadh located in Saudi Arabia which is at 13.5 per cent.
