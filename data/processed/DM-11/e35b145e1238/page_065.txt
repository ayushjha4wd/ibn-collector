3.9 Registration of Birth & Death within the prescribed time limit of 21 days
3.9.1 Statement 13 shows the percentage of births and deaths registered within the prescribed
time limit of 21 days. The registered births and deaths have been classified into four categories
depending upon their period of registration. The four time periods considered for the purpose
are: (i) within the prescribed time limit (up to 21 days), (ii) After 21 days but within 30 days,
(iii) After 30 days but within 1 year, and (iv) More than 1 year. For 2023, Thirty-five (35)
States/UTs have provided data on Births and Deaths Registration within the prescribed time
limit (i.e. within 21 days).
