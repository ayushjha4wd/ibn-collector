CHAPTER – III
A BRIEF ANALYSIS OF DATA ON REGISTERED
BIRTHS, DEATHS AND INFANT DEATHS
3.1 Births and deaths are the two most important vital events in the life of an individual
and for society as a whole. Based on the data collected under the Civil Registration System
on registered births and deaths, this chapter presents the trend in the number of registered
events and other related indicators, at the State and National level.
3.2 The Registration Unit in the Civil Registration System is the most important entity
responsible for registering events such as births and deaths, issuing of certificates,
maintenance of CRS records and reporting to higher authorities. The number of
registration units for the year 2023 at the national level and the States/UTs with
rural/urban details may be seen in Statement 8.
3.3 The highest number of registration units i.e. 62780 were working in Uttar Pradesh,
followed by 43794 in Maharashtra, 33124 in Karnataka and 24550 in Madhya Pradesh.
Registration Units in 10 major States, namely, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka,
Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and
Rajasthan accounts for about (252778 nos.) i.e. about 88.2% of the total registration units
of the country. In the case of urban areas, the highest number of registration units are in
Tamil Nadu(1985), followed by 1822 in Uttar Pradesh.
3.4 At the national level, about 3.4 percent of Registration Units are located in Urban
areas and 90.3% of registration Units are in Rural areas. The rural-urban bifurcation of
Registration Units of Chhattisgarh, which accounted for another 6.3% is not available.
