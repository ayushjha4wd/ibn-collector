Assumptions regarding fertility
SRS and NFHS provide estimates of TFR for States of the country. The SRS data has been used as base
level estimates, these being available as a time series. The SRS data shows that in most of the States, the level of
total fertility has been falling since 1971. It may be seen from the SRS data that there has been consistent
decline in fertility during the last two decades. The trend analysis of TFR in terms of Exponential, Log linear,
logistic and Gompertz model may be used for projecting the future levels of TFR in all the States as well as for
the country as a whole. Following are the mathematical forms of the three models.
1. Log linear: Ln (TFR-L) = a+b (time)
2. Gompertz:
TFR – L = (a)b^t
U – L
or alternatively,
Ln [- (Ln (TFR-L)/(U-L))] = Ln (-Ln.a) + (time).Ln.b
3. Logistic: Ln [(TFR-L)/(U-TFR)] =a+b(time)
Where U and L are the upper and lower limits of TFR respectively and a and b are constants.
For all the States except Himachal Pradesh, the observed values of TFRs from 1991-2017 were
considered for projecting the future levels of TFR as the reliable estimates from SRS are available from 1991
onwards. In fitting the Gompertz model, three types of upper asymptotes have been taken for the States
depending upon whether the particular State is a higher or medium or low TFR State. The upper asymptote
(U) has been taken as 4 for southern states and 6 for the northern states. For western and eastern states, U has
been taken as 5. The projected values of TFR for India for the quinquennial period 2011-2035 at five yearly
intervals have been presented below in Statement 2.
