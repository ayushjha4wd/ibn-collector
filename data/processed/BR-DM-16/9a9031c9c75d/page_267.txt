Subject: Constitution of a Technical Group on Population Projections.
In order to provide population projections which can be used by the National Commission on
Population, Planning Commission and various stakeholders & it has been decided to constitute a Technical
Group on Population Projections for the period 2011-2035. The composition of the Technical Group will be as
follows:
Chariman
1. Registrar General of India
Members
2. Additional Registrar General of India, RGI
3. Additional Director (Demography & Training), RGI
4. Advisor (Health), Planning Commission
5. Advisor (Perspective Planning Division), Planning Commission
6. Representative of Central Statistical Office
7. Representative of National Sample Survey Office
8. Representative of Ministry of Women and Child Development
9. Representative of Ministry of Human Resource Development
10. Representative of Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
11. Representative of Ministry of Minority Affairs
12. Chief Director (Statistics), Department of Health and Family Welfare
13. Representative of Institute of Applied Manpower Research, New Delhi
14. Representative of Indian Council of Medical Research, New Delhi
15. Director, International Institute for Population Studies, Mumbai
16. Prof. P.M. Kulkarni, Jawahar Lal Nehru University, New Delhi
17. Director, National Institute of Medical Statistics, ICMR, New Delhi
18. Shri K.S. Natarajan, Former Deputy Registrar General of India
19. Joint Secretary (NCP) – Convener
2. Terms of reference of the Technical Group on Population Projections are given below :
(a) To review the methodology of population projections adopted in the past.
(b) To prepare fresh projections of fertility rate.
(c) To prepare fresh projections of mortality rate.
(d) To prepare fresh population projections up to the year 2035 with age, sex, urban-rural and State–UT
break ups for five year intervals.
(e) To prepare projections of the possible period when the population replacement level TFR 2.1 will be
reached by States/Uts and the country as a whole. The Group may project reversal of the
trend/continuation of trend of TFR in the States where it is below or replacement level of 2.1.
(f) In undertaking the above exercise, the Group may take into consideration data/information brought
out by Census 2011, SRS, NSS, NFHS and other sources as may be needed.
