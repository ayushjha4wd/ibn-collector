Sex Ratio at Birth (SRB) and Population Sex Ratio
Sex Ratio at Birth (number of females per thousand males) for the period 2009-11 has been presented
in the report of the Sample Registration System, 2011 published by the Office of the Registrar General, India,
for the country and the major states. These have been used for the present projection exercise. The Population
Sex Ratio that has been used for the projection purposes has been taken from the data of Census 2011. Sex ratio
at birth are kept varying at state level during the projection period. In the projections, the Sex Ratio at Birth are
linearly projected in 2031 as 909 for the states (except Tamilnadu) in which in 2015-17, SRB were lower than
909. The states are Maharashtra, Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Gujarat, Uttarakhand and
NCT of Delhi. For the remaining states, the projected SRB in 2031 is taken at constant level of 943 (converging
SRB as per the international standards). Statement 4 showing the SRBs for the states, which have been
considered for the projection exercise, is as follows:
