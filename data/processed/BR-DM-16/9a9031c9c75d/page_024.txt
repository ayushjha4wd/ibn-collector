Assumptions regarding migration
Based on the migration data of Census of India 2011, inter-state net migrants during decade 2001-11
has been assumed to remain constant throughout the projection periods for all the states, where Component
Method of projections were used. The component of international migration has been assumed to be
negligible, so it has not been considered for projection exercise.
Population Projections for smaller states and Union Territories
The states for which projection has been made using the Component Method constitute about 99
percent of the total population as of 2011. The projection of the seven north-eastern states (excluding Assam)
has also been carried out as a whole using the Component Method. For the remaining State- Goa and Union
territories, contribute only 1 percent of the total population of India. These are Andaman & Nicobar Islands,
Chandigarh, Dadra & Nagar Haveli, Daman & Diu, Goa, Lakshadweep, and Puducherry. The population
projection of these State/Union territories has been projected by Mathematical method using exponential
growth rates. In order to project the future population, the exponential growth rates are estimated using the
method of Least Squares, the steps of which are mentioned below:
Step 1: Using the Method of Least Square, Exponential Growth Rates are being estimated for 2021,
2031 and 2041. The data used pertains to the growth rates of 1981, 1991, 2001 and 2011.
Step 2: To get closer value of population, growth rate of 2016 has been estimated by using Geometric
Mean (GM) of 2011 and 2021 & for 2026, the GM of 2021 and 2031. As such the most probable
growth gate of 2016 and 2026 are obtained.
Step 3: The growth rate of 2016 has been used to estimate the population of 2012 to 2016, the growth
rate of 2021 has been used to estimate the population of 2017 to 2021 and so on.
Projections for combined North-Eastern States (excluding Assam)
An attempt has been made for working out the population projections of North- Eastern States
combined (excluding Assam) by Component method. The states included in the North-Eastern States are
Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim and Tripura. This was recommended
by the Technical Group. In so far as the projection for the North –Eastern States, combined is concerned, TFRs
for earlier years based on available data from 1995 onwards till 2017 has been used to project the future levels.
