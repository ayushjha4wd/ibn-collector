Birth interval
3.33 The percentage distribution of second and higher order live births by interval
between current and previous live birth is shown below in Statement 37 for India and bigger
States for the year 2014. At the National level, 1.3 per cent of the live births occur within
one year from the previous live birth. Such percentage varies from 0.2 in Odisha to 2.6 in
Haryana and Punjab. The percentage of births beyond three years of birth interval from the
previous live birth for India is 56.9. It varies from 46.8 in Madhya Pradesh to 75.3 in
Assam.
