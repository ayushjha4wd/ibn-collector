3.20 Another refined measure of fertility which accounts for future mothers is the Gross
Reproduction Rate (GRR). It measures the average number of female children a woman is
expected to give birth during her entire reproductive span conforming to the age specific
fertility rates (ASFRs) for a given year, if there is no mortality. The estimated value of GRR
for India in the year 2014 is 1.1 and varies from 0.8 in urban areas to 1.2 in rural areas. Values
of GRR by residence for India and bigger States for the year 2014 are presented below in
Statement 26.
