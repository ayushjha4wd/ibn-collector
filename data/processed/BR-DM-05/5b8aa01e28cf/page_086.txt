3.32 Statement 36 below presents the percentage distribution of current live birth by birth
order and residence for India and bigger States. It may be observed that the percentage of
current live births for ‘first’ and ‘second’ order taken together is generally higher for urban
areas if compared to the rural areas for bigger States except Delhi, Andhra Pradesh, Kerala
and Maharashtra. For ‘third’, ‘fourth & above’ order live births, the percentage contribution
at National level is significantly higher in rural areas (28.6) compared to urban areas (18.5).
