Medical attention before death
4.22 The type of medical attention received before death is also collected in the SRS. In
the new samples from 2004 onwards, the options on types of medical attention received
before death have been modified to capture the deaths occurred specifically at private
hospital/nursing homes. The options include ‘Government Hospital’, ‘Private Hospital’,
‘Qualified professional’, ‘Untrained functionary’ and ‘others’. The options for the sample
based on 1991 sample were ‘Institutional’; ‘Medical attentions other than institution’; and
‘No medical attention’. Statement 57 given below presents the percentage distribution of
deaths by type of medical attention received before death for India and bigger States
separately for rural and urban areas for the year 2014. At the National level, 26.8 per cent
of the deaths occurred at Government Hospitals and varies from 24.4 per cent in rural
areas to 34.4 per cent in urban areas. Among the bigger States, Himachal Pradesh 51.0 has
the highest percentage of deaths in Government Hospitals and Jharkhand (17.8) and
Punjab (17.8) the lowest. Deaths in private hospitals account for 15.8 per cent and it
varies from 10.1 per cent in West Bengal to 34.7 per cent in Kerala. About 22.4 per cent
deaths were attended by untrained functionaries and others.
