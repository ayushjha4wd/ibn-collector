3.14 The fertility pattern by age groups in different States is presented below in Statement
21. Except for Jammu & Kashmir , Kerala, Bihar, Gujarat, Uttarakhand and Uttar Pradesh
where fertility reached its peak in the age group 25-29, the highest fertility in all the other
bigger States has been attained in the age group 20-24. Fertility, however, declines from age
30 in all the bigger States. The ASFR in the younger age group 15-19 varies from 4.3 in
Jammu & Kashmir to 55.9 in Assam. In the age group 30-34, the variation in the level of
ASFR is from 24.3 in West Bengal to 141.8 in Uttar Pradesh. The rural-urban levels in
