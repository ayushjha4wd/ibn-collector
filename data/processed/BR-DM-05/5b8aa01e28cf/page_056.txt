3.6 Statement 15 below provides the percentage change in average crude birth rate between
the periods 2002-04 and 2012-14 for India and bigger States separately for rural and urban
areas. At the national level, the rate of decline in birth rate is 13.4 per cent. The rate of decline
in average birth rate varies from 21.5 per cent in Punjab to 2.8 per cent in Delhi. Such decline
in rural areas is from 21.6 per cent in Punjab to 3.1 per cent in Delhi. In urban areas, the decline
ranges from 27.8 per cent in Himachal Pradesh to 2.9 per cent in Delhi. In Tamil Nadu, the 3-
year average crude birth rate in rural areas is nearly same as in the urban areas during 2012-14.
