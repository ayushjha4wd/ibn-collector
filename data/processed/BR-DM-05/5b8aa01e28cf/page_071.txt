3.17 The cumulative value of the age specific fertility rates at the end of the child bearing
ages gives a measure of fertility known as Total Fertility Rate (TFR). TFR indicates the
average number of children expected to be born per woman during her entire span of
reproductive period assuming that the age specific fertility rates, to which she is exposed to,
continue to be the same and that there is no mortality. The TFRs worked out on the basis of the
ASFRs for the year 2014 are given below in Statement 24 for India and bigger States
separately for rural and urban areas. The TFR for India in the year 2014 was 2.3 per woman
and varies from 2.5 in rural areas to 1.8 in urban areas. Among the bigger States, it varies from
1.6 in West Bengal to 3.2 in Bihar and Uttar Pradesh. For rural areas, it varies from 1.7 in
Himachal Pradesh, Punjab , West Bengal and Tamil Nadu to 3.4 in Uttar Pradesh. For urban
areas, such variation is from 1.2 in Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir and West Bengal to
2.5 in Uttar Pradesh. Chart 15 presents levels of TFR by residence for India and bigger States,
2014. Distribution of bigger States by level of TFR in rural and urban areas is shown in Chart
