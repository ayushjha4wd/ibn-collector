4.11 Statement 46 given below presents Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) by sex and
residence for the year 2014 for India and bigger States. Infant Mortality Rate is defined
as the infant deaths (less than one year) per thousand live births. At the National level,
IMR is reported to be 39 and varies from 43 in rural areas to 26 in urban areas. Among
the bigger States, it varies from 12 in Kerala to 52 in Madhya Pradesh. Female infants
experienced a higher mortality than male infants in all States except Tamil Nadu and
Assam where both are the same. Chart 20 provides the values of IMR by residence for
India and bigger States. Distribution of bigger States by level of IMR in rural and
urban areas is shown in Chart 21. The estimates on child and infant mortality indicators
