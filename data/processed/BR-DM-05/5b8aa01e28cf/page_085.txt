3.30 Information on order of the live birth and interval between current and previous live
births are also collected in SRS from 1990 onwards. These provide useful information on
spacing of children and level of fertility. The estimated percentages on order of live birth and
birth interval for India and bigger States are given in Tables 5, 6 and 7. Based on these tables,
two State-wise comparative Statements are presented below.
3.31 The percentage distribution of live births by birth order for India and bigger States for
the year 2014 is presented below in Statement 35. It is observed that 43.0 percent of the current
live births in India are first order births, and 31.0 percent of total births are second order births.
The fourth and higher order births account for 10.8 percent of the total births. Among the
bigger States, the percentage share of first order birth varies from 29.4 percent in Bihar to 57
and 56.9 percent in West Bengal and Andhra Pradesh respectively. On the other hand, the
percentage share of fourth and higher order births varies from 1.0 percent in Tamil Nadu to
20.5 percent in Bihar.
