4.14 Early neo-natal mortality rate i.e. number of infant deaths less than seven days of
life per thousand live births forms an important component of infant mortality rate and
more specifically of the neo-natal mortality rate. Statement 49 given below presents the
early neo-natal mortality rate along with the percentage of early neo-natal deaths to the
total infant deaths. At the National level, the early neo-natal mortality rate for the year
2014 has been estimated at 20 and ranges from 23 in rural areas to 11 in urban areas.
Among the bigger States, Kerala 5 and Madhya Pradesh and Odisha with 26 are the
extremes. The percentage of early neo-natal deaths to the total infant deaths during the
year 2014, at the National level, has been 52.0 and it varies from 53.9 in rural areas to 42.7
in urban areas. In this indicator, in most of the States, rural proportion is relatively higher
than the urban proportion except for Punjab, Delhi, Gujarat and Chhattisgarh. Among the
bigger States the percentage for total, varies from 39.0 in Kerala to 66.5 in Uttarakhand.
