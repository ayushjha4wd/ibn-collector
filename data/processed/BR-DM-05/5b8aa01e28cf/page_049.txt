age 15 years and above by residence. At the National level, 71.4 percent of married

West Bengal to 32.2 in Jammu & Kashmir. The maximum percentage of
Widowed/Divorced/Separated females is 13.5 in Kerala and the minimum 4.8 in Haryana.
