EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The Sample Registration System(SRS) is the largest demographic survey in the
country mandated to provide annual estimates of fertility as well as mortality indicators
at the State and National level. The present Report contains data on fertility and
mortality indicators for the year 2014 for India and bigger States. The estimates are
segregated by residence and also by gender, wherever required.
2. The salient features of the report are as summarized below.
 The Crude Birth Rate(CBR) at the National level during 2014 stands at 21.0
exhibiting a decline of 0.4 points over 2013. The maximum CBR has been
reported in Uttar Pradesh(27.0) and the minimum in Kerala (14.8).
 There has been a decline of 1.5 points in the CBR for the country from 2009 to
2014. The corresponding decline in rural CBR is1.4 points and in urban CBR
0.9 points.
 The Crude Death Rate(CDR)for the countryis 6.7 in 2014.The maximum CDR
has been reported for Odisha (7.9) and the minimum for Delhi(3.8).
 During last five years, the decline in National CDR has been to the tune of 0.6
points. The corresponding decline in female CDR is 0.5 points whereas in male
CDR it is 0.7 points.
 The Infant Mortality Rate(IMR) has also registered 1 points decline to 39 in
2014from 40 in 2013 at the National level. The maximum IMR has been
observed in Madhya Pradesh (52) and the minimum in Kerala (12).During the
corresponding period, Neo-natal Mortality Rate(NNMR) has declined by 2
point.
 IMR for the country has come down to 39 in 2014 from 50 in 2009, a decline of
11 points over last 5 years and an annual average decline of about 1.6 points.
The corresponding decline in rural IMR has been to the tune of 12 points (55 in
2009 to 43 in 2014) against a decline of 8 points in urban IMR (34 in 2009 to 26
in 2014). Both the gender have shown decline in the period 2009-14.
 Despite this decline, one in every 26infant at the National level, one in every
23infant in rural areas and one in every 38infant in urban areas still die within
one year of life.
 In2014, U5MR for the country has shown a decline of 0.4 points over 2013(45
in 2014 against 49 in 2013). There has been decline of 5 points in male U5MR
and 4 points in female U5MR during the period.
 Sex Ratio at Birth for the country has gone down by 3 pointto 906 in 2012-
2014from 909 in 2011-2013.Kerala has reported the highest Sex Ratio at Birth
(974) while Haryana, the lowest (866).
