3.7 The Statement 16 below presents sex ratio at birth during the period 2013-15. The
sex ratio at birth for the country for the period 2013-15 (3-years average) has been estimated
as 900. At National level, it is 903 in rural area and 890 in urban area. Among the bigger
States/UTs, the sex ratio at birth varies from 967 in Kerala to 831 in Haryana. In the rural
areas, the highest and the lowest sex ratio at birth are in the States of Chhattisgarh 987 and
Haryana 836 respectively. The sex ratio in urban areas varies from 954 in Madhya Pradesh
to 821 in Haryana. Chart 32 depicts the variation in sex ratio by residence among bigger
States/UTs in the country.
