4.11 Statement 46 given below presents Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) by sex and
residence for the year 2015 for India and bigger States/UTs. Infant Mortality Rate is
defined as the infant deaths (less than one year) per thousand live births. At the
National level, IMR is reported to be 37 and varies from 41 in rural areas to 25 in urban
areas. Among the bigger States/UTs, it varies from 12 in Kerala to 50 in Madhya
Pradesh. Female infants experienced a higher mortality than male infants in all States
except Himachal Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh where both are the same. Chart 82
provides the values of IMR by residence for India and bigger States/UTs. Distribution
of bigger States by level of IMR in rural and urban areas is shown in Chart 85. The
