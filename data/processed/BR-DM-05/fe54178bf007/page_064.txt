2.15 In addition to the marital status of male and female, information on the number of
females who got effectively married during the reference period of six months of each half
yearly survey is also being collected in the SRS from 1990. Based on the information on the
number of females, who got effectively married during the last one year, mean age at
effective marriage for female has been worked out. Statement 12 given below presents
percentage distribution of such females by age at effective marriage separately for rural and
urban areas. The percentage of females who got effectively married before reaching 18
years of age is 2.2 at the National level and varies from 0.8 in Delhi to 3.7 in West Bengal.
Percentage of female marrying at 21 years and above is 62.6 at the National level and varies
from 48.3 in West Bengal to 86.7 in Jammu & Kashmir. About 40 % of the rural female, at
the National level, marry between 18 and 20 years of age compared to 26% in urban areas.
Chart 20, 21 and 22 shows the distribution of such female by age at effective marriage of
India and bigger States/UTs for Total, Rural and Urban areas respectively.
