4.6 Statement 41 given below shows the percentage change in the level of Crude
Death Rate between the period 2003-05 and 2013-15 for India and bigger States/UTs.
During this period CDR in India has declined by 13.0 per cent. Among the bigger
States/UTs, decline in CDR varies from 22.5 per cent in Jharkhand to 2.9 per cent in
Himachal Pradesh. In Kerala, average CDR have shown marginal increase during 2003-05
to 2013-15 period, which could possibly be due to the changes in age structure of the
population.
