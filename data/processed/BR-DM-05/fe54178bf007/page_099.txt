specific age group has been worked out by adding the ASFRs up to that age group. The
percentage share of fertility by woman in the age group 15-19 varies from 0.8 in Jammu &
Kashmir to 5.8 in West Bengal. By the age 34, the percentage share of cumulative fertility
varies from 89.7 in Jharkhand to 97.3 in Himachal Pradesh. The percentage share in
cumulative fertility for women by the age 39 years varies from 96.2 in Jharkhand to 99.6 in
Himachal Pradesh. At the national level, less than 2 per cent fertility is from women aged 40
years and above. Chart 44 gives percentage cumulative age specific fertility rate for India by
residence.
