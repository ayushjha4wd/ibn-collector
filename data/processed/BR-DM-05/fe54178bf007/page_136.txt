Birth interval
3.34 The percentage distribution of second and higher order live births by interval
between current and previous live birth is shown below in Statement 37 for India and bigger
States/UTs for the year 2015. At the National level, 2.1 per cent of the live births occur
within one year from the previous live birth. Such percentage varies from 0.4 in Kerala to
4.4 in Jammu & Kashmir. The percentage of births beyond three years of birth interval
from the previous live birth for India is 59.4. It varies from 48.9 in Maharashtra to 77.8 in
Assam.
