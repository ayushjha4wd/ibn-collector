Medical attention before death
4.22 The type of medical attention received before death is also collected in the SRS. In
the new samples from 2004 onwards, the options on types of medical attention received
before death have been modified to capture the deaths occurred specifically at private
hospital/nursing homes. The options include ‘Government Hospital’, ‘Private Hospital’,
‘Qualified professional’, ‘Untrained functionary’ and ‘others’. The options for the sample
based on 1991 sample were ‘Institutional’; ‘Medical attentions other than institution’; and
‘No medical attention’. Statement 57 given below presents the percentage distribution of
deaths by type of medical attention received before death for India and bigger States/UTs
separately for rural and urban areas for the year 2015. At the National level, 27.5 per cent
of the deaths occurred at Government Hospitals and varies from 25.0 per cent in rural
areas to 34.0 per cent in urban areas. Among the bigger States/UTs, Himachal Pradesh
51.3 has the highest percentage of deaths in Government Hospitals in Jharkhand (18.1) the
lowest. Deaths in private hospitals account for 16.9 per cent and it varies from 10.5 per
cent in West Bengal to 35.3 per cent in Kerala. About 20.7 per cent deaths were attended
by untrained functionaries and others.
