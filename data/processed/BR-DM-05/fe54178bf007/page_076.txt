3.6 Statement 15 below provides the percentage change in average crude birth rate between
the periods 2003-05 and 2013-15 for India and bigger States/UTs separately for rural and
urban areas. At the national level, the rate of decline in birth rate is 12.8 per cent. The rate of
decline in average birth rate varies from 19.5 per cent in West Bengal to 5.1 per cent in Kerala.
Such decline in rural areas is from 19.7 per cent in West Bengal to 5.7 per cent in Kerala. In
urban areas, the decline ranges from 22.9 per cent in Himachal Pradesh to 2.5 per cent in
Assam. In Jharkhand and Uttarakhand, the 3-year average crude birth rate in urban areas has
increased by 1.6 per cent and 3.1 per cent respectively. The 3-year average crude birth rate of
Tamil Nadu is nearly same in rural and urban areas during 2013-15.
