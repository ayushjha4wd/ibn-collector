57.1. Among the bigger States/UTs Uttarakhand (52.4) recorded the lowest percentage of
married population whereas the highest percentage was recorded for Andhra Pradesh
(62.5). Chart 11, 12 and 13 shows the distribution of population aged 10 year and above
by marital status of India and bigger States/UTs for Person, Male and Female respectively.
