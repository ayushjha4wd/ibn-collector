3.23 Values of the age specific marital fertility rates for the year 2013 in bigger States are
given below in Statement 29. It is observed that the age pattern of marital fertility is almost
similar to the pattern of age specific fertility. The marital fertility in the age group 15-19 is
lesser than national average in Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Gujarat, Haryana,
Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Punjab and Rajasthan. ASMFR is at peak level in the age group
20-24 in all the bigger States except Assam, Jammu & Kashmir, Kerala, Odisha, Tamil Nadu
and West Bengal. There is significant decline in marital fertility for women aged 30 years
onwards, for all major States.
