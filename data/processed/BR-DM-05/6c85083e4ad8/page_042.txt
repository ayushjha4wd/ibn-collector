Marital Status
2.9 Besides age, the other important demographic characteristic collected and
subsequently updated during each half yearly survey of the SRS is the marital status.
Distribution of the population by marital status is thus available from SRS.
2.10 Statement 8 given below shows the percentage distribution of population by sex and
marital status for India and bigger States for the year 2013. During this year, 47.7 percent of
the population at the National level is reported as never married. Married population
constitutes 47.1 percent of the total population whereas widowed/divorced/separated
constitutes 5.2 percent. The proportion of W/D/S female population is higher than males at
the National level as well as in the bigger States. The gap is significantly wide (more than 10
percentage) in respect of Karnataka, Kerala and Tamil Nadu. Andhra Pradesh (53.0)
recorded the highest percentage of married population whereas the lowest was recorded for
Uttar Pradesh (41.5). Married Females recorded a higher percentage as compared to Males
at the National level and similarly for all of the bigger States. The percentage distribution of
population by sex, marital status and age-group for urban and rural areas of India and bigger
States is detailed in Table-2.
