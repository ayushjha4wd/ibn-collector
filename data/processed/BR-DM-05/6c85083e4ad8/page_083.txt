3.31 Information on order of the live birth and interval between current and previous live

3.32 The percentage distribution of live births by birth order for India and bigger States for
the year 2013 is presented below in Statement 36. It is observed 43.6 percent of the current live
births in India are first order births, and 32.4 percent of total births are second order births. The
fourth and higher order births account for 10.5 percent of the total births. Among the bigger
States, the percentage share of first order birth varies from 35.4 percent in Bihar to 57.4 percent
in West Bengal. On the other hand, the percentage share of fourth and higher order births
varies from 1.2 percent in Andhra Pradesh to 19.6 percent in Bihar.
