EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The Sample Registration System (SRS) is the largest demographic survey in the
country mandated to provide annual estimates of fertility as well as mortality indicators
at the State and National level. The present Report contains data on fertility and
mortality indicators for the year 2013 for India and bigger States. The estimates are
segregated by residence and also by gender, wherever required.
2. The salient features of the report are as summarized below.
 The Crude Birth Rate(CBR) at the National level during 2013 stands at 21.4
exhibiting a decline of 0.2 points over 2012. The maximum CBR has been
reported in Bihar (27.6) and the minimum in Kerala (14.7).
 There has been a decline of 1.4 points in the CBR for the country from 2008 to
2013. The corresponding decline in rural CBR is 1.5 points and in urban CBR
1.2 points.
 The Crude Death Rate (CDR) for the country is stable at 7.0 in 2013&2012.The
maximum CDR has been reported for Odisha (8.4) and the minimum for Delhi
(4.1).
 During last five years, the decline in National CDR has been to the tune of 0.4
points. The corresponding decline in female CDR is 0.5 points whereas in male
CDR it is 0.4 points.
 The Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) has also registered 2 points decline to 40 in
2013 from 42 in 2012 at the National level. The maximum IMR has been
observed in Assam & Madhya Pradesh (54) and the minimum in Kerala (12).
During the corresponding period, Neo-natal Mortality Rate (NNMR) has
declined by 1point.
 IMR for the country has come down to 40 in 2013 from 53 in 2008, a decline of
13 points over last 5 years and an annual average decline of about 2.6 points.
The corresponding decline in rural IMR has been to the tune of 14 points (58 in
2008 to 44 in 2013) against a decline of 9 points in urban IMR (36 in 2008 to 27
in 2013). Both the gender have shown similar decline in the period 2008-13.
 Despite this decline, one in every 25 infant at the National level, one in every 23
infant in rural areas and one in every 37 infant in urban areas still die within one
year of life.
 In 2013, U5MR for the country has shown a decline of 3 points over 2012 (49
in 2013 against 52 in 2012). There has been decline of 2 points in male U5MR
and 3 points in female U5MR during the period.
 Sex Ratio at Birth for the country has gone up by 1point to 909 in 2011-2013
from 908 in 2010-2012.Chhattisgarh has reported the highest Sex Ratio at Birth
(970) while Haryana, the lowest (864).
