age 15 years and above by residence. At the National level, 67.8 percent of married
females exists in age 15+ and 68.4 percent comes under rural areas. Among the bigger
States, percentage of such married female varies from 58.6 in Jammu & Kashmir to 71.5
in Madhya Pradesh. The percentage of never married female varies from 15.6 in Andhra
Pradesh to 33.6 in Jammu & Kashmir. The maximum percentage of
Widowed/Divorced/Separated females is in Tamil Nadu 16.9 and the minimum 7.4 in
Bihar.
