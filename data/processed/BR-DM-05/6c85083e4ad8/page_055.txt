3.7 The Statement 16 below presents sex ratio at birth during the period 2011-13. The
sex ratio at birth for the country for the period 2011-13 (3-years average) has been estimated
as 909. At National level, it is 910 in rural area and 906 in urban area. Among the bigger
States, the sex ratio at birth varies from 970 in Chhattisgarh to 864 in Haryana. In the rural
areas, the highest and the lowest sex ratio at birth are in the States of Chhattisgarh 977 and
Punjab 861 respectively. The sex ratio in urban areas varies from 983 in Kerala to 854 in
Jharkhand. Chart 10 depicts the variation in sex ratio by residence among bigger States in
the country.
