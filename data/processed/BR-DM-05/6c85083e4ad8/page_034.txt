2.5 Percentages share of population in the selected age groups for bigger States by
residence and sex are given in Statements 4 to 7. Statement 4 gives the percentage of
population below 5 years of age. At the National level, 7.9 percent population in urban areas
constitutes below 5 years of age as against 10.0 percent for rural area. Sex differences in the
share of child population are negligible both in rural and urban areas. The difference in the
share of male child population is maximum (about 1 percent higher) in rural area of Kerala.
Among the bigger States, the percentage of child population in rural areas varies from 7.0 in
Punjab to 13.4 in Bihar. In urban areas, such variation is from 5.2 in Himachal Pradesh to
10.4 in Bihar. Chart 3 shows the pictorial presentation of percentage of child population for
bigger States.
