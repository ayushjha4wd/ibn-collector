Uttarakhand and West Bengal. For ‘third’, ‘fourth & above’ order live births, the percentage
contribution at National level is significantly higher in rural areas (24.3) compared to urban
areas (14.9). Charts 64 and 65 depict the percentage distribution of current live births by
birth order for rural and urban areas respectively.
