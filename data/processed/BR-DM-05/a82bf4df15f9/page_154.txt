all the bigger States/UTs, except for West Bengal. The States having death rate higher
than or equal to the National level are Assam 6.5, Karnataka 6.5, Telangana 6.6, Himachal
Pradesh 6.6, Uttarakhand 6.7, Tamil Nadu 6.7, Uttar Praradesh 6.7, Madhya Pradesh 6.8,
