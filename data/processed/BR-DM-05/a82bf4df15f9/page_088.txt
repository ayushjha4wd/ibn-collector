3.7 The Statement 16 below presents sex ratio at birth during the period 2015-17. The
sex ratio at birth for the country for the period 2015-17 (3-years average) has been estimated
at 896. At National level, it is 898 in rural areas and 890 in urban areas. Among the bigger
States/UTs, the sex ratio at birth varies from 961 in Chhattisgarh to 833 in Haryana. In the
rural areas, the highest and the lowest sex ratio at birth are in the States of Chhattisgarh
(985) and Haryana (828) respectively. The sex ratio at birth in urban areas varies from 950
in Madhya Pradesh to 816 in Uttarakhand. Chart 32 depicts the variation in sex ratio at birth
by residence among bigger States/UTs in the country.
