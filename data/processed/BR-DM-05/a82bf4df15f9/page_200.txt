estimated to be 0.6. Rural-urban differentials exist with the urban areas registering
lower death rates as compared to that in rural areas in majority of the States. Among the
bigger States/UTs, the lowest death rate of 0.2 in this age group is registered in Kerala,
Himachal Pradesh and Delhi; and the highest in Odisha at 1.1.
