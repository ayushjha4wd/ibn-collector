3.6 Statement 15 below provides the percentage change in average crude birth rate between
the periods 1999-01 and 2009-11 for India and bigger States separately for rural and urban
areas. At the national level, the rate of decline in birth rate is 14.0 per cent. The rate of decline
in average birth rate varies from 24.2 per cent in Himachal Pradesh to 13.3 per cent in Bihar.
Such decline in rural areas is from 23.7 per cent in Himachal Pradesh to 12.5 per cent in
Assam. In urban areas, the decline ranges from 32.5 per cent in Himachal Pradesh to 8.4 per
cent in Rajasthan. In Kerala, the average crude birth rate in rural areas is nearly same as in the
urban areas during 2009-11.
