3.17 The difference in the pattern of child bearing can be measured in term of the mean age
of fertility, which describes the age pattern of fertility of synthetic cohort of hypothetical group
of women viewed as having, in their lifetime, similar fertility experience recorded in a calendar
year. The mean age of fertility has been calculated from the age specific fertility rates. The
associated standard deviations are also shown. The States having lower values of standard
deviation recorded higher concentration of fertility about the mean age as compared to those
with higher values. Statement 24 below shows the mean age of fertility for India and bigger
States. At the National level, the mean age of fertility is 26.5 years and varies from 24.3 in
Andhra Pradesh to 29.6 years in Jammu & Kashmir with standard deviation 5.5, 4.2 and 5.9
respectively.
