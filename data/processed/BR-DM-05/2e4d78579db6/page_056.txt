3.7 The Statement 16 below presents sex ratio at birth during the period 2009-11. The
sex ratio at birth for the country for the period 2009-11 (3-years average) has been estimated
as 906. At National level, it is 907 in rural area and 900 in urban area. Among the bigger
States, the sex ratio at birth varies from 991 in Chhattisgarh to 841 in Punjab. In the rural
areas, the highest and the lowest sex ratio at birth are in the States of Chhattisgarh (1001)
and Punjab (829) respectively. The sex ratio in urban areas varies from 977 in Kerala to 844
in Jharkhand. Chart 10 depicts the variation in sex ratio by residence among bigger States in
