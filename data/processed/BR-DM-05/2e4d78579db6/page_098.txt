IMR is reported to be 44 and varies from 48 in rural areas to 29 in urban areas. Among
the bigger States, it varies from 12 in Kerala to 59 in Madhya Pradesh. Female infants
experienced a higher mortality than male infants in all States. Chart 20 provides the
