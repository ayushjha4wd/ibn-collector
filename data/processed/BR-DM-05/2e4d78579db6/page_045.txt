2.11 Persons below 10 years of age, in the SRS, are being treated as unmarried. Marital
distribution of population would be more meaningful if such percentages are worked out
for persons of age 10 years and above. Statement 9 given below provides percentage
distribution of population for age 10 years and above by marital status and sex for India
and bigger States. At the National level, the percent share of married population is 58.0.
Among the bigger States Jammu & Kashmir (50.1) recorded the lowest percentage of
married population whereas the highest percentage was recorded for Andhra Pradesh
(62.7).
