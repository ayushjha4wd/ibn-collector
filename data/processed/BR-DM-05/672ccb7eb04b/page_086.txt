3.7 The Statement 16 below presents sex ratio at birth during the period 2016-18. The
sex ratio at birth for the country for the period 2016-18 (3-years average) has been estimated
at 899. At National level, it is 900 in rural areas and 897 in urban areas. Among the bigger
States/UTs, the sex ratio at birth varies from 958 in Chhattisgarh to 840 in Uttarakhand. In
the rural areas, the highest and the lowest sex ratio at birth are in the States of Chhattisgarh
(976) and Haryana (840) respectively. The sex ratio at birth in urban areas varies from 968
in Madhya Pradesh to 810 in Uttarakhand. Chart 32 depicts the variation in sex ratio at birth
by residence among bigger States/UTs in the country.
