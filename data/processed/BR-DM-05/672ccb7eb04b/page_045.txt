2.6 Percentage share of population in the selected age groups for bigger States/UTs by
residence and sex are given in Statements 4 to 7. Statement 4 gives the percentage of population
below 5 years of age. At the National level, 6.8 percent population in urban areas constitutes
below 5 years of age as against 8.6 percent for rural areas. Sex differences in the share of child
population at the national level are negligible both in rural and urban areas. The difference in the
share of male child population to female child is maximum (about 1 percent higher) in rural areas
of Uttarakhand. Among the bigger States/UTs, the percentage of child population in rural areas
varies from 5.3 in Telangana to 11.9 in Bihar. In urban areas, such variation is from 5.4 in
Himachal Pradesh to 10.6 in Uttarakhand. Chart 4 shows the pictorial presentation of percentage
of child population for bigger States/UTs.
