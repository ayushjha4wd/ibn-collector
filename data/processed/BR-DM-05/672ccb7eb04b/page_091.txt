3.10 Changes in the average level of General Fertility Rate (GFR) between 2006-08 and
2016-18 for India and bigger States/UTs are shown below in Statement 18. At the all India
level, a decline of 19.8 percent in GFR has been registered during the decade and it varies from
20.1 percent in rural to 14.1 percent in urban areas. Among the bigger States/UTs, the
percentage decline in GFR varies from 26.8 in Jammu & Kashmir to 4.8 in Kerala. Charts 35,
36 and 37 depict the average GFR between 2006-08 and 2016-18 of India and bigger
States/UTs for Total, Rural and Urban areas respectively. Chart 38 shows the percentage
change in average GFR between 2006-08 and 2016-18 by residence for India and bigger
States/UTs.
