Birth interval
3.34 The percentage distribution of second and higher order live births by interval
between current and previous live birth is shown below in Statement 37 for India and bigger
States/UTs for the year 2018. At the National level, 1.6 percent of the live births occur
within one year from the previous live birth. Such percentage varies from 0.4 in Kerala to
3.2 in Jammu & Kashmir. The percentage of births beyond three years of birth interval
from the previous live birth for India is 49.6. It varies from 37.1 in Bihar to 73.7 in West
Bengal. Chart 66 presents the percentage distribution of second and higher order live births
by interval for India and bigger States/UTs.
