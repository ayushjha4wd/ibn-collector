4.5 Statement 40 below provides the Crude Death Rate (CDR) by residence for India
and bigger States/UTs for the year 2018. The CDR at national level is 6.2 varying from
6.7 in rural to 5.1 in urban areas. Among the bigger States/UTs, it varies from 3.3 in Delhi
to 8.0 in Chhattisgarh. The CDR of the rural areas is higher than that of the urban areas in
all the bigger States/UTs, except for West Bengal. The States having death rate higher
than or equal to the National level are Telangana 6.3, Karnataka 6.3, Assam 6.4, Tamil
Nadu 6.5, Punjab 6.6, Uttar Pradesh 6.6, Madhya Pradesh 6.7, Andhra Pradesh 6.7, Kerala
6.9, Himachal Pradesh 6.9, Odisha 7.3 and Chhattisgarh 8.0. The graphical representation
of death rate of bigger States/UTs by residence is depicted in Chart 72. Distribution of
bigger States/UTs by level of CDR in rural and urban areas is shown in Chart 73. The
