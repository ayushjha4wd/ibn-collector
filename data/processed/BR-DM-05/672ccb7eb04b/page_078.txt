3.5 Statement 14 below provides the Crude Birth Rate (CBR) by residence for India and
bigger States/UTs for the year 2018. The CBR at national level is 20.0 varying from 21.6 in
rural to16.7 in urban areas. Andhra Pradesh, Delhi, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu &
Kashmir, Karnataka, Kerala, Maharashtra, Odisha, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Telangana,
Uttarakhand and West Bengal are the States having birth rate below 20. On the other hand,
Bihar has the highest birth rate in rural areas (26.8) followed by Uttar Pradesh (26.6); whereas
Uttar Pradesh has the highest birth rate in urban areas (22.5) followed by Bihar (21.9). The
lowest CBR was recorded in rural areas of Kerala (13.8) and in urban areas of Himachal
Pradesh (10.3). Based on the figures in the Statement 14 given below, the graphical
