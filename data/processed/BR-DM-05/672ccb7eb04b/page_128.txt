Fertility by level of education of the women
3.26 Education, more precisely the female education, has a direct impact on fertility. To
ascertain levels of fertility by educational status of the women, three indicators viz. general
fertility rate, age-specific fertility rate and total fertility rate by educational status of the
women have been worked out separately for rural and urban areas. These are discussed in
the subsequent paragraphs.
3.27 Statement 31 below indicates the percentage of female population in the age group
15-49 by level of education, at the National level and for the bigger States/UTs. At the
National level, 13.0 percent of the female population is reported 'Illiterate' as against 87.0
percent in the 'Literate' category. About 18.9 percent have education up to Class X, 12.5
percent women have education level of Class XII, and only 9.8 percent have reported
education level of graduate and above. Among the illiterates, Kerala 0.5 has the lowest and
Bihar 23.5 the highest percentage of illiterate women.
