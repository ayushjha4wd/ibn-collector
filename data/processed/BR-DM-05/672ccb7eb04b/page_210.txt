Medical attention before death
4.22 The type of medical attention received before death is also collected in the SRS. In
the new samples from 2004 onwards, the options on types of medical attention received
before death have been modified to capture the deaths occurred specifically at private
hospital/nursing homes. The options include ‘Government Hospital’, ‘Private Hospital’,
‘Qualified professional’, ‘Untrained functionary’ and ‘others’. The options for the sample
based on 1991 sample were ‘Institutional’; ‘Medical attention other than institution’; and
‘No medical attention’. Statement 57 given below presents the percentage distribution of
deaths by type of medical attention received before death for India and bigger States/UTs
separately for rural and urban areas for the year 2018. At the National level, 29.4 percent
of the deaths occurred at Government Hospitals and varies from 27.1 percent in rural areas
to 35.4 percent in urban areas. Among the bigger States/UTs, Himachal Pradesh 51.0 has
the highest percentage of deaths in Government Hospitals and Bihar (19.9) has the lowest.
Deaths in private hospitals account for 18.5 percent and it varies from 11.9 percent in
Jammu & Kashmir and West Bengal to 36.8 percent in Kerala. About 19.2 percent deaths
were attended by untrained functionaries and others.
