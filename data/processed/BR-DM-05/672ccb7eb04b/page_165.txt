in Statement 45 for India and bigger States/UTs. At the National level, such share in the

urban areas. In rural areas, Kerala registered 1.8 percent, the lowest percentage share of
infant deaths as compared to 19.4 in Madhya Pradesh. In urban areas, the variation is
from 1.1 percent in Kerala to 14.8 percent in Uttar Pradesh.
