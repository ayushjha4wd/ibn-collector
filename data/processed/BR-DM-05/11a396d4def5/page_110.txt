Mean Age of Fertility:
3.18 The difference in the pattern of child bearing can be assessed in terms of the Mean Age of
Fertility, which describes the age pattern of fertility of synthetic cohort of hypothetical group of
women viewed as having, in their lifetime, similar fertility experience recorded in a given year.
The Mean Age of Fertility has been calculated from the Age-Specific Fertility Rate. The Mean
Age of Fertility is the average age at which women give birth to their first child. The associated
standard deviations are also shown. The States/UTs having lower values of standard deviation
recorded higher concentrations of fertility about the mean age as compared to those with higher
values. Statement 23 below shows the Mean Age of Fertility for India and bigger States/UTs in
2023. The Mean Age of Fertility for India was 28.3 years, ranging from 26.5 years in West
Bengal to 31.8 years in Jammu & Kashmir. The Mean Age of Fertility is slightly higher in urban
areas (28.7 years) compared to rural areas (28.2 years). Chart 48 shows the Mean Age of Fertility
of India and bigger States/UTs by residence.
