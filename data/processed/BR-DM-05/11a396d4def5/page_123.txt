Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharastra, Odisha, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu and Telangana,
the ASMFR is at peak level in the age-group 20-24. There is a significant decline in marital
fertility for women aged 30 years onwards, for all bigger States/UTs.
