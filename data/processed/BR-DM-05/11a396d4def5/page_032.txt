1.15 In urban areas, the categories of towns/cities have been divided into four strata based on
the size classes in contrast to the six strata in the earlier sampling frame. Towns with population
less than one lakh have been placed under stratum I, towns/cities with population one lakh or
more but less than 5 lakhs under stratum II, towns/cities with population 5 lakh or more under
stratum III and four metro cities of Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata as separate strata viz.
stratum IV. The sampling unit in urban area is a Census Enumeration Block. The Census
Enumeration Blocks within each size stratum were ordered by the female literacy rate based on
the Census 2011 data, and three equal-size sub-strata were established. The sample Census
Enumeration Block within each substratum was selected at random with equal probability. A
simple random sample of these enumeration blocks has been selected within each sub-stratum
without replacement from each of the size classes of towns/cities in each State/Union Territory.
Sample Size:
1.16 The Infant Mortality is the decisive indicator for estimation of sample size. The
permissible level of error has been taken as within 15 prse (percentage relative standard error) at
Natural Division level in respect of bigger States/UTs having population more than 10 million as
per Census 2011. For minor States/UTs, 15 prse has been fixed at the total State level. By and
large the above criteria have been followed. However, there have been a few exceptions on
account of operational constraints. Half Yearly Survey 2023 was conducted in 8804 sample units,
covering about 8.8 million sample population.
