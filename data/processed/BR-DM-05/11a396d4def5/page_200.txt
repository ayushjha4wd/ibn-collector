Survival to Age-5:
4.19 Survival to Age-5 or the Probability of Survival to Age-5 is defined as the probability
that a child born during the specified year will survive till age 5, subject to current Age-Specific
Mortality Rates. Survival to Age 5 or Probability of Survival to Age 5 is calculated as the
complement of the Under-5 Mortality Rate. Probability of Survival to Age-5 by sex and
residence for the year 2023 is given in statement 54 for India and bigger States/UTs. The
probability of survival to Age 5 in the year 2023 was estimated at 0.971 for the country as a
whole, 0.970 for males, and 0.971 for females. The Probability of Survival to Age 5 was
estimated as 0.967 for Rural areas and 0.980 for Urban Areas during 2023. The probability of
Survival to Age-5 varies from 0.956 in Madhya Pradesh to 0.992 in Kerala.
