4.11 Statement 47 given below presents Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) by sex and
residence for the year 2012 for India and bigger States. Infant Mortality Rate is defined
as the infant deaths (less than one year) per thousand live births. At the National level,
IMR is reported to be 42 and varies from 46 in rural areas to 28 in urban areas. Among
the bigger States, it varies from 12 in Kerala to 56 in Madhya Pradesh. Female infants
experienced a higher mortality than male infants in all States. Chart 20 provides the
