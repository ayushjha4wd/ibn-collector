Fertility by level of education of the women
3.26 Education, more precisely the female education, has a direct impact on fertility. To
ascertain levels of fertility by educational status of the women three indicators viz. general
fertility rate, age-specific fertility rate and total fertility rate by educational status of the
women have been worked out separately for rural and urban areas. These are discussed in
the subsequent paragraphs.
3.27 Statement 32 below indicates the percentage of female population in the age group
15-49 by level of education, at the National level and for the bigger States. At the National
level, 30.1 per cent of the female population is reported 'Illiterate' as against 69.9 per cent in
the 'Literate' category. Of the literate women, about 81.8 per cent have education up to
Class X, 10.7 per cent women have education level of Class XII, and only 7.4 per cent have
reported education level of graduate and above. Among the illiterates, Kerala (1.2) has the
lowest and Bihar (49.3) the highest percentage of illiterate women.
