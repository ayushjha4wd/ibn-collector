3.7 The Statement 16 below presents sex ratio at birth during the period 2010-12. The
sex ratio at birth for the country for the period 2010-12 (3-years average) has been estimated
as 908. At National level, it is 909 in rural area and 904 in urban area. Among the bigger
States, the sex ratio at birth varies from 979 in Chhattisgarh to 857 in Haryana. In the rural
areas, the highest and the lowest sex ratio at birth are in the States of Chhattisgarh (986) and
Punjab (856) respectively. The sex ratio in urban areas varies from 979 in Kerala to 852 in
Haryana. Chart 10 depicts the variation in sex ratio by residence among bigger States in the
country.
