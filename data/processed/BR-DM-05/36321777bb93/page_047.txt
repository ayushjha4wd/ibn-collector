age 15 years and above by residence. At the National level, 68.3 percent of married
females exists in age 15+ and 69.0 percent comes under rural areas. Among the bigger
States, percentage of such married female varies from 59.0 in Jammu & Kashmir to 72.4
in Bihar. The percentage of never married female varies from 15.5 in Andhra Pradesh to
33.1 in Jammu & Kashmir. The maximum percentage of Widowed/Divorced/Separated
females is in Tamil Nadu (16.5) and the minimum 7.4 in Bihar.
