2.12 Statement 10 placed below shows the percentage distribution of Female age 10 years
and more by marital status and residence. At the National level, 59.8 per cent of such
females are married. The proportion of married female is slightly higher in rural areas than
that of urban areas in India . However the trend in the States of Assam, Gujarat, Haryana,
Jammu & Kashmir, Karnataka, Kerala, Odisha, Rajasthan, Punjab and Tamil Nadu is
otherwise. Among the bigger States the lowest percentage of married female is found to be
in Jammu & Kashmir (52.7) and the highest in Haryana (63.1). Percentage of never married
Female in the bigger States varies from 24.0 in Kerala to 40.2 in Jammu & Kashmir. The
percentage of female in the category Widowed/Divorced/Separated varies from 6.2 in Bihar
to 15.1 in Tamil Nadu.
