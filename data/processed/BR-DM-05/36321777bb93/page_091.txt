population and it varies from 7.6 in rural areas to 5.6 in urban areas. Among the bigger
States, it varies from 4.2 in Delhi to 8.5 in Odisha. The States having death rate higher
than or equal to the National level are Andhra Pradesh (7.4), Assam (7.9), Chhattisgarh
(7.9), Karnataka (7.1), Madhya Pradesh (8.1), Odisha (8.5), Uttar Pradesh (7.7) and Tamil
Nadu (7.4). Distribution of bigger States by level of CDR in rural and urban areas is
shown in Chart 19. The estimated age-specific death rate by sex and residence for India
