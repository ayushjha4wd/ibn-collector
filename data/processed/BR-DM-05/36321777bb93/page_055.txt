3.6 Statement 15 below provides the percentage change in average crude birth rate between
the periods 2000-02 and 2010-12 for India and bigger States separately for rural and urban
areas. At the national level, the rate of decline in birth rate is 14.2 per cent. The rate of decline
in average birth rate varies from 23.6 per cent in Punjab to 9.6 per cent in Gujarat. Such
decline in rural areas is from 23.5 per cent in Punjab to 10.1 per cent in Gujarat. In urban areas,
the decline ranges from 33.3 per cent in Himachal Pradesh to 8.9 per cent in Rajasthan. In
Kerala and Tamil Nadu, the average crude birth rate in rural areas is nearly same as in the
urban areas during 2010-12.
