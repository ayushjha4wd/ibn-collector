2.18 Statement 9 given below provides percentage distribution of population for age 10 years
and above by Marital Status and Sex for India and bigger States/UTs for the year 2021. The data
is given in terms of percentage of individuals who are never married, married and
widowed/divorced/separated, divided by sex. In 2021, for India as a whole, 41.8% of the
population aged 10 and above were never married, while 54.2% were married, and 4% were
widowed/divorced/separated. Among males, 47.8% were never married, while 50.4% were
married and 1.8% were widowed/divorced/separated. Among females, 35.7% were never
married, while 58.1% were married and 6.2% were widowed/divorced/separated.
2.19 The data show some variation across the different States/UTs. For instance, the percentage
of the population aged 10 and above who have never married is lowest in Kerala (34.5%) and
highest in Jammu & Kashmir (50.8%) in 2021. The percentage of individuals who are married is
highest in Andhra Pradesh (59.3%) and lowest in Jammu & Kashmir (47.1%). The percentage of
individuals who are widowed/divorced/separated is highest in Tamil Nadu (6.7%) and lowest in
Bihar and Jammu & Kashmir (2.1%).
2.20 The percentage of males above 10 years who were never married in 2021 is highest in
Jammu and Kashmir (54.1%) and lowest in Andhra Pradesh (40.8%). The percentage of married
males during 2021 is found to highest in Andhra Pradesh (56.6%) and lowest in Jammu &
Kashmir (44.7%). The widowed/divorced/separated status in case of males is highest in Tamil
Nadu (2.7%) and lowest in Delhi and Jammu & Kashmir (1.2%). In case of females, the highest
percentage of never married is found in Jammu & Kashmir (47.4%) and lowest in Kerala
(26.5%). The highest proportion of married females is found in Kerala (62.9%) and lowest in
Jammu & Kashmir (49.6%). The widowed/divorced/separated status in case of females is highest
in Tamil Nadu (10.7%) and lowest in Bihar (2.8%). Charts 15, 16 and 17 show the distribution
