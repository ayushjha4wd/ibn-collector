Death Rate for Children aged 5-14 years:
4.20 Death Rates for children in the age group 5-14 for the year 2021 are given below in
Statement 55. The overall Death Rate for children aged 5-14 years in India was 0.5, with slightly
higher rates for females (0.5) than for males (0.4) and higher rates in rural areas (0.5) than in
urban areas (0.3). Among the bigger States and UTs, Madhya Pradesh had the highest death rate
for children aged 5-14 years (0.8), followed by Jharkhand (0.7), Chhattisgarh (0.6), Odisha (0.6),
Assam (0.6) and Bihar (0.6). Delhi and Jammu & Kashmir had the lowest death rate (0.1) for the
children in the age group 5-14. There were also considerable differences in death rates between
rural and urban areas within each state/UT. Chart 106 to 108 shows death rates for children aged
5-14 years by Sex for India and bigger States/UTs by place of residence viz, Total, Rural and
Urban.
