3.31 Information on order of the live birth and interval between current and previous live births
are also collected in SRS from 1990 onwards. These provide useful information on spacing of
children and level of fertility. The estimated percentages on order of live birth and birth interval for
India and bigger States/UTs are given in Tables 5, 6 and 7. Based on these tables, State-wise
comparative Statements are presented below.
3.32 The percentage distribution of live births by birth order for India and bigger States/UTs for
the year 2021 is presented below in Statement 35. As per 2021 data, in India, 60.6% of current live
births are first-order births, 25.5% are second-order births, 9.0% are third-order births and 4.9% are
fourth or higher-order births. As per 2021 data, among the bigger States/UTs, Delhi has the highest
percentage of first-order births at 82.4%, while Madhya Pradesh has the lowest at 44.7%. Kerala has
the highest percentage of second-order births at 36.6%, while Delhi has the lowest at 11.8%. Bihar
has the highest percentage of third-order births at 16.0%, while Delhi has the lowest at 4.2%. Bihar
also has the highest percentage of fourth or higher-order births at 11.0%, while Andhra Pradesh has
the lowest at 0.7%. Chart 65 shows the percentage distribution of current live births by birth order
for India and bigger States/UTs.
