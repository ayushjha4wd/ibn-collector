3.10 Changes in the average level of General Fertility Rate (GFR) between 2009-11 and 2019-
21 for India and bigger States/UTs in terms of residence is given in Statement 18. Overall, the data
shows that the GFR has decreased in India and for most of the bigger States/UTs between 2009-11
and 2019-21. The total GFR in India has decreased from 83.9 in 2009-11 to 67.6 in 2019-21, which
represents a 19.4% decrease. The rural GFR has decreased by 19.0%, from 91.9 to 74.4, while the
urban GFR has decreased by 15.2%, from 64.0 to 54.3.
3.11 Among the States/UTs, Bihar had the highest GFR in both 2009-11 and 2019-21 of 113.7
and 94.9 respectively. Delhi has experienced the largest percent decrease among all States/UTs, with
the GFR falling by 27.5% from 65.4 to 47.4. Kerala had the lowest GFR among all states/UTs in
both 2009-11 and 2019-21, and it has also experienced 2nd smallest percent decrease of 13.6% after
Himachal Pradesh of 12.5%. Bihar has the smallest percent decrease during last 10 years of 3.6%
followed by West Bengal (9.7%) and Madhya Pradesh (9.9%) in Urban GFR. The highest decrease
in Urban GFR during last decade is observed in Delhi (26.2%) followed by Jammu and Kashmir
(21.2%) and Odisha (19.7%). The smallest percent decrease in the rural GFR is observed in
Himachal Pradesh (12.7%) followed by Kerala (14.0%) and Madhya Pradesh (14.1%), while highest
percent decrease in the rural GFR is observed in Delhi (27.8%) followed by Jammu & Kashmir
(24.8%). Charts 38, 39 and 40 depict the average GFR between 2009-11 and 2019-21 and percent
