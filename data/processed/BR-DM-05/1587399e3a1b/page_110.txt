Mean Age of Fertility:
3.18 The difference in the pattern of child bearing can be measured in terms of the mean age of
fertility, which describes the age pattern of fertility of synthetic cohort of hypothetical group of
women viewed as having, in their lifetime, similar fertility experience recorded in a calendar year.
The mean age of fertility has been calculated from the Age-Specific Fertility Rates. The mean age
of fertility is the average age at which women give birth to their first child. The associated standard
deviations are also shown. The States having lower values of standard deviation recorded higher
concentrations of fertility about the mean age as compared to those with higher values. Statement
23 below shows the mean age of fertility for India and bigger States/UTs in 2021. The mean age of
fertility for India was 28.4 years, ranging from 26.3 years in West Bengal to 32.0 years in Jammu &
Kashmir. The mean age of fertility is slightly higher in urban areas (28.7 years) compared to rural
areas (28.3 years). Chart 48 shows the mean age of fertility of India and bigger States/UTs by
residence.
