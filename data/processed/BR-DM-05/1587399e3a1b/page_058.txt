Marital Status
2.15 Besides age, the other important demographic characteristic collected and subsequently
updated during each half-yearly survey of the SRS is marital status. Distribution of the population
by marital status is thus available from SRS.
2.16 The statement 8 presents the percentage distribution of the population by marital status and
sex for India and bigger States/UTs in India in the year 2021. The data is presented in three
categories: Never married, Married and Widowed/Divorced/Separated (W/D/S). For India in 2021,
51.2% of the total population had never been married, with a higher percentage among males
(56.4%) than females (45.8%). On the other hand, 45.5% of the total population was reported as
married, with a higher percentage among females (49.0%) than males (42.1%). The remaining 3.3%
of the population was either widowed, divorced or separated, with a higher percentage among
females (5.2%) than males (1.5%).
2.17 Among the major States/UTs, the percentage distribution of the population by marital status
and sex varied considerably in 2021. For instance, Bihar has the highest percentage of the never
married population (58.9%), while Kerala has the lowest percentage (43.0%). The highest
percentage of the population was reported as widowed, divorced or separated in Tamil Nadu (5.9%)
and lowest in Bihar (1.7%). The highest and lowest percentage of married population was reported
in Andhra Pradesh (51.7%) and Bihar (39.4%). Chart 12-14 represents the distribution of population
according to marital status for Persons, Males and Females respectively.
