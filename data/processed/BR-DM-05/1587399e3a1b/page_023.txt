EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The Sample Registration System (SRS) is the largest demographic survey in the
Country mandated to provide annual estimates of fertility as well as mortality indicators
at the State and National level. The present Report contains data on fertility and mortality
indicators for the year 2021 for India and bigger States/UTs. The estimates are segregated
by residence and also by gender, wherever required.
2. The salient features of the report are as summarized below.
❖ The Crude Birth Rate (CBR) at the National level during 2021 stands at 19.3
exhibiting a decline of 0.2 points over 2020. Among the bigger States/UTs, the
maximum CBR has been reported in Bihar (25.6) and the minimum in Kerala
(12.9).
❖ There has been a decline of 1.1 points in the CBR for the Country from 2016 to
2021. The corresponding decline in rural CBR is 1.2 points and in urban CBR 1.1
points.
❖ The Crude Death Rate (CDR) for the Country is 7.5 in 2021. Among the bigger
States/UTs, the maximum CDR has been reported for Chhattisgarh (10.1) and the
minimum for Delhi (5.4).
❖ The Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) has also registered 1-point decline to 27 in 2021
from 28 in 2020 at the National level. Among the bigger States/UTs, the
maximum IMR has been observed in Madhya Pradesh (41) and the minimum in
Kerala (6).
❖ IMR for the Country has come down to 27 in 2021 from 34 in 2016, a decline of
7 points over last 5 years and an annual average decline of about 1.4 points. The
corresponding decline in rural IMR has been to the tune of 8 points (38 in 2016
to 30 in 2021) against a decline of 5 points in urban IMR (23 in 2016 to 18 in
2021). Both the genders have shown decline in the period 2016-21.
❖ Despite this decline, one in every 37 infants at the National level, one in every 33
infants in rural areas and one in every 56 infants in urban areas die within one
year of life.
❖ In 2021, U5MR for the Country has shown a decline of 1 point from 2020 (31 in
2021 against 32 in 2020). There has been no a decline in male U5MR and 2 points
decline in female U5MR during the corresponding period.
❖ Sex Ratio at Birth for the Country has gone up by 6 points to 913 in 2019-21 from
907 in 2018-20. Kerala has reported the highest Sex Ratio at Birth (962) while
Uttarakhand, the lowest (852). Sex Ratio at Birth for Rural and Urban areas was
912 and 918 respectively during 2019-21.
