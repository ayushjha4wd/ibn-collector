2.5 Percentage share of the population in the selected age groups for bigger States/UTs by
residence and sex for the year 2021 are given in Statements 4 to 7. Statement 4 gives state-wise
percentage of the child population (below 5 years of age). In India, the percentage of the population
in the age group 0-4 years is 8.0%, with males and females being 8.2% and 7.8%, respectively. In
rural areas, it is 8.7%, with males and females being 8.9% and 8.4%, respectively. In urban areas, it
is 6.7%, with males and females being 6.9% and 6.5%, respectively.
2.6 Among the bigger States/UTs, Bihar has the highest percentage of the population in the age
group 0-4 years with 11.2%, followed by Uttarakhand and Madhya Pradesh (9.3%) and Rajasthan
(9.2%). The lowest percentage is in Punjab and Tamil Nadu with 6.0%. In rural areas also, Bihar
has the highest percentage with 11.5%, followed by Madhya Pradesh, Jharkhand and Rajasthan
(9.8%), and Chhattisgarh (9.6%). The lowest percentage is in Telangana with 6.0%. In urban areas,
Uttarakhand has the highest percentage of the population in the age group 0-4 years with 9.2%,
followed by Bihar (8.7%), and Haryana (7.6%). The lowest percentage is in Himachal Pradesh with
5.0%.
2.7 Among the bigger States/UTs, the percentage of the child population in rural areas varies from
6.0% in Telangana to 11.5% in Bihar. In urban areas, it varies from 5.0% in Himachal Pradesh to
9.2% in Uttarakhand. In terms of total population, it varies from 6.0% in Punjab and Tamil Nadu to
11.2% in Bihar. The percentage of males in the 0-4 age group is slightly higher than females in most
States. However, the difference is relatively small, with the largest gap observed in Uttarakhand
where the percentage of males is 0.9 percentage points higher than females. Chart 4 and 5 shows the
percentage of child population for bigger States/UTs by residence and sex respectively.
