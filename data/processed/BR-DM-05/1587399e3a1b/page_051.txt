2.10 Statement 6 provides information on the percentage of the population in the working age
group 15-59 years by sex and residence for India and bigger States/UTs in 2021. At the National
level, the total percentage of the population in the age group 15-59 years in India is 66.2%, with
65.8% for males and 66.5% for females. The rural areas have a slightly lower percentage of the
population in this age group (64.8%) compared to urban areas (69.0%). Among the States,
Telangana and Delhi had the highest percentage of the population in the age group 15-59 years,
with 70.5%, followed by Andhra Pradesh (70.2%) and Punjab (69.5%). Bihar has the lowest
percentage of the population in this age group at 60.6%, followed by Madhya Pradesh (63.6%)
and Rajasthan (63.8%).
2.11 There are also variations in the percentage of males and females in this age group across
different States and between rural and urban areas. For instance, Telangana has the highest
percentage of females in this age group in rural areas at 70.5%, while Assam has the highest
percentage of females in the age group 15-59 years in urban areas at 73.1%. Delhi has the highest
percentage of males in the age group 15-59 years in rural areas at 71.4%, while Himachal Pradesh
has the highest percentage of males in this age group in urban areas at 71.2%. Chart 8 and 9 gives
the pictorial presentation of percentage of population in the age group 15-59 years for bigger
States/UTs by Residence and Sex respectively.
