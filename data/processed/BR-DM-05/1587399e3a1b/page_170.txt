Infant Mortality Rate:
4.11 Statement 46 given below presents Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) by sex and residence for
the year 2021 for India and States/UTs. The IMR varies across different States/UTs in India,
ranging from 4 in Sikkim and Manipur to 41 in Madhya Pradesh. In most cases, the IMR is higher
in rural areas compared to urban areas. Assam had the highest difference in IMR between rural
(36) and urban areas (16). There was also the discernible difference in the IMR between Males
and Females in Some of the States/UTs. Himachal Pradesh had the highest difference in IMR
between males and females, with a rate of 22 for males and 12 for females. Lakshadweep had the
highest IMR among the smaller UTs with a rate of 16, and Meghalaya had the highest IMR
among the smaller States with a rate of 30. Chart 84-86 below represents the IMR by Sex for
Total, Rural and Urban. Chart 87 presents the distribution of bigger States/UTs by Rural and
Urban IMR.
