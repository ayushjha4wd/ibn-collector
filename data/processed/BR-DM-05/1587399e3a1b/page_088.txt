3.7 Statement 15 below provides the percentage change in average Crude Birth Rate between
the periods 2009-11 and 2019-21 for India and the bigger States/UTs separately for rural and urban
areas. Between 2009-11 and 2019-21, there was an overall 12.2% decrease in the average CBR for
India. Among the States/UTs, the percentage change in the average CBR ranged from -20.2% for
Delhi to -8.9% for Bihar. In terms of residence, the 3-year average Crude Birth Rate has declined in
both rural and urban areas of all bigger States/UTs and the rural CBR was higher than the urban
CBR in all bigger States/UTs. The percentage change in rural CBR ranged from -21.4% for Delhi
to -8.7% for Bihar whereas the percentage change in urban CBR ranged from -18.9% for Delhi to
-2.5% for Uttarakhand. Charts 31, 32 and 33 depict the average birth rate between 2009-11 and
2019-21 of India and bigger States/UTs for Total, Rural and Urban areas respectively along with
respective rate of change. Chart 34 shows the year-wise birth rate from 1985 to 2021 by Residence
for India.
