2.21 Statement 10 placed below shows the percentage distribution of Females aged 10 years
and more by marital status and residence in India and bigger States/Union Territories (UTs) in
2021. At the National level, 35.7% of females aged 10 and above were never married, 58.1%
were married and 6.2% were either widowed, divorced or separated. In terms of residence, 36.0%
of rural females and 35.2% of urban females were never married 58.0% of rural females and
58.1% of urban females were married and 6.0% of rural females and 6.7% of urban females were
either widowed, divorced or separated.
2.22 Among the bigger States/UTs, the proportion of never-married females is lowest in Kerala
(26.5%) and highest in Jammu & Kashmir (47.4%). The proportion of married females is lowest
in Jammu & Kashmir (49.6%) and highest in Kerala (62.9%). The proportion of
widowed/divorced/separated females is highest in Tamil Nadu (10.7%) and lowest in Bihar
(2.8%). Charts 18 and 19 show the distribution of the female population aged 10 years and above
by marital status of India and bigger States/UTs for Rural and Urban areas respectively.
