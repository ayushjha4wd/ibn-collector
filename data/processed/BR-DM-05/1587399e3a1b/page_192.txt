Death Rate for Children aged 0-4 years:
4.17 The death rates for children below age 5 by residence are given below in Statement 52
separately for males and females for the year 2021. While the total death rate for India was 7.3 in
2021, the male death rate was 7.3 and the female death rate was slightly higher at 7.4. The death rate
in rural areas was however much higher at 8.3 than that of urban areas at 4.9. Among the bigger
States, Madhya Pradesh had the highest total death rate for children below 5 years of age at 12.1,
with the male death rate at 12.5 and the female death rate at 11.6. Kerala had the lowest total death
rate at 1.5, with the male death rate at 1.8 and the female death rate at 1.3. Chart 100 to 102 shows
Death rate for children age 0-4 years by Sex for India and bigger States/UTs 2021 by place of
residence viz, Total, Rural and Urban respectively.
