3.35 Statement 38 below presents the percentage distribution of second and higher-order live
births by the interval between current and previous live birth separately for rural and urban areas
in 2021. In India, 1.8% of second and higher-order live births in rural areas occurred within 10-
12 months of the previous live birth, while 19.6%, 27.6% and 51.0% of births occurred within
12-24 months, 24-36 months and 36+ months respectively. In urban areas, the percentage
distribution of live births by interval was 1.2%, 15.3%, 26.6% and 57.0%, respectively. The
percentage distribution of live births by interval and residence varied across the different States
and UTs. Charts 69 and 70 give the percentage distribution of second and higher order live births
by interval for rural and urban areas respectively.
