Death Rate for persons aged 60 years and above:
4.23 Death rates for persons aged 60 years and above for the year 2021 by sex and residence
are given below in Statement 58. At the National level, death rate in this age group is estimated
to be 48.6 and males have higher mortality rate (55.3) than females (42.0). This difference exists
in both rural and urban areas and across States. Chhattisgarh had the highest death rate for persons
aged 60 years and above with a death rate of 72.2 for the total population, 86.9 for males and
59.1 for females. The state of Jammu & Kashmir had lowest death rate of 36.1 for the total
population, 37.4 for males and 34.8 for females. Chart 112 to 114 represents death rates for
Persons aged 60 years and above by sex for India and bigger States/UTs by place of residence
viz, Total, Rural and Urban respectively.
