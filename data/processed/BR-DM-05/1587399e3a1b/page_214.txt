Medical Attention before Death:
4.24 The type of medical attention received before death is also collected in the SRS. In the new
samples from 2004 onwards, the options on types of medical attention received before death have
been modified to capture the deaths occurred specifically at private hospital/nursing homes. The
options include ‘Government Hospital’, ‘Private Hospital’, ‘Qualified professional’, ‘Untrained
functionary’ and ‘others’. The options for the sample based on 1991 sample were ‘Institutional’;
‘Medical attention other than institution’ and ‘No medical attention’. Statement 59 given below
presents the percentage distribution of deaths by type of medical attention received before death for
India and bigger States/UTs separately for rural and urban areas for the year 2021. At the National
level, 25.1% of deaths occurred in government hospitals, 14.2% occurred in private hospitals, 13.3%
were attended by a qualified professional and 47.4% had no medical attention or were attended by
an untrained functionary before death in 2021.
4.25 In rural areas, the percentage of deaths that occurred with no medical attention or an
untrained functionary was higher at 51.4%, compared to 37.1% in urban areas. The distribution of
deaths by type of medical attention received before death varies considerably across the States/UTs.
For example, in Delhi, 50.2% of deaths occurred in government hospitals, while only 27.0% had no
medical attention or were attended by an untrained functionary before death. In contrast, in
Jharkhand, 71.1% of deaths occurred with no medical attention or an untrained functionary, while
only 3.6% were attended by a qualified professional.
4.26 In case of rural areas, Untrained functionary & No medical attention received before deaths
covers about 51.4% deaths ranging from 22.2% in Delhi to 74.6% in Jharkhand. While in urban
areas, about 37.1% people have not received medical attention or attended by Untrained
functionaries ranging from 19.5% in West Bengal to 59.2% in Bihar. Chart 115 to 117 represents
distribution of deaths by Type of Medical Attention received before death for India & bigger
States/UTs by place of residence viz, Total, Rural and Urban respectively.
