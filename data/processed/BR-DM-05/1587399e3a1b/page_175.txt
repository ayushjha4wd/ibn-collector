4.12 The percentage change in the average level of Infant Mortality between 2009-11 and
2019-21 in India and the bigger States/Union Territories is given below in Statement 47. The
data reveals that the overall Infant Mortality Rate in India has decreased by 39.8% from 47.2 in
2009-11 to 28.4 in 2019-21. The rural Infant Mortality Rate has decreased by 38.5%, while the
urban Infant Mortality Rate has decreased by 38.7%. Among the bigger States/UTs, the highest
decline in percentage change for Infant Mortality Rate was observed in Delhi, which is 61.8%.
Chhattisgarh had the lowest decline in the percentage for Infant Mortality Rate which is 24.0%.
Madhya Pradesh had the highest Infant Mortality Rate in both periods and has shown a decrease
of 30.7%. Chart 88 to 90 represents the Infant Mortality Rate between 2009-11 & 2019-21 and
the percent change by residence viz, Total, Rural and Urban for India and bigger States/UTs.
