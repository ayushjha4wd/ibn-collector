General Fertility Rate:
3.9 Estimates of General Fertility Rate (GFR), a refined measure of fertility, being defined as
the number of live births per thousand women in the reproductive age group 15-49 years, is a
useful tool for measuring fertility. The GFR for all-India and bigger States/UTs for the year 2021
are given below in Statement 17, separately for rural and urban areas. The overall GFR for India
was 67.2 in 2021, with higher rates in rural areas (74.0) compared to urban areas (53.9). Among
the bigger States/UTs, Bihar had the highest GFR (94.1), followed by Uttar Pradesh (87.6) and
Madhya Pradesh (86.8). These States also had higher GFR in rural areas compared to urban areas.
Kerala had the lowest GFR among the bigger States/UTs (45.8), and there is no significant
difference between rural and urban areas. Chart 36 gives the distribution GFR for bigger
States/UTs in rural and urban areas.
