3.20 Statement 25 below presents the percentage change in the average level of TFR between the
periods 2009-11 and 2019-21 in India and bigger States/UTs by residence. Overall average TFR in
India has decreased from 2.5 in 2009-11 to 2.0 in 2019-21, which is a decrease of 20%. The rural
TFR decreased by 21.4% (from 2.8 to 2.2), while the urban TFR decreased by 15.8% (from 1.9 to
1.6). Among the bigger States/UTs, Delhi had the highest percentage decrease in average TFR
between 2009-11 and 2019-21 (26.3%), followed by Jammu & Kashmir (25%) and Rajasthan
(22.6%) and lowest percentage decrease is observed in Tamil Nadu (11.8%) followed by Bihar
(14.3%) and Maharashtra (15.8%). Charts 51, 52 and 53 depict the average TFR and corresponding
percent change between 2009-11 and 2019-21 for India and bigger States/UTs in terms of Total,
Rural and Urban areas respectively. Chart 54 shows the trend in year-wise TFR from 1985 to 2021
by residence for India.
