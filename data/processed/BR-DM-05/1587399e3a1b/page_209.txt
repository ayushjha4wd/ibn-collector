Adult Survival Rate:
4.22 Adult Survival rate is the probability of survival from age 15 to age 60, based on estimated
mortality rates over this age range. The adult Survival Rate is calculated as a complement to the
probability of dying between the age group 15-59. The statement 57 below gives the Adult
Survival Rates by Sex and Residence for India and bigger States/UTs of the Country. The Adult
Survival Rate for country was 0.840 in 2021 ranging from 0.770 in Chhattisgarh to 0.884 in
Jammu & Kashmir. The Adult Survival Rate for male and female was 0.803 and 0.880
respectively. It was 0.832 for rural and 0.857 for urban areas of the country during 2021.
