Survival to Age-5:
4.19 Survival to Age-5 or the Probability of Survival to Age-5 is defined as the probability of
child born during the specified year will survive till age 5 if subject to current Age-Specific
Mortality Rates. Survival to Age 5 or Probability of Survival to age 5 is calculated as the
complement of the Under-5 Mortality Rate. Probability of Survival to Age-5 by sex and residence
for the year 2021 is given in statement 54 for India and bigger States/UTs. Probability of Survival
to Age-5 was estimated as 0.969 for the Country and both Male and Female during 2021. The
Probability of Survival to Age 5 was estimated as 0.965 for Rural areas and 0.979 for Urban
Areas during 2021.The probability of Survival to Age-5 varies from 0.951 in Madhya Pradesh to
0.992 in Kerala.
