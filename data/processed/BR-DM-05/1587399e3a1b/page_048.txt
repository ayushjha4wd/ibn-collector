2.8 Percentage of the population of age less than 15 years for India and bigger States/UTs by
Sex and Residence for the year 2021 is given below in Statement 5. The data shows that the
percentage of the population in the age group 0-14 years in India is 24.8%. Among them, the
percentage of less than 15 years of males is 25.4% and that of females is 24.2%. The data further
shows that the percentage of the population in the age group 0-14 years in rural areas of India is
26.5%, and in urban areas, it is 21.5%. Among the bigger States/UTs, Bihar has the highest
percentage of population in the age group 0-14 years with 32.5%, followed by Jharkhand (28.4%),
and Madhya Pradesh (28.3%). States like Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and Punjab have relatively
lower percentages with 19.2%, 19.7% and 19.9% respectively.
2.9 The data shows that the percentage of males in the 0-14 age group is higher than females for
all the States/UTs except in urban areas of West Bengal, where the percentage of females is higher
than males. The data also shows that the percentage of the population in the age group 0-14 years is
higher in rural areas compared to urban areas in all the bigger States/UTs except for the State of
Andhra Pradesh, Delhi and Telangana. Chart 6 and 7 gives the pictorial presentation of the
percentage of persons below 15 years of age for bigger States/UTs by Residence and Sex
respectively.
