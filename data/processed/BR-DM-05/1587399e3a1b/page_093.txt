Sex Ratio at Birth:
3.8 Statement 16 below presents Sex Ratio (number of females per 1000 males) at birth
during the period 2019-21 in India and bigger States/Union Territories in 2019-2021, categorized
by residence. Overall, India had a Sex Ratio of 913 females per 1000 males at birth during the
period 2019-21 (3-years average). Kerala had the highest sex ratio at birth with 962 females per
1000 males, while Uttarakhand has the lowest sex ratio at birth with 852 females per 1000 males.
When categorized by residence, the urban areas had a higher sex ratio than the rural areas in 11
bigger States/UTs. In Chhattisgarh, the sex ratio was significantly lower in urban areas compared
to rural areas. Chart 35 depicts the variation in Sex Ratio at birth by residence among bigger
States/UTs in the Country.
