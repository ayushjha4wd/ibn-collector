4.6 Statement 41 given below shows the percentage change in the level of Crude Death Rate
between the period 2009-11 and 2019-21 for India and bigger States/UTs. Overall, there was a
decrease in the Crude Death Rate in India from 7.2% in 2009-11 to 6.5% in 2019-21, representing
a decline by 9.7%. The rural Crude Death Rate also decreased from 7.7% to 6.9%, a percentage
change of -10.4%, while the urban Crude Death Rate decreased from 5.8% to 5.6%, a percentage
change of -3.4%. Among the bigger States and Union Territories, there was a significant decrease
in the Crude Death Rate for Assam (-22.0%), Jharkhand (-18.6%), and Uttar Pradesh (-17.5%),
while the highest increase in the Crude Death Rate was for Kerala (11.6%) followed by Punjab
(8.7%), Himachal Pradesh (7.2%) and Chhattisgarh (5.0%). Other States and Union Territories
showed more modest changes in the Crude Death Rate over the period. Charts 76, 77 and 78 depict
the average death rate between 2009-11 and 2019-21 in India and bigger States/UTs for Total, Rural
and Urban areas respectively along with the respective rate of change. Chart 79 shows the year-wise
death rate from 1985 to 2021 by Residence for India.
