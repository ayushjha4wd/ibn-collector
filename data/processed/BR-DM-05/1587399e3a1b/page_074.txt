2.25 In addition to the marital status of males and females, information on the number of
females who got effectively married during the reference period of six months of each half yearly
survey is also being collected in the SRS from 1990. Based on the information on the number of
females who got effectively married during the last one year, mean age at effective marriage for
females has been worked out. Statement 12 given below presents the percentage distribution of
such females by age at effective marriage separately for rural and urban areas in three categories,
before the age of 18, between 18-20 years and at age of 21 or above.
2.26 For India as a whole, 2.6% of females got married before the age of 18, 27.6% got married
between the ages of 18-20 and 69.8% got married at the age of 21 or above. In rural areas, the
percentages are 3.0%, 30.4% and 66.7% respectively, while in urban areas, they are 1.8%, 20.2%,
and 78.0% respectively.
2.27 Among the bigger States and Union Territories, the percentage of females getting married
before the age of 18 is the highest in West Bengal (6.5%) followed by Chhattisgarh and Assam
(3.3%) and is lowest in Jammu & Kashmir (0.1%) followed by Kerala (0.2%) and Maharashtra
(0.5%). The percentage of females getting married between the ages of 18-20 is the highest in
West Bengal (48.9%) followed by Assam (40.7%) and the lowest in Jammu & Kashmir (9.2%).
The percentage of females getting married at the age of 21 or above is the highest in Jammu &
Kashmir (90.7%) followed by Haryana (87.4%) and the lowest in West Bengal (44.5%) followed
by Assam (56.0%).
2.28 In case of rural areas, percentage of female getting married before age of 18 is highest in
West Bengal (6.0%) followed by Jharkhand (5.8%) and Chhattisgarh (4.0%) and is lowest in
Delhi (0.0%) followed by Jammu & Kashmir (0.2%) and Kerala and Maharashtra (0.5%). In
case of urban areas, percentage of female getting married before age of 18 is highest in West
Bengal (8.2%) followed by Uttarakhand (4.3%) and Odisha (3.4%) and is lowest in Himachal
Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Kerala, Punjab and Telangana (0.0%). Charts 23, 24 and 25 show
the distribution of females, who got effectively married during the last one year, by age at
effective marriage of India and bigger States/UTs for Total, Rural and Urban areas respectively.
