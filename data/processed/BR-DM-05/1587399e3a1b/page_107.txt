Age-Specific Fertility Rate across bigger States/UTs:
3.16 The fertility pattern by age groups in different States/UTs in India for 2021 is presented
below in Statement 21. The data shows that the highest fertility rate was for the age group of 25-
29 years with a value of 144.4 for India in 2021. This is followed by the age group of 20-24 years
having ASFR of 115.3. The data also indicates significant variations in fertility rates across
States/UTs. Bihar has the highest ASFR for the age group of 30-34 years with a rate of 138.8,
whereas the state of Andhra Pradesh had the lowest ASFR of 35.3 for the same age group. The
Age-Specific Fertility Rate peaks at the age group 25-29 years for India and all States/UTs,
except for UT of Jammu & Kashmir, where it peaks at the age-group of 30-34 years. Age-specific
fertility rate of age group 15-19 years, which is also known as Adolescent Birth Rate (ABR) is
an important indicator and is 11.3 for the year 2021 for a whole Country. The State of West
Bengal had the highest ASFR for 15-19 years or ABR of 23.7 followed by Assam (19.0) and
Jharkhand (15.7), whereas NCT of Delhi had the lowest ABR of 2.7 followed by Jammu Kashmir
(4.1) and Kerala (4.9). The rural-urban levels in ASFR for bigger States/UTs are shown in Table
3.
