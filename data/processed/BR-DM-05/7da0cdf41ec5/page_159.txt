urban areas. In rural areas, Kerala registered 2.0, the lowest percentage share of infant
deaths as compared to 17.9 in Madhya Pradesh. In urban areas, the variation is from 1.8
percent in Kerala to 14.1 percent in Uttar Pradesh.
