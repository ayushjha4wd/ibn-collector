and bigger States/UTs for the year 2016. The CDR at national level is 6.4 varying from
6.9 in rural to5.4 in urban areas. Among the bigger States/UTs, it varies from 4.0 in Delhi
to 7.8 in Odisha. The States having death rate higher than or equal to the National level

Andhra Pradesh 6.8, Uttar Praradesh 6.9, Madhya Pradesh 7.1, Chhattisgarh 7.4, Kerala
7.6 and Odisha 7.8. The graphical representation of death rate of bigger States/UTs by
