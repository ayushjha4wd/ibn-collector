2.12 Persons below 10 years of age, in the SRS, are being treated as unmarried. Marital
distribution of population would be more meaningful if such percentages are worked out
for persons of age 10 years and above. Statement 9 given below provides percentage
distribution of population for age 10 years and above by marital status and sex for India
and bigger States/UTs. At the National level, the percentage share of married population
is 56.2. Among the bigger States/UTs Jammu & Kashmir (51.1) recorded the lowest
percentage of married population whereas the highest percentage was recorded for Andhra
Pradesh (61.7). Charts 11, 12 and 13 show the distribution of population aged 10 years and
above by marital status of India and bigger States/UTs for Person, Male and Female
respectively.
