Infant Mortality Rate:
4.11 Statement 46 given below presents Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) by sex and residence for
the year 2022 for India and States/UTs. The IMR varies across different States/UTs in India,
ranging from 3 in Manipur to 40 in Madhya Pradesh. In most cases, the IMR is higher in rural
areas compared to urban areas. Assam had the highest difference in IMR between rural (34) and
urban areas (15). There was also the discernible difference in the IMR between Males and
Females in some of the States/UTs. Himachal Pradesh had the highest difference in IMR between
males and females, with a rate of 20 for males and 13 for females. Lakshadweep had the highest
IMR among the smaller UTs with a rate of 14, and Meghalaya had the highest IMR among the
smaller States with a rate of 30. Chart 84-86 below represents the IMR by Sex for Total, Rural
and Urban. Chart 87 presents the distribution of bigger States/UTs by Rural and Urban IMR.
