2.10 Statement 6 provides information on the percentage of the population in the working age
group 15-59 years by sex and residence for India and bigger States/UTs in 2022. At the National
level, the total percentage of the population in the age group 15-59 years in India is 66.3%, with
66.0%for males and 66.7%for females. The rural areas have a slightly lower percentage of the
population in this age group 64.9% compared to urban areas 69.2% Among the States/UTs, Delhi
has the highest percentage of the population in the age group 15-59 years, with 70.7%, followed
by Telangana 70.4% and Andhra Pradesh (70.2%). Bihar has the lowest percentage of the
population in this age group at 60.8%, followed by Rajasthan 63.6% and Madhya Pradesh 63.9%.
2.11 There are also variations in the percentage of males and females in this age group across
different States/UTs and between rural and urban areas. For instance, Delhi has the highest
percentage of females in this age group in rural areas at 70.7%, while Assam has the highest
percentage of females in the age group 15-59 years in urban areas at 73.0%. Delhi has the highest
percentage of males in the age group 15-59 years in rural areas at 71.4% while Himachal Pradesh
has the highest percentage of males in this age group in urban areas at 71.4%. Chart 8 and 9 gives
