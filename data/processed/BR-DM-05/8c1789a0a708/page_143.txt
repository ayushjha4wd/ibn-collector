Birth Interval:
3.34 The percentage distribution of second and higher-order live births by interval between
current and previous live birth is shown below in Statement 37 for India and bigger States/UTs
for the year 2022. For India as a whole, 1.5% of second and higher-order live-births occurred
within 10-12 months of the previous live birth, 18.9% occurred within 12-24 months, 25.2%
occurred within 24-36 months, and 54.3% occurred 36 months or more after the previous live
birth. Looking at the individual States and UTs, we can see that the percentage distributions vary
considerably. The percentage of second and higher-order live-births occurred within 10-12
months of the previous live birth were ranging from 0.2% in Assam to 3.4% in Punjab. The
percentage of second and higher-order live-births occurred after 36 months or more after the
previous live birth is ranging from 39.9% in Madhya Pradesh to 83.2% in Assam. Chart 68
presents the percentage distribution of second and higher-order live-births by interval for India
and bigger States/UTs.
