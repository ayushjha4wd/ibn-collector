Death Rate for Children aged 5-14 years:
4.20 Death Rates for children in the age group 5-14 for the year 2022 are given below in
Statement 55. The overall death rate for children aged 5-14 years in India was 0.7, with slightly
higher rates for male (0.7) than for female (0.6) and higher rates in urban areas (0.7) than in rural
areas (0.6). Among the bigger States and UTs, Chhattisgarh had the highest death rate for children
aged 5-14 years (1.1), followed by Odisha (0.9) and Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand,
Karnataka, Punjab, Telangana at 0.8. Uttarakhand, Kerala and Jammu & Kashmir had the lowest
death rate at 0.4 for the children in the age group 5-14. There were also considerable differences
in death rates between rural and urban areas within each state/UT. Chart 106 to 108 shows death
rates for children aged 5-14 years by Sex for India and bigger States/UTs by place of residence
viz, Total, Rural and Urban.
