Death Rate for Children aged 0-4 years:
4.17 The Death Rates for children below age 5 by residence are given below in Statement 52
separately for males and females for the year 2022. While the total death rate for India was 7.0 in
2022, the male death rate was 7.1 and the female death rate was 6.8. The death rate in rural areas
was however much higher at 7.9 than that of urban areas at 4.6. Among the bigger States/UTs,
Madhya Pradesh had the highest total death rate for children below 5 years of age at 11.4, with the
male death rate at 11.4 and the female death rate at 11.4. Kerala had the lowest total death rate at
1.8, with the male death rate at 2.2 and the female death rate at 1.5. Chart 100 to 102 shows Death
Rate for children age 0-4 years by Sex for India and bigger States/UTs 2022 by place of residence
viz, Total, Rural and Urban respectively.
