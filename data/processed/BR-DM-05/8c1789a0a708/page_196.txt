Under-5 Mortality Rate:
4.18 The Under-Five Mortality Rates by residence are given below in Statement 53 separately
for males and females for the year 2022. The U5MR indicates the probability of a child dying
before their fifth birthday, expressed as the number of deaths per 1,000 live births. For India as a
whole, the U5MR was 30 in 2022, which means that out of every 1,000 live births, 30 children
died before reaching the age of five. The U5MR is higher in rural areas (34) compared to urban
areas (20). Among the bigger States/UTs, the U5MR ranges from 9 in Kerala to 47 in Madhya
Pradesh for the year 2022. Kerala (9) had the lowest U5MR, followed by Tamil Nadu (13) and
Delhi (15). Madhya Pradesh (47) had the highest U5MR, followed by Uttar Pradesh (42) and
Chhattisgarh (41). The U5MR is generally higher in rural areas compared to urban areas for most
States/UTs. Chart 103 to 105 represents Under-Five Mortality Rates (U5MR) by Sex for India &
bigger States/UTs by place of residence viz, Total, Rural and Urban respectively.
