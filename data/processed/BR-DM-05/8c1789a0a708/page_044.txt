respectively. In rural areas also, Bihar has the highest percentage with 11.7%, followed by Rajasthan
(10.0). The lowest percentage is in Tamil Nadu with (6.1%). In urban areas, Bihar has the highest
percentage of the population in the age group 0-4 years with (9.0%), followed by Uttarakhand
(8.7%), and Rajasthan (8.3%). The lowest percentage is in Himachal Pradesh with (4.8%).
