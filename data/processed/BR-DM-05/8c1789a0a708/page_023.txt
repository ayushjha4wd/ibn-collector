EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The Sample Registration System (SRS) is the largest demographic survey in the
Country mandated to provide annual estimates of fertility as well as mortality indicators
at the State and National level. The present Report contains data on fertility and mortality
indicators for the year 2022 for India and bigger States/UTs. The estimates are segregated
by residence and also by gender, wherever required.
2. The salient features of the report are as summarized below.
❖ The Crude Birth Rate (CBR) at the National level during 2022 stands at 19.1
exhibiting a decline of 0.2 points over 2021. Among the bigger States/UTs, the
maximum CBR has been reported in Bihar (26.9) and the minimum in Tamil
Nadu (12.1).
❖ There has been a decline of 1.1 points in the CBR for the Country from 2017 to
2022. The corresponding decline in rural CBR is 1.0 points and in urban CBR 1.3
points.
❖ The Crude Death Rate (CDR) for the Country is 6.8 in 2022. Among the bigger
States/UTs, the maximum CDR has been reported for Chhattisgarh (9.0) and the
minimum for Delhi (4.6).
❖ The Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) has also registered 1-point decline to 26 in 2022
from 27 in 2021 at the National level. Among the bigger States/UTs, the
maximum IMR has been observed in Madhya Pradesh (40) and the minimum in
Kerala (7).
❖ IMR for the Country has come down to 26 in 2022 from 33 in 2017, a decline of
7 points over last 5 years and an annual average decline of about 1.4 points. The
corresponding decline in rural IMR has been to the tune of 8 points (37 in 2017
to 29 in 2022) against a decline of 5 points in urban IMR (23 in 2017 to 18 in
2022). Both the genders have shown decline in the period 2017-22.
❖ Despite this decline, one in every 37 infants at the National level, one in every 33
infants in rural areas and one in every 56 infants in urban areas still die within
one year of life.
❖ In 2022, U5MR for the Country has shown a decline of 1 point from 2021 (30 in
2022 against 31 in 2021). There has been a decline of 1 point in male U5MR and
female U5MR during the corresponding period.
❖ Sex Ratio at Birth for the Country has gone up by 1 point to 914 in 2021-22 from
913 in 2020-21. Kerala and Chhattisgarh has reported the highest Sex Ratio at
Birth (970) while Uttarakhand, the lowest (857). Sex Ratio at Birth for Rural and
Urban areas was 912 and 921 respectively during 2021-22.
