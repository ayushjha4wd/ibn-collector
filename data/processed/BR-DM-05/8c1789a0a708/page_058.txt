Marital Status
2.15 Besides age, the other important demographic characteristic collected and subsequently
updated during each half-yearly survey of the SRS is marital status. Distribution of the population
by marital status is thus available from SRS.
2.16 The statement 8 presents the percentage distribution of the population by marital status and
sex for India and bigger States/UTs in India in the year 2022. The data is presented in three
categories: Never married, Married and Widowed/Divorced/Separated (W/D/S). For India in 2022,
51.1% of the total population had never been married, with a higher percentage among males
(56.3%) than females (45.7%). On the other hand, 45.5% of the total population was reported as
married, with a higher percentage among females (49.0%) than males (42.2%). The remaining 3.3%
of the population was either widowed, divorced or separated, with a higher percentage among
females (5.2%) than males (1.5%).
2.17 Among the bigger States/UTs, the percentage distribution of the population by marital status
and sex varied considerably in 2022. For instance, Bihar and Jammu & Kashmir has the highest
percentage of the never married population (58.8%), while Tamil Nadu has the lowest percentage
(42.3%). The highest percentage of the population was reported as widowed, divorced or separated
in Tamil Nadu (6.2%) and lowest in Bihar (1.6%). The highest and lowest percentage of married
population was reported in Kerala (51.6%) and Jammu & Kashmir (39.5%). Chart 12-14 represents
the distribution of population according to marital status for Persons, Males and Females
respectively.
