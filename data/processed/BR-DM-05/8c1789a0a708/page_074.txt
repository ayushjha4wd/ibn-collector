2.25 In addition to the marital status of males and females, information on the number of
females who got effectively married during the reference period of six months of each half yearly
survey is also being collected in the SRS from 1990. Based on the information on the number of
females who got effectively married during the last one year, mean age at effective marriage for
females has been worked out. Statement 12 given below presents the percentage distribution of
such females by age at effective marriage separately for rural and urban areas in three categories,
before the age of 18, between 18-20 years and at age of 21 or above.
2.26 For India as a whole, 2.3% of females got married before the age of 18, 27.0% got married
between the ages of 18-20 and 70.6% got married at the age of 21 or above. In rural areas, the
percentages are 2.7%, 30.1% and 67.3% respectively, while in urban areas, they are 1.6%, 19.4%
and 79.0% respectively.
2.27 Among the major States and Union Territories, the percentage of females getting married
before the age of 18 is the highest in West Bengal (6.5%) followed by Jharkhand (5.0%) and is
lowest in Kerala (0.2%) followed by Haryana and Himachal Pradesh (0.7%) respectively. The
percentage of females getting married between the ages of 18-20 is the highest in West Bengal
(47.7%) followed by Jharkhand (43.8%) and the lowest in Jammu & Kashmir (10.7%). The
percentage of females getting married at the age of 21 or above is the highest in Jammu &
Kashmir (88.1%) followed by Haryana (85.9%) and the lowest in West Bengal (45.9%) followed
by Jharkhand (51.2%).
2.28 In case of rural areas, percentage of female getting married before age of 18 is highest in
West Bengal (5.8%) followed by Jharkhand (5.7%) and is lowest in Delhi (0.0%) followed by
Kerala (0.3%). In case of urban areas, percentage of female getting married before age of 18 is
highest in West Bengal (8.3%) followed by Jammu & Kashmir (3.6%) and Odisha (3.4%) and
is lowest in Himachal Pradesh and Kerala (0.0%) followed by Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka
(0.4%).Charts 23, 24 and 25 show the distribution of females, who got effectively married during
the last one year, by age at effective marriage of India and bigger States/UTs for Total, Rural and
Urban areas respectively.
