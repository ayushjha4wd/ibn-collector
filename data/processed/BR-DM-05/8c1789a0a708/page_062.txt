2.18 Statement 9 given below provides percentage distribution of population for age 10 years
and above by Marital Status and Sex for India and bigger States/UTs for the year 2022. The data
is given in terms of percentage of individuals who are never married, married and
widowed/divorced/separated, divided by sex. In 2022, for India as a whole, 41.8% of the
population aged 10 and above were never married, while 54.2% were married, and 4% were
widowed/divorced/separated. Among males, 47.7% were never married, while 50.5% were
married and 1.8% were widowed/divorced/separated. Among females, 35.7% were never
married, while 58.1% were married and 6.2% were widowed/divorced/separated.
2.19 The data show some variation across the different States/UTs. For instance, the percentage
of the population aged 10 and above who have never married is lowest in Kerala (34.5%) and
highest in Jammu & Kashmir (52.5%) in 2022. The percentage of individuals who are married is
highest in Kerala (59.1%) and lowest in Jammu & Kashmir (45.6%). The percentage of
individuals who are widowed/divorced/separated is highest in Tamil Nadu ((7.0%) and lowest in
Jammu & Kashmir (2.0%).
