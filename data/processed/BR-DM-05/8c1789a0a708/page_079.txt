2.29 Statement 13 given below provides the mean age at effective marriage of the females
by residence for India and bigger States/UTs in 2022. The data is divided into age groups of
less than 18, 18-20, 21+, and all ages. The mean age at effective marriage for females in India
is 22.7 years. This means that, on an average, females in India get married at the age of 22.7
years. There is a significant difference in the mean age at effective marriage between rural
(22.2 years) and urban areas (23.9 years). In both India and individual States, the mean age at
effective marriage is higher in urban areas than in rural areas.
2.30 In most States/UTs, the majority of females get married after the age of 18. However,
in some States/UT such as West Bengal, Jharkhand and Assam, a significant proportion of
females get married before the age of 18. The mean age at effective marriage also varies widely
across States, ranging from 21.1 years in West Bengal to 25.4 years in Jammu & Kashmir.
2.31 It can be observed that the mean age at effective marriage for females is highest in the
urban areas compared to rural areas for most States/UTs. In case of rural areas, the mean age
at effective marriage is highest in Jammu & Kashmir (25.5 years) followed by Himachal
Pradesh (24.2 years) and Punjab (24.1 years) for all ages, while it is lowest in West Bengal
(20.7 years), Jharkhand (20.8 years) and Madhya Pradesh (21.4 years). In case of Urban areas,
the mean age at effective marriage is highest in Punjab (25.1 years) followed by Himachal
Pradesh, Assam and Chhattisgarh at 24.9 years and Maharashtra (24.7 years) for all ages, while
it is lowest in West Bengal (22.0 years), Rajasthan (23.0 years) and Bihar and Jharkhand (23.1
years). Charts 26, 27 and 28 show the distribution of females, who got married in the last one
