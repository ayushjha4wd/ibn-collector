3.17 The percentage distribution of cumulative fertility by woman's age is shown below in
Statement 22 for India and bigger States/UTs for the year 2022. Overall, the data indicates that the
cumulative fertility rate increases with age among women in India and all the major States and UTs.
However, there are some variations in the cumulative fertility rate among different States/UTs. West
Bengal had the highest percentage of cumulative fertility rate for women aged 15-19, 15-24, 15-34,
and 15-39. Andhra Pradesh had the highest percentage of cumulative fertility rate for women in the
age group 15-29 while Delhi had the lowest percentage of cumulative fertility rate for women aged
15-19 and Jammu & Kashmir had lowest percentage of cumulative fertility rate for women for
higher age-groups. Chart 47 gives percentage cumulative Age-Specific Fertility Rate for India by
residence. Chart 47B gives contribution of various age-groups in Total Fertility by Residence for
India for the year 2022.
