3.10 Changes in the average level of General Fertility Rate (GFR) between 2010-12 and 2020-
22 for India and bigger States/UTs in terms of residence is given in Statement 18. Overall, the data
shows that the GFR has decreased in India and in most of the bigger States/UTs between 2010-12
and 2020-22. The total GFR in India has decreased from 81.8 in 2010-12 to 66.7 in 2020-22, which
represents a 18.5% decrease. The rural GFR has decreased by 17.8%, from 89.5 to 73.6, while the
urban GFR has decreased by 14.7%, from 62.5 to 53.3.
3.11 Among the bigger States/UTs, Bihar had the highest GFR in both 2010-12 and 2020-22 of
110.6 and 95.3 respectively. Delhi has experienced the largest percent decrease among all
States/UTs, with the GFR falling by 27.7% from 63.8 to 46.1. Kerala had the lowest GFR among
all States/UTs in both 2010-12 and 2020-22, and it has also experienced 3rd smallest percent
decrease of 15.7% after Himachal Pradesh (11.5%) and Bihar (13.8%) . Bihar has the smallest
percent decrease of 1.1% during last 10 years followed by West Bengal (7.7%) and Assam (10.8%)
in Urban GFR. The highest decrease in Urban GFR during last decade is observed in Delhi (33.0%)
followed by Odisha (18.5%) and Karnataka (17.1%). The smallest percent decrease in the Rural
GFR is observed in Himachal Pradesh (11.7%) followed by Haryana (14.9%) and Bihar (15.3%),
while highest percent decrease in the Rural GFR is observed in Delhi (28.2%) followed by Jammu
