4.16 Percentage share of deaths to children below age five to total deaths by residence
for India and bigger States/UTs is shown below in Statement 51. The National average of
percentage of deaths in the age group 0-4 years to total deaths in India was 8.3% in 2022,
with a higher proportion in rural areas (9.6%) compared to urban areas (5.1%). Among the
States and UTs, Uttar Pradesh had the highest percentage (15.0%) of deaths in the age group
0-4 years to total deaths, followed by Madhya Pradesh (14.9%) and Rajasthan (13.0%).
Kerala had the lowest percentage (1.5%) of such deaths followed by Tamil Nadu (2.2%). In
general, the percentage of such deaths is higher in rural areas than in urban areas across all
States and UTs, with some exceptions like Delhi, Kerala, Punjab and Telangana where the
proportion is higher in urban areas. Chart 99 shows the Percentage of deaths in the age group
0-4 years to total deaths for India and bigger States/UTs.
