3.20 Statement 25 below presents the percentage change in the average level of TFR between the
periods 2010-12 and 2020-22 in India and bigger States/UTs by residence. Overall average TFR in
India has decreased from 2.4 in 2010-12 to 2.0 in 2020-22, which is a decrease of 16.7%. The
rural TFR decreased by 18.5% (from 2.7 to 2.2), while the urban TFR decreased by 15.8% (from
1.9 to 1.6). Among the bigger States/UTs, Delhi had the highest percentage decrease in average TFR
between 2010-12 and 2020-22 (22.2%), followed by Jammu & Kashmir (21.1%) and Jharkhand
(20.7%) and lowest percentage decrease is observed in Punjab and Bihar at 11.8% each followed
by Assam (12.5%) and Haryana (13.0%). Charts 51, 52 and 53 depict the average TFR and
corresponding percent change between 2010-12 and 2020-22 for India and bigger States/UTs in
terms of Total, Rural and Urban areas respectively. Chart 54 shows the trend in year-wise TFR from
1985 to 2022 by residence for India.
