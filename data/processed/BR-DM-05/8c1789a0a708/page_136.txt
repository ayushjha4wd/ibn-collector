3.30 Total Fertility Rates by education levels of the women for India and bigger States/UTs
for 2022 are presented below in Statement 34. For India as a whole, the TFR was 3.0 for illiterate
women and 1.9 for literate women in 2022. Among literate women, those without any formal
education had the TFR at 2.4, followed by those with below primary education (2.5), primary
education (2.3), middle education (2.1), class X (1.9), class XII (1.8), and graduate and above
(1.6). The TFR classified by education levels also varies across different States/UTs of India.
Among the bigger States/UTs, Chhattisgarh had highest TFR among illiterate women (3.9), while
Bihar had the highest TFR for literate women (2.9). On the other hand, Tamil Nadu had the
lowest TFR for illiterate women (1.6) and Delhi & Tamil Nadu has lowest TFR for literate
women at 1.3. The TFR for other States/UTs falls between these ranges. Chart 64 presents, at
the National level, Total Fertility Rate by level of education of women separately for rural and
urban areas for the year 2022. The TFR by levels of education of the women for India and bigger
States/UTs by place of residence are given in Table-4.
