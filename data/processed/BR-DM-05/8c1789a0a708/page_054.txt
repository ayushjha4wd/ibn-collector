2.12 Percentage of aged persons (60 years and above) for India and bigger States/UTs by Sex and
Residence for the year 2022 is given below in Statement 7. At the National level, 9.0% of the total
population is aged 60 years and above, with a slightly higher percentage for females (9.3%) than
males (8.8%). In rural areas, 8.8% of the population is aged 60 years and above, while in urban
areas, the percentage is slightly higher at 9.5%. Overall, the percentage of the population aged 60
years and above varies across different States and UTs in India, with some having a much higher
proportion of elderly citizens than others.
2.13 In the bigger States/UTs, the data reveals that Kerala has the highest percentage of population
in the age group of 60 years and above among all the States/UTs in India with 14.4% of the total
population falling under this category. Tamil Nadu (13.1%) and Himachal Pradesh (12.3%) are the
next two States with the highest percentage of elderly population. On the other hand, Bihar (6.9%),
Assam (7.1%), NCT of Delhi (7.3%) and Jharkhand (7.3%) have the lowest percentage of the
population in the age group of 60 years and above.
2.14 The percentage of elderly females is higher than elderly males in most States/UTs. Percentage
of elderly males is highest in Kerala with 13.8% followed by Tamil Nadu (12.9%) and Himachal
Pradesh (12.0%). It is lowest in Delhi and Bihar at 7.0% each. followed by Jharkhand (7.1%). In
case of female, percentage of elderly population is highest in Kerala (15.0%) followed by Tamil
Nadu (13.3%) and Himachal Pradesh (12.6%) and it is lowest in Bihar (6.7%) followed by Assam
(6.8%). In case of rural areas, the percentage of elderly population is highest in Kerala (14.8%)
followed by Himachal Pradesh (12.5%) and Odisha (11.7%). It is lowest in Bihar (6.6%) followed
by Assam and Delhi at 6.8% each. In urban areas, the percentage of elderly population is highest in
Tamil Nadu (14.3%) followed by Kerala (14.0%) and West Bengal (11.7%) and it is lowest in
Chhattisgarh (6.6%) followed by Telangana (7.0%). The pictorial presentation of percentage of old
