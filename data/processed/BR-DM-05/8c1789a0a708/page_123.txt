3.23 Values of the Age-Specific Marital Fertility Rates (ASMFR) for the year 2022 in bigger
States/UTs are given below in Statement 28. Age-Specific Marital Fertility Rate is at peak level in
the age-group 20-24 at the National level and the majority of bigger States/UTs; however, for the
States/UTs of Assam, Delhi, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand,Kerala and West Bengal, the ASMFR is
at peak level in the age-group 15-19. There is a significant decline in marital fertility for women
aged 30 years onwards, for all bigger States/UTs.
