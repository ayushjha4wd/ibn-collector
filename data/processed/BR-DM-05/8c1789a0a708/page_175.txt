4.12 The percentage change in the average level of Infant Mortality between 2010-12 and
2020-22 in India and the bigger States/Union Territories is given below in Statement 47. The
data reveals that the overall Infant Mortality Rate in India has decreased by 39.1% from 44.5 in
2010-12 to 27.1 in 2020-22. The rural Infant Mortality Rate has decreased by 37.7%, while the
urban Infant Mortality Rate has decreased by 37.9%. Among the bigger States/UTs, the highest
decline in percentage change for Infant Mortality Rate was observed in Jammu & Kashmir, which
is 62.4%. Chhattisgarh had the lowest decline in the percentage for Infant Mortality Rate which
is 21.8%. Madhya Pradesh had the highest Infant Mortality Rate in both periods and has shown
a decrease of 30.2%. Chart 88 to 90 represents the Infant Mortality Rate between 2010-12 &
2020-22 and the percent change by residence viz, Total, Rural and Urban for India and bigger
States/UTs.
