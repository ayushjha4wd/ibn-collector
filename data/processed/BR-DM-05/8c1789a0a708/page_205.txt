Death Rate for persons aged 15-59 years:
4.21 Death Rates in the age group 15-59 years for the year 2022, by Sex and Residence, are
given below in Statement 56. Overall, the death rate for this age group in India was 3.2, with a
higher rate for males (4.0) than females (2.4). The rural death rate (3.4) was higher than the urban
death rate (2.8). Among the States and Union Territories, Chhattisgarh had the highest death rate
(4.8), followed by Punjab (4.1) and Andhra Pradesh & Assam at 3.8 each. Delhi had the lowest
death rate (2.1), followed by Himachal Pradesh (2.2) and Kerala (2.4) for the persons in age-
group 15-59 years. In most cases, the death rate was higher for males than females and higher in
rural areas than urban areas. Chart 109 to 111 shows the death rates for Persons aged 15-59 years
by Sex for India and bigger States/UTs by place of residence viz, Total, Rural and Urban
respectively.
