4.6 Statement 41 given below shows the percentage change in the level of Crude Death Rate
between the period 2010-12 and 2020-22 for India and bigger States/UTs. Overall, there was a
decrease in the Crude Death Rate in India from 7.1% in 2010-12 to 6.8% in 2020-22, representing
a decline by 4.2%. The rural Crude Death Rate also decreased from 7.6% to 7.2%, a percentage
change of -5.3%, while the urban Crude Death Rate decreased from 5.7% to 5.9%, a percentage
change of 3.5%. Among the bigger States and Union Territories, there was a significant decrease in
the Crude Death Rate for Assam (-20.0%), Uttar Pradesh (-15.2%) and Jharkhand (-14.5%), while
the highest increase in the Crude Death Rate was for Punjab (17.6%) followed by Chhattisgarh
(13.9%), Kerala (12.9%) and Haryana (10.8%). Other States and Union Territories showed more
modest changes in the Crude Death Rate over the period. Chart 76, 77 and 78 depict the average
death rate between 2010-12 and 2020-22 in India and bigger States/UTs for Total, Rural and Urban
areas respectively along with the respective rate of change. Chart 79 shows the year-wise death rate
from 1985 to 2022 by Residence for India.
