Birth interval
3.34 The percentage distribution of second and higher order live births by interval between
current and previous live birth is shown below in Statement 37 for India and bigger States/UTs
for the year 2020. At the National level, 1.5 percent of the live births occur within one year
from the previous live birth. Such percentage varies from almost 0 percent in Delhi to 3.2
percent in Haryana. The percentage of births beyond three years of birth interval from the
previous live birth for India is 52.3. It varies from 43.0 percent in Bihar to 81.1 percent in West
Bengal. Chart 68 presents the percentage distribution of second and higher order live births by
interval for India and bigger States/UTs.
