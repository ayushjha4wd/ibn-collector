Maharashtra and Uttarakhand. For ‘third’, ‘fourth & above’ order live births, the percentage
contribution at National level is significantly higher in rural areas (17.6) compared to urban
areas (9.6). Charts 66 and 67 depict the percentage distribution of current live births by birth
order for rural and urban areas respectively.
