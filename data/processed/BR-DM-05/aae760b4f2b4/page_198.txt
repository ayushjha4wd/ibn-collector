4.21 Death rates for persons aged 60 years and above by sex and residence are given
below in Statement 56. At the National level, death rate in this age group is estimated
to be 42.3 and males have higher mortality rate (46.6) than females (38.1). This
difference exists in both rural and urban areas. The death rate for this age group,
among the bigger States/UTs, varies from 30.6 in Jammu & Kashmir to 59.2 in
Chhattisgarh.
