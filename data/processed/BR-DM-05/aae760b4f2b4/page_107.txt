3.19 Statement 25 below presents the percentage change in the average level of TFR
between the periods 2008-10 and 2018-20 in India and bigger States/UTs. During the period
TFR has declined by 19.2 percent at the National level. Such decline was 17.9 percent in Rural
areas and 10.5 percent in Urban areas. Among the bigger States/UTs, the decline in TFR varies
from 31.8 percent in Jammu & Kashmir to 5.9 percent in Kerala. All bigger States/UTs have
shown decline in both rural and urban areas.
