4.11 Statement 46 given below presents Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) by sex and
residence for the year 2020 for India and bigger States/UTs. Infant Mortality Rate is
defined as the infant deaths (less than one year) per thousand live births. At the
National level, IMR is reported to be 28 and varies from 31 in rural areas to 19 in urban
areas. Among the bigger States/UTs, it varies from 6 in Kerala to 43 in Madhya
Pradesh. At the national level, mortality for female infants is at par with male infants;
however, there are inter-state variations in this. Charts 84, 85 and 86 provide the values
of IMR by residence for India and bigger States/UTs for total, rural and urban areas
respectively. Distribution of bigger States by level of IMR in rural and urban areas is
shown in Chart 87. The estimates on child and infant mortality indicators by residence
