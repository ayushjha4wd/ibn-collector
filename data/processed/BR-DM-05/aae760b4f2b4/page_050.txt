5.9 percent in Telangana to 12.8 percent in Tamil Nadu. In case of females, aged population (60
years and above) varies from 6.0 percent in Assam to 13.6 percent in Kerala. The same varies
from 6.1 percent in Delhi to 12.2 percent in Kerala for Males. The pictorial presentation of
percentage of old age population by Residence and Sex for bigger States/UTs is given in Chart 10
