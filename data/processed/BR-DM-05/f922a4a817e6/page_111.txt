Mean Age of Fertility:
3.18 The difference in the pattern of child bearing can be assessed in terms of the Mean Age of
Fertility, which describes the age pattern of fertility of synthetic cohort of hypothetical group of
women viewed as having, in their lifetime, similar fertility experience recorded in a given year.
The Mean Age of Fertility has been calculated from the Age-Specific Fertility Rate. The Mean
Age of Fertility is the average age at which women give birth to their first child. The associated
standard deviations are also shown. Statement 23 below shows the Mean Age of Fertility for India
and bigger States/UTs in 2024. The Mean Age of Fertility for India was 28.4 years, ranging from
26.5 years in West Bengal to 31.3 years in Jammu & Kashmir. The Mean Age of Fertility is
slightly higher in urban areas (28.8 years) compared to rural areas (28.3 years). Chart 48 shows the
Mean Age of Fertility for India and bigger States/UTs by residence.
