Adult Survival Rate:
4.22 Adult Survival rate is the probability of survival from age 15 to age 60, based on
estimated mortality rates over this age range. The adult Survival Rate is calculated as a
complement to the probability of dying between the age-group 15-59. The statement 57 below
gives the Adult Survival Rates by sex and residence for India and bigger States/UTs of the
Country. The Adult Survival Rate for Country was 0.874 in 2024 ranging from 0.809 in
Chhattisgarh to 0.917 in Delhi. The Adult Survival Rate for male and female was 0.841 and
0.909 respectively. It was 0.865 for rural and 0.890 for urban areas of the Country during 2024.
