Medical Attention before Death:
4.24 The type of medical attention received before death is also collected in the SRS. In the new
samples from 2004 onwards, the options on types of medical attention received before death have
been modified to capture the deaths occurred specifically at private hospital/nursing homes. The
options include ‘Government Hospital’, ‘Private Hospital’, ‘Qualified professional’, ‘Untrained
functionary’ and ‘others’. The options for the sample based on 1991 sample were ‘Institutional’;
‘Medical attention other than Institution’ and ‘No medical attention’. Statement 59 given below
presents the percentage distribution of deaths by type of medical attention received before death
for India and bigger States/UTs separately for rural and urban areas for the year 2024. At the
National level, 24.7% of deaths occurred in government hospitals, 15.5% occurred in private
hospitals, 14.3% were attended by a qualified professional and 45.5% had no medical attention or
were attended by an untrained functionary before death in 2024.
4.25 In rural areas, the percentage of deaths that occurred with no medical attention or an
untrained functionary was higher at 48.9%, compared to 36.1% in urban areas. The distribution of
deaths by type of medical attention received before death varies considerably across the
States/UTs. For example, in Delhi, 36.9% of deaths occurred in government hospitals, while only
39.0% had no medical attention or were attended by an untrained functionary before death. In
contrast, in Jharkhand, 61.8% of deaths occurred with no medical attention or an untrained
functionary, while only 5.8% were attended by a qualified professional.
4.26 In case of rural areas, Untrained functionary & No medical attention received before
deaths covers about 48.9% deaths ranging from 28.2% in Kerala to 68.9% in Bihar. While in
urban areas, about 36.1% people have not received medical attention or attended by Untrained
functionaries ranging from 22% in Karnataka to 57.9% in Bihar. Chart 115 to 117 represents
distribution of deaths by Type of Medical Attention received before death for India, & bigger
States/UTs by place of residence viz, Total, Rural, and Urban respectively.
