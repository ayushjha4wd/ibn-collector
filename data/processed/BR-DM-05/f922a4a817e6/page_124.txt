States/UTs of Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Haryana, Jammu & Kashmir, Madhya
Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana, the ASMFR is at
peak level in the age-group 20-24. There is a significant decline in marital fertility for women aged
30 years onwards, for all bigger States/UTs.
