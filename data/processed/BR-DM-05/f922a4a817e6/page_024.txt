EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The Sample Registration System (SRS) is the largest demographic survey in the
Country mandated to provide annual estimates of fertility as well as mortality indicators
at the State and National level. The present Report contains data on fertility and mortality
indicators for the year 2024 for India and bigger States/UTs. The estimates are segregated
by residence and also by gender, wherever required.
2. The salient features of the report are summarized below.
❖ The Crude Birth Rate (CBR) at the National level during 2024 stands at 18.3
exhibiting a decline of 0.1 points over 2023. Among the bigger States/UTs, the
maximum CBR has been reported in Bihar (26.8) and the minimum in Kerala
(11.1).
❖ There has been a decline of 1.4 points in the CBR for the Country from 2019 to
2024. The corresponding decline in rural CBR is 1.2 points and in urban CBR is
of 1.7 points.
❖ The Crude Death Rate (CDR) for the Country is 6.4 in 2024. Among the bigger
States/UTs, the maximum CDR has been reported for Chhattisgarh (8.4) and the
minimum for Delhi (4.5).
❖ The Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) declined by one point to 24 in 2024 at the
National level. Among the bigger States/UTs, Chhattisgarh recorded the highest
IMR at 36, while Kerala reported the lowest at 8.
❖ IMR for the Country stands at 24 in 2024 from 30 in 2019, a decline of 6 points
over last 5 years and an annual average decline of about 1.0 point. The
corresponding decline in rural IMR has been to the tune of 7 points (34 in 2019
to 27 in 2024) against a decline of 3 points in urban IMR (20 in 2019 to 17 in
2024). Both the genders have shown decline during the period 2019-24.
❖ Despite this decline, one in every 42 infants at the National level, one in every 37
infants in rural areas and one in every 59 infants in urban areas still die within
one year of life.
❖ In 2024, U5MR for the Country has shown a decline of 1 point from 2024 (28 in
2024 against 29 in 2023). There has been a decline of 2 points in male U5MR and
of 1 point in female U5MR respectively during the corresponding period.
❖ Sex Ratio at Birth for the Country has gone up by 1 point to 918 in 2022-24 from
917 in 2021-23. Chhattisgarh and Kerala have reported the highest Sex Ratio at
Birth at 978 and 974 respectively, while Uttarakhand has the lowest (872). Sex
Ratio at Birth for Rural and Urban areas were 914 and 928 respectively during
2022-24.
❖ The Total Fertility Rate (TFR) for the Country is estimated at 1.9 in 2024. In the
same year, Bihar recorded the highest TFR at 2.9, while Delhi had the lowest at
1.2. Notably, the National TFR has fallen below the replacement level of 2.1.
