3.10 Changes in the average level of General Fertility Rate (GFR) between 2012-14 and
2022-24 for India and bigger States/UTs in terms of residence is given in Statement 18. Overall,
the data shows that the GFR has decreased in India and in most of the bigger States/UTs between
2012-14 and 2022-24. The total GFR in India has decreased from 78.8 in 2012-14 to 64.6 in
2022-24, which represents a 18.0% decrease. The rural GFR has decreased by 16.6%, from 86.2
to 71.9, while the urban GFR has decreased by 16.7%, from 61.2 to 51.0.
3.11 Among the bigger States/UTs, Bihar had the highest GFR in both 2012-14 and 2022-24 of
105.4 and 96.0 respectively. Delhi has experienced the largest percent decrease among all
States/UTs, with the GFR falling by 29.9% from 61.5 to 43.1. Kerala had the lowest GFR among
all States/UTs in 2012-14 and Tamil Nadu had the lowest GFR among all States/UTs in 2022-24.
Over the past 10 years, Bihar has experienced a slight increase of 2.1% in urban GFR, while
Jammu & Kashmir recorded the smallest decline at 6.6%, followed by West Bengal (6.9%) and
Rajasthan (11.0%). The highest decrease in urban GFR during last decade is observed in Delhi
(35.6%) followed by Tamil Nadu (23.5%) and Odisha (19.8%). The smallest percent decrease in
the rural GFR is observed in Bihar (10.1%) followed by Himachal Pradesh (12.3%), Chhattisgarh
(12.4%) and Punjab (14.6%), while highest percent decrease in the rural GFR is observed in Delhi
(28.9%) followed by Tamil Nadu (24.5%). Chart 38, 39 and 40 depicts the average GFR between
2012-14 and 2022-24 and percent change for India and bigger States/UTs for Total, Rural, and
Urban areas respectively. Chart 41 shows the year-wise General Fertility Rate from 1985 to 2024
