2.12 Percentage of aged persons (60 years and above) for India and bigger States/UTs by Sex
and Residence for the year 2024 is given below in Statement 7. At the National level, 9.7% of the
total population is aged 60 years and above, with a slightly higher percentage for females 10.1%
than males 9.3%. In rural areas, 9.4% of the population is aged 60 years and above, while in urban
areas, the percentage is slightly higher at 10.1%. Overall, the percentage of the population aged 60
years and above varies across different States and UTs in India, with some having a much higher
proportion of elderly citizens than others.
2.13 In the bigger States/UTs, the data reveals that Kerala has the highest percentage of
population in the age group of 60 years and above with 15.1% of the total population falling under
this category. Tamil Nadu (14.2 %) and Himachal Pradesh (13.0 %) are the next two States with
the highest percentage of elderly population. On the other hand, Assam (7.6%), Jharkhand (7.7),
Bihar (7.8%), and NCT of Delhi (7.8%) have the lowest percentage of the population in the age-
group of 60 years and above.
2.14 The percentage of elderly females is either same or higher than elderly males in most
States/UTs except for state of Assam, Jammu & Kashmir, and West Bengal. Percentage of elderly
males is highest in Kerala with 14.4% followed by Tamil Nadu (13.6 %) and Himachal Pradesh
(12.4%). It is lowest in Delhi and Jharkhand at 7.6% each, followed by Bihar, and Chhattisgarh at
7.8%. In case of females, percentage of elderly population is highest in Kerala (15.8%) followed
by Tamil Nadu (14.8 %) and Himachal Pradesh (13.6%) and it is lowest in Assam (7.2%)
followed by Bihar, and Jharkhand at 7.8%. In case of rural areas, the percentage of elderly
population is highest in Kerala (15.3%) followed by Tamil Nadu (13.4%) and Himachal Pradesh
(13.2%). It is lowest in Assam (7.2%) followed by and Delhi (7.3%), and Jharkhand (7.4%). In
urban areas, the percentage of elderly population is highest in Kerala (15.0%) followed by Tamil
Nadu (14.9 %) and West Bengal (12.2 %) and it is lowest in Telangana, and Chhattisgarh at 7.3%.
