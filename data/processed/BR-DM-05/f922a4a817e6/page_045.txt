2.5 Percentage share of the population in the selected age-groups for bigger States/UTs by
residence and sex for the year 2024 are given in Statements 4 to 7. Statement 4 gives State-wise
percentage of the child population (below 5 years of age). In India, the percentage of the
population in the age-group 0-4 years is 7.9%, with males and females being 8.1% and 7.7%,
respectively. In rural areas, it is 8.6%, with males and females being 8.8% and 8.3%, respectively.
In urban areas, it is 6.6%, with males and females being 6.8% and 6.4% respectively.
2.6 Among the bigger States/UTs, Bihar has the highest percentage of the population in the age
group 0-4 years with 11.1%, followed by both Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh at 9.4 %, and
Chhattisgarh at 9.1%. The lowest percentage is in Tamil Nadu (5.7%) followed by Jammu &
Kashmir and Punjab with 5.9 % and 6.0 % respectively. In rural areas also, Bihar has the highest
percentage with 11.4% followed by Madhya Pradesh (10.0 %), Chhattisgarh, and Rajasthan each
at 9.7%. The lowest percentage is in Tamil Nadu with 5.9%. In urban areas, Bihar has the highest
percentage of the population in the age group 0-4 years with 9.0 %, followed by Rajasthan (8.6%).
The lowest percentage is in Himachal Pradesh, and West Bengal with 5.1 %.
2.7 In terms of total population, it varies from 5.7% in Tamil Nadu to 11.1% in Bihar. The
percentage of males in the 0-4 age group is slightly higher than females in all the States, except in
Andhra Pradesh and Telangana for urban areas and in Delhi for rural areas. However, the
difference is relatively small, with the largest gap observed in Bihar where the percentage of males
is 1.5 percentage points higher than females. Chart 4 and 5 shows the percentage of child
population for bigger States/UTs by residence and sex respectively.
