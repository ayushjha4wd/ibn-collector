3.20 Statement 25 below presents the percentage change in the average level of TFR between
the periods 2012-14 and 2022-24 in India and bigger States/UTs by residence. Overall average
TFR in India has decreased from 2.3 in 2012-14 to 1.9 in 2022-24, which is a decrease of 17.4%.
The rural TFR decreased by 19.2% (from 2.6 to 2.1), while the urban TFR decreased by 16.7%
(from 1.8 to 1.5). Among the bigger States/UTs, Delhi had the highest percentage decrease in
average TFR between 2012-14 and 2022-24 (29.4%), followed by Gujarat (25.0%) and Tamil
Nadu (23.5%) respectively and lowest percentage decrease is observed in Bihar at 9.4% followed
by Chhattisgarh (11.5%), Himachal Pradesh (11.8%), and Punjab (11.8%). Charts 51, 52 and 53
depict the average TFR and corresponding percent change between 2012-14 and 2022-24 for
India and bigger States/UTs in terms of Total, Rural and Urban areas respectively. Chart 54 shows
the trend in year-wise TFR from 1985 to 2024 by residence for India.
