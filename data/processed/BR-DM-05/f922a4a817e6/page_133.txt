education. For all other groups of women with different levels of education, the Age-Specific
Fertility Rate is highest in 25-29 age-group. The Age-Specific Fertility Rate is highest for
illiterate women and decreases with an increase in the level of education. The rate was higher in
rural areas than in urban areas for almost all age groups and education levels. Chart 61, 62, and
63 depict the Age-Specific Fertility Rate by level of education of women for Total, Rural, and
Urban areas respectively.
