4.6 Statement 41 given below shows the percentage change in the level of Crude Death Rate
between the period 2012-14 and 2022-24 for India and bigger States/UTs. Overall, there was a
decrease in the Crude Death Rate in India from 6.9% in 2012-14 to 6.5% in 2022-24, representing
a decline by 5.8%. The rural Crude Death Rate also decreased from 7.5% to 6.9%, a percentage
change of -8.0%, while the urban Crude Death Rate increased from 5.6% to 5.8%, a percentage
change of 3.6%. Among the bigger States and Union Territories, there was a significant decrease
in the Crude Death Rate for Assam (-17.1%), Uttar Pradesh (-14.5%) and Madhya Pradesh
(-12.7%), while the highest increase in the Crude Death Rate was for Punjab (15.2%) followed by
Haryana (11.1%), Chhattisgarh (10.3%) and Delhi (9.8%). Other States and Union Territories
showed more modest changes in the Crude Death Rate over the period. Charts 76, 77, and 78
depict the average death rate between 2012-14 and 2022-24 in India and bigger States/UTs for
