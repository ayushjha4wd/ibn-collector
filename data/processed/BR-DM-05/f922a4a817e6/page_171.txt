Infant Mortality Rate:
4.11 Statement 46 given below presents Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) by sex and residence
for the year 2024 for India and States/UTs. The IMR varies across different States/UTs in India,
ranging from 2 in Manipur to 36 in Chhattisgarh. In most cases, the IMR is higher in rural areas
compared to urban areas. Assam had the highest difference in IMR between rural (31) and
urban areas (14). There was also the discernible difference in the IMR between Males and
Females in some of the States/UTs. Among the bigger States/UTs, Bihar and Jammu &
Kashmir showed the highest gender gap in IMR. In Bihar, the IMR was 21 for males and 25 for
females, while in Jammu & Kashmir, it was 16 for males and 12 for females. A & N Islands
and DNH had the highest IMR among the UTs with a rate of 9, and Meghalaya had the highest
IMR among the smaller States with a rate of 31. Chart 84-86 below represents the IMR by sex
for Total, Rural and Urban. Chart 87 presents the distribution of bigger States/UTs by Rural
and Urban IMR.
