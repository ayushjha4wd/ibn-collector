2.8 Statement 5 presents the percentage of the population aged below 15 years in India and bigger
States/ UTs, disaggregated by sex and residence for the year 2024. At the National level, 24.0 % of the
population falls within the 0–14 year’s age-group. Within this cohort, males constitute 24.5% and
females 23.3%. A rural-urban comparison reveals that 25.6% of the rural population is under 15 years
of age, compared to 20.9% in urban areas. Among the bigger States/UTs, Bihar records the highest
percentage of population in the 0–14 years age-group at 31.5%, followed by Madhya Pradesh at 27.4
% , Rajasthan, and Uttar Pradesh each at 27.3%. In contrast, States such as Tamil Nadu (18.0 %),
Andhra Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir (each at 19.0%), and Punjab (19.2%) report comparatively lower
proportions of population in this age-group.
2.9 The data indicate that, across all States /UTs, the percentage of males in the 0-14 age group is
higher than that of females, except in the rural areas of Delhi, where the proportion of females exceeds
that of males. Additionally, the percentage of the population aged 0-14 years is generally higher in
rural areas compared to urban areas across bigger States/UTs, except Telangana. Charts 6 and 7
provide a visual representation of the percentage of persons below 15 years of age by residence and
sex, respectively, for the bigger States/UTs.
