2.21 Statement 10 placed below shows the percentage distribution of Females aged 10 years
and more by marital status and residence in India and bigger States/UTs in 2024. At the
National level, 35.1% of females aged 10 and above were never married, 58.5% were married
and 6.4% were either widowed, divorced or separated. In terms of residence, 35.3% of rural
females and 34.8 % of urban females were never married, 58.6% of rural females and 58.2% of
urban females were married and 6.1% of rural females and 7.0% of urban females were either
widowed, divorced or separated.
2.22 Among the bigger States/UTs, the proportion of never-married females is lowest in
Kerala (26.5%) and highest in Jammu & Kashmir (48.1%). The proportion of married females
is lowest in Jammu & Kashmir (49.1%) and highest in Himachal Pradesh (64.0%). The
proportion of widowed/divorced/separated females is highest in Tamil Nadu (13.0%) and
lowest in Bihar (2.5%). Charts 18 and 19 show the distribution of the female population aged
10 years and above by marital status of India and bigger States/UTs for Rural and Urban areas
respectively.
