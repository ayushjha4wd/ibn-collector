4.12 The percentage change in the average level of Infant Mortality between 2012-14 and
2022-24 in India and the bigger States/Union Territories is given below in Statement 47. The
data reveals that the overall Infant Mortality Rate in India has decreased by 37.4% from 40.6 in
2012-14 to 25.4 in 2022-24. The rural Infant Mortality Rate has decreased by 36%, while the
urban Infant Mortality Rate has decreased by 35.1%. Among the bigger States/UTs, the highest
decline in percentage change for Infant Mortality Rate was observed in Jammu & Kashmir,
which is 62.7%. Chhattisgarh had the lowest decline in the percentage for Infant Mortality Rate
which is 18.3%. Madhya Pradesh had the highest Infant Mortality Rate in both periods and has
shown a decrease of 30.9%. Chart 88 to 90 represents the Infant Mortality Rate between
2012-14 & 2022-24 and the percent change by residence viz, Total, Rural, and Urban for India
and bigger States/UTs.
