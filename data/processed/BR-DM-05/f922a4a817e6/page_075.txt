2.25 Since 1990, the Sample Registration System (SRS) has been systematically collecting data on the
marital status of both males and females, as well as on the number of females who entered into effective
marriage during each six-month reference period of the half yearly survey. Based on this information, the
mean age at effective marriage for females has been estimated for 2024. Statement 12 presents the
percentage distribution of these females by age at effective marriage, classified into three categories: below
18 years, 18–20 years, and 21 years or above. The data is disaggregated by rural and urban areas to
highlight regional variations.
2.26 At the National level, 2.1% of females got married before the age of 18, 24.5% got married between
the ages of 18-20 and 73.5% got married at the age of 21 or above. In rural areas, the corresponding
percentages are 2.4%, 27.4% and 70.2% respectively, while in urban areas, they are 1.1%, 16.6%, and
82.2% respectively.
2.27 Amongst the Bigger States/UTs, the highest proportion of females getting married before the age of
18 is observed in West Bengal (6.3%) followed by Jharkhand (4.9%) and the lowest is observed in Delhi at
0% and Kerala at 0.04% followed by Himachal Pradesh and Haryana recorded at 0.4% and 0.7%
respectively. The highest proportion of females getting married in the age interval 18-20 years is observed
in West Bengal (44.5%) followed by Jharkhand (40.5%) and the lowest in Jammu & Kashmir (9.4%). The
proportion of females getting married at the age of 21 or above is the highest in Jammu & Kashmir (89.4%)
followed by Himachal Pradesh (86.2%) and the lowest in West Bengal (49.2%) followed by Jharkhand
(54.7%).
2.28 In rural areas, the highest percentage of females marrying before the age of 18 is recorded in West
Bengal (5.9%), followed by Jharkhand (5.8%). The lowest percentages are observed in Delhi (0.0%) and
Kerala (0.1%). In urban areas, West Bengal again reports the highest proportion of females marrying before
18 years (7.6%), followed by Jammu & Kashmir (2.9%) and Odisha (2.8%). The lowest percentages in
urban areas are found in Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Delhi, and Kerala (each 0.0%), followed by
Karnataka (0.1%) and Madhya Pradesh (0.5%). Charts 23, 24, and 25 illustrate the age distribution at
effective marriage of females for India and for the bigger States and Union Territories, disaggregated by
total, rural, and urban areas respectively.
