Under-5 Mortality Rate:
4.18 The Under-Five Mortality Rates by residence are given below in Statement 53
separately for males and females for the year 2024. The U5MR indicates the probability of a
child dying before their fifth birthday, expressed as the number of deaths per 1,000 live births.
For India, the U5MR was 28 in 2024, which means that out of every 1,000 live births, 28
children died before reaching the age of five. The U5MR is higher in rural areas (32) compared
to urban areas (19). Among the bigger States/UTs, the U5MR ranges from 9 in Kerala to 41 in
Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh for the year 2024. Kerala (9) had the lowest U5MR,
followed by Delhi (13), Tamil Nadu (14), Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, and
Maharashtra at 15 respectively. Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh (41) had the highest U5MR,
followed by Chhattisgarh (39). The U5MR is either constant or higher in rural areas than in
urban areas across most States/UTs, except in Kerala and West Bengal, where it is observed to
be lower in rural areas. Chart 103 to 105 represents Under-Five Mortality Rates (U5MR) by sex
for India & bigger States/UTs by place of residence viz, Total, Rural, and Urban respectively.
