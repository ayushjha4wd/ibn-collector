Death Rate for Children Aged 5-14 years:
4.20 Death rates for children in the age-group 5-14 for the year 2024 are given below in
Statement 55. The overall death rate for children aged 5–14 years in India was recorded at 0.5
for the country, and 0.5 for male and 0.6 for female respectively. The rural death rate (0.6) was
higher than the urban death rate (0.5). Among the bigger States and UTs, Chhattisgarh had the
highest death rate for children aged 5-14 years (0.9), followed by Bihar, Jammu & Kashmir,
Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha and Punjab at (0.7). Himachal Pradesh, Maharashtra, and
Uttarakhand had the lowest death rate at 0.3 for the children in the age-group 5-14. There were
also considerable differences in death rates between rural and urban areas within each
State/UT. Chart 106 to 108 shows death rates for children aged 5-14 years by sex for India and
bigger States/UTs by place of residence viz, Total, Rural, and Urban.
