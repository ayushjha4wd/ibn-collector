Marital Status
2.15 Besides age, the other important demographic characteristic collected and subsequently
updated during each half-yearly survey of the SRS is marital status. Distribution of the population
by marital status is thus available from SRS.
2.16 The statement 8 presents the percentage distribution of the population by marital status and
sex for India and bigger States/UTs for the year 2024. The data is presented in three categories:
Never married, Married and Widowed/Divorced/Separated (W/D/S). For India in 2024, 50.3% of
the total population had never been married, with a higher percentage among males (55.4%) than
females (45.0 %). On the other hand, 46.2 % of the total population was reported as married, with
a higher percentage among females (49.6 %) than males (43.0 %). The remaining 3.5% of the
population was either widowed, divorced or separated, with a higher percentage among females
(5.4 %) than males (1.6 %).
2.17 Among the bigger States/UTs, the percentage distribution of the population by marital status
and sex varied considerably in 2024. For instance, Bihar and Jammu & Kashmir has the highest
percentage of the never married population at 58.0% and 57.9% respectively, while Tamil Nadu
has the lowest percentage (40.9%). The highest percentage of the population was reported as
widowed, divorced or separated in Tamil Nadu (7.2%) and lowest in Bihar (1.5%). The highest
and lowest percentage of married population was reported in Tamil Nadu (51.9%) and Jammu &
Kashmir (40.4%). Chart 12-14 represents the distribution of population according to marital status
for Persons, Males, and Females respectively.
