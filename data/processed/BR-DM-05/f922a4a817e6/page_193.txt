Death Rate for Children aged 0-4 years:
4.17 The death rates for children below age 5 by residence are given below in Statement 52
separately for males and females for the year 2024. In 2024, the death rate for children aged 0–4
years in India was 6.4 and was identical across both sexes. However, the death rate in rural areas
(7.3) was much higher than the urban areas (4.2). Among the bigger States/UTs, Uttar Pradesh had
the highest total death rate for children below 5 years of age at 10.3, with the highest male (10.6)
and female (10.0) death rate. Kerala had the lowest total death rate at 1.6, with the male death rate
at 1.5 and the female death rate at 1.6. Chart 100 to 102 shows Death rate for children age 0-4
years by sex for India and bigger States/UTs 2024 by place of residence viz, Total, Rural, and
Urban respectively.
