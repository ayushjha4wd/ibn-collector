3.7 Statement 15 below provides the percentage change in Average Crude Birth Rate between
the periods 2012-14 and 2022-24 for India and the bigger States/UTs separately for rural and
urban areas. Between 2012-14 and 2022-24, there was an overall 12.7% decrease in the average
CBR for India. Among the bigger States/UTs, the percentage change in the average CBR ranged
from -24.0% for Delhi to -3.7% for Bihar. In terms of residence, the 3-year average Crude Birth
Rate has declined in both rural and urban areas for all bigger States/UTs except for the State of
Bihar (Urban). Further, the rural CBR was higher than the urban CBR in all bigger States/UTs
except Kerala for 2022-24. The percentage change in rural CBR ranged from -23.0% for Delhi to
-3.2% for Bihar whereas the percentage change in urban CBR ranged from -29.3% for Delhi to
1.0% for Bihar. Chart 31, 32 and 33 depict the average birth rate between 2012-14 and 2022-24
of India and bigger States/UTs for Total, Rural and Urban areas respectively along with respective
rate of change. Chart 34 shows the year-wise birth rate from 1985 to 2024 by Residence for India.
