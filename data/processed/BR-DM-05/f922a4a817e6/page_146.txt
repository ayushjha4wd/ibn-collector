3.35 Statement 38 below presents the percentage distribution of second and higher-order live
births by the interval between current and previous live birth separately for rural and urban
areas in 2024. In India, 1.4% of second and higher-order live births in rural areas occurred
within 10-12 months of the previous live birth, while 20.6%, 26.6%, and 51.4% of births
occurred within 12-24 months, 24-36 months and 36+ months respectively. In urban areas, the
percentage distribution of live births by interval was 1.3%, 15.5%, 23.7%, and 59.5%
respectively. The percentage distribution of live births by interval and residence varied across
different States and UTs. Charts 69 and 70 give the percentage distribution of second and
higher order live births by interval for rural and urban areas respectively.
