2.10 Statement 6 provides information on the percentage of the population in the working age-
group 15-59 years by sex and residence for India and bigger States/UTs in 2024. At the National
level, the total percentage of the population in the age-group 15-59 years in India is 66.4%, with
66.2% for males and 66.6% for females. The rural areas have a slightly lower percentage of the
population in this age-group 65.0% compared to urban areas 69.0 %. Among the States/UTs, Delhi
has the highest percentage of the population in the age-group 15-59 years, with 71.1%, followed by
Telangana (70.5%), and Andhra Pradesh (70.4%). Bihar has the lowest percentage of the population
in this age group at 60.7%, followed by Rajasthan (63.7%), Madhya Pradesh (64.1), and Uttar
Pradesh (64.2).
2.11 There are also variations in the percentage of males and females in this age-group across
different States/UTs and between rural and urban areas. For instance, Jammu & Kashmir has the
highest percentage of females in this age-group in rural areas at 70.1%, and also has the highest
percentage of females in the age-group 15-59 years in urban areas at 72.8%. Delhi has the highest
percentage of males in the age-group 15-59 years in rural areas at 72.4% while Telangana has the
highest percentage of males in this age-group in urban areas at 71.3%. Chart 8 and 9 gives the
pictorial presentation of percentage of population in the age-group 15-59 years for bigger
States/UTs by Residence and Sex respectively.
