2.18 Statement 9 given below provides percentage distribution of population for age 10 years
and above by Marital Status and Sex for India and bigger States/UTs for the year 2024. The
data is given in terms of percentage of individuals who are never married, married and
widowed/divorced/separated, divided by sex. In 2024, for India, 41.1% of the population aged
10 and above were never married, while 54.8% were married, and 4.1% were
widowed/divorced/separated. Among males, 46.9% were never married, while 51.2% were
married and 1.8% were widowed/divorced/separated. Among females, 35.1% were never
married, while 58.5 % were married and 6.4% were widowed/divorced/separated.
2.19 The data show some variation across the different States/UTs. For instance, the
percentage of the population aged 10 and above who have never married is lowest in Tamil
Nadu (33.2%) and highest in Jammu & Kashmir (52.2%) in 2024. The percentage of
individuals who are married is highest in Himachal Pradesh (59.5%) and lowest in Jammu &
Kashmir (45.9%). The percentage of individuals who are widowed/divorced/separated is
highest in Tamil Nadu (8.2%) and lowest in Bihar and Jammu & Kashmir (1.9%).
2.20 The percentage of males above 10 years who were never married in 2024 is highest in
Jammu and Kashmir (56.2%) and lowest in Tamil Nadu (39.4%). The percentage of married
males during 2024 is found to highest in Tamil Nadu (57.3%) and lowest in Jammu & Kashmir
(42.8%). The widowed/divorced/separated status in case of males is highest in Tamil Nadu
(3.3%) and lowest in Jammu & Kashmir (1.0%) followed by Delhi (1.1%) and Haryana (1.2%).

widowed/divorced/separated status in case of females is highest in Tamil Nadu (13.0%) and
