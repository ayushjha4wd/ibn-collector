INCORRECT CLASSIFICATION OF EXPENDITURE

classification of plan expenditure indicating the Major/Minor head as “2406-
Forestry and Wildlife” Centrally Sponsored Scheme/Central Plan
Scheme/State Plan Scheme. This creates serious problem at the time of
classification and booking of expenditure under the proper head of accounts.
