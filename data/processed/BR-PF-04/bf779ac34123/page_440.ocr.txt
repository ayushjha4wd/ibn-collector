Chart 11.5 : Area-wise percentage distribution of bank branches in Bihar (as on March 31, 2024)

Source ; State Level Bankers' Committee Report

As of March 31, 2024, the distribution of Bank branches in Bihar across urban, semi-urban, and
rural areas is presented in Chart 11.5. Rural areas account for 47 percent of the total Bank branches,
followed by semi-urban regions with 32 percent and urban areas with 21 percent. Chart 11.6 details
the Bank group-wise distribution of branches in the rural, semi-urban and urban areas as of March
31, 2024.

Public Sector Banks have the largest network in the state, comprising 3,979 branches. This is
followed by Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) with 2,105 branches and Private Sector Banks with
1,329 branches. Co-operative and Small Finance Banks (SFBs) have 290 and 342 branches,
respectively. In rural Bihar, Public Sector Banks own 43.2 percent of the branches, while RRBs
account for 38.4 percent. In urban areas, 60.6 percent of branches belong to Public Sector Banks,
and 25.4 percent are of Private Sector Banks.

The distribution of Bank branches by bank group in rural, semi-urban and urban of the state is as
follows:

e Public Sector Banks have 41.0 percent of their branches in rural areas, 32.9 percent in semi-
urban areas, and 26.2 percent in urban areas.

¢ RRBs focus significantly on rural areas, with 68.9 percent of their branches located there and
26.1 percent in semi-urban areas,

e Co-operative Banks have 62.4 percent of their branches in rural areas, 18.3 percent in semi-
urban areas, and 19.3 percent in urban areas of the state.

e Private Sector Banks have 28.3 percent of their branches in rural areas, 38.9 percent in
semi-urban areas, and 32.8 percent in urban areas.
