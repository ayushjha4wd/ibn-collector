3.1 Land-use Pattern

In Bihar, 54.6 percent of the land was utilised for crop production in 2022-23. Over the years, the
net sown area has fluctuated within a narrow range. In 2022-23, the net sown area was 5112.8
thousand hectares, the largest extent of land under cultivation in the last four years (between
2019-20 and 2022-23). In 2022-23, the gross sown area was 7268.2 thousand hectares, registering
a marginal fall from 7328.6 thousand hectares in 2021-22. Cropping intensity indicates the use of
land for multiple crop production cycles within a single production year; a higher cropping
intensity signifies more number of crops cultivated during a production year. In Bihar, over the
past four years (2019-20 to 2022-23), cropping intensity has not changed much. District-wise
disaggregation suggests that, in Buxar and Bhojpur districts, more than 80 percent of the
geographical area was utilised for crop production. The cropping intensity was highest in Supaul
at 1.82, followed by Jehanabad at 1.75 in 2022-23 (for details see Table A 3.1).
