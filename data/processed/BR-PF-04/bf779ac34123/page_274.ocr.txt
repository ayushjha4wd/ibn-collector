6.3 Road Network

Bihar is the third largest state in terms of population, having 8.6 percent of the country’s population
in 2011. The state is 12th largest in the country on account of area holding, which is 2.86 percent
of the national area. In terms of road network, Bihar has around 3 lakh kms of road network and
is placed on 8th rank among all Indian states as of 2019. The priority has been given to the hassle-
free smooth traffic movement for passengers as well as freights from one place to another across
the state, Chart 6.3 presents the road length of National Highways (NH), State Highways (SH) and
Major District Roads (MDR) by their width, till September 2024. Nearly 90 percent of NHs are
double or more lane. Similarly, around 85 percent SHs are double or more lane; however, 48
percent MDRs are intermediate type and 32 percent single lane. The state is progressing fast to
