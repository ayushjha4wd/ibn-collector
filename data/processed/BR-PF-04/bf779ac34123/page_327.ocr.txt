Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) under Bihar Rajya Fasal Sahayata Yojana (BRFSY) : The
introduction of an online registration system under the Bihar Rajya Fasal Sahayata Yojana has
simplified access for farmers, allowing them to avail benefits with ease. The integration of mobile
field inspections and the Aadhaar-enabled Payment System (AEPS) have expedited the
verification process, ensuring that eligible farmers receive financial assistance without delays.

Mukhyamantri Harit Krishi Sanyantra Yojana : A mobile application has been developed to
support farmers in availing agricultural equipment under the Mukhyamantri Harit Krishi Sanyantra
Yojana. This App enables the electronic hiring of equipment, providing a seamless and accessible
platform for farmers to rent machinery from PACS. The initiative is a step forward in promoting
agriculture mechanization, enhancing productivity, and reducing manual labour for farmers.

Cooperative Court Information System : It has been designed to automate and streamline the
management of court cases related to cooperative societies. This system has drastically improved
case processing efficiency, transparency, and accessibility. The digital management of cases
facilitates better record-keeping, timely notifications, and easier access to case details for all
stakeholders. In order to provide real-time information and grievance redressal services to farmers,
a 24x7 Sugam Call Center has been established, ensuring prompt and efficient support.
