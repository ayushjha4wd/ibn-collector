To encourage mushroom production, the State government has initiated the Mushroom Production
Scheme under the National Agricultural Development Scheme. Mushroom cultivation has
emerged as a lucrative agribusiness opportunity in Bihar in recent years. Bihar is now the leading
producer of mushroom in the country, Considering the nutritional and economic benefits, the
Department of Agriculture has made multiple efforts in the form of financial assistance. Mushroom
kits (compost and spawn) are distributed to landless farmers at a 90 percent subsidy. Similarly,
for mushroom production in thatch houses, a 50 percent subsidy has been announced, In view of
year-round mushroom production under a controlled environment, ‘agripreneurs’ can avail
themselves of a subsidy of Rs. 10 lakh (maximum) for air-conditioned mushroom production units.
Likewise, they can also avail themselves of a subsidy of Rs. 10 lakh per unit and Rs, 7.5 lakh per
unit for mushroom compost units and mushroom spawn units, respectively. The State government
has approved a budget of Rs. 359.0 lakh for the Mushroom Production Scheme in Hut. One village
per Krishi Vigyan Kendra is being developed as a Mushroom Village using paddy straw for
mushroom production.
