Storage, Warehouse and Procurement

The Bihar State Warehousing Corporation (BSWC) arranges storage for agricultural and other
notified goods. BSWC builds or rents godowns to store agricultural products, fertilisers, etc, In
2023-24, 54 centres were operational with a total storage capacity of 5.79 lakh tonnes. It can be
observed from Table 3.56 that even though the owned storage capacity remained constant, the
hired storage capacity has increased over the years. Between 2019-20 and 2023-24, the hired
storage capacity has increased by 53,8 percent, The utilised capacity in 2023-24 was 5.61 lakh
tonnes, or 97 percent of the available capacity. Although the Food Corporation of India (FCI)
continues to be the main depositor in the utilisation of storage capacity, the share of other PSUs in
total deposits has been increasing over the years, For instance, the share of other PSUs in the total
deposits was 17.8 percent in 2019-20, and that has increased to 30.0 percent in 2023-24,

In the case of procurement, rice and wheat were obtained (see Table 3.58). In 2023-24, the target
for rice procurement was 4,500 thousand tonnes. while the actual procurement was 3,079.45
thousand tonnes. For wheat, the total procured quantity was 601.19 thousand tonnes against the
target of 1,000 thousand tonnes in 2023-24. The storage and procurement of essential commodities
serve two important purposes. First, they insulate farmers from general price declines immediately
after the harvest of crops and provide them with the benefit of availing the Minimum Support
Prices (MSP). Second, from the consumer’s perspective, it ensures food security in the state.
