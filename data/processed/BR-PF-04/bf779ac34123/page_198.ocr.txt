The Bihar government recognizes the importance of both large-scale industries and small and
informal enterprises in shaping the economic landscape. While large industries offer high-scale
production and employment, small and marginal enterprises are critical in driving local economic
activities and providing grassroots employment opportunities. Understanding the dynamics of
industrial growth in Bihar thus requires a dual focus on both formal and informal sectors, This
chapter aims to explore the trajectory of industrial growth in Bihar by utilizing data from the
Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) and the Annual Survey of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises
(ASUSE), These datasets provide valuable insights into the performance and challenges of both
formal industries and small-scale manufacturing activities, highlighting the complexities of
fostering industrial growth in a state like Bihar. Moreover, the role of government policies in
promoting industrialization, particularly those targeting youth entrepreneurship and small
enterprise development, will be discussed.

In the structural composition of any economy, the secondary sector consists of mining and
quarrying; manufacturing; electricity, gas, water supply, and other utility services; and
construction, Over the years, construction has been the most important activity in Bihar,
Construction activities grew at the rate of 22 percent between 2021-22 and 2022-23. It contributed
50.2 percent of the GSDP of the secondary sector, followed by manufacturing. In 2022-23, the
manufacturing sector contributed 37.0 percent of the GSDP of the secondary sector, In other
words, construction and manufacturing were the drivers of the secondary sector growth in Bihar.
