An upgraded version of the Child Labour Tracking System (CLTS) software has been introduced
to monitor and facilitate the rehabilitation of rescued child labourers, Each released child labourer
registered in the CLTS receives Rs. 25,000 from the Chief Minister's Relief Fund. A total of
Rs. 6.64 crore has been distributed to 2,658 children at the rate of Rs. 25,000 per child, according
to the available data. Additionally, the "Dhaava Dal" application has been developed to streamline
the monitoring of child labour release processes.

The Commission has also implemented affirmative actions to promote the welfare of children of
brick kiln workers. During the brick-making season in Bihar, workers from various districts
migrate to work in brick kilns. In the absence of caregivers at home, these workers are compelled
to bring their children to the kilns. It is often observed that the children of these workers engage in
various activities within the brick kilns, exposing them to hazardous and polluted environments.
Furthermore, these children are deprived of educational opportunities, To address this issue, the
Commission is working to establish temporary créches for children under the age of 7 years and
temporary schools for children aged 6 to 14 years, ensuring a safer and more supportive
environment for their growth and learning.

Employees’ State Insurance Scheme (ESIS) : The primary objective of the Employees’ State
Insurance Scheme is to provide medical facilities and benefits to 4,1 1,210 insured individuals and
their dependents. These individuals receive medical services through 17 ESI dispensaries and 40
affiliated medical centres.

Any establishment with ten or more employees is required to register under the Employees’ State
Insurance Act. The ESIC, established under the Employees’ State Insurance Act 1948, provides
insurance coverage for employees. Currently, employees contribute 0.75 percent of their wages,
while employers contribute 3.25 percent of the wages paid or payable during each wage period.

In 2023-24, more than 43 types of medicines have been procured from the Bihar Medical Services
& Infrastructure Corporation Limited (BMSICL), Patna. The procured medicine was distributed
among the ESI dispensaries. During this period, 25.6 thousand employees and their dependents
have received treatment through these dispensaries.
