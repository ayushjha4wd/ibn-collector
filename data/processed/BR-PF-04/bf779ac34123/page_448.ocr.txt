As of September 2024, the credit-deposit (CD) ratio for Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) in Bihar
was 60.8 percent, while for Cooperative Banks, it was 45.6 percent. RRBs in Bihar mobilised
deposits amounting to Rs, 44,589 crore and made advances totaling Rs, 27,100 crore. Small
Finance Banks (SFBs) recorded advances of Rs. 9193 crore and collected deposits of Rs. 2238
crore, resulting in an impressive CD ratio of 410.7 percent, However, the share of SFBs in the total
deposits and advances in the state remained relatively low compared to other banks, accounting
for only 0.42 percent of total deposits and 3.09 percent of total advances.
