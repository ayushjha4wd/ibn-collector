Table 2.17 presents the revenue collected by the Commercial Tax Department of the State
government from 2019-20 to 2023-24, covering various tax categories that are not included under
the Goods and Services Tax (GST). The total revenue collected by the Commercial Tax
Department under these heads rose significantly, from Rs. 6.820 crore in 2019-20 to Rs. 10,437.3
crore in 2023-24, A substantial portion of this revenue comes from the value-added tax imposed
by the State government, which increased from Rs. 6,188.5 crore in 2019-20 to Rs. 9,392.7 crore
in 2023-24. Other significant contributors to the tax revenue include the Electricity Duty and
Professional Tax, with contributions of Rs. 845.7 crore and Rs. 187.4 crore, respectively, in
2023-24. The collection from Professional Tax has demonstrated impressive growth over the past
four years, with a notable increase of 15.8 percent in 2023-24 and 13.7 percent in 2022-23

compared to the previous year.
