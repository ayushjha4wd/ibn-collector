CHIEF MINISTER
BIHAR

Message

Bihar has made significant achievements in its joumey of
development with justice in the last two decades. Development with justice
means development of all areas and upliftment of every section of the
society. This edition of the Economic Survey (2024-25) captures our
consistent performance on growth and development parameters over these

two decades.

Bihar, today, is being known for its achievements in the fields of
infrastructure, power, education, public health, good governance,
employment and especially women empowerment. Now Bihar has also
taken important steps towards development of sports and we have started
preparing such sportspersons who will earn prestige and glory for our state
as well as our country. We have continued to provide employment to youth
at large scale so that the standard of living of the people continues to
improve. The positive impact of the government's efforts is visible in all
the spheres. Now women are exercising their right to secure, regulate and

control their lives in every area, inside and outside the home.

I am confident that this Economic Survey will prove useful to policy

makers, economists, educationists and other readers.

(Nitish Kumar)
