Between September 2023 and September 2024, the deposits mobilized by these banks increased
by 18.6 percent, rising from Rs. 1887.9 crore to Rs. 2238.2 crore. Simultaneously, advances also
grew, rising from Rs. 7726.9 crore to Rs. 9193.2 crore, which represents a rise of 19.0 percent.
This led to an increase in the CD ratio of SFBs from 409.3 percent to 410.7 percent. Among the
SFBs, Utkarsh SFB holds the largest share in advances, totaling Rs. 5247.4 crore, followed by
Ujjivan SFB with Rs, 1998.0 crore, and Jana SFB with Rs, 1123.3 crore.

The distribution of deposit, credit, and CD (credit-deposit) ratios for banks in Rural, Semi-Urban,

September 2024. The CD ratio in Rural areas decreased from 60.2 percent in September 2023 to
59.8 percent in September 2024, In contrast, the CD ratio in Semi-Urban areas increased from 52.0
percent to 54.1 percent, and the CD ratio in Urban areas rose from 52.5 percent to 54.3 percent
during this period.
