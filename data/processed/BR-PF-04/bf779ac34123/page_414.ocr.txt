Under the scheme, an allocation of Rs. 500 crore will be made annually for 2024-25 and 2025-26
from the state plan. The Urban Development and Housing Department will distribute these funds
on a district basis and further allocate them among urban bodies according to their population, The
funds will be utilized through the Bihar Urban Development Agency (BUDA) or BUIDCO, with
the procedures established in consultation with the Finance Department.

To prevent duplication, 251 selected schemes under the scheme will exclude those that are already
included in other departmental or scheme proposals. If any work listed as a priority is executed
under another scheme during a financial year, it will be removed from the priority list.

Mukhyamantri Shahri Peyajal Nishchay Yojana ; The Mukhyamantri Shahri Peyajal Nishchay
Yojana aims to provide access to potable water within the household premises in the urban areas
of Bihar, The scheme has nearly achieved its target. As of September 2024, 18.47 lakh households
have received water connections within their premises, and only 75,000 households remain to be
connected. It is important to note that, in the past, 4000 households have declined the water
pipeline connections offered under this scheme. The primary responsibility for implementing the
scheme rests with the ULBs and BUIDCO. Together, these two agencies have executed the scheme
in 3158 wards out of 3398, providing piped water connections to 14.58 lakh households. The
Public Health Engineering Department (PHED) also served as an implementing agency for the
scheme.
