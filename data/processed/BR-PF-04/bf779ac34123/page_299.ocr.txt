6.14 Bihar State Building Construction Corporation Limited (BSBCCL)

The State government established the Bihar State Building Construction Corporation Limited
(BSBCCL) in 2008 under the Building Construction Department (BCD). This Corporation has
played an important role in providing adequate building infrastructure for the state administration.

in a stipulated time, Out of 2483 projects, the Corporation has completed 2154 projects, Till March
2024, the total project's expenditure Rs. 6532 crore. The rest projects of the BSBCCL are under
different phases. An overview of the working conditions of BSBCCL shown through Table 6.12

6.15 Bihar Police Building Construction Corporation Limited (BPBCCL)

The State government is committed to maintain law and order in the state and police modernization
remains a top priority. The State government has revamped the Bihar Police Building Construction
Corporation Limited (BPBCCL) in an unprecedented manner to construct the infrastructure
(buildings) equipped with safety and security features. It is another profit-making Corporation of
the state. It maintains its efficient financial management and timely completion of projects. An
overview of the physical and financial progress of the BPBCCL during the last decade (2013-14
to 2023-24) is presented in Table 6.13. As per the data shown in the table, the sanctioned amount
for 7093 projects is Rs. 5730 crore during 2013-14 to 2023-24. The Corporation has also donated
in the Chief Minister’s Relief Fund and spent under its Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)
initiative.
