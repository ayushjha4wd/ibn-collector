the provisional estimate (P) for 2022-23; these estimated figures are subject to revision within a
timeframe of one to two years. Final figures are available up to 2021-22. The quick and provisional
estimates, along with actual numbers from recent years, are utilised in the Economic Survey to
analyse trends and changes in economic activities. Additional data on various components of the
GSDP and NSDP at current prices and constant (2011-12) prices for Bihar, from 2018-19 to
2023-24 (Q), can be found in the appendix of this chapter.
