surveyed homeless ST families, 3,352 were provided with land.

The Abhiyan Basera Programme in Bihar is an initiative to provide housing rights to economically
weaker sections, particularly landless and homeless families. The programme ensures secure
ownership of homestead land, enabling beneficiaries to construct their homes. It emphasizes
addressing the socio-economic challenges of marginalized groups. including SC / ST / BC
(schedule 1) and BC schedule 1).
