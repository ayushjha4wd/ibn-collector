infrastructure by providing purchase incentives on the purchase of charging station equipment
under various categories of chargers.

Bihar Clean Fuel Policy 2019 and 2023: The State government has issued a notification banning
all government vehicles that are more than 15 years old. Plying of commercial vehicles that are
more than 15 years old has been banned in Patna, Gaya and Muzaffarpur. Further, green tax has
been levied on commercial vehicles that are more than 12 years old. In addition, the State
government has launched the Bihar Clean Fuel Policy 2019 that prohibits plying diesel-operated
three-wheelers in the areas of Patna Municipal Corporation, Danapur Nagar Parishad, Khagaul
Nagar Parishad and Phulwari Sharif Nagar Parishad from March 31, 2022 and Muzaffarpur and
Gaya from October 01, 2023, Diesel three-wheelers have been replaced by CNG three-wheelers,
while petrol three-wheelers have been retrofitted with CNG kits, Till date, a total of 1566 three-
wheelers* retrofitting’ conversion has been done with a total expenditure of Rs. 4.18 crore as
subsidy. Further, a subsidy of Rs. 4.725 crore has been given to 63 diesel city-bus owners in Patna

for replacement with CNG buses under the Bihar Clean Fuel (City Bus Incentive) scheme, 2023,

PM e-Bus Sewa: The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India has marked
significant steps towards its commitment for decarbonizing the transportation system and
augmenting the urban bus operation. Six cities of Bihar (Patna, Bhagalpur, Darbhanga, Gaya,
Muzaffarpur and Purnea) have been selected under the scheme for procurement of 400 e-buses
with the Central assistance of Rs. 671.6 crore, Further, financial assistance will also be provided
for the development of behind-the-meter power infrastructure and depot infrastructure for seamless

movement and maintenance of buses for the period of 12 years.
