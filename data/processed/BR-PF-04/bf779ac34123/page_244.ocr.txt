Levels of Earnings from Employment

In PLFS, information on earnings from employment was collected for all three categories of
workers, viz., self-employed persons, regular wage/salaried employees and casual labour. For the
information on earnings, PLFS uses the current weekly status. For self-employed persons in
current weekly status, information on earnings during the last 30 days from self-employment
activity was collected. For regular wage/salaried employees in current weekly status, information
on earnings during the preceding calendar month from such work was collected. For casual labour,
information on earnings was collected for each day of the reference week.

The self-employed category was a numerically dominant category across all states of India, As
previously noted, 61.2 percent of male workers and 83.7 percent of female workers were self-
employed. The average gross monthly income of a male self-employed worker in rural Bihar was
Rs. 10480.2 compared to Rs. 11088.0 at the rural India level. For a female worker, the average
monthly income in rural Bihar was Rs. 4434.8, which is only 42 percent of the average monthly
income of a male self-employed worker. For regular wage/salaried employees, the average
monthly income of a male worker in rural Bihar was Rs. 18504.0 (Table 5.10). The male-female
wage differential was lower for regular wage/salaried employees than the earnings from self-
