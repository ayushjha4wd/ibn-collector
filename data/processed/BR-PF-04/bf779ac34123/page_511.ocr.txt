Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act 1989, Rules 1995 ;
These aim to provide relief to the members of SC/ST community from harassment by members of
other communities. This is a centrally sponsored scheme whose expenditure is shared equally by
the Central and State governments. Under this, financial assistance is provided to the families
facing harassment. In 2024-25, a budget provision of Rs. 10.03 crore under establishment and
committed expenditure and Rs. 100.00 crore jointly as central and state share under plan
expenditure has been made.

Hostel Food Grain Scheme : Approval has been given to provide 15 kgs of food grains (9 kgs
rice and 6 kgs wheat) per month to students living in SC/ST hostels.

Chief Minister Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe Hostel Grant Scheme: This is a state
government scheme under which Rs 1000 cash assistance per month is provided to SC/ST students
living in state government hostels.

Pre-Exam Coaching Centres : A total of 10 coaching centres are being run in the state to provide
coaching to the candidates belonging to SC/ST communities, appearing in UPSC, BPSC, Banking,
Railway and other competitive cxaminations. These coaching centres are running in the
universities of Patna, Darbhanga, Bhagalpur, Gaya, Ara, Chhapra, Muzaffarpur, Saharsa, Purnea
and Munger. Besides this, a student guidance centre is running in the Chandragupt Institute of
Management, Patna, where coaching is provided for GMAT/CAT examination. At present, there is
a provision for coaching of 2460 candidates at various centres.

Tharuhat Area Development : In 2009, the Integrated Tharuhat Development Authority was
formed for the development of ST communities (including the Tharu tribe) in West Champaran. A
total of Rs. 14989.93 lakh was made available to the Authority for Tharuhat area development
from financial year 2010-11 to 2023-24, under which the work was completed in 256 schemes out
of total 331. There is a budget provision of Rs. 30.00 crore in the year 2024-25. An amount of
Rs. 14989.93 lakh was made available to the Authority in the past years for connectivity path,
water supply, library, weavers’ building, health sub-centre, stadium, computer laboratory building,
common room for girl students, etc. have been constructed in this area. Apart from this, Youth
Development and Vocational Training Scheme are being implemented through the Agency.
