Storm Water Drainage Scheme

Waterlogging in urban areas poses significant challenges for residents. To tackle this issue, the
State government has implemented the Storm Water Drainage Scheme. The Department of Urban
Development and Housing is actively addressing the problem through the construction of drainage
systems. Under the Chief Minister’s Urban Drainage and Lane Paying Scheme, small drains are

being constructed throughout the cities.

In the initial phase of the scheme, priority was given to municipal corporation areas. Consequently,
administrative approval has been granted for nine projects designed to alleviate the waterlogging
problem in Patna and its surrounding regions. To address the waterlogging issue in Patna city,
projects have been approved for the installation of new drainage pumps, construction of drains,
cleaning of existing drains, and the installation of pumps and motors at drainage pumping stations.
The estimated cost of implementing the Storm Water Drainage Scheme is Rs, 957.51 crore. To
further expand this initiative, 11 additional cities have been included. The approved budget for

these projects amounts to Rs. 1,034.68 crore.

This scheme is currently being implemented at various locations in Bihar. In this context,
stormwater drainage plans have been approved to resolve waterlogging issues in the cities of
Saharsa, Sasaram, Madhubani, Supaul, Chhapra, Katihar, Darbhanga, Phulwarisharif. Barh, and
Mokama, with implementation overseen by the Bihar Urban Infrastructure Development
Corporation (BUIDCO),
