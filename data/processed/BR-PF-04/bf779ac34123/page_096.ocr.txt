2.3 Deficit Management

The gross fiscal deficit reflects the extent of the resource gap in fiscal management by the State
government, It represents the total financing the State government needs, primarily obtained from
its cash and investment balances with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and through borrowing.
This financing is necessary to cover the excess of revenue and capital expenditures (including
loans and advances) over revenue and non-debt receipts.
