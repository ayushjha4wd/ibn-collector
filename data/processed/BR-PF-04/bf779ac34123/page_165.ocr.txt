3.6 Other Important Initiatives for Agriculture and Related Activities
Jaivik Kheti Protsahan Yojana

Green manure refers to undecomposed plant material used as a natural fertiliser. Generally, to
retain the nitrogen balance of soil, the farmers grow green manure crops like Dhaincha. To
encourage farmers to grow Dhaincha, a subsidy of 90 percent was given on seeds. The Bihar Rajya
Beej Nigam Ltd. (BRBN) is tasked with ensuring the quality of the seed. The Jaivik Kheti
Protsahan Yojana covers 38 districts to promote organic farming. For 2024-25, the Yojana has
been sanctioned for three components: pucca vermi compost unit, commercial vermi compost unit,
and Gobar/biogas unit, and allotment for the same is given, The details of the three components
sanctioned under the scheme are provided as follows.

Pucca Vermi compost Unit : Vermi compost is a vital source of organic carbon that significantly
enhances soil fertility and plays an essential role in organic farming. Initially, beneficiaries of the
vermi compost unit receive assistance with earthworms, which is a one-time provision,
Additionally, farmers practising agriculture and livestock management can benefit from a 50
percent grant for establishing a pucca vermi compost unit, with a maximum subsidy of Rs. 5000.
This assistance applies to a maximum of three vermi pits per farmer. For 2024-25, 20.000 vermi
pits are sanctioned for construction.

inputs, the State government provides subsidies to individual entrepreneurs, Krishi Vigyan
Kendras (KVKs), and Government Seed Production Farms/Non-governmental Organizations
