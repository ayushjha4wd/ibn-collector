50 percent subsidy on the cultivation of Indian gooseberry, lemon, guava, and apple ber, and has
additionally announced support for micro irrigation on a priority basis. The State government is
providing an 80 percent subsidy to small and marginal farmers on drip and micro sprinkler systems
under the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) to promote the efficient and
judicious use of water for irrigation.

To reduce the post-harvest loss and enhance market prices of horticultural crops, the State
government is focusing on strengthening the cold chain infrastructure. The construction of cold
storage allows farmers to choose the price at which they wish to sell their products, minimising
distress sales, In the 12 districts without any cold storage facilities, the State has provided financial
assistance for construction of new cold storage units. To meet the demands of small and medium
‘agripreneurs’, the Micro Solar Cold Room Scheme has been launched with the financial
assistance of 50 percent. Considering the significant energy demand for operating cold storages, a
50 percent subsidy is proposed on the project cost of Rs. 35 lakh for conversion to solar power in
existing cold storages.

For the quality production of horticulture crops in the state, the availability of quality planting
material is critical. Two Centers of Excellence (CoE) — Chandi, in the Nalanda district for
vegetables and Desari, in the Vaishali district for fruits — have been established to help facilitate
the timely availability of good quality planting materials to the farmers. To meet the increasing
demand for quality planting material, the Department of Agriculture is establishing plug-type
nurseries across the state and is rejuvenating departmental nurseries. As part of the rejuvenation
effort, the focus is also on maintaining and upgrading the pedigree of the mother orchards.

Besides various production interventions, crop-specific promotional awareness is crucial for the
overall growth of the sector. Bagwani Mahotsav, Aam Mahotsav, and Makhana Mahotsav have
been successfully organised by the Department of Agriculture. In light of the high demand and
future export potential of makhana, the Department has organised a makhana-related promotional
event and workshop in Bengaluru.

In Bihar, the total number of livestock has increased significantly in the last one and a half decades.
