Irrigation

Irrigation is pivotal in crop production and in other related activities. The availability of irrigation
increases crop production, productivity, and intensity. Major sources of irrigation include canals,
tanks, various types of wells, etc. Table 3.35 indicates that groundwater irrigation has been the
primary source of irrigation in Bihar. In 2022-23, irrigation through accessing groundwater using
different types of wells was carried out in 64.1 percent of the gross irrigated area. Surface irrigation
via canals was available in 30.3 percent of the gross irrigated area.
