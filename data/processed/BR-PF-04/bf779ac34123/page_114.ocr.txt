The district-wise revenue collection from Stamp Duty and Registration Fees, along with the target
and achievement rates for 2023-24, are reported in Table 2.21. In this period, the State government
collected Rs. 6,170.9 crore from Stamp Duty and Registration Fees based on 14,09,237 documents.
This revenue was collected against a target of Rs. 7,000.0 crore, resulting in an achievement rate
of 88.2 percent of the set target for the year. There is a significant variation in the average revenue
collection per document across different districts. Overall, the average revenue receipt per
document in the state for 2023-24 was Rs. 43,789. Patna recorded the highest average revenue per
document at Rs. 1,08.936.0, while Kishanganj had the lowest at Rs. 22,745.8.
