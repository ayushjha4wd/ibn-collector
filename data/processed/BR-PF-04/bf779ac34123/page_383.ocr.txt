Satat Jeevikoparjan Yojana

The State government launched the Ultra-Poor Graduation Programme in 2018. This initiative
aims to create sustainable livelihood opportunities for ultra-poor households traditionally
involved in producing, transporting and selling country liquor/toddy, as well as for ultra-poor
individuals from SC/ST and other communities, The programme emphasizes livelihood
diversification, capacity building and enhanced financial access.

Under the scheme, 2,01,218 households have been identified and trained to undertake income-
generating activities, as illustrated in the table below. The initiative emphasizes building
confidence and developing enterprise skills among these households. Upon completion of
training, a Livelihood Investment Fund of up to Rs. 2 lakh is allocated to each targeted
household for asset creation. A Livelihood Gap Assistance Fund is also provided as
consumption support to meet the households! essential needs until their new livelihood ventures
generate income.

The sustainability of this approach is reinforced through strategic integration with government
programmes, ensuring that ultra-poor households gain access to essential entitlements,
especially during crises like the COVID-19 pandemic. JEEVIKA has ensured that 100 percent
of beneficiaries possess key documents, such as Aadhaar cards and bank accounts while
securing food security through PDS and providing financial protection via insurance coverage,
housing, drinking water and health care. As of September 2024, over 1.52 lakh households
were considered safe and secure, with 17.42 lakh households ensuring access to safe drinking
water, Additionally, around 1.61 lakh households were food secure under the PDS, and 1.69
lakh households were covered under Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) and
Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY).

The Graduation Indicators Assessment Survey, conducted in June-July 2023, assessed the
progress of the second cohort of SJY beneficiary households after 18-24 months of asset-based
enterprise support. Covering 1,917 households (79.87 percent of the target sample) across 35
districts, the survey used telephonic and in-person methods to ensure accuracy.

The survey confirmed the programme's sustainability by evaluating core and additional
