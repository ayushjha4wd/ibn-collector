10.4 Patna Metro Rail Project

The Patna Metro Rail Project has been initiated under a tripartite agreement signed between the
Central government, the State government, and the Patna Metro Rail Corporation Limited, Both
Central and State governments bear 20 percent each of the total project cost, while remaining 60
percent will be financed through a soft loan from an external financial institution, the Japan
International Cooperation Agency (JICA). The cost associated with land acquisition,
rehabilitation, and resettlement will be solely borne by the State government. Under this proposed
project, two corridors have been selected for the Patna Metro, Corridor-I has a total length of 17,93
km, while Corridor-I1 is 14.57 km long.

The Patna Metro Rail project has made significant progress, as 78.02 acres of private land has been
acquired, and the acquisition of the remaining 2.75 acres is underway. Additionally, NoC has been
received for 17.15 acres of land owned by various departments of the Government of India and
the State government.

As of now, Rs. 3402.09 crore has been allocated for the project, comprising Rs. 872.50 crore from
the Central government and Rs. 2529.59 crore from the State government, For 2024-25, a
provision of Rs. 380.00 crore has been made for the Project.
