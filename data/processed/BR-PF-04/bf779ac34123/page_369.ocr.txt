RAID and FIR Management System: A separate WhatsApp number has been provided for
lodging complaints exclusively related to power theft. A new facility has been introduced from
August, 01 2023 for maintaining RAID and FIR records through online mode which was being
done manually earlier and was difficult for tracking and monitoring of the records at the central
level. A unique system-generated reference number is being provided for each RAID/FIR entry
done by DISCOM officials. This application is directly linked with collection counters for
centralised cash collection under this head. Tracking and monitoring of records are made easy
through various MIS reports generated in this module. By utilizing this application, it has become
easy to find how many RAID/FIRs have been conducted by field officials in their respective
Circle/Division/Sub-Division/Sections. Consumers for whom RAID/FIR has been conducted will
get an SMS at every stage of the process to make them aware of the process and can also pay the
RAID/FIR amount using their reference number at the Collection Counter. This application has
also significantly helped in revenue generation.
