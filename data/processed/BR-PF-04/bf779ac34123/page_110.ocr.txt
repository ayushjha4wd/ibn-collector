Table 2.15 presents the State government’s non-tax revenue from various sources from 2019-20
to 2023-24, along with the budgeted estimate for 2024-25. A significant portion of the non-tax
revenue comes from the Non-Ferrous Mining and Metallurgical Industries and Interest Receipts.
In the last two years, revenue collection from this segment has grown substantially, It increased
by 54.6 percent, reaching Rs, 2,730 crore in 2022-23, followed by a further rise of 14.1 percent to
Rs. 3,115 crore in 2023-24. The percentage share of revenue from the Non-Ferrous Mining and
Metallurgical Industries in the state’s total non-tax revenue from its own sources has risen from
42.5 percent in 2019-20 to 59.2 percent in 2023-24. In contrast, revenue from Interest Receipts has
declined from Rs. 1,416 crore to Rs. 897 crore over the same period, However, there was a notable
increase of 39.7 percent in Interest Receipts in 2023-24 compared to the previous fiscal year.
