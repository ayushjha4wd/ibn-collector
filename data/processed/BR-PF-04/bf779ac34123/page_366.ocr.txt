State Government Schemes

Smart Metering with Prepaid Functionality: Since their establishment in November 2012, the
two distribution companies in Bihar, NBPDCL and SBPDCL, have made significant progress in
achieving universal electrification within the state and offering electricity connections to all
households that are interested. Nevertheless, the composition of consumers and sales for these two
distribution companies remains challenging and differs considerably from that of most distribution
companies across the country. In Bihar, out of 1.90 crore consumers, around 88 percent are
domestic and therefore there is a constant challenge of timely billing and payments, especially in
rural areas. During the last few years, both DISCOMs have successfully implemented the practice
of spot-billing. However, the dependency of quality meter-reading is another constant challenge
for success of spot-billing. Coupled with this, there have been considerable challenges due to
wrong billing, non-payment by consumers, non-recording of consumer loads and theft, etc., thus
resulting in high AT&C loss.

Initially, both Discoms began installing basic prepaid meters at certain locations during 2013-14.
This led to positive outcomes regarding billing and revenue collection. Nonetheless, there were
constraints and difficulties associated with accounting and the execution of tariff alterations. To
address these issues, the State government decided to implement smart metering with prepaid
functionality in 2019, which started from urban areas, covering 23,50 lakh consumers, An
agreement was signed in January 2021 with M/S Energy Efficiency Services Ltd. (EESL) for the
installation of smart meters with prepaid functionality for 23.50 lakh consumers in urban areas and
consumers under 'Har Ghar Nal ka ‘Jal’ category and agriculture consumers. After successful
implementation of the scheme in urban areas, the State government also decided to start its
implementation in rural areas in a time-bound manner to cover consumers of both urban and rural
areas. Accordingly, approval was accorded by the State government in October 2022 for the
installation of 1.48 crore smart prepaid meters to rural and left-over urban consumers of NBPDCL
and SBPDCL at the cost of Rs. 15,074.12 crore, which is targeted to be completed by 2025-26.
