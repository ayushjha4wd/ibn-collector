Literacy Rate

In India, literates in 2011 constituted 74 percent of the total population aged seven years and above,
as compared to 65 percent in 2001. While comparing decadal variation in literacy rates of Hindi
heartland states, it is seen that during the last decade the literacy rate of Bihar has improved
significantly, from 47.0 percent in 2001 to 61.8 percent in 2011. This implies an increase of 14.8
percentage points. It is worthy to note that this decadal increase is not only the highest among all
decadal growth rates in Bihar since 1971, it is also the highest among all the states for the decade
2001-11. Chart 12.11 presents the trend of literacy rates in the Hindi heartland states between 1971
and 2011.
