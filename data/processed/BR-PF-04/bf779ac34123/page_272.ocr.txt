PHYSICAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Physical Infrastructure brings socio-economic development in the society, It is regarded as
engine of growth and development and also improves regional equality through opening up
development opportunities to remote and underserved areas. The infrastructure is very much
essential in expanding the production base, enlarging trade and linking together resources
and markets into an integrated economy. It also helps in strengthening rural economy by
connecting with towns and market centers and links farm with non-farm sector, rural economic
activities with urban ones, and brings underdeveloped and developed regions closer to one
another. The State government has considerably invested in this sector. As an outcome, during
the last one and a half decades, the transport and communication sector has proved to be one
of the major growth drivers of the Bihar’s economy. Bihar has also witnessed improvement in
rural infrastructure and development has been recorded in the rural areas of the state. The
state has achieved the goal of reaching Patna in five hours from any corner of the state and
has been moving faster towards getting a congestion-free traffic by building expressways and
new highways. The registration of vehicles in Bihar has recorded one of the highest growths
in the country and revenue collection under the motor vehicle tax has also recorded a huge
growth in the recent times, Majority of infrastructure corporations are building state-of-the-
art infrastructures in the state.

6.1 Growth of Transport, Storage and Communication Sector

Economic development needs some basic foundation to move forward. Among all factors of
economic development, physical infrastructure is the major one. It opens up multiple opportunities
for the region to connect with the rest of the world. The State government has endeavoured to
build adequate physical infrastructure during the last two decades. The growth of the transport,
storage and communication sector of major states of India is presented in Chart 6.1 during 2011-
12 to 2023-24. Bihar registered the third highest growth (7.6 percent) in this sector during this
period, after Uttar Pradesh (10.1 percent) and Karnataka (7.7 percent) among major states of India.
Further, Chart 6.2 gives the detail of growth recorded in this sector and also gives trend of the
contribution of this sector in the Gross State Value Added (GSVA). As a result of high growth
recorded in this sector, the contribution of this sector has gradually increased and reached
Rs. 47,313 crore in 2023-24 from Rs. 17,545 crore in 2011-12 at constant (2011-12) prices. The
contribution of this sector in the overall GSVA has also gone up by 3.0 percentage points from 7.3
percent in 2011-12 to 10.2 percent in 2023-24,
