The State Investment Promotion Board (SIPB) plays an important role in attracting industries to
the state. It completes the application process in an online mode and adopts a transparent,
accountable, and reliable method for selecting investment proposals in the state. As of September
2024, SIPB has received 3,752 online applications, of which 3,150 investment proposals received
initial clearance for an investment amounting to Rs. 75,293.76 crore. In 2023-24, the total amount
of proposed investments was Rs. 5,642.57 crore from 706 proposed projects. In Bihar, 769 units
are currently operational. The total investment in these functioning units was Rs. 8,464.35 crore,
and they generated employment for 31,749 workers, Under the Bihar Industrial Investment
Promotion Policy 2016, the Government of Bihar awarded Rs. 1,615.37 crore as grants to these
working units (see Table 4.27).

attracted substantial investments in Bihar in the last few years. As of September 2024, 12 units
were operating in Bihar, and the total investment was Rs. 1,363.38 crore. The proposed investment
