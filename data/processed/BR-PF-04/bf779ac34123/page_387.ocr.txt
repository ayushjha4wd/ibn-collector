9.3 Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Gramin (PMAY-G) and Homestead Land
Distribution Scheme

The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) was launched in 2016 to provide "Housing

for All" in rural India by 2024. The scheme focuses on constructing pucca houses with basic

amenities for households living in kaccha houses, Beneficiaries are identified through the Socio-

Economic and Caste Census (SECC) 2011 data, ensuring targeted and inclusive implementation.
