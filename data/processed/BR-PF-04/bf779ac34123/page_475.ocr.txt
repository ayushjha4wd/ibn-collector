The efficiency of public health facilities can be assessed on the basis of outpatient visits and
inpatient services. The hospitals of Bihar collect data on outpatients and inpatients through the
Hospital Management Information System (HMIS), which is a Government of India portal. For
the state as a whole, the average number of outpatient visits increased 1.2 times from 312 in
2019-20 to 373 in 2023-24. The Bed Occupancy Rates and length of stay are also two measures
that reflect the functional ability of a hospital. Between 2019-20 and 2023-24, the overall Bed
Occupancy Rate of Bihar decreased from 55 percent in 2019-20 to 47 percent in 2023-24.
However, as per data available for the last six months, both indicators show a rise in numbers. The
outpatient visits per day till September 2024 in 2023-24 is 382 and the in-patient bed occupancy
rate is 54 percent. Table A 12.6 (Appendix) shows that the average number of outpatient visits per
day in 17 districts of the state was more than the state average at 373. Also, the bed occupancy rate

was more than the state average (47 percent) in 21 districts of Bihar.
