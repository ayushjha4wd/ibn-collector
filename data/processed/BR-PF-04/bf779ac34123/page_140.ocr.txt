Under the Fourth Agriculture Road Map and the scheme named ‘Promoting Agriculture for
Revolutionizing Agrarian Growth’ (PARAG), the State government has initiated the Beekeeping
and Honey Production Programme to enhance the quality of honey production. A provision of 75
percent subsidy for non-SC/ST producers (and a 90 percent subsidy for SC/ST producers) on bee
boxes (with beehives), and honey extractors and food-grade containers have been made. This will

ownership. The State government has allocated Rs. 2440.92 lakh for 2023-24 under the state plan.

Vegetable Production

The extent of land cultivated with vegetables was 894.01 thousand hectares in 2023-24, which was
0.5 percent more than the land used for vegetable cultivation in 2022-23, Among the vegetables,
the largest area of land was cultivated with potatoes, accounting for 39.6 percent of the total land
under vegetables cultivation in 2023-24, In 2023-24, 329.5 thousand hectares of land were
cultivated with potatoes and 8842.6 thousand tonnes of potatoes were produced. Except for a few
vegetables, almost all vegetables showed growth in production in 2023-24 compared to 2022-23.

Assessing market demand and supply, the State government has been providing financial
assistance to producers of high-value vegetables, hybrid vegetables, and onions. Farmers
producing these crops receive a 75 percent subsidy. For the first time, onion cultivation in kharif
season has been promoted in 500 hectares of land with subsidy of 50 percent and a component for
onion storage has been added with a subsidy of 75 percent. The provision of storage subsidy will
facilitate farmers in timely storage and reduce the distress sales, Due to the increase in demand for
Kufri Chipsona 1, a variety of potato, the Department is providing a 75 percent subsidy at a unit
cost of Rs. 1.25 lakh per hectare for its production in selected potato-growing districts. Similarly,
the farmers who are growing spices (coriander and fenugreek) can avail a 50 percent subsidy at a
unit cost of Rs. 30,000 per hectare.

For 2023-24, the State government has allocated Rs. 1303.56 lakh to implement the Vegetable
Development Scheme under the Bihar Horticulture Development Society. This scheme is being
implemented across 23 districts in Bihar. These initiatives aim to boost vegetable production,
improve storage infrastructure, and support farmers by reducing input costs and increase in
profitability,
