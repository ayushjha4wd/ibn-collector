8.7 Transmission

In order to improve the power supply position, the State government has adopted various measures
for capacity enhancement, improvement in the Transmission & Distribution (T&D) network and
energy conservation. The robust transmission system supports the increasing requirement of
generation and distribution. To meet the growing demands of power, there is a requirement for a
strong and reliable network of inter-state as well as intra-state transmission. The existing
transmission system, therefore, needs to be strengthened with a proper planning. The required
