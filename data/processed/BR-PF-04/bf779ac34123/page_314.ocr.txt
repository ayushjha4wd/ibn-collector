CHAKRA : This application was handed over to STF, Bihar by CID (prohibition) that has been
operational since January 2023, The important features of the CHAKRA App include powered
criminal registration and a search platform for Bihar Police. Further, a criminal database prepared
on the basis of face recognition, gang analysis and such advanced features are used for criminal
identification, criminal history, speedy arrest, effective crime prevention, detection and control. It
also assists the police in analysing the connection between different criminals, criminal networks
and gang analysis. During the last five years, nearly 10 lakh criminals’ data have been uploaded
to the system.

Cyber Security : A total of 70,057 complaints of financial fraud were registered till November
2024. After verifying these frauds, 3668 FIRs were lodged, Cyber cells in different district and
cyber police stations received 15.72 lakh calls regarding cybercrime. A total of 560 cybercriminals
have been arrested. The Economic and Cyber Crime wing has developed ‘Cyber Prahar’ for raids
and arrests of cybercriminals.
