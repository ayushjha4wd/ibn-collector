Physical modelling Centre (PMC) at Birpur as Centre of Excellence : With the establishment

of this Centre, initially, model testing of various reaches (including embankments and spurs)
of Kosi River will be done with the help of experts. These model studies will be beneficial
for better flood management and improvement in flood risk management. The construction
of PMC is in the final stage. The new technical and specialist posts have been created to

make PMC functional and some of the engineers have already been posted at PMC.

Mathematical Modelling Centre (MMC) at Patna as Centre of Excellence : MMC was

established as a Centre of Excellence with a vision to have in-house capacity to develop and
use mathematical models for solving water related problems in Bihar. Presently at MMC,
flood forecasting is done for five river basins namely Gandak. Bagmati-Adhwara, Kosi,

Kamla Balan Embankment (R.K.B.E.) Phase— | has been granted, costing Rs. 32,512.32
