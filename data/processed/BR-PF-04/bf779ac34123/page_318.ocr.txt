T-HCBS : The Government of Bihar has approved the Scheme for installation of ‘Towers of
Harmonious Call Blocking System’ (T-HCBS) in 15 prisons of Bihar to prevent unauthorized
communication from prisons. The system would block incoming/outgoing calls, SMS and data
services on prison premises. Telecom Service Providers have optimized their Radio Access
Network in the vicinity of 15 prison complexes in Bihar.

GIS mapping and Geo-fencing layers in TERA Maps for Prisons (iBhugoal) : The Latitude
and Longitude of prisons of Bihar have been taken for GIS mapping and Geo-fencing layers and
integrated in Tera Maps of GIS. It provides a single window for detailed information on
topography, geography, infrastructure and socio-economic indicators. Integration of GIS and MIS
data is achieved for planning and decision making.

Mission Vihaan : This is a software application through which the prison headquarter monitors
day-to-day activities in the prisons of Bihar.

e-Aushadhi and telemedicine in prisons : e-Aushadhi is being used for providing medicines to
prisoners and maintain the medicine stocks in prisons. In addition, Telemedicine has been
introduced in Central Prisons of Bihar for medical diagnosis, timely treatment and medical care of
prisoners, which also helps during the visit of prisoners to district hospitals and other hospitals.

Court Case Monitoring System : The Court Case Monitoring System (CCMS) is an online
application for Government departments to manage their case information and make updates. The
system is used to set alerts like next date of hearing, case compliance, and case history. This system
is integrated with the Court Information System to receive Cause lists and Final Orders.
Additionally, CCMS works as a digital diary to simplify court case tracking, which keeps
everything organized and easily accessible.

ICJS Software Application : In order to enable seamless transfer of data and information among
different pillars of the criminal justice system (courts, police and forensic science laboratories),
‘Inter-operable Criminal Justice System’ (ICJS) software application has been developed. At
present, prisons are taking prisoners’ admission through ICJS module. It is an effective tool for
case and court management, as all the relevant information of a case will be available in real time
for courts’ use. The ICJS enhances the productivity of the criminal justice system and also of
prisons and correctional services, both qualitatively and quantitatively. The core target of ICJS is
to climinate the manual entry of a person’s details and act as a facilitator to exchange the data
across pillars like Police, Court, FSL, Prosecution and Prisons. Police personnel are being
encouraged to use the ICJS portal more and more.
