Bihar Right to Public Services Act, 2011 : To ensure transparent, accountable and time-bound
service delivery, the Government of Bihar in its Good Governance agenda of 2010 listed a legal
right to citizens as its number one priority. Currently, the number of services offered stands at 153
from 14 departments. Out of 42.31 crore applications received till November, 2024, the system
has ensured that 42.11 crore are disposed of.

Bihar Right to Public Grievance Redressal Act, 2015 ; In most grievance redressal systems, the
procedures involve functionaries of the same institution where the grievances arise, therefore the
absence of an independent decision-making authority. Bihar is the first state in India and probably
the first government in the world to consider public grievance redressal as a legal right. Every
citizen has a right to time-bound (60 working days) grievance redressal. The Act covers the entire
state (45 Departments). An independent administrative structure of Public Grievance Redressal
Officers (PGROs) with quasi-judicial powers would take cognizance of the complaints. 16.75 lakh
grievances have been registered since implementation of the Act and 16.32 lakh of them have been
disposed of,

Bihar Government Servants Grievance Redressal System : It is a system in which service-
related and retiral benefit complaints are heard by Service Grievance Redressal Officers designated
in the district and departments in the presence of the complainant and the officer responsible for
redressal and the decision is taken in a time limit of 60 working days. As of November 2024, 11920
complaints have been filed under this system, and 10983 have been successfully disposed of. The
rest are under the process of hearing and disposal.

Waste Management : All waste collecting vehicles are GPS-enabled in Patna Municipal
Corporation. GPS ensures that garbage collection vehicles follow the shortest and most efficient
routes, saving time and fuel. It also ensures that drivers and workers complete their tasks without
deviation or misuse of resources. Vehicles can quickly address sanitation complaints or emergency
cleanups, minimizing health risks. It also ensures timely cleaning of critical areas such as hospitals,
markets, and residential zones.
