to 2020-21, there has been a slight decrease in the number of Child Development Project Officers
(CDPO) in the subsequent years. But, inversely, there is an increase in the number of Lady
Supervisors and Anganwadi Workers after 2020-21. Regarding Anganwadi Helpers, the number
of staffs working against the sanctioned posts, almost remains the same. According to Table13.5,
