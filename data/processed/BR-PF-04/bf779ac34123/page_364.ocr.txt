Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme (RDSS): The Central government has introduced the
Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme (RDSS), aimed at enhancing the operational effectiveness
and financial viability of DISCOMs through a reform-focused and outcome-oriented approach.

The objectives of the scheme are to:

(a) Improve the quality, reliability and affordability of power supply to consumers through a
financially sustainable and operationally efficient Distribution Sector,

(b) Reduce the AT&C losses to pan-India levels of 12-15 percent by 2024-25, and

(c) Reduce the gap between Average Cost of Supply (ACS) and Average Revenue Realised
(ARR) to zero by 2024-25 (Chart 8.12 and Chart 8.13).

A scheme amounting to Rs. 7081.05 crore has been sanctioned under the Loss Reduction
component of RDSS for NBPDCL and SBPDCL, out of which 60 percent of the amount is being
provided by the Central government as grant and the balance 40 percent is being arranged by the
State government. The State government has further sanctioned an additional amount of Rs. 224.00
crore for the unsanctioned part of the work under Information Technology/Operational
Technology (IT/OT). The scope of work under this scheme is — (a) Construction of Agriculture
feeders. (b) Bifurcation of long feeders, (c) Replacement of bare conductor of LT lines by Aerial
Bunched Cable (AB Cable), (d) High Voltage Distribution System (HVDS), (e) [T/OT, and (£)

Installation of Smart Meters in urban areas, These works are presently under progress.
