4.6 Udyog Mitra

Udyog Mitra is one of the important institutions ensuring ease of doing business in Bihar. Udyog
Mitra assists new entrepreneurs in setting up industries in Bihar by providing advice on project
profiles, among other services, In 2023-24, 2,153 visiting entrepreneurs benefited from the
services provided by Udyog Mitra. In addition, Udyog Mitra organises seminars, training
programs, and investors’ meetings, One such investors’ meet for the textile industry was organised
by Udyog Mitra in July 2024. To support various functions of Udyog Mitra, the State government
allocated Rs. 200.0 lakh in 2023-24, and the entire allocation was utilised in the financial year.

4.7 Chief Minister’s Schemes to Promote Entrepreneurship

To encourage youth from different sections of society, the State government has initiated various
schemes of self-employment. Currently, the Mukhyamantri Mahila Udyami Yojana, the
Mukhyamantri Yuva Udyami Yojana, the Mukhyamantri Scheduled Castes/Scheduled
Tribes/EBC Udyami Yojana, the Mukhyamantri Minority Udyami Yojana, and the Bihar Small
Entrepreneurs Yojana are operational in Bihar. Among all the schemes, the Mukhyamantri
Minority Udyami Yojana and the Bihar Small Entrepreneurs Yojana were launched in 2023-24. In
2023-24, the Bihar Small Enterprises Yojana was the most prominent scheme. Based on the Bihar
Caste-based Survey 2023, the State government has realised that there are a large number of poor
families/households living in Bihar. The Bihar Small Entrepreneurship Yojana targeted these poor
families/households and aimed to uplift their economic status. To implement the scheme, the State
government has allocated a budget of Rs. 250.0 crore in 2023-24 and Rs. 1000.0 crore in 2024-25.
The State government has also decided to provide additional funding in the event of receiving a
large number of applications from the economically weaker sections of the population. In
2024-25, a total expenditure of Rs. 100.0 crore was proposed, Out of Rs. 100.0 crore, an
expenditure of Rs. 20.0 crore was proposed for the Scheduled Caste-dominated areas and Rs. 2.0
crore for the Scheduled Tribe-dominated areas.
