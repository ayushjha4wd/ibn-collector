Digital Governance in Power Sector : The e-governance and digital process re-engineering
initiatives taken by BSPHCL has successfully laid the foundation for the forthcoming Al
revolution. DISCOMs are registering profit for the first time since their inception. AT&C losses
are dropping below 19.94 percent for 2023-24 with a net profit of Rs. 1,410 crore, consequently
reducing the tariff by 15 paise.

DISCOM Power Supply Operations & Revenue Management ; Power procurement upholds
more than 80 percent share of the total expenses incurred by the energy utilities in the state. To
curtail difficulties in decision-making, the SAMADHAN portal was developed as a central
integrator of national-level grid information systems and internal databases and has been designed
as an easy-to-use dashboard to boost up the real-time decision-making, The impacts of these
decisions are quantified in terms of money. Coupled with Energy Payment Prioritization
Monitoring System (EPPMS), these innovative developments have been a major contributor to the
DISCOM's profitability. SAMEEKSHA, SAMPURN, SCADA, SANGRAHAN and Raid & FIR
Management System RFMS are the other platforms which have been crafted to keep a check on
leakages and losses in distribution. They also help the field teams with participatory, accountable,
transparent, and quantified MIS systems.
