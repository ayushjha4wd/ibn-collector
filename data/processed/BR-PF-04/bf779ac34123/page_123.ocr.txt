Within Social Services, expenditure on Education, Sports, and Arts and Culture constitutes the
largest share. The expenditure on these heads has increased from Rs. 26,353 crore in 2019-20 to
Rs. 45,481 crore in 2023-24. Salary expenditure accounted for 16.7 percent of the revenue
expenditure, which was Rs. 40,890 crore in 2023-24. Additionally, the capital outlay for this sector
rose from 0.8 percent of the total expenditure in 2019-20 to 6.6 percent in 2023-24.
