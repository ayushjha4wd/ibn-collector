Under the Bihar Small Entrepreneurship Scheme, 2,02,014 applications were received in 2023-24.
Out of 2,02.014 applications, the Department of Industries has approved 40,102 applications, and
almost all of them have received benefits under this scheme. To provide financial support to 40,099
small entrepreneurs, the State government has spent Rs. 200.47 lakh in 2023-24. In addition to the
small entrepreneurship scheme, the women entrepreneurship scheme has received Rs. 70.95 lakh
against 1,614 approved applications. In 2023-24, the State government has spent Rs. 524.93 lakh
to provide financial support to 47,404 applicants across all the entrepreneurship schemes
