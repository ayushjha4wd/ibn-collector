Three stormwater drainage schemes have been approved for Patna, Bhagalpur, and Muzaffarpur,
with an estimated cost of Rs. 237.99 crore. The stormwater drainage projects in Bhagalpur and
Patna, specifically the section from Beur to Mithapur, have been completed. To date.
Rs. 109.20 crore has been spent on the implementation of these schemes.

Stormwater drainage projects were implemented in three cities, with a financial outlay of
Rs. 240.03 crores. Out of these three projects, two have been completed. Under the state plan,
work on the stormwater drainage scheme was initiated in 15 other cities, with a total administrative
approval of Rs. 859.63 crores, of which two projects have been completed, Under the Atmanirbhar
Bihar Saat Nishchay-2, stormwater drainage schemes were implemented in 13 cities, with an
administrative approval of Rs. 2,031.32 crore. The work on these projects is under various stages
of completion.

Under AMRUT 2.0, all municipal bodies have prepared City Water Balance Plans (CWBP) and
uploaded them to the AMRUT-2.0 portal. For the financial year 2024-25, an allocation of
Rs.1382.00 crore has been made under AMRUT 2.0 to facilitate the implementation of this
ambitious initiative.

In 2023-24, total financial allocations made for the water supply scheme (Phases 1 and 11), park
development, and drainage scheme was Rs. 2586.3 crore. Of this allocated fund, 89.7 percent was
spent on the water supply scheme (Phases | and IJ), implemented in 21 towns. The park
development scheme was executed in 20 towns across Bihar, with a total cost of Rs, 31.1 crore
(Table 10.14). The State government has allocated Rs. 30.48 crore for the same. As in some
instances, land-related issues prevented the State government from implementing the project,
resulting in the surrender of allocated funds. For the drainage scheme, the total allocation was
Rs. 234.3 crore, representing nine percent of the overall allocation under AMRUT. The town-wise
distribution of funds for the water supply schemes across AMRUT and AMRUT 2.0 is presented
