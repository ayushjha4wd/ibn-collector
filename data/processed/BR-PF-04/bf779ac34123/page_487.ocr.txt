of the families reside in rural areas and depend entirely on government schools.

Enrolment at Elementary Level

The status of total enrolment in elementary education during 2019-20 to 2023-24, for both primary
and upper primary classes, is presented in Table 12.13. The total enrolment at primary level was

level, the total enrolment is 56.22 lakh, among which 28.48 lakh are boys and 27.74 lakh are girls.
As a whole, the total enrolment, taking primary and upper primary together, stood at 168.3 Llakh,
of which 86.67 lakh are boys and 81.64 lakh are girls. The total enrolment for SC and ST students
at the elementary level stood at 33.50 lakh for SC and 3.26 lakh for ST students respectively. The
district-wise total enrolment is given in the Appendix Table A 12.17 (All students), A 12.18 (SC)
