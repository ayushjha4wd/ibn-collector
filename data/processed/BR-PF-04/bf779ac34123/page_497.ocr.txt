Recruitment

To ensure quality technical education and uphold transparency in the recruitment process, teachers
are appointed through the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC), Recently, 2,185 permanent
teachers have been recruited across various categories, including 1,458 for government
engineering colleges and 727 for government polytechnic institutes. In line with the state
government's policy, 35 percent of the seats are reserved for women candidates. As a result, 398
women faculty members have been recruited in government engineering colleges, and 261 in
government polytechnic institutes. This recruitment includes principals, professors. associate
professors, assistant professors, departmental heads, and lecturers. In addition to the teaching staff,
the recruitment process has already been initiated for non-teaching and support staff at the institute
level.

Courses in Emerging Technology

The courses in emerging technologies have been introduced across various engineering and
polytechnic institutes, These courses aim to boost students’ efficiency and productivity in their
specialized fields, ensuring they remain relevant and competitive in the job market. In addition to
traditional offerings, new courses in cutting-edge areas such as the Internet of Things (101),
Artificial Intelligence (AT), Machine Learning (ML), Data Science, Cybersecurity, Blockchain
Technology, Biomedical Robotic Engineering, Networking, Web Technologies, Image
Processing, Geo-Informatics, Geotechnical Engineering, and Transportation Engineering were
started from the academic session 2022-23 (Table 12.22 and Table 12.23). Furthermore, in
response to service demands within the construction industry, a Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch,)
course has been introduced at the Gaya Engineering College, beginning with the academic session
2023-24.
