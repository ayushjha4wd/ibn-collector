Flagship Schemes for Child Development

Mukhyamantri Balika/Balak Cycle Yojana

To increase the attendance and retention rate of students in higher classes, the Mukhyamantri
Balika Cycle Yojana was started in 2007-08 and Mukhyamantri Balak Cycle Yojana was started
in 2009-10. Under the schemes, an amount of Rs. 3000 is transferred via DBT mode to the
students to purchase a bicycle. This incentive amount is given to the students studying in Class
IX and who must have completed 75 percent of their attendance. In 2023-24, Rs. 141.58 crore
has been distributed to 4.72 lakh students for purchasing bicycles.

Mukhyamantri Balika /Balak Poshak Yojana

The Mukhyamantri Balika Poshak Yojana was started in 2008-09 for female students studying
in elementary schools (Class | to VIII) of the state. Under this scheme, based on 75 percent
attendance, the amount is transferred to the students. In 2023-24, with an expenditure of Rs.
282.22 crore, a total of 43.71 lakh girl students were benefitted. The Mukhyamantri Balak
Poshak Yojana, a similar scheme, was also started in 2008-09 to encourage the enrolment of
boys in elementary schools of the state. Similarly, in 2023-24, Rs. 228.90 crore was spent on
distributing uniforms among 37.70 lakh boy students.

Mukhyamantri Balika/Balak Protsahan Evam Chhatrayritti Yojana

The scholarship scheme for girls was started in 2009-10 and that for boys in 2015-16. Under
this, the girls and boys who pass their Tenth class Board Examination conducted by the Bihar
School Education Board in the first division gets an encouragement amount of Rs. 10,000. The
benefit of this scheme is availed by the girls of the General and BC (II) categories and boys of
the General category, including the Minority, The amount will be transferred to their bank
accounts. Under the scheme, students (boys and girls) of Class IX and X belonging to the
General category will get an amount of Rs, 1800 as a scholarship per year. In 2023-24,
Rs. 90.35 crore was distributed to 0.90 lakh students.

Bihar Shatabdi Mukhyamantri Balika Poshak Yojana

The scheme was started in 2011-12 to promote education among girls and bring them to the
mainstream. The benefit of the scheme is availed by girls studying in classes IX to XII. Under
this, Rs. 1500 is transferred to the bank account of the students for purchasing school uniform.
In 2023-24, Rs. 123.01 crore was distributed among 8.20 lakh girls.

Mukhyamantri Kishori Swasthya Yojana

The scheme was started in 2014-15 to sensitise girls regarding their health needs. Under this,
15, 300 is transferred to the bank account of the girl students of classes VII and XII. The amount
is transferred only when they complete 75 percent of their attendance in government schools.
In 2023-24, 9.47 lakh students got the benefit of the scheme and Rs. 28.40 crore was distributed
under the scheme.
