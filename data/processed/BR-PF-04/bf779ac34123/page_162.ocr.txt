Centrally-sponsored Schemes for Irrigation

Prime Minister Agricultural Irrigation Scheme (Per Drop More Crop): Micro-irrigation,
particularly drip irrigation, conserves water, reduces fertilizer use, minimizes pests and diseases,
and lowers production costs. It also enhances crop quality, helping farmers secure better prices
while preventing groundwater pollution and rapid water depletion. Under the Pradhan Mantri
Krishi Sinchai Yojana (Per Drop More Crop), the total spending was Rs. 3417.69 lakh in 2023-24,
The scheme covers all districts under the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana and aims to
implement micro-irrigation in 9,654 hectares during the same period.
