Solar Power Project at Water Bodies: Bihar is a state with limited land resources, but has
abundant solar potential. 50, BSPGCL finds various ways to harness solar energy in water
bodies, such as canals, dams and reservoirs, BSPGCL has identified Bikram Lakh at Patna
for the installation of a 2-~MW Solar Power Plant on land available alongside the (Chart Land)
Canal as a pilot project. The Water Resource Department has provided the No Objection
Certificate (NOC) for this project. BSPGCL has selected a RESCO or Renewable Energy
developer for the execution and operation of the project for 25 years. For this project, PPA
has been signed with M/s Naw Dharam Energy Private Limited. The entire project is
expected to be completed by March 2025. Additionally, 20-MW at five different locations
have been identified for developing solar generating parcels at Khatauna, Madhubani (10
MW on the Western Kosi main canal), Indrapuri, Rohtas (5 MW on the Western link canal),
Right Kamla Balan Embankment (2 MW at Tardih, Darbhanga), Left Kamla Balan
Embankment (2 MW at Madhupur, Madhubani) and Saran Main Canal (1 MW at
Gopalganj).

BSPGCL has also identified Phulwaria Dam in the Nawada district as an ideal location for
the installation of 10-MW floating solar power plant. This innovative project will serve a
dual purpose of nurturing aquatic life below the water surface while harnessing solar energy
above it, aligning with the mission for sustainable green energy (as per Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali
mission’s objective). The NOC has been obtained, from the Water Resources Department,
Government of Bihar for further processing. PPA has been done, and the work is in progress.
A similar plan is also in the pipeline for Durgawati Dam for a capacity of 10-MW solar
project. The NOC is being obtained from the Water Resources Department.
