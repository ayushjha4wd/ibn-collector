Domain Skilling: This programme was introduced to provide advanced professional training to
the workers who meet minimum eligibility according to course or scheme guidelines. BSDM
coordinated with 15 departments to design the training programme. In 2023-24, out of 15.470
enrolled trainees, 6,897 trainees could complete the training (Table 5.29), Further, 1,416 trainees

were certified, and 260 of them obtained jobs.

Recruit-Train-Deploy (RTD) Scheme: Under this scheme, the State government provides
training to candidates (aged 15-45 years) based on industry standards specific to industry needs.
BSDM funds the training expenses under this scheme. In 2023-24, this programme was conducted
from 15 centres, where 4,546 candidates were enrolled, and 3,307 candidates have completed their
training. Of 1,248 certified candidates, 479 candidates were offered jobs (Table 5.29), It must be

highlighted that out of every three certified candidates, one candidate received employment.

Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL): Within the framework of the National Skill Qualification
Framework (NSQF), RPL offers training to individuals (aged 18-59 years) with pre-existing
knowledge and skills. In 2023-24, this programme was conducted from 49 centres with 5,888
enrolled individuals (Table 5.29), Of these, 1,216 candidates have received certificates after

completion of their training.

Bihar State Certificate in Financial Accounting (BS-CFA): BSDM introduced the BS-CFA
course, which provides financial accounting training for aged 17-45 with educational qualification
of 12th grade. From 78 centres, 7,755 candidates have received training, and 4,267 trainees could
complete the training programme. Under the programme, 4,204 candidates have received
certificates (Table 5.29).
