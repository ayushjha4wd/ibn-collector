Inclusive Education for Children with Special Needs (CWSN)

The objective of Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan is to provide quality and life skills to CWSN, according
to their needs, with the non-disabled children of the age group 6-18 years in schools. It is to make
sure that no child is left out from the mainstream educational system. To achieve these objectives,
different approaches, substitutes, and strategies have been adopted by the State government, so
that CWSN will learn according to their talent. Some of the initiatives include:

Sports Events ; A Khel Mahotsay was held in every district of Bihar between late 2022 and
early 2023, The event took place on December 3, 2022 (World Disability Day), and January
4, 2023 (Louis Braille Day). Various activities such as drawing and painting, quiz
competitions, and musical performances offered children with special needs a chance to
showcase their talents even during the pandemic. The event promoted the inclusion of
children with disabilities and encouraged them to foster healthy competition, sportsmanship,
teamwork, and leadership skills,

Braille stationary material : For the help of CWSN, who are unable to read and write the
printed material, the State government has provided Braille printed material, Brallier, Braille
Slate, Abacus and teller frame, etc. and helped 813 students. Using these equipments,
students learn reading and writing, receive quality education and thereby become better
participants in regular class activities.
