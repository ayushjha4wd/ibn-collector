A comparison of the Deposits, Advances, and CD Ratio of Private Banks in Bihar on September
30, 2023 and September 30, 2024 is illustrated in Table 11.10. HDFC Bank stands out among
Private Sector Banks in Bihar in terms of the size of deposits collected and advances made,
followed by ICICI Bank and AXIS Bank. The advances by HDFC Bank in Bihar increased from
Rs. 18,463.8 crore in September 2023 to Rs. 22,776.4 crore in September 2024, marking a rise of
23.4 percent. Meanwhile, advances from AXIS Bank and ICICI Bank increased by 26.9 percent
and 16.1 percent during the same period.

The CD ratio of HDFC Bank grew by five percentage points, rising from 90.3 percent in September
2023 to 95.3 percent in September 2024, In contrast, AXIS Bank's CD ratio improved from 66,5
percent to 74.0 percent, while ICICI Bank's CD ratio rose from 90.4 percent to 93.1 percent.
Notably, RBL Bank reported the highest CD ratio among Private Sector Banks at 1047.8 percent
in September 2024, followed by IndusInd Bank at 231.3 percent and Bandhan Bank at 198.8
percent. Overall, the CD ratio for Private Sector Banks in Bihar was 104.2 percent as of September

2024, an increase of 2.1 percentage points compared to September 2023.

The Deposits, Advances, and CD Ratio of Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) in Bihar as of September
30, 2023 and September 30, 2024, are presented in Table 11.11. As of September 30, 2024, RRBs
in Bihar extended credit amounting to Rs. 24,099.8 crore and collected deposits totalling
Rs. 44,588.6 crore, This represents an 8.3 percent increase in deposits, while the credit extended
by these banks rose by 9.8 percent. Consequently, the CD (Credit to Deposit) ratio of RRBs in
Bihar increased from 59.9 percent in September 2023 to 60.8 percent in September 2024.
