One Nation, One Tariff

To enhance the financial viability of the DISCOMs, the State government has been advocating for
the implementation of a 'One Nation, One Tariff’ policy. With the State government having
transferred its generation units to NTPC, Bihar now relies predominantly on power supplies from
the Central government. However, this power is supplied to Bihar at a rate higher than what other
states pay. The simultaneous provision of subsidies to consumers, combined with the higher cost
of purchasing power from the Central government, places significant financial strain on the
DISCOMs. Previously, the Central government defended the differences in power tariffs across
the country by citing varying transmission congestion in different areas. However, given that the
country has now synchronized its power grids, there are no barriers to implementing a rational
‘One Nation, One Tariff’ policy. By complementing the benefits achieved through the installation
of smart meters by DISCOMs with a uniform tariff from the Central government, the financial
health of the DISCOMs can gradually improve, Chart 8.14 shows the status of power purchase
and sale.
