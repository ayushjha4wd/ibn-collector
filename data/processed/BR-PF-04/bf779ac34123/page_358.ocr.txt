8.5 Distribution Companies (DISCOMs)

The two DISCOMs in Bihar have implemented several schemes to expand and strengthen their
distribution networks. These schemes include construction of new Power Sub-Stations, HT/LT
lines and Distribution Sub-Stations. There has been augmentation of the capacity of existing Power
Sub-Stations and Distribution Sub-Stations and implementation of Information Technology in
revenue management and other activities of the power distribution sector. The existing old and
damaged conductors have also been replaced for smooth supply of power.
