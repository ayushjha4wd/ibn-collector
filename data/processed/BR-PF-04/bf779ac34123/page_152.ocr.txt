3.5 Agricultural Inputs

This section deals with the availability and usage of inputs for agricultural production.
Furthermore, we discuss the schemes and programmes of the State government and central

Seed

The crop yield is directly determined by the quality and timely availability of seeds. The mismatch
between the demand and supply of seeds in Bihar has decreased over the years. Given the cereal-
dominated cropping pattern in Bihar, the gap between the requirement and supply of rice seeds has
declined significantly in the last three years. For instance, in 2021-22, the demand for rice seeds
was 495 thousand quintals, and the supply was 387,2 thousand quintals, resulting in a shortfall of
107.8 thousand quintals. The situation has improved significantly in 2023-24, when the gap
between the supply of and demand for rice seed has declined to 64.6 thousand quintals, For wheat,
maize, and other crops, the supply of seeds was greater than their respective demands. The seed
replacement rate (SRR), an indicator of improved yield, has improved in Bihar. For kharif rice,
