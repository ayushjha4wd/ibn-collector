Fertiliser

In Bihar, fertiliser use has been increasing over the years. Total fertiliser use was 3085.7 thousand
tonnes in 2021-22 and that has increased to 3867.1 thousand tonnes in 2023-24. This shows an
increase of 25,3 percent over the two-year period. Of the total fertiliser used, almost two-third was
urea. This indicates the overuse of urea during crop production. The data also suggest inter-season
variation in fertiliser use. In 2023-24, the consumption of fertiliser per hectare of land in the kharif
season was 177.7 kg and 211.1 kg in the rabi season, showing an inter-season gap of 33.4 kg.

The State government promotes integrated nutrient management, emphasizing the balanced
application of organic, biological, and chemical fertilizers, with a recommended NPK ratio of
4:2:1, A state soil-testing programme supports this initiative. In 2022-23, the NPK ratio was
14.1:4.7:1, and 13.4:4.1:1 in 2023-24. Furthermore, fertilizer consumption per hectare has
decreased from 207.0 kg in 2022-23 to 195.6 kg in 2023-24. District-wise details of different
fertilizers’ consumption are presented in Table A 3.11.
