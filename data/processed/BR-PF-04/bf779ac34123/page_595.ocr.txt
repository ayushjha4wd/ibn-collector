average, Bihar's hot summers (40-42°C) and mild winters (7-8°C) define its humid subtropical
climate. Chart 14.2 gives the annual minimum, maximum and average temperature data. The
maximum temperature (46.4 ९ Celsius) was recorded during 2012, while minimum (1.1° Celsius)
was registered during 2013. The 13 years annual average is 23.6° Celsius.
