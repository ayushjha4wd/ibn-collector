= Safar Suvidha

To enhance the safety of women and instill a sense of security among those travelling in Bihar,
the “Surakhsit Safar Suvidha” initiative was launched by the Police (Home) Department. This
initiative allows for the registration and real-time tracking of journeys through the Emergency
Response Support System (ERSS), providing a reliable support system for women traveling
alone or with family. The initiative was rolled out across the state in September 2024. Bihar is
the third state to implement such a scheme, following Telangana and Haryana.

As of December 2024, the initiative has successfully assisted in all 70 requests received since
its launch. By registering for this service, women’s travel can be tracked, offering an additional
layer of safety and ensuring prompt assistance in case of emergencies. This service is
accessible by dialing 112, the ERSS number for Bihar.
