CHAPTER - IV
ENTERPRISES SECTOR

Abstract

This chapter explores the trajectory of industrial growth in Bihar, emphasizing the dual role
of large-scale industries and small, informal enterprises in shaping the state's economic
landscape. Despite its potential, Bihar faces unique challenges in fostering industrialization.
However, recent government initiatives focus on attracting large investments and promoting
entrepreneurship. Utilizing data from the Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) and the Annual
Survey of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises (ASUSE), the chapter assesses the performance
and challenges of both the formal and informal industrial sectors in Bihar. The secondary
sector, particularly construction and manufacturing, has been a major driver of Bihar's
industrial growth, with construction contributing 50.2 percent of the Gross State Domestic
Product (GSDP) of the secondary sector in 2022-23. The manufacturing sector followed with
a contribution of 37 percent. Bihar has seen significant growth in ‘fixed capital and industrial
output’, with notable improvements in productivity and investment. The state's industrial
growth is evident in the increase in fixed capital per factory and the value of output, surpassing
the national average in certain performance metrics. Furthermore, the informal sector in
Bihar, dominated by small-scale, labour-intensive enterprises, accounts for a large portion of
the state's non-agricultural establishments. The chapter highlights the importance of fostering
this sector through entrepreneurship and skill development pragrammes aimed at enhancing
productivity. The Bihar Industrial Investment Promotion Policy 2016 aims to foster industrial
growth and employment by leveraging the state's sectoral advantages. It identifies priority
sectors such as food processing, tourism, IT, renewable energy, and healthcare. The State
Investment Promotion Board (SIPB) facilitates investments, leading to substantial financial
inflows and job creation. The government has launched various schemes like the
Mukhyamantri: Udyami Yojanas to promote entrepreneurship, alongside sector-specific
policies for textiles, biofuels, and startups, Kev sectors like mining and tourism have also
contributed significantly to the state's revenue. Bihar’s integrated approach has significantly
accelerated sustainable industrial growth, economic development, and job creation.

Introduction

The industrial sector is a cornerstone of long-term economic development, driving sustainable
growth, creating employment, and producing goods that cater to both domestic and global markets.
Bihar stands at a pivotal moment in its industrial evolution. Despite its rich history and potential,
the state faces unique challenges in terms of industrial growth. However, recent government
initiatives have emphasized the role of industrialization in Bihar’s economic transformation, with
a particular focus on attracting large-scale investments and fostering entrepreneurship.
