Table 4.12 suggests that, at the all-India level, there was a stark difference in the performances
between OAEs and HWEs in rural and urban areas. Furthermore, the urban market establishments
outperformed rural market establishments in terms of annual GVA per establishment. In rural
India, the annual GVA of an OAE was only 13 percent of the annual GVA of an HWE. A similar
pattern of differences in the annual GVA of HWE and OAE establishments of rural areas was
observed across all the states, In Bihar, the annual GVA of a rural OAE was Rs, 1.08,690.0 and
that of an HWE was Rs. 8,07,420.0, demonstrating a marked difference between the annual GVA
of OAEs and HWEs. It can also be noted that the OAEs and HWEs in rural Bihar were adding
more value, on an average, than those at the rural all-India level. Apparently, the reason for the
lower annual GVA of OAEs compared to the annual GVA of HWEs of rural areas was the
difference in the scale of production and the difference in the level of efficiency, especially in
terms of labour productivity. Table 4.13 provides information on the annual GVA per worker for
market establishments, which is an indicator of labour productivity. The data suggest that the
annual GVA per worker was higher for HWEs than for OAEs in rural and urban areas.
Additionally, the annual GVA per worker for urban market establishments was greater than that
for rural market establishments. In Bihar, the annual GVA per worker of OAEs in rural areas was
Rs. 93,558.0 compared to Rs. 1,08,944.0 of OAEs in urban areas. Moreover, comparing with the
all-India average, the annual GVA per worker of OAEs in rural Bihar was higher than the average
annual GVA per worker of OAEs in rural India. In rural Bihar, the difference in the annual GVA
