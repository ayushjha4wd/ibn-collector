Table 2.5 displays the Gross Fiscal Deficit (GFD) figures for the major states in India from
2021-22 to 2023-24. The figures for 2023-24 are based on Revised Estimates (RE), except for
Bihar, for which actual numbers are available when preparing this document and included in the
table. The data for 2024-25 are the Budget Estimates (BE), It is observed that Bihar’s GFD has
significantly decreased from Rs. 44,823 crore in 2022-23 to Rs, 35,660 crore in 2023-24, In
contrast, the GFD figures have increased for most of the major states in India during this period,
with the exceptions of Bihar, Punjab, and Haryana. Among these states, the reductions in GFD
were 20.4 percent for Bihar, 10.7 percent for Punjab, and 1.2 percent for Haryana in 2023-24 over
the previous year.
