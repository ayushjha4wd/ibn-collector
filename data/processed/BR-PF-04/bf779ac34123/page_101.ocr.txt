The State government is required to maintain a minimum cash balance of Rs, 1.73 crore with the
Reserve Bank of India (RBI). The government can finance the gap by obtaining Ordinary and
Special Ways and Means Advances or Overdratts if the balance falls below this minimum on any
given day. The limit for Ordinary Ways and Means Advances to the State government is Rs. 425
crore, effective April 1, 2006. The RBI occasionally revises the limit for Special Ways and Means
Advances. As a result of effective cash management, the State government has successfully
maintained its minimum daily balance requirement with the RBI throughout the year and did not
need to utilise any Ordinary or Special Ways and Means Advances.

The State government extends guarantees on principal repayment and interest payments on loans
raised by Statutory Corporations, Government Companies, Local Bodies and Other Institutions.
During the year 2023-24, the State issued guarantees of Rs. 26,359.34 crore and Rs. 7.75 crore
were receivable towards guarantee commission from the Bihar State Food and Civil Supplies
Corporation Limited. As a result, the amount of total outstanding guarantee was Rs, 28,040.95
crore (Principal: Rs. 26,715.26 crore and Interest: Rs. 1,325.69 crore) as on 31 March 2024, There
were outstanding guarantees of Rs. 25,939.25 crore (Principal: Rs, 25,256.75 crore and
Interest: Rs, 682.50 crore) as on April 1, 2023. During the year 2023-24, the State government
issued guarantee of Rs. 26,359.34 crore.

The power sector in Bihar, which includes the Bihar State Power Transmission Company Ltd.,
South Bihar Power Distribution Company Ltd., and North Bihar Power Distribution Company
Ltd., accounts for the largest share of outstanding guarantees provided by the State government in
Bihar. By the end of the financial year 2023-24, the total outstanding guarantees to the power
sector, comprising both capital and interest, increased by 33.9 percent, thus rising to
Rs. 18,164.7 crore from Rs. 13,569.9 crore at the end of 2022-23. Additionally, the outstanding
guarantees extended by the State government to the Bihar State Food and Civil Supplies
Corporation also saw an increase, rising by 8.8 percent from Rs. 8,653.0 crore at the end of
2022-23 to Rs. 9,417.6 crore by the end of 2023-24.
