Institute of Driving and Traffic Research (IDTR), Aurangabad and Patna: It is a joint venture
between the Department of Transport, Government of Bihar and Maruti Suzuki India Ltd. A total
of 1,18,881 Light Motor Vehicle DL tests are done at IDTR Patna, while Aurangabad has executed
7519 LMV DL tests till September, 2024. In totality, 8678 violators, 4329 traffic police, 487
BSRTC bus drivers* and 349 flying squads have been trained by these institutes,

Bus-Stops Construction: Constructing bus stops in rural areas with a focus on safe boarding and
deboarding of passengers is an important step towards improving road safety and public
transportation infrastructure. In three phases, a total of 1582 Bus Stops are proposed for
construction, out of which 929 have been constructed till date. The design encompasses prioritizing
the safety of passengers and pedestrians, considering the covered waiting area, raised platform,
ramp and handrails and seating facility for passengers.

Handheld Devices: District Transport Officers, Motor Vehicle Inspectors and Enforcement
Officers of Transport Department and Police issue e-challans using nearly 2000 handheld devices,
gradually replacing manual challans, This transition offers multiple benefits, including improved
efficiency, real-time monitoring, digital record-keeping, transparency, convenient payment

options, faster resolution, and behavior modification.

Compensation in cases of Hit-and-Run Road Accidents: It is important to not only focus on
reducing accidents but also on providing support to those who have been affected by them, The
Transport Department is taking proactive steps to ensure seamless compensation for victims of
road accidents through rigorous follow-up with the General Insurance Corporation (GIC). This
initiative provides monetary support of Rs. 2.0 lakh in case of death and Rs. 50 thousand in case
of grievous injury. A total of 3516 persons have got compensation till September 2024.

CNG and Electric Vehicles: The department has taken various initiatives to promote EV or CNG
based vehicles. A rebate of 50 percent on road tax is given by the State government for battery-

operated vehicles including e-rickshaws. Fuel CNG Stations have been established across Bihar.
