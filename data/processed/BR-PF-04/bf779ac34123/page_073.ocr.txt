1.8 Outline of Bihar Economic Survey 2024-25

The Bihar Economic Survey 2024-25 offers a detailed picture of numerous facets of the state's
economy, providing a detailed continuation of the introduction regarding the current status of
economic activities in Bihar outlined in this chapter. This comprehensive Economic Survey relies
on a robust set of data derived from secondary sources, meticulously compiled by an array of
government departments. Furthermore, it incorporates data and insights from various publications
by both State and Central government institutions, ensuring a well-rounded perspective on the
economic landscape.

In addition to statistical data, the survey includes brief reports that highlight recent policy
interventions enacted by the State government. These initiatives are critical in steering Bihar
toward its overarching development objectives. The Economic Survey strongly emphasises
granularity by presenting relevant data in disaggregated form whenever possible, allowing for a
thorough understanding of economic trends not just at the state level but extending to the districts.

The current Economic Survey (2024-25) is organized into 14 chapters, each focusing on different
aspects of the Bihar economy. Chapter-l, titled ‘Bihar Economy: An Overview,’ comprehensively
summarises the entire survey. The remaining 13 chapters examine the status and growth patterns
of various elements of Bihar's socio-economic development. These chapters include: State
Finances (Chapter-I]); Agriculture and Allied Sectors (Chapter-II]); Enterprises Sector
(Chapter-lV); Labour, Employment and Skill (Chapter-V); Physical Infrastructure (Chapter-V1);
E-Governance (Chapter-V1); Power Sector (Chapter-VIII); Rural Development (Chapter-LX);
Urban Development (Chapter-X); Financial Institutions (Chapter-XI); Human Development
(Chapter-XII); Child Development (Chapter-XII]); and, Environment, Climate Change and
Disaster Management (Chapter-XIV). This structure provides a detailed insight into the various
challenges and opportunities within the Bihar economy.

The overview of the Bihar economy, based on recent aggregate data on economic activities
throughout the state, is presented in Chapter-I. This chapter also presents disaggregated data up to
the district level that is relevant to its themes, wherever available. Chapter-IL provides a detailed
discussion of the State government's finances. The State government has consistently increased
its spending on improving essential infrastructure to boost economic activities and various aspects
of the social sector. Despite this growth in expenditure, the management of state finances has

remained sustainable.
