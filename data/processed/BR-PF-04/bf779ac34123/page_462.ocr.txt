SIDBI Cluster Development Fund (SCDF)

The SIDBI Cluster Development Fund (SCDF) has been established to enhance basic
infrastructure in the state's Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSME) clusters. The SCDF
supports funding for three main categories of activities: (1) Industrial and Agri-allied sectors within
the MSME ecosystem, (ii) Social Sector Projects in and around MSME clusters, and (iii)
Connectivity initiatives to improve access to MSME clusters. Loans provided under this fund have
an interest rate that is 1.5 percent lower than the Bank rate.
