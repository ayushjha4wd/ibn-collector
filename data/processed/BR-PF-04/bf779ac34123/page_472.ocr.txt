In addition to LEB, there are six other health indicators for which comparable data is available:
Crude Birth Rate (CBR), Infant Mortality Rate (IMR), Child Mortality Rate (CMR), Under-Five
Mortality Rate (USMR), Neo-Natal Mortality Rate (NMR), and Peri-Natal Mortality Rate (PMR).
Table 12.3 presents important data for these indicators for both Bihar and India, covering the years
2015 and 2020. The Crude Birth Rate (CBR) is consistently higher in Bihar; for instance, in 2020,
it was recorded at 25.5 per 1000 person for Bihar compared to 19.5 for India, reflecting a difference
of 6.0 percentage points. However, over the period from 2015 to 2020, the gap between the CBR
in Bihar and that in India has narrowed slightly, When examining the IMR, it is notable that the
state figure has considerably dropped from 42 in 2015 to 27 in 2020. In contrast, the decline for
India was less pronounced, decreasing from 37 in 2015 to 28 in 2020. By 2020, the IMRs for rural
and urban areas in Bihar stood at 27 and 25, respectively, while the corresponding figures for India
were 31 for rural and 19 for urban. This significant reduction in IMR can be attributed to the
concentrated efforts of the State government. Concerning the Child Mortality Rate (CMR), it saw
a decrease of 5.1 percentage points in Bihar, falling from 12.0 in 2015 to 6.9 in 2020, In contrast,
India experienced a smaller reduction of 2.0 percentage points, from 10.0 in 2015 to 8.0 in 2020.
Concerning the other three indicators, Bihar demonstrates notable declines of 18 and 7 percentage
points, respectively. In comparison, this decrease for India was lesser than that of Bihar by 11 and
5 percentage points, respectively. The performance of these indicators in both rural and urban areas
followed a similar trend.
