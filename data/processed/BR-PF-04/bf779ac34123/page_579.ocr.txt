Pre-School Education Programme

The AWCs offer pre-school education to children aged 3 to 6 years. Those enrolled in these centers
receive snacks and hot, cooked meals. These meals are prepared in accordance with the caloric,
protein, and micronutrient requirements specified by the National Food Security Act of 2013. The
typical enrolment in an AWC ranges from approximately 35 to 40 children. The Anganwadi
Worker (AWWs) is tasked with conducting the pre-school sessions at the centre. The ECCE
curriculum is implemented with the assistance of the Anganwadi Worker and the Anganwadi
Helper. As of October 2024, 5.35 million children are enrolled across 115,009 AWCs in Bihar.

Pre-School Education Kit

It is critical to make available teaching kits and ensure its usage to quality teaching learning
processes in AWCs. Pre-school kit at the cost of Rs. 3000 per centre is provided to 1,15,009 AWCs
of the state. Items for all domains, for example cognitive, socio emotional, foundational literacy
and numeracy and cultural and artistic development, are procured for its use in teaching and
learning process. Necessary steps have been taken to procure items those are safe to use and long
lasting. ECCE curriculum Adharshila is shared with the district to be followed at AWCs which

Child Assessment Cards

Child Assessment Cards have been created to evaluate the learning outcomes of children aged 3
to 6 years. The learning indicators have been established following the National Curriculum
Framework, To facilitate the implementation of the assessment cards, age-appropriate higher-order
skills have been integrated into the Poshan Bhi Padhai Bhi report card.
