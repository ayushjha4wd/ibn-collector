11.5 Non-Performing Assets of Banks

Non-Performing Assets (NPA) refer to loans or advances from banks and financial institutions that
are no longer generating income. An asset is classified as non-performing when the borrower fails
to pay the principal and interest for at least 90 days. In recent years, the reduction in NPAs of
banks in Bihar has shown consistent improvement, This reduction in NPAs can be attributed to the
collaborative efforts of both the banks and the government. The government has supported banks
in recovering NPAs through the prompt execution of cases under the SARFAES] Act and various
certificate cases. Meanwhile, banks have focused on enhancing their asset quality by issuing better

loans and advances.

The bank group-wise percentage of Non-Performing Assets (NPA) in Bihar for 2023 and 2024 is
illustrated in Chart 11.13. The NPA of commercial banks has decreased from 7.5 percent in March
2023 to 5.9 percent in March 2024. Likewise, there has been a significant improvement in the NPA
of Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) in Bihar, which has fallen from 30.4 percent in March 2023 to
23.8 percent in March 2024, representing a reduction of 6.6 percentage points. Although Small
Finance Banks hold a small share of total advances in the state, they reported the lowest NPA at
1.9 percent as of March 2024.
