Ongoing Ambitious Projects of BRPNNL

Double-Deck Flyover at Ashok Rajpath: BRPNNL has been assigned work for construction
of an ambitious project of Rs. 422.00 crore on Ashok Rajpath in Patna. The alignment of this
Double-Deck Flyover stretches from Kargil Chowk to NIT More via PMCH. It is the busiest
road having medical and educational institutions along its side. All types of vehicles (light,
heavy and commercial) ply through this stretch, often resulting in severe congestion on the
road, posing extreme difficulties to commuters. Therefore, for minimum land to be acquired
in such stretches, construction of a Double-Deck Flyover is only the best remedy to overcome
the heavy traffic congestion on Ashok Rajpath.

The total length of the Lower Deck of the flyover is 1.5 km, including the ascending ramp near
Patna College and descending ramp near BN College. The total length of the Upper Deck of
the flyover is 2.2 kms, including the ascending ramp near Kargil Chowk and descending ramp
near Patna University and Science College. The proposed bridge project will help overpass the
traffic coming from Gandhi Maidan and will connect directly to PMCH through three Multi-
Level Car Parks (MLCPs). The flyover will split into two separate routes — (i) leading towards
Patna University Gate that will connect to Krishna Ghat and provide direct connectivity to
Ganga Path, and (ii) leading towards NIT more.

Double-Deck Flyover in Chhapra: BRPNNL has been assigned work for construction of a
prestigious project in Chhapra town of Saran District under the Central Road Investment Fund
(CRIF) at an estimated cost of Rs. 411.3 crore. The alignment stretches from Bhikhari Thakur
Chowk to Bus Stand via Gandhi Chowk, Mauna Chowk and Municipality Chowk, It is the
busiest commercial location of Chhapra, where all types of vehicles, coming from local area
as well as outside of Chhapra, ply.

The Lower Deck of the flyover (total length of 2.8 kms) (including one ascending Leg/Ramp
of 285-metre length proposed at Gandhi Chowk for vehicles coming from Garkha,
Muzaffarpur sides) starts from Police Station and ends near Rajendra Sarowar via Gandhi
Chowk and Municipality Chowk. This Deck may be used by the vehicles coming from Ara,
Hazipur and Patna sides to go towards Chhapra Collectoriate, Bus Stand and Siwan. The
Flyover would benefit the neighbouring Uttar Pradesh too and would boost trade and economy.

settlement is necessary to utilize the fullest development opportunity in that area. In order to boost
the rural economy and provide hassle-free traffic movement, the State government has strategically
