Population Projection for Bihar

Bihar is a densely populated state, and the changes occurring within its population critically
influence the overall population dynamics of the country. Therefore, projecting the population is
essential for both the state and the country. It is also important to understand their respective
populations to formulate policies at the district level. However, there are currently no official
projections available for district populations. In light of this, the current survey aims to provide
population estimates for each district in Bihar for the years 201 1 to 2041. The projections are based
on the annual population growth rates of Bihar, as estimated in the Economic Survey of India
2018-19, Bihar’s population is anticipated to rise from 1041 lakh in 2011 to 1230 lakh in 2021,
reflecting a 1.82 percent annual increase, and then to 1395 lakh in 2031, which represents a 1.34
percent annual increase, and finally to 1534 lakh in 2041, marking a 1.00 percent annual increase.
