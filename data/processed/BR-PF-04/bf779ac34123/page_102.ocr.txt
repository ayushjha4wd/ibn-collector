2.5 Debt Management

This section provides an overview of the debt dynamics of the State government, focusing on
borrowings and outstanding liabilities over the past five years. Table 2.8 presents the total
outstanding liabilities of the State government, excluding guarantees, and details the composition
of these liabilities for the period from 2019-20 to 2023-24. The budgeted estimate for 2024-25 is
also included in the table. As of March 31, 2024, the outstanding debt of the State government
amounted to Rs. 3,32,741 crore, which is 38,9 percent of the State’s Gross State Domestic Product
(GSDP).

Of the total outstanding debt at the end of the 2023-24 fiscal year, 84.2 percent was as public debt,
which includes internal debt and loans and advances from the Government of India. Within the
public debt, 84.3 percent came from the sources of internal debt, while 15.7 percent was from loans
and advances from the Central government. The two major components of internal debt are market
loans and loans from financial institutions. As of 2023-24, the outstanding liability from internal
