Neera Intervention: Launched in 2017-18 in partnership with the Industries Department,
Government of Bihar, JEEVIKA's Neera initiative promotes the production and sale of
Neera, a traditional drink made from palm sap, along with by-products like jaggery and peda,
By 2018, it was scaled across all 38 districts of Bihar. As of September 2024, 1.53 crore litres
of Neera were collected, and 1.48 crore litres were sold, reflecting high demand, robust
operations and market linkage. Under this intervention, 16,200 toddy tapper trainers were
trained and provided with licenses to maintain quality standards. Additionally, 516 Neera
Producer Groups were formed, boosting collective production and rural incomes. Producers
were also enrolled in social security schemes like Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana
(PMJJBY) and Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY), providing life and
accident insurance, ensuring livelihood enhancement alongside social security.

Custom Hiring Centers (CHCs) and Village Tool Banks: JEEVIKA, in collaboration with
the Agriculture Department, operates 513 CHCs across Bihar to provide farmers with
essential machinery for efficient harvesting and post-harvesting activities, These centers
address labour shortages and enhance agricultural productivity through the timely
performance of agricultural operations. An upgraded CHC mobile app simplifies machine
booking, optimizes logistics, and ensures financial transparency. By 2023-24, 55,852
farmers were registered on the CHC portal, with 53,727 accessing services, bridging resource
gaps and promoting mechanized farming for smallholder farmers.

Agri-entrepreneurs Initiative: This initiative empowers rural youth by training them as
Agri Entrepreneurs (AEs) to provide essential agricultural services to local farmers. AEs
supply quality inputs, offer crop advisory services, facilitate financial transactions, and
connect farmers in markets, enhancing productivity and profitability. Each AE supports 200-
300 farmers across 3-4 villages, leveraging digital tools to streamline agricultural operations.
