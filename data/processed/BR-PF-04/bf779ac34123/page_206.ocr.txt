Across India, 10.96 crore workers were employed in unincorporated non-agricultural enterprises
in 2022-23, About 5.23 crore workers were working in rural areas, and 5.72 crore workers were
working in urban areas, In ASUSE, the workers were classified as working owners, hired workers,
unpaid family members, and others. In rural India, OAEs and HWEs have engaged 3.93 crore and
1.31 crore workers, respectively. A similar distribution of workers between OAEs and HWEs was
observed in urban India. In Bihar, 59 lakh workers were working in unincorporated
non-agricultural enterprises in 2022-23. Out of 59 lakh workers, 43.6 lakh were employed in rural
establishments. Furthermore, out of 43.6 lakh workers engaged in establishments in rural Bihar,
68 percent were working in OAEs, while the remaining 32 percent worked in HWEs. About 16.6
lakh workers were employed in unincorporated non-agricultural enterprises in urban Bihar.
