In 2023-24, the total outlay for eco-tourism and park development was Rs. 100.00 crore,
constituting 21.6 percent of the total outlay of the Department of Environment, Forest, and Climate
Change. In 2023-24, the financial allocation for park development was Rs. 87.2 crore. The Patna
Park division has received the largest allocation of Rs. 15.97 crore, followed by the Nalanda
division (Rs. 11.33 crore). Overall, the fund utilisation rate was high at 96.1 percent considering

all the divisions.
