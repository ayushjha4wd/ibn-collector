Primary Deficit : The Primary Deficit, defined as the gross fiscal deficit minus interest
payments, is a crucial indicator for assessing the vulnerability of state finances. Interest
payments made by the State government are related to liabilities incurred in the past.
Therefore, the Primary Deficit helps evaluate the impact of the current policies of the State
government on its finances. In the fiscal year 2023-24, the Primary Deficit of the State
government decreased by 39.1 percent, dropping to Rs. 18,054 crore from Rs. 29,640 crore
in 2022-23, indicating a positive development in the management of state finances.

Revenue Deficit ; The deficit in the revenue account of the State government is calculated
as the difference between receipt and expenditure in the revenue account. Due to prudent
financial management, the State government achieved a revenue surplus of Rs. 2,833 crore

in 2023-24, in contrast to a revenue deficit of Rs. 11,288 crore in the 2022-23.
