Under the Agri Investment Promotion Policy, makhana production units have been given credit-
linked capital subsidies to increase production capacity and to improve efficiency in the production
process. Provisions of the additional subsidy were made for entrepreneurs of disadvantaged social
groups (such as Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), Extremely Backward Class (EBC),
women, and differently abled).

Under the Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises Scheme, makhana
was identified as ‘one district one product.’ This scheme benefits makhana producers in various
ways: providing credit-linked capital subsidy, seed capital to SHG members, support for the
common infrastructure, support for incubation centers, branding and marketing support. The
support is limited to 50 percent of the total expenditure.

To promote tea in the Kishanganj district, the State government initiated the Tea Development
Scheme. This scheme offers a 50 percent subsidy for new and existing tea estates, including
support for mechanisation. To promote the holistic growth of the tea sector, a Center of Excellence
for Tea is proposed to be established in the Kishanganj district. Under the Fourth Agriculture Road
Map. Rs. 4,950.83 lakh has been approved for 2023-24 to 2024-25, of which Rs. 4,024.58 lakh is
allocated for 2023-24.
