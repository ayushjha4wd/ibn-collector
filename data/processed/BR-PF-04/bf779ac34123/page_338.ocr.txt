7.7 Green Initiatives

The Department of Environment, Forest and Climate Change has adopted several
e-Governance initiatives to enhance its efficiency, transparency and public engagement. These
programmes leverage technology to streamline processes and support sustainable development.

Forest Management Information System (FMIS) : This system is a micro-level approach of
managing the Forest Plantations, Nurseries, Welfare Schemes covered under Joint Forest
Management, Forest Offences, Wildlife Activities, Accounting-Budgeting, Human Resource
Management System and Eco-Tourism Development in the state through ICT use of integrated
Web, GIS and Mobile technologies. The project implementation covers all 8 circles, 28 divisions
and 110 range offices of the department. Several e-governance projects have been implemented
by the Department.
