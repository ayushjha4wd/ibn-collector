Furthermore, daily milk collection per working dairy has increased in dairy cooperative societies
of all the unions and projects. In 2022-23, COMFED has collected 81.4 kg milk per day per
functional dairy cooperative society and that has increased to 110.9 kg in 2023-24.

The expansion of retail outlets continues in Bihar. Between 2022-23 and 2023-24, the number of
retail outlets increased by 8.8 percent. Sudha, the marketing brand of COMFED, sells various
value-added products also. Table 4.22 shows that among all the products, the sales of dahi, ice
cream, ghee, and gulab jamun have increased between 2022-23 and 2023-24.

Services such as artificial insemination, vaccination, de-worming, seed distribution, and cattle feed
distribution are also provided by COMFED., The number of vaccinations increased by 35 percent
between 2022-23 and 2023-24 (Table 4.22), Seed distribution and cattle feed distribution also
increased significantly between 2022-23 and 2023-24.
