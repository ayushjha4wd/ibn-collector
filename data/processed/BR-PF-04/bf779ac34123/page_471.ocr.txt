12.2 Health Status

Health and socio-economic developments are so closely entwined that it is impossible to achieve
progress on one count, without registering it on the other. Providing equitable, affordable, and
high-quality healthcare services to all citizens, with a particular focus on marginalized and
vulnerable sections, is the main objective of the government. The healthcare network in the state
is well-developed and includes primary, secondary and tertiary care. In the recent past, there has
been a tremendous improvement in the quality of healthcare services in Bihar. This is illustrated
by the significant improvement in healthcare indicators such as Life Expectancy at Birth (LEB),
Infant Mortality Rate (IMR), and Maternal Mortality Rate (MMR) over the period. This section
will present the relevant information on various initiatives taken by the State government to

improve the health services in the state.

Selected Health Indicators in Bihar

Over the years, there has been a significant improvement in various health parameters of the state.
Life Expectancy at Birth (LEB) is one of them, which is a proxy indicator for improvements in
nutrition, education, sanitation, and knowledge of diseases. LEB reflects the average number of
years a newborn is expected to survive under the current mortality schedule. The LEB for Bihar
has significantly changed from 65.8 in 2006-10 to 69.5 years in 2016-20, registering an increase
of 3.7 years in a decade, as depicted in Table 12.2. In 2016-20, the life expectancy of females and
that of males in Bihar were almost equal at 69 years. In the case of India, the LEB for females was
higher at 71.4 years compared to males at 68.6 years. The LEB of Bihar in 2016-20 has increased
by 3.5 years in the rural and 4.0 years in the urban areas as compared to 2006-10, For India as a
whole, LEB in 2016-20 has increased by 4.0 years for males and 3.7 years for females over 2006-
10. Similarly, the LEB for rural areas has increased by 3.7 years and for urban areas by 3.6 years
over 2006-10.
