Agro- Forestry- Other Species Scheme: Under this agro-forestry scheme, other species
like Teak, Mahogany, Jamun, Semal, Gamhar, Kadam, Eucalyptus, etc. have been planted
on farmer's field. It has been operational since 2012-13. Saplings are made available to the
entrepreneurs/farmers/beneficiaries from the local nurseries of the Department of
Environment, Forest, and Climate Change at a price of Rs. 10 per sapling during July-
September. An incentive amount is also given to the farmers for taking care of the plants.
After three years, there is a provision to pay an incentive amount of Rs. 60 per plant and a

reserve price of Rs, 10, if the survival rate is 50 percent or more.

Chief Minister Private ‘Poplar Nursery’ Scheme: This scheme was started to ensure the
availability of saplings under the Krishi Vaniki Yojana. Poplar cuttings are made available
free of cost from the local poplar nurseries of the Department to the selected
entrepreneurs/farmers/land owners for setting up poplar nurseries on their land during
December-January. The prepared poplar plants are then purchased by the Department at a
pre-determined rate. In the current financial year, this rate has been fixed at Rs. 13 to 15 per
plant on the basis of survival. In 2023-24, 3.2 lakh ETPs have been planted in 32 nurseries
under this scheme.
