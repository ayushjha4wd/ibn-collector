Under the Government of India's ambitious initiative, “Passport Aapke Dwar’, Bihar has two new
passport service centres and 35 post office passport service centres. Further, three more post office
centres will be made operational very soon, This project aims to make passport application services

more accessible to residents by allowing applications to be submitted nearer to their home.

In addition to that, the Ministry of External Affairs provided a "Passport Mobile Van" to the
Regional Passport Office, Patna in 2024. The mobile service has started to organise camps in
different educational institutions in Bihar and achieve success in attracting fresh passport

applications.

Currently, the Passport Mobile Van is utilised in areas where applicants face difficulties in
scheduling appointments, offering a convenient solution for on-the-spot application processing.
The Regional Passport Office, Patna, plans to extend the mobile services to more locations,

reinforcing its commitment to improve access and service.
