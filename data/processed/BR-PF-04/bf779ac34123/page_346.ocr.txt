8.3 Demand and Supply of Power

The power sector plays a leading role in the economic growth of any region. Demand of power in
Bihar has increased substantially in the last decade. In order to meet the power demand, the State
government has ensured almost cent-percent power supply and thus the energy deficit has been
continuously declining. In fact, the state recorded an energy surplus of 4034 MU in 2022-23. The
peak demand increased by almost 1.4 times during 2017-18 to 2023-24, while power supply also
increased by 1.5 times during the same period. Chart 8.2 presents an overview of power demand
and supply, while Chart 8.3 shows the trend of power deficit or surplus in the state.
