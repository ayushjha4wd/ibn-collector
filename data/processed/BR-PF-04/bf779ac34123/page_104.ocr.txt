The State government borrowed under only two categories of internal debt: market loans and loans
from financial institutions. Borrowings through market loans reached Rs. 36,800 crore in 2022-23
and increased to Rs. 47,612 crore in 2023-24. Consequently, the outstanding liability from market
loans rose from Rs. 1,84,275 crore on March 31, 2023 to Rs. 2.14,418 crore on March 31, 2024.
Notably, the State government did not need to use any financing through Ways and Means
Advances (WMA) from the Reserve Bank of India during these years,

The other significant source of borrowing was loans from financial institutions, which accounted
for Rs. 1,329 crore in 2022-23 and Rs. 1,934 crore in 2023-24. As a result of these new borrowings
and repayments, the outstanding liability from loans from financial institutions increased from
Rs. 9,776 crore on March 31, 2023 to Rs. 9,862 crore on March 31, 2024. Additionally, fresh
borrowings under the category loans and advances from the Central government totalled
Rs. 10,155 crore in 2022-23 and increased to Rs. 10,672 crore in 2023-24,
