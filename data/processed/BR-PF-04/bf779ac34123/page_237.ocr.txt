Using the usual activity status, the unemployment rate is defined as the share of unemployed
persons in the total population of employed and unemployed persons. In developing economies
where a large portion of the working-age population is engaged in informal activities,
unemployment rates have been low, and India is no exception. The unemployment rate in usual
status presents open unemployment for a longer period and masked ‘disguised unemployment’, In
a country like India, most of the working-age population is poor and unskilled, Therefore, they
cannot remain unemployed for a long time in search of better job opportunities, They accept any
employment opportunity they obtain to maintain their daily consumption needs. This is another
reason for the lower levels of open unemployment in developing countries, including India. In
Bihar, unemployment rates for males and females were 3.6 percent and 1.4 percent, respectively.
Further, the unemployment rate in urban areas, for both males and females, was higher than in
rural areas, The incidence of unemployment in urban Bihar for male and female were 6.9 percent

and 9.1 percent, respectively.
