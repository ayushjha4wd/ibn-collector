sector is the Annual Survey of Industries (ASI). According to the report, the primary units of
enumeration in the survey are the following: for manufacturing industries, the unit of enumeration
is a factory; for repair services, the unit of enumeration is a workshop; for electricity, gas, and
water supply undertakings, the unit of enumeration is an establishment or a licensee; for bidi and
cigar industries, the unit of enumeration is an establishment,
