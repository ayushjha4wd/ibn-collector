11.4 Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Kisan Credit Cards (KCC) are issued to farmers by various financial institutions, including
commercial banks, regional rural banks, state cooperative banks, central cooperative banks, and
primary agricultural cooperative societies (PACS). KCC holders are provided with loans through
a revolving cash credit facility, allowing multiple withdrawals and repayments within a specified
limit.
