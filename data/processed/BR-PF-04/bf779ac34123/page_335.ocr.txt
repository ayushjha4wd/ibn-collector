To avoid the impact of lightning on lives, the Earth-network web application has been developed
to capture block-wise lightning alerts before 30 to 45 minutes within a radius of 20 kms of the
mapped latitude and longitude. It works with the real-time Google Location. Any lightning alert
generated by the Earth-network is dispatched to concerned BDO and district administration/DEOC
through email. The DMD also sends block-wise alert notifications to the concerned officers, like
Panchayat Representatives. BDOs, COs, SHOs, and DEOCs with the help of the CDAC-ESMS
Application. Apart from this App, Indravajra, an ICT solution, provides real-time lightning alerts
to users, and has been developed by the Indian Meteorological Department (IMD). The App also
provides information on the number of lightning strikes in the last 30 minutes and the last hour.
Overall, Indravajra is a useful tool for people living in areas with high lightning activity, and
provides timely alerts and safety guidelines. In addition, the SACHET App and Portal warn the
public before an earthquake and suggest ways for mitigation or preparedness and capacity building
to deal with disaster situations. It also suggests measures through the geo-targeted location-based
system where people receive alerts of an impending disaster. Any disaster alert generated by Alert
Generating Agencies are pushed to SDMA/SEOC Sachet Portal, where, after proper authentication
and verification, they are disseminated among people of a specific location for which the alerts
have been generated.

Disaster Relief and Infrastructure Reconstruction : The Disaster Relief and Rescue Team
Tracker System has been developed for relief and rescue tracking, It also provides vital information
about the team's movement and activities, such as when the team has left headquarters for the task,
whether the operation is underway or completed, etc. Furthermore, Aapda Sampoorti Portal has
been developed to provide assistance to the victims’ families in case a disaster strikes. This portal
is used for compensating disaster victims in the optimum time by maintaining a pre-verified list of
inhabitants living in flood-prone and drought-prone areas. During the disaster, the community
kitchen and shelter homes are provided to the destitute and disaster-affected families through
‘Community Kitchen and Shelter Home Geo-Tagging’.

The Disaster Management Department (DMD) prepares quick damage reports of resources and
infrastructure through EOC Reporting App. Using this App, government officials can report
damages caused by hazards and relief activities performed by the respective district/block. The
officials can immediately report damage to resources and properties from the incident site. They
can also report the relief delivered to the affected people or households.
