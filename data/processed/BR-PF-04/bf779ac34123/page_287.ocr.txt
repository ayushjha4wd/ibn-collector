Mukhya Mantri Gram Pariyahan Yojana (MMGPY): Development of a strong and efficient
transportation system for the rural hinterlands is of prime eminence for the socio-economic
development of Bihar. The MMGPY addresses the issue of passenger transportation through
providing self-employment opportunities to more than 57,000 unemployed rural population of the
state. The scheme provides 50 percent subsidies on purchase price subject to maximum subsidy
of Rs. 70,000 on E-Rickshaw, Rs. 1,00,000 on passenger vehicle and Rs, 2,00,000 on the purchase
of an ambulance. As of June, 2024, a total of 44,754 beneficiaries have been given subsidies of
Rs. 460 crore. Further, more than 6500 applications have been received under phase-X1.

E-governance initiatives: Under e-governance, all types of licenses, vehicle registration and
permit fees are paid online using e-payment on a specified portal. The online-payment facility is
operational throughout the state and is helping to increase the revenue of the transport department,
This e-payment is executed through the OGRAS portal of the Finance Department. The details of
the e-governance programmes are discussed in a separate chapter (Chapter-VII).
