13.6 Drinking Water and Sanitation

Hygiene is the most effective way of controlling diseases and drinking water and sanitation play a
major role in this. Thus, a child cannot get hygienic environment until the supply of drinking water
and sanitation facilities are adequate in schools, The situation of a school plays a significant role
in children’s lives, as they acquire many social habits and behaviours within that setting. Children
are entitled to a conducive learning environment that includes easy access to safe and sustainable
water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) services. Consequently, maintaining WASH facilities in
schools should be prioritized. As per the latest SDG India Index 2023-24, on the child indicator
denoting the percentage of schools with separate toilet facilities for girls, which comes under goal
6 of the SDGs, Bihar’s score is 92 percent. Thus, ensuring the availability of adequate safe water
and proper sanitation facilities is an essential component of a school infrastructure. Chart 13.7
(A+B) shows the status of functional drinking water facilities and toilets in schools of Bihar in
2021-22. Presently, against the approved 1,15,009 AWCs, 1,02,242 have the facility of drinking
water and 96,251 centres have the toilet facility.
