7.2 Enforcement of Law and Administration

Good governance is highly dependent on the maintenance of law and order. In the current world
of cybercrimes and data frauds, it has become extremely difficult and challenging for the state to
control unlawful activities and safeguard its citizens and businesses. At the same time. the State
government has wisely used Artificial Intelligence (Al), Cybersecurity, Internet of Things (101),
ete., which are playing a major role in establishing strong foundations for the maintenance of law
and order. Some of the important e-governance programmes related to it are:

Crime and Criminal Tracking Network and System (CCTNS) ; The objective of rolling out
the CCTNS is to create a comprehensive and integrated system for enhancing the efficiency of
policing at all levels through adopting e-Governance measures. It transforms the police force into
a knowledge-based force. It improves the delivery of citizen-centric services by creating a platform
for sharing crime and criminal information on a real-time basis across the police stations in the
country.
