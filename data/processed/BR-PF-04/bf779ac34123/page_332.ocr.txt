Mukhyamantri Digital Health Yojana (MDHY) ; It is also Known as BHAVYA (Bihar Health
Application Visionary Yojna for All), BHAVYA is a one-stop digital platform that supports
everyday functions at the government health facilities of Bihar and streamlines the OPD and IPD
functionalities, right from a patient's registration till discharge. It even automates community
health initiatives through standardized workflows across the state. The implemented platform
includes more than 20 different modules such as patient registration, OPD & IPD management,
doctor’s scheduling and roster management, pharmacy, radiology, and lab information modules,
all operational across the state. The platform is also integrated with the legacy or existing
standalone applications used by the State government, creating a unified HIMS. Not only the
operations but the performance of these facilities, medical professionals, the region's health profile.
and the outcomes are monitored at the State/District Command-and-Control Centre (CCC).

sanitation facilities and services programme in the state are systematically monitored and feedback
used for correcting deficiencies. Additionally, a report provides a summary of new hand pump
installations and repair and maintenance activities documented through the mobile application.

Social Security Pension MIS : SSPMIS is a web-based application envisaged to keep track of the
entire social security beneficiary sanctioning process, which includes addition, deletion and
integration through assigning a code to each beneficiary for its unique identification leading to
effective utilization of funds. This application is being used for nearly one crore beneficiaries of
different social security pension schemes of the Central and State governments.

E-labharthi Portal : The database of all pensioners is maintained on the E-labarthi portal and
monthly DBT is done through PFMS from this portal. Pension payment is being made to 107.65
lakh pensioners.

E-Suvidha Portal ; Through the E-suvidha portal payments are made under the Chief Minister
Family Benefit Scheme, Kabir Antyeshti Anudan Yojana, Bihar Shatabdi Leper Welfare Scheme,
Chief Minister Disables Marriage Grant Scheme and Inter-caste Mariage Grant Scheme. Data of
National Family Benefit Scheme beneficiaries obtained from the District Social Security Cell is
also accepted and sent to the NSAP-PPS for payment.
