Information related to Employment Exchanges

The primary function of an Employment Exchange is to register job seekers. In Bihar, 7,39,492
people were registered with various Employment Exchanges in 2023-24. The Employment
Exchanges have been regularly organising camps to offer guidance, and opportunities through job
camps, and job fairs, In 2023-24, 3228 guidance camps were organised and 1,17,925 individuals
benefited from these, Furthermore, 1376 job camps were organised, which benefited 31,784
individuals. In 2023-24, the Employment Exchanges organised 40 job fairs, benefiting 25,951
aspirants. Here, we mention two important initiatives adopted by the Employment Exchanges,
namely, ‘Tool Kit distribution’ and “Study Kit distribution’,

Under the Expansion of Employment Services Scheme, Employment Exchanges have initiated the
Tool Kit distribution programme for registered candidates (including Divyangs, Transgenders,
Women, and those belonging to SCs, STs, OBCs, and EBCs) who are skilled in various trades
such as electrician, fitter, mobile repair, beautician, plumber, electronic repair. It has been decided
that 30 percent of the total beneficiaries will be women. The maximum cost of a tool kit is Rs.
15,000. In 2023-24, tool kits were provided free of cost to 1666 registered candidates. In 2023-24,
the State government has spent Rs. 79.64 lakh on the supply of tool kits.

Apart from the ‘Tool Kit distribution’ initiative, the Employment Exchanges have also begun
distributing ‘Study Kits’ to the registered candidates (including Divyangs, Transgenders, Women,
and those belonging to SCs, STs, OBCs, and EBCs). Under the Employment-cum-Guidance
Scheme, the ‘Study Kit distribution” programme assists the registered and eligible candidates in
preparing for government jobs and other competitive examinations. The maximum purchase price
of a study kit is Rs. 5,000. In 2023-24, ‘Study Kits’ were provided free of cost to 2436 registered
candidates. In 2023-24, the State government has spent Rs. 144.14 lakh on strengthening of
Employment-cum-Guidance Programme,
