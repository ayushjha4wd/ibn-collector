5.4 Skill Development

The Bihar Skill Development Mission (BSDM) aims to prepare a skilled workforce to enhance the
employability of the working population of the State. The primary focus of BSDM is on imparting
employment-oriented training and capacity building, etc. Since its inception, over 20 lakh people
have received training, and the network of training centres is well-established throughout the State.
As part of the new initiatives, BSDM has started conducting employment-oriented training
programmes, especially for women, in partnership with the Patna Municipal Corporation, Besides
providing training in traditional fields like information technology, healthcare, retail.
manufacturing, computing, and electronics, BSDM also offers modern and advanced courses, such
as drone technology, cloud computing, web development, and multi-skill technician training.
Collaborating with various departments of the State government, these efforts aim to equip the
youth with skills relevant to today’s evolving industry and job markets, helping them to advance
in their careers. BSDM is also committed to provide continuous training in the State's primary
sectors, including agriculture and allied services. Efforts have been made to train farmers and
entrepreneurs in areas such as mushroom cultivation and link them to self-employment

opportunities. As a result, the State now ranks first in the country for mushroom production,

Additionally, BSDM has opened new pathways for skill development in the State’s informal
sectors, including for street food vendors, tyre fitters, retail vendors, and sandal makers, providing

valuable recognition for their skills.

Kushal Yuva Programme (KYP) : This programme was conducted from 1886 centres with more
than 4.7 lakh enrolled trainees in 2023-24. A diametric shift in the gender ratio was observed in
KYP. Over the years, the female participation in the training has increased substantially. For
instance, in December 2016, the male-female ratio of the trainee was 80:20, which has changed to
42:58 by January 2024. The encouraging change indicates a positive shift in women’s perceptions
of skill development programmes. Now, more women are opting for skill training to improve their
skills and take part in the economic process in the State. Under KYP, training programmes were
designed exclusively for women. As part of the State government's "Saat Nishchay" scheme, under
"Arthik Hal Yuyaon Ko Bal," the Skilled Youth Programme was designed to provide 240 hours of
training for employability to youth in Bihar who are 10th pass and in the age group of
15-28 years (33 years for SC/ST and Divyang, and 31 years for OBC), In 2023-24, more than 2.5

lakh trainees received certificates after successfully completing their training (Table 5.29).
