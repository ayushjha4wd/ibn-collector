Farm Interventions

(a) Enhancing Agricultural Productivity: Since its inception, JEEVIKA has focused on
boosting agricultural productivity through implementing cost-effective, sustainable
technologies. Integrating scientific methods with traditional knowledge promotes soil
improvement and water, plant, and nutrient management, enhancing food security and
incomes for farming households.

In 2024-25 (up to September 2024), the improved production practices were adopted by
14.72 lakh paddy farmers and 10.78 lakh vegetable farmers. Kitchen gardens gained
popularity, with 17.76 lakh farmers growing seasonal vegetables like ladyfinger, tomatoes,
and leafy greens, improving nutrition at low costs. Additionally, 5.84 lakh farmers cultivated
maize, and 2.11 lakh focused on pulses, further expanding agricultural output and income
opportunities.

owned institutions. These WFPCs aggregate produce, streamline input supplies, and gain
better market access, enabling higher returns and reduced costs for farmers. WFPCs engage
in crop aggregation and marketing (¢.g., maize, wheat, paddy, vegetables, mustard, and
lentils) and distribute essential inputs like seeds and fertilizers. They also diversify into
fertilizer distribution, certified seed production, and value-added processing. Products are
