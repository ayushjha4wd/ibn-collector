WRD Scheme Implementation and Monitoring System (WSIMS) : WRD Scheme
Implementation and Monitoring System (WSIMS) is designed and developed for monitoring of
physical and financial progress of the ongoing schemes of the Department, The activities involved
in planning, approval, tendering, job award, work agreement, physical progress and financial
progress of schemes have been taken care of by this software. Modules developed in WSIMS are
Administrative Approval & Technical Sanction, BOQ Sanction, and contractor details, Tender
Details, Work Agreement, Fund Allotment, Physical Progress Entry, Financial Progress Entry,
Physical Progress Report and Financial Progress Report, Reports can be generated date wise and
month wise for the ongoing schemes.

Physical Modelling Centre (PMC) at Birpur : With the help of the World Bank-assisted Bihar
Kosi Basin Development Project (BK BDP), the government is setting up a Physical Modelling
Centre (PMC) at Birpur (Supaul) as ‘Centre of Excellence’ (CoE) for Research and Development
under the Water Resources Department, The major objectives of the PMC are: (i) to study hydraulic
properties of rivers, (ii) to study hydraulic structure & its effect, (iii) to study morphological
behaviour of rivers, river meandering & channel activation, (iv) to support in designing appropriate
projects for flood protection & anti-erosion works, and (v) to study other complex behaviour of
rivers. The various components of functioning of the PMC are Administrative Building, Workshop
and Laboratory, Water Circulatory System, Kosi River Model, Kosi Barrage Model, Four Model
Trays and Store Room.
