A summary of the percentage distribution of the receipt and expenditure in the consolidated fund
of the State government for the years 2022-23 and 2023-24, along with the budgeted figures for
2024-25, is presented in Table 2.35. In 2023-24, the total receipts comprised 23.7 percent as capital
receipts and 76.3 percent as revenue receipts. Of these total receipts, 63.9 percent came from tax
revenue, 10.3 percent from grants-in-aid and contributions, 2.1 percent from non-tax revenue, and
23.7 percent from public debt. Notably, the share of both receipts and expenditures in the capital
account has increased over these years.

On the expenditure side, the allocations to General Services, Social Services, and Economic
Services represented 23.5 percent, 33.0 percent, and 19.1 percent of the total State government
expenditure in 2023-24, respectively. Additionally, the share of capital outlay in total expenditure
rose from 13.6 percent in 2022-23 to 14.5 percent in 2023-24. Likewise, the share of public debt
discharge increased from 6.2 percent to 9.1 percent during the same period. Overall, the State
government's distribution of receipts and expenditures in 2023-24 reflects prudent fiscal
management, emphasising support for the state’s long-term economic growth.
