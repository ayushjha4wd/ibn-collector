The following are upcoming thermal power projects in Bihar to meet the present and future demand
for electricity:

Power Project in Buxar: M/s Satluj Jal Vidyut Nigam Ltd. (SJVNL) is the executing agency for
the construction of a green field power project at Chausa (Buxar), having 2 units of 660 MW each.
Its foundation stone was laid in March, 2019, The work is under progress and Unit | is expected
to be commissioned in 2024-25. Unit 2 is scheduled to be commissioned by 2025-26. As per the
Power Purchase Agreement (PPA), DISCOMs of Bihar will get 85 percent of the generated power
from this project.

Ultra-Mega Power Project (Banka): A proposal has been prepared to set up an Ultra Mega Power
Project in Banka, with a capacity of around 4000 MW, for which 2500 acres of land has already
been earmarked, The Central Water Commission has approved the allocation of 120 Cusecs of
water (1 Cusec = flow of 28.317 litres of water per second) from the Ganga river. The Power
Finance Corporation (PFC) has incorporated two Special Purpose Vehicles (SPV) to carry out the
pre-award project activities. The Union Ministry of Power has allocated Pirpainti and Barahat Coal
Blocks for the project. Bihar will get 2000 MW of power from this project.

B. Non-Conventional (Renewable Energy)

In order to address the issues of climate change and global warming, renewable energy stands out
as a feasible solution for preserving the environment for future generations. However, Bihar faces
limitations in the availability of natural resources for generating renewable energy. Due to its
geographical position in the plains, the potential for hydroelectric power production is quite low,
Likewise, ideal locations for setting up wind turbines to generate electricity are typically found
offshore, in coastal regions, on rounded hilltops, or in open valleys with strong and consistent wind
currents. As a result, the state also cannot take full advantage of wind energy for power generation.
