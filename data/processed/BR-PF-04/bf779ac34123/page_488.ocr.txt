Dropout Rate

The achievement of high enrollment is significant only if the enrolled children continue their
education in school. The reasons for drop-out can generally be grouped into several categories:
social, economic, cultural, teaching methods, administrative practices, and infrastructure. In Bihar,
all these factors are present to different extents, It is essential to thoughtfully plan preventive
actions and intervention strategies that could be implemented to support the students who are at a
risk of dropping out. It is observed from Table 12.14 that the drop-out rates have significantly
fallen over the years. The decline at the secondary level is 62.25 percentage points between
2019-20 and 2023-24, It is also laudable that in the same period, the decline in the drop-out rate

of girls is higher compared to that of the boys.
