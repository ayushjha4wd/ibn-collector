In 2024-25 (up to September 2024), through different forms of intervention, JEEVIKA has
achieved significant milestones in agriculture, livestock, and allied sectors. 14.72 lakh farmers
have adopted better paddy cultivation practices, 13.01 lakh farmers for wheat, and 10.78 lakh
farmers for vegetables. Kitchen gardening has gained popularity, benefiting 17.76 lakh farmers by
enabling year-round, low-cost production of vegetables and fruits that help in improving nutrition
for SHG members, Additionally, 5.84 lakh farmers were engaged in maize cultivation, and 2.1]
lakh were focused on pulses, boosting production and income opportunities,

Under the Integrated Poultry Development Scheme (IPDS), backyard poultry interventions are
being implemented across 310 blocks of 38 districts in Bihar. Beneficiaries receive 45 chicks in
two batches, reared at Mother Units for 28 days. As of September 2024, 2 lakh SHG members
were involved in this initiative.

In collaboration with COMFED, JEEVIKA is linking cattle-rearing SHG members to Dairy
Cooperative Societies (DCSs) and establishing new women-run DCSs. By September 2024, 1,041
women DCSs were formed, connecting 76,130 households, The Kaushikee Mahila Milk Producer
Company operates 836 milk pooling points, procuring an average of 77.087 litres of milk daily.
