10.3 Centre and State-specific Urban Development Programme
Deendayal Antyoday Yojana — National Urban Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NULM)

Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana — National Urban Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NULM) has been
implemented in all municipal bodies since 2016. This initiative provides free skill training to urban
poor youth, along with shelter for the urban poor and designated vending zones for street vendors.
The primary objective of the scheme is to create self-employment opportunities for poor people.
Since 2015-16, 51,560 young men and women have received training. Of these, 37,296 trainees
have undergone assessment and certification, and 12,214 trainees have successfully secured
employment.

The number of SHGs formed under DAY-NULM was 5154, with 48296 new members added in
2023-24. Furthermore, during the same period, 181 Area-Level Federations (ALFs) and four
City-Level Federations were established. Moreover, 3,653 SHGs have received a Revolving Fund
(RF). The achievement under the Financial Inclusion and Self-Employment Programme is
presented in Table 10.7.
