Internship for students at Government Engineering Colleges

The Government of Bihar has approved an internship policy for seventh-semester students at the
government engineering colleges, offering them Rs. 10,000 upon successful completion of their
internships. Students will have the opportunity to participate in projects run by the government
departments, corporations, societies, and similar organizations. A dedicated portal will be
developed to facilitate project allocation and ensure timely disbursement of funds to the successful

students.

Co-curricular activities and other activities

Establishment of language lab

To improve communication skills of
students, a fully equipped language lab has
been set-up at every government
engineering college and government
polytechnic institute. Apart from English, a
foreign language will also be taught in these
language labs.

Classes for mental health

To promote mental well-being, improve
concentration, and reduce stress, mental
health classes are being organized at the
institute level to enhance students' overall
academic performance. Students and faculty
are encouraged to participate in these classes
regularly.

Parent-Teacher Meeting

A bi-monthly parent-teacher meeting is organized at the institute level to gather feedback from
parents and update them on student’s progress. Issues raised during the meeting are
consolidated at the department level, and roles and responsibilities are assigned to address these
concerns.

Pahal

PAHAL is an initiative to support students, particularly those from government schools, in
preparing for competitive exams while also encouraging faculty members to take on social
responsibilities. Faculties at the institutes are offering guidance classes in Mathematics,
Science and English to the interested students in grades 9 to 12, thus helping them prepare for
various competitive exams.

Umang

Various sports and other activities are organized at the institute level to promote students’
health and showcase their potential beyond academics. Winners of the institute-level events
participate in competitions organized at the divisional level. Those who succeed at the
divisional level go on to compete in a state-level fest, which is proposed to be held every
February.
