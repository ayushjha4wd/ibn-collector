1.9 State Profile

While analysing the state’s economic scenario, including sector-specific activities, it is helpful to
consider the demographic structure, as it significantly influences individual economic outcomes.
Additionally, information from the various administrative divisions of the state helps in
understanding the spatial aspects of economic activities. A brief profile of the state is outlined in

this section.

Geographically, Bihar is a land-locked state located in the eastern part of the country, between the
longitude 83°-19'-50" to 88°-17'-40" E and latitude 24°-20'-10" to 27°-31'-15" N. The state’s
geographical area is 94,163 square kms, with the 12th rank in terms of area among the states in the
country, The Ganga river divides the state into two (unequal) parts, North and South Bihar. The
state also shares an international boundary with Nepal to its North. Many rivers flowing through
the state, especially in its northern part, originate in the neighbouring country, The state’s climate
is sub-tropical with four seasons: Spring, Summer, Rainy, and Winter. The state is administratively
divided into nine divisions - Patna, Magadh, Saran, Tirhut, Darbhanga, Kosi, Purnea, Bhagalpur
and Munger; 38 Districts, 101 Sub-divisions and 534 Community Development Blocks. The
legislature in the state is bicameral, with 75 members in the legislative council and 243 in the

legislative assembly.
