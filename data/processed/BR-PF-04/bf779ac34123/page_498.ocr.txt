Intake capacity and reservation

The intake capacity for each course is established by the AICTE, considering the available
infrastructure and facilities at the institutions. There has been a consistent increase in the intake
capacity of engineering and polytechnic institutes each academic session, indicating substantial
growth in government engineering colleges and polytechnic institutions. Currently, the approved
intake capacity is 17,054 for government polytechnic institutes and 14,469 for government
engineering colleges (Chart 12.14). Additionally, the number of M. Tech seats has increased to
612. In line with the Government of Bihar’s policy to encourage higher technical education for
female students, 33 percent (one-third) of the total seats at the degree-level and above courses are
reserved for girls and women in all institutions affiliated with the Bihar Engineering University.
In the academic year 2023-24, a total of 11,001 students (8,429 regular and 2,572 lateral) were
admitted to government engineering colleges, while 15,178 students (14,017 regular and 1,161
lateral) enrolled in government polytechnic institutes.
