Samagra Gavya Vikas Yojana : In 2024-25, under the state plane, the State government has
approved Rs. 48.48 crore to establish dairy units (of 2, 4, 15, and 20) high-breed milk cattle/heifers.
A provision of 75 percent subsidy has been made for the dairy farmers belonging to the EBC, SC,
and ST, while other dairy farmers receive a 50 percent subsidy. Furthermore, for 15-20 cattle units,
a subsidy of 40 percent is provided for all categories of beneficiaries for setting up units and 114
dairy units for milch cattle/heifers were set up. Under this scheme, between 2021-22 and 2023-24,
5,873 units were set up against the physical target of 7,194 units, and Rs. 102.56 crore was
disbursed out of the target of Rs, 106.98 crore.

In 2024-25, approval has been granted for providing free training in modern scientific dairying to
all sections of dairy farmers under the state plan, The estimated cost of the training programmes is
Rs. 7.99 crore in 2024-25, Between 2021-22 and 2023-24, 18,401 stakeholders were trained in
national-level institutes. For the training programmes, the State government has disbursed
Rs. 10.61 crore out of the target of Rs. 11.64 crore.

Desi Gaupalan Protsahan Yojana ; 1 2024-25, under the Saat Nishchay-2, approval has been
granted for establishing dairy units of 2 and 4 indigenous cows/heifers under the Desi Gaupalan
Protsahan Yojana, The estimated cost of implementing the scheme was Rs. 25.46 crore. Under this
scheme, a provision has been made to offer a 75 percent grant to the beneficiaries of EBC, SC, and
ST communities, and a 50 percent grant to all others for establishing units with 2 and 4 indigenous
cows/heifers units. In 2023-24, 1,214 units were established against the target of 1,545 units. To
establish 1,214 units, the State government has disbursed Rs. 34.30 crore against the allocated fund
of Rs. 37,05 crore in 2023-24,

In 2024-25, under Saat Nishchay-2, an insurance scheme for milch cattle has been approved, with
an estimated cost of Rs. 2.99 crore. The scheme offers financial security through livestock
insurance in the event of death, illness, or other reasons. In 2023-24, 3,947 milch cattle were
insured, achieving 79 percent of the target. The expenditure amounted to Rs. 1.35 crore.
