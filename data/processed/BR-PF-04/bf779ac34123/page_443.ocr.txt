Microfinance Institutions (MFI)

Microfinance Institutions (MFIs) offer small loans to low-income houscholds at the bottom of the
economic pyramid. While their interest rates are higher than those of Scheduled Commercial
Banks (SCBs), MFIs provide essential access to credit, often with minimal collateral. They support
income-generating activities in both rural and urban areas, promoting economic growth.

By expanding credit facilities, MFIs enhance financial inclusion, especially in areas lacking access
to SCBs. Various entities involved in microfinance in India include NBFC-MFls, Banks, Small
Finance Banks, and nonprofit MFIs. The National Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM) plays a
crucial role through its Self-Help Group-Bank Linkage Programme. In Bihar, MFIs have
successfully collaborated with Self-Help Groups (SHGs) to provide small to moderate loans,
significantly improving financial inclusion through the State government's JEEVIKA initiative.
