The Bihar Rajya Bee} Nigam Ltd. produces foundation and certified seeds for key kharif and rabi
crops. Certified seeds are sourced from seed-producing farmers, progressive farmers, and
government fields in limited quantities, The capitalist and progressive farmers in the state produce
foundation seeds and it is sold to the producers. This entire activity is supervised by the
corporation. The registration and certification of seed crop areas are managed through the seed
certification agency, which also conducts final inspections to determine eligibility for seed
purchases.

The corporation ensures that farmers receive a purchase price higher than the market rate or the
minimum support price, whichever is greater. Since kharif 2009-10, seeds have been purchased
based on cultivated seed rather than raw seed, Unsold seeds are disposed of through e-auction by
NCDEX e Markets Limited (NeML), where NCDEX stands for the National Commodity and
Derivatives Exchange Limited. Currently, 11,955 seed-producing farmers are registered with the
Bihar Rajya Beej Nigam Ltd, Furthermore, in 2019-20, the corporation had achieved a total
production of 1,66,995.91 quintals. Under the Mission 4.0, the target for increasing seed
production was set at 4.00 lakh quintals. In 2020-21, 3.07 lakh quintals of seeds were produced,
surpassing targets through enhanced crop production. Under the Mission, in 2021-22, the total
seed collection reached 2.93 lakh quintals. For 2022-23 (kharif and rabi seasons), the corporation
collected 2.13 lakh quintals of raw seeds. In 2023-24, the total seed production was 2.56 lakh
quintals. The targeted seed production for 2024-25 is 5.48 lakh quintals.

The Bihar Rajya Beej Nigam Ltd. has five processing units at their regional offices, another 10
(one tonne per hour or TPH) processing plants have been installed in 10 seed multiplication farms
and one non-operational processing unit in Bihta, Patna. The annual processing capacity of the
corporation is 7.15 lakh quintals. The corporation has established its largest unit in Kudra, Kaimur
district with processing capacity of 5.0 lakh quintals per year. The corporation has the storage
capacity of 10.82 lakh quintals in 2024-25, which increased from 6.72 lakh quintals in 2022-23.
To improve seed quality, the corporation has installed dehumidifiers at nine locations and seed
dryers at six.

The Bihar Rajya Beej Nigam Ltd. supplies seeds to implement various schemes like the state plan,
the National Food Security Mission, and the National Agricultural Development Plan, In 2023, for
summer crops, 38,857 quintals of seeds were distributed to 5,48,984 farmers. For kharif 2023, the
corporation has distributed 70,605 quintals of seeds to 2,86,055 farmers. Furthermore, for rabi
2023-24, 4,65,594 quintals of seeds were distributed to 11,18,667 farmers.

The Chief Minister Crash Seed Programme promotes the use of base/certified seeds for seed
production. These seeds are supplied at a 90 percent subsidy. Adopting new seed varieties (of
cereals, pulses, and oilseeds) across villages is facilitated by exchanging sceds produced within
the village. In 2023-24, the achievements of the programme for rice and wheat seeds were 98.5
and 75.8 percent, respectively. In 2024-25 (up to September 2024), the Department of Agriculture
has distributed 6,48,144 quintals of seeds among 20,99,263 farmers.
