Anganwadi Services (AS)

The Anganwadi Services, formerly known as the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS),
is one of the flagship programmes of the Government of India and represents one of the world’s
largest and exclusive programmes for early childhood care and development. It shows the
country’s commitment to its children and nursing mothers, as a response to the challenge of
providing pre-school non-formal education on the one hand and breaking the vicious cycle of
malnutrition, morbidity, reduced learning capacity and mortality on the other. The beneficiaries
under this scheme are children in the age group of 6 months to 6 years, Pregnant Women and
Lactating Mothers and Adolescent Girls in the age group of 14 to 18 years in Aspirational Districts
of the States and the North Eastern Region. The ICDS was initiated on October 2, 1975 as a
remedial programme aimed at children between the age of 0 and 6 years. The programme aimed
to enhance childcare, early stimulation and learning, health, and nutrition in the long term. The
ICDS scheme was first introduced to achieve the National Policy for Children in 33 pilot blocks,
which included 4,891 Anganwadi Centres (AWCs) across the country, with three of those blocks
located in Bihar. The AWCs gradually expanded with 5652 Projects and 6 lakh sanctioned AWCs
in the country, by the end of the Ninth Plan. The coverage of Anganwadi Services Scheme, till the
Ninth Plan, was not universal, Now, the Mission Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0 operates
through a network of 7075 fully operational Projects and 13.95 lakh operational AWCs.

The ICDS provides a package of six services which are — (i) Supplementary Nutrition Programme
(SNP), (ii) Preschool Non-Formal Education (for children between the ages of 3 and 6 years), (iii)
Nutrition and Health Education, (iv) Immunization, (v) Health Check-up, and (vi) Referral
Services. Three of the six services, viz., Immunization, Health Check-up and Referral Services are
related to health and are provided through NHM and Public Health Infrastructure. The target group
of ICDS is reached through the Anganwadi Centers (AWC). The personnel for ICDS include Child
Development Project Officer (CDPO), Lady Supervisor (LS), Anganwadi Worker (AWW) and
Anganwadi Helper (A WH).

The main beneficiaries of the programme comprise children aged 6 months to 6 years.
