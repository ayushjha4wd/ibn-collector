Under Saat Nishchay-2, approval has been given to expand the marketing system in urban and

essential milk products.

In recent years, goat rearing has gained importance, and as a result, the Directorate has initiated
different schemes to attract people to goat rearing. The State government has announced subsidies
to establish goat farms of different sizes. In this direction, the State government has set the aim of
establishing 590 goat rearing farms with different capacities and allocating Rs. 1755.99 lakh in
2023-24. Furthermore, the State government has made provisions for the distribution of female
goats to families below the poverty line (BPL). In 2023-24, the State government has targeted the
provision of three breedable female goats (per family) to 3,941 families through JEEVIKA. For
this purpose, the expected expenditure is Rs, 522.85 lakh.
