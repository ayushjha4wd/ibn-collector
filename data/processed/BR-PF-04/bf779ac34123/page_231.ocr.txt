4.10 Tourism

The Department of Tourism has developed a comprehensive tourism policy, ‘Bihar Tourism Policy
2023.’ This policy aims to integrate the tourism industry with the economic progress of the state.
Through this initiative, the State government wants to develop tourism infrastructure, improve
accessibility, and provide a safe and memorable experience to the tourists. The tourism industry
has the potential to provide large-scale, quality employment to the trained youth and boost local
economies. A comprehensive road map of community participation has also been specified in the

policy document.

Through this policy, the State government laid down a detailed incentive structure for private
investment in infrastructure and services. As part of the fiscal incentives, all eligible projects would
receive a subsidy based on the approved project costs. The subsidy can be availed either through
the capital subsidy route or the interest subvention route.

The State government has notified the Bihar Tourism Branding and Marketing Policy 2024 to
promote tourism both nationally and internationally using modem marketing technology. The aim
of this policy is to inform people from various parts of India and abroad about the rich tourism
heritage, including the Ramayana Circuit, the Buddhist Circuit, and the Jain Circuit. Among the
various branding methods, digital display boards and glass panels in different cities in India are
effectively used to attract tourists to Bihar.

The tourist footfall has significantly increased in 2023, with 821.33 lakh people visiting Bihar
(see Table 4.37). Out of 821.33 lakh people, 815.86 lakh were domestic tourists, while 5.47 lakh
were foreign tourists. The number of both domestic and foreign tourists in 2023 returned to its
pre-Covid-19 pandemic levels. Between 2020 and 2022, the number of visitors in Bihar had
plummeted because of the pandemic.
