6.8 Road Transport Sector

The road transport emerged as one of the growth drivers of Bihar’s economy during the last two
decades. As evident from Chart 6.6, a double-digit growth has been recorded throughout the period
from 2012-2024. As a result, the size of the transport sector increased by three times to reach Rs.
25,100 crore in 2023-24 from Rs. 8400 crore in 2011-12.
