The allocation of total expenditure between revenue and capital expenditure is a part of expenditure
management by the State government. Additionally, the expenditures made by the State
government are also classified into Developmental and Non-Developmental categories. Table 2.26
presents the pattern of revenue and capital expenditure in Bihar from 2019-20 to 2023-24, along
with the budget estimates for 2024-25. It also includes the year-on-year growth rates of these

expenditures and their ratios as a percentage of the Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP).

From 2019-20 to 2023-24, the expenditure from the revenue account of the State government
increased from Rs. 1,26,017 crore to Rs. 1,90,514 crore. Meanwhile, capital expenditure rose from
Rs. 20,080 crore to Rs. 61,568 crore during the same period. In 2023-24, revenue receipts
accounted for 76.7 percent of total expenditure, while revenue expenditure comprised 75.6 percent
