The district-wise credit-deposit (CD) ratios in Bihar for the years 2022-23, 2023-24, and
2024-25 (as of September 2024) are presented in Table 11.15. Overall, Bihar has seen a significant
increase in its CD ratios in recent years, indicating growing credit utilization, However, there is
considerable variation in CD ratios among different districts within the state.

As of September 2024, 18 districts in Bihar reported CD ratios exceeding the state average. Among
these, Purnea has the highest CD ratio at 92.3 percent, followed by Araria at 89.3 percent and West
Champaran at 83.3 percent, In contrast, the three districts with the lowest CD ratios are Munger at
33.5 percent, Bhojpur at 40.7 percent, and Nalanda at 41.3 percent.
