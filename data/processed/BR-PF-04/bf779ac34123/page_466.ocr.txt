Investments in the social sector tend to benefit the poor and potentially improve the human capital,
leading to both immediate growth effects and broader economic benefits. In Bihar, government
spending on social initiatives is significant because a higher percentage of impoverished
households depend on government services and it is important to achieve well-defined goals in the
social sector, such as the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Public Expenditure on Social
Sector including health and education is a major concern to improve the Human Development
Index, Table 12.1 shows the trend of expenditure on social services during the last 18 years. The
expenditure has increased 13 times between 2005-06 and 2023-24. Following the same pattern,
expenditure on health increased 13 times and that on education increased 10 times. In absolute
terms, expenditure on health increased from Rs. 1015 crore in 2005-06 to Rs. 13015 crore in
2023-24, and on education it increased from Rs. 4366 crore to Rs. 44784 crore in the same period.
The share of social services in the total expenditure between 2005-06 and 2023-24 recorded an
increase of 4.5 percentage points. Showing a positive trend, the Per Capita Expenditure on Social
Services and the Per Capita Development Expenditure (PCDE) of the state in these 18 years
increased eight times. Interestingly, between 2015-16 and 2023-24, the per capita expenditure on
social services grew at the rate of 9.4 percent and the PCDE grew at the rate of 6.9 percent annually
(Table 12.1 and Chart 12.1).
