Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalaya (KGBYV)

Enrolment and Retention of children in schools are important for assessing any educational system.
The education of girls, especially those belonging to the deprived sections, is a primary concern,
and efforts are being made to focus on gender concerns in various Samgra Shiksha activities. The
Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalaya (KGBV) scheme is an innovative step to strengthen the
education of disadvantaged girls. The scheme of KGBY has played a significant role in narrowing
the gender gap in the educationally backward blocks (EBB) of the state. The scheme was launched
in July 2004-05 for classes VI to VIII. In the state, all KGBVs are of model I, 11 and IV type i.e.
hostel attached with existing Middle/High schools. In 2010-11, KGBV has been extended to all
EBBs with rural female literacy below the national average, as per 2001 census. As per the NEP
2020, all KGBVs are to be upgraded to Class XII up to 2025-26. For this, additional construction
in existing KGBV buildings is to be carried out in a phased manner. Three types of KGBVs are
operational in the state, the details of which are given in Table 12.18.

Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan (PM-USHA)

Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan (PM-USHA) is a centrally sponsored programme
which aims to work with 300-plus state universities and their affiliated colleges. Launched in 2013,
the PM-USHA aims to provide strategic funding to eligible state higher educational institutions. It
is based on central funding norms and is outcome-dependent. Funds flow from the central ministry
through the state governments/union territories before reaching the identified institutions. Funding
to states is made on the basis of the critical appraisal of State Higher Education Plans, which enlists
each state's strategy to address issues of equity, access and excellence in higher education. PM-
USHA places greater emphasis on the improvement of the quality of teaching-learning processes
in order to produce employable and competitive graduates, post-graduates and Ph.D.
