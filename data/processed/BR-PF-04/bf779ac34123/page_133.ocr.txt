Land is the most crucial factor of production and has historically been controlled by men in Indian
society, with Bihar being no exception. In 2015-16, out of 16,412 thousand holdings in Bihar,
women controlled only 2,297 thousand holdings, which constituted 14 percent of the total. In terms
of area, women controlled only 892 thousand hectares out of 6,457 thousand hectares in 2015-16,
Nevertheless, between 2010-11 and 2015-16, the area of operational holdings under female control
increased by 5.1 percent. Another significant characteristic of the distribution of operational
holdings in Bihar is the predominance of marginal (less than one hectare) holdings. Out of 16412
thousand holdings, 14,970 thousand holdings belonged to the size class of marginal land holdings
(less than one hectare) in 2015-16. In terms of area, 3,727 thousand hectares out of 6,457 thousand
hectares were distributed in the size class of marginal land holdings. In fact, larger landholdings
have been subdivided and reduced to marginal and small landholdings over the years.
