Housing for All (Urban) HFA

To address the housing problem, the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana offers financial assistance to
the families living in urban areas to construct houses. Under the Beneficiary-led Individual
Housing (BLC) scheme, financial support is offered to the beneficiaries, who privately own land,
to construct a new house. Each beneficiary receives a grant of Rs. 2.00 lakh, shared in a 75:25
ratio, with Rs. 1.50 lakh contributed by the Central government and Rs. 50,000 by the State
government. The construction of the house is to be carried out by the beneficiary. Table 10,11
indicates that the total expenditure was Rs, 3893.21 crore up to September 2024.
