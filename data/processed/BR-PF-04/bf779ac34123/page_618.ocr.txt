Tal Development Plan ; The Department is determined to make better use and management
of water in the Tal area of Patna, Nalanda, Sheikhpura and Lakhisarai at an estimated cost of
Rs. 1178.50 crore. An ambitious scheme has been prepared towards the construction of 108
kms. of embankments and 27 weirs, in addition to 15 check dams, base cleaning of 74 pynes,
construction of 42 box culverts and construction of 101 parpath. The scheme also envisages
to develop fish and aquatic production by deepening the existing reservoirs in the Tal area
and make drainage system more effective by desilting the local Pynes. This scheme will
benefit Fatuha, Daniyawa, Khusrupur, Bakhtiyarpur, Pandarak, Belchi, Mokama and
Ghoswari blocks of Patna; Harnaut, Chandi, Bind and Sarmera blocks of Nalanda;
Sheikhpura and Ghat Kusumbha blocks of Sheikhpura; and, Badahiya block of Lakhisarai.
Present progress of scheme is 33 percent.

Gayaji Dam (Rubber Dam) on the Falgu River ; The Gayaji Dam, India's longest rubber
dam, has been constructed near the Vishnupad temple of Gaya to address water scarcity in
the Falgu river. The dam has been designed to store water during the monsoon season and
release it throughout the year so as to provide relief to the devotees performing ‘Pind Daan’
and also help in conserving the Falgu river's ecosystem. It has been built 360 meters
downstream the temple. This is the first of its kind in the state with an estimated cost of
Rs. 334 crore. Its length is 411 meters and height is 3 meters, and it comprises 6 spans, each
65 meters in length. The pneumatic tube membrane allows for easy filling and emptying,
ensuring that the dam's flow and water level are not disturbed. The dam has minimal
mechanical equipment, making it eco-friendly, and has low operation and maintenance costs.
