Smart City Mission

In Bihar, four cities - Bhagalpur, Muzaffarpur, Patna, and Biharsharif - have been designated for
development as Smart Cities under the Smart City Mission. Development activities are currently
underway in all four cities. For 2024-25, an allocation of Rs. 640.0 crore has been made. Under
the Smart City Mission, the Central and State governments will share the costs equally, with each
contributing Rs.100.00 crore per smart city over five years.

A total of Rs. 826.82 crore has been released to the Bhagalpur Smart City, which includes
contributions from both Central and State governments. Patna has received Rs. 497.53 crore,
Muzaffarpur has received Rs. 773.64 crore, and Biharsharif has received Rs. 729.40 crore. The
details of the fund allocation across various towns and components are presented in Table 10,15.
