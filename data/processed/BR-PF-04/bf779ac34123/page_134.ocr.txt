3.2 Crop Production

Cereal production continues to dominate the cropping pattern in the state. In 2023-24 agricultural
year, 6357.9 thousand hectares of land were cultivated with cereals. Between 2022-23 and
2023-24 agricultural years, the area dedicated to cereals increased by 7.1 percent. Additionally,
the area under pulses and oilseeds has seen significant growth. The area allocated to fibre crops,
especially jute, rose substantially by 10.6 percent between 2022-23 and 2023-24. However, in
2023-24, the area under sugarcane cultivation has declined. Overall, the area under crop production

has increased significantly.

Rice in Kharif and wheat in Rabi constitute the primary crop cycles in Bihar. In 2023-24, the total
rice production was 9,522.9 thousand tonnes, with 90.1 percent being rice (aghani). Between
2022-23 and 2023-24, rice production has increased by 21.0 percent, Wheat is the main rabi crop
in Bihar, with its total production in the state being 7,306.7 thousand tonnes in 2023-24, reflecting
an increase of 10.7 percent over 2022-23,

In recent years, maize cultivation has attracted significant interest due to its high demand for
various industrial uses, Between 2020-21 and 2023-24, maize production rose from 3,521.4

thousand tonnes to 5,864.9 thousand tonnes, an increase of 66.6 percent.

In contrast to rice, wheat, and maize, the production levels of other crops, such as oilseeds and
pulses, were small and primarily produced for subsistence. Among the fibre crops, jute is the major
crop produced in Bihar. Between 2022-23 and 2023-24, jute production has increased by 24.6
percent. The sugarcane production has declined marginally between 2022-23 and 2023-24.
