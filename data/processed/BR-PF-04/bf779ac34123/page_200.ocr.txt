To understand the nature and scale of operations of industries in Bihar, a few important parameters
are considered here for discussion. These parameters are — the number of factories, fixed capital,
working capital, persons engaged (workers, employees other than workers, and unpaid family
members/proprietors, etc.), the value of output, and net value added. The position of Bihar, in the
context of national scenario of industries, is presented in Table 4.4. Though the number of factories
in Bihar has varied within a small range over the last few years, the quantum of fixed capital has
increased significantly. For instance, in 2022-23, the amount of fixed capital in the factories of
Bihar was Rs. 31.95 thousand crore, reflecting an increase of 60 percent in a year. The share of
fixed capital of Bihar in the total fixed capital of India has increased from 0.54 percent in 2021-22
to 0,78 percent in 2022-23, The value of output has grown by 53 percent in Bihar between
2021-22 and 2022-23. Furthermore, the share of the value of output produced in Bihar, in the total
value of output produced in India, was 0.98 percent in 2022-23, showing an increase from 0.78
percent in 2021-22. All these factors indicate a steady growth of the industrial output in Bihar.
