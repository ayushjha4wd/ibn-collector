9.4 Public Distribution System (PDS)

The Bihar State Food and Civil Supplies Corporation Ltd., established in 1973 under the
Companies Act of 1956, operates under the Food and Consumer Protection Department of the
Government of Bihar. It manages one of India’s largest Public PDS with 49.400 outlets,
distributing essential commodities like wheat and rice to beneficiaries under the Antyodaya and
National Food Security Act. As the nodal agency for implementing PDS in Bihar, the corporation
has introduced the e-PDS system to enhance transparency, ensure accountability, and prevent

irregularities in the distribution process.

The Public Distribution System is a shared responsibility between the Central and State
governments. It was introduced to manage and distribute essential food grains, addressing
malnutrition among the poor. Designed as a supplementary scheme, PDS provides basic
commodities at subsidized rates, including wheat, rice, and pulses. The Central government
oversees the procurement, storage, transportation, and allocation of commodities to the states. In
contrast, State governments are tasked with identifying eligible families, issuing ration cards, and

managing the operation of Fair Price Shops.

Considering social and economic backgrounds, the strategic allocation of Fair Price Shops (FPS)
and PDS benefits is critical for promoting social harmony and uplifting marginalized and
disadvantaged communities within the State. Ensuring the allotment of FPS according to social
background helps maintain societal harmony and fosters inclusivity. Data presented in Table 9,12
provides aggregated information on PDS shop ownership by social background within the State.
The number of PDS outlets increased from 48,512 in 2021 to 49,400 in 2024. The share of SC and
BC PDS outlet ownership has risen from 18.7 percent and 27.5 percent in 2022 to 19.0 percent
and 27.9 percent in 2024, respectively. For other categories, the percentage share remains largely

unchanged compared to previous years.
