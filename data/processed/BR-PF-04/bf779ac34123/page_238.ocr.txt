Pattern of Employment

The PLFS considers three employment statuses according to the nature of employment. These are:
self-employed, regular wage/salary earners, and casual labour. Self-employed status is further
subdivided into own-account workers, employers, and helpers in household enterprises, The data
reported in Table 5.4 suggests that a large portion of self-employed workers were either engaged
in agricultural activities or run their own enterprises. In Bihar, 61.2 percent of male workers were
self-employed. Further, 28.5 percent of all male workers in Bihar were casual labour, working on
daily wages without any job contracts or job security, The proportion of male self-employed and
male casual workers in Bihar was higher than the all-India average. In contrast, only 10.3 percent
of male workers received regular wage/salary earnings. The share of regular wage/salary earners
in Bihar was 15.1 percentage points lower than that at the all-India level. The employment statuses
of male workers in Bihar as well as in other states of India, suggest that a large number of workers

were engaged in the informal sector where the nature of work is precarious.
