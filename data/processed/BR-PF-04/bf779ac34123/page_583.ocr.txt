Khelo-India : The Khelo India Programme is a national scheme for the development of sports in
India. It was launched in 2018 with the objective of mainstreaming sports as an instrument for
national development. The Khelo India Youth Games Programme has been started to take the
talent of players to new heights. With this important step, efforts are being made to improve India's
ranking in the Olympics as well. It is a Central Sector Scheme which is 100% funded by the Central
government. The selected athletes will be entitled to a scholarship amount of Rs.5 lakh for eight
consecutive years. To enable sportsmen to pursue both studies and sports, the programme aims to
identify and promote 20 universities in the country. The Khelo-India programme is being
conducted in all the schools of Bihar.

Availability of National Initiative for Proficiency in Reading with Understanding and
Numeracy (NIPUN) Teaching Learning Material (TLM) kit in Schools : All government
primary schools have been provided with the TLM Kit for Toy-based pedagogy in the classroom,
as stated in the NEP to make classrooms resourceful and empowering the teachers with a ‘tool kit’
to achieve the targeted learning outcomes of students, to help them understand complex concepts
and to develop curiosity towards learning. It consists of Alphabet, Flash Cards & Story Books,
Chain Link, 3-D shapes, students’ and schools’ kits. Five separate sets of school kits for Grade |
to 5 were provided under the TLM kit. Each set consists of materials for Language and
Mathematics, including grade-specific posters and charts. Children Kit is also provided with 4
workbooks of Hindi. English, Maths and Urdu for around 55 lakh students of Grade | to 3.

The entire kit underwent vetting process at the State Council of Educational Research and Training
(SCERT), Bihar, After the TLM reaches schools, an online orientation is organized to orient
district-level officials, block-level officials and head teachers on the use of this Kit. The same kit
is now being used in government primary schools of the state.
