e-Shikshakosh ; The education value chain in Bihar runs on several independent systems in bulk
data through yarious information systems like Shaala Siddhi, BEST, UDISE+, Online Medha soft
Platform for DBT, etc. The State government has an integrated digital platform called e-
Shikshakosh to ensure the robust governance of schools and effective service delivery. It serves as
a single centralized dynamic platform for monitoring all key parameters to ensure the effective
delivery of quality education to all the schools in the state. It also serves as a two-way
communication platform to address issues and grievances of schools, and education officials at
multiple levels.

It has different modules : (i) Master Module (to manage the data of State, District, Block, Village
& Panchayats), (ii) School Management Module (managing the details of teachers and students),
(iii) Teacher Management Module (for managing various sub-modules such as the Teacher
Registration and Approval, Update Service Status, Teacher Deputation, Promotion and Transfer,
Industrial Training, Leave, Training, and MIS Report in the application), (iv) Grievance
Management Module (help Teachers and Public register their grievances), (vy) Student
Management Module (maintains and manages all the Academic details, Achievements, and
Progression of the Student), (vi) User Management Module, (vii) Mid-Day Meal Management
Module (maintains and manages the details of items available in the daily menu), (viii) Committee
Management Module (maintains all the details of the School Committees such as Member details,
Meetings, Committee Reconstitution, and Bank Details), (ix) Grant and Fund Management
Module, (x) Holiday Management Module, (xi) Notification Management Module, (xii) Online
assessment and Question bank, (xiii) School Inventory Module, (xiv) Library Inventory Module,
(xv) School Monitoring and (xvi) Selfie Based Teacher Attendance through Mobile App.
