1.6 Prices and Inflation

Price changes significantly impact economic activities, influencing decisions related to
consumption, saving, and investment. The Central Statistical Office (CSO) tracks the evolution of
prices in the country through the Consumer Price Index (CPI). The CSO provides data both at the
all-India level and for individual states, and it regularly publishes price indices for rural and urban
areas. The percentage increase in the prices of commodities and services over a specific period is
referred to as the inflation rate.

Table 1.9 presents the state-wise Consumer Price Index (CPI) for September 2023 and September
2024, along with the year-on-year percentage changes in the price index over this period. In Bihar,
the year-on-year percentage change in prices was 7.6 percent in rural areas and 7.1 percent in urban
areas, resulting in an overall increase of 7.5 percent in general prices across the state during this
period, Compared to the base year price of 2012, the CPI for Bihar as of September 2024 remains
lower than that of many other states in the country for both rural and urban areas.
