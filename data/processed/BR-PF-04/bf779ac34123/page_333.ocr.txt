Aangan App for Supervision & Monitoring of All AWCs by Lady Supervisors & CDPOs :
This application is used during the inspection done by Lady Supervisors and CDPOs. The
inspection report is instantly uploaded on the Aangan Dashboard on the NIC Server which
provides Real-Time data on how many Anganwadi Centers are opening, how many are providing
Supplementary Nutrition, how many children are present, etc. It is also used to record the
attendance of Anganwadi Workers and Helpers, and upload the vouchers of purchases done for
the Supplementary Nutrition Programme (SNP). Based on this attendance and uploaded vouchers,
the Anganwadi Workers are paid their honorarium through DBT and the SNP fund is sent to the
Bank account of the Anganwadi Vikas Samiti.
