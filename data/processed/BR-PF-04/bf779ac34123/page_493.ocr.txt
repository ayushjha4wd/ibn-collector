Mukhyamanrti Balika (Intermediate) Protsaahan Yojana

The state scheme was started in 2018-19 to empower girls and encourage them to enroll for Higher
education. Under the scheme, an incentive amount of Rs. 25,000 is given to unmarried girls who
have passed intermediate. Previously, an incentive amount of Rs. 10,000 was given, which was
enhanced to Rs. 25000 in 2022-23. In the year 2023-24, Rs. 1390.33 crore was distributed among
5.56 lakh girls.

Mukhyamantri Balika (Snatak) Protsaahan Yojana

Under the scheme, Rs. 50,000 is given to the unmarried/married girl students who haye cleared
their graduation examination. The scheme was started in 2018-19 and previously, an incentive
amount of Rs, 25000 was given as encourage amount, but from April 01, 2021 the amount has
been revised and now Rs. 50,000 is given to each girl student who has passed her graduation
examination, Under this scheme, Rs. 600 crore was distributed among 1.40 lakh
unmarried/married girls in 2023-24.

Mukhyamantri Kishori Swasthya Yojana

The scheme was started in 2014-15 to sensitise girls regarding their health needs. Under this
scheme, Rs. 300 is being transferred to the account of the girl student studying between class VII
and XII. The amount will be transferred to their bank accounts only when they achieve 75 percent
attendance in their government schools. In 2023-24, Rs. 28.40 crore was distributed among 9.46
