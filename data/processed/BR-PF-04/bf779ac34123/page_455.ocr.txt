11.3 Achievements under Annual Credit Plan (ACP)

Banks play a vital role in supporting various sectors of the economy through the development of
the Annual Credit Plan (ACP). This plan outlines the credit allocation for different sectors and
serves as a benchmark for assessing progress. The State Level Bankers' Committee and the Reserve
Bank of India review the ACP and its achievements to ensure that credit flows effectively to all

regions and sectors.
