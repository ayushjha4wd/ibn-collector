Residential Training for Agricultural Equipment Repair

To train mechanics in agricultural equipment repair, Rs. 531.75 lakh has been approved under the
Agricultural Mechanization State Scheme. Training is conducted at the Bihar Agricultural
University, Bhagalpur and Dr. Rajendra Prasad Central Agricultural University, Pusa, Samastipur,
and agricultural engineering workshops in Bhojpur, Muzaffarpur, and Purnea. The trainees receive
26-day residential training and are awarded a certificate along with a free toolkit worth Rs. 4200
upon completion. In 2023-24, 769 youths were trained through 32 training sessions.

Sub Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM) 2023-24

For the Sub Mission on Agricultural Mechanization, the Government of India allocated
Rs. 3866.23 lakh (60 percent) and the State government Rs. 2577.48 lakh (40 percent), totalling
Rs. 6443.71 lakh. Under the Custom Hiring Centre, Rs, 2000 lakh was allocated for 500 centres,
with a subsidy of 40 percent (up to Rs, 4 lakh) on centres, costing up to Rs. 10 lakh. To establish
the agricultural equipment bank in selected villages, the State government has allocated Rs, 1000
lakh for 125 agricultural equipment banks with an 80 percent subsidy (up to Rs. 8 lakh) on
equipment costing up to Rs. 10 lakh, To establish the Special Custom Hiring Centre for Crop
Residue Management, the State government has allocated Rs, 1044.00 lakh for 87 centres costing
up to Rs, 20 lakh each with subsidy of Rs, 12 lakh (80 percent subsidy on equipment). These
centres were opened in Patna and Magadh divisions to reduce crop residue burning. In 2023-24,
to subsidise agricultural equipment, the total allocation was Rs. 2316.16 lakh for 20 types of
agricultural equipment, in which the shares of the Central and State governments were Rs. 1389.70
lakh and Rs. 926.46 lakh, respectively. For the district-wise details of farm implements, see Table
