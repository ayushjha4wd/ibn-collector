Air Quality Index (AQD

The BSPCB provides information on ambient air quality and the Air Quality Index (001) through
an electronic display board and the CPCB/BSPCB websites. The AQI acts as a tool that simplifies
complex air quality data from various pollutants into a single numerical value (Index Value), along
with a name and colour coding. There are six categories of AQ: Good (0-50), Satisfactory
(51-100), Moderate (101-200), Poor (201-300), Very Poor (301-400), and Severe (>401). In
winters, air quality deteriorates to very poor and severe levels due to the condensation of fine
particulate matter closer to the ground. The levels of particulate matter in the air are influenced by
human activities and geographical factors. The observations of the same are presented in Table
14.17 for three cities during 2021-22 to 2023-24.

noise levels in a number of neighbourhoods in Patna in order to develop monitoring systems and
