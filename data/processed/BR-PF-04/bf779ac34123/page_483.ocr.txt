Lohiya Swachh Bihar Abhiyan (LSBA)

The Lohiya Swachh Bihar Abhiyan Phase-I] and SBM specifically enhance the abilities of
individuals and communities in rural Bihar, fostering a people’s movement aimed at ensuring the
sustainability of Open Defecation Free (ODF) practices. The community members continue to
engage in safe and hygienic practices, and every village has established systems for managing
solid and liquid waste. Currently, solid and liquid waste management initiatives have commenced
in 7764 Gram Panchayats, For 2024-25, specific objectives have been outlined for the Lohiya
Swachh Bihar Abhiyan - Phase II, all aimed at fulfilling the overarching aim of “Swachh Gaon-
Samriddh Gaon”, one of the Saat Nishchay goals:

> To provide new and migrant families access to individual toilets, 6.66 lakh Individual
Household Latrines (IHHLs) are proposed to be constructed. Beneficiaries will receive

incentive money after completing the construction themselves.

> More Community Sanitary Complexes will be constructed.
