Future Power Availability

It is important to see the power demand and supply in the upcoming years from policy perspective.
As shown in Table 8.4, the projected peak availability of power is estimated to be in a deficit of
7.1 percent than the projected peak demand as per the state generation plan during 2024-25 and
15.1 percent in 2025-26. However, there is surplus energy availability of 14.3 percent in 2024-25
and 6.3 percent during 2025-26 as per the estimated energy requirement at the state periphery. The
deficit in peak demand availability will be bridged by additional power purchase from the Energy
Exchange.
