In Bihar, it is estimated that 53.53 lakh hectares of land can be brought under major and medium
irrigation projects, while 64.01 lakh hectares of land can be irrigated through minor irrigation
facilities. As of 2023-24, the irrigation through major and medium irrigation projects is available
in 37,72 lakh hectares out of 53.53 lakh hectares. This implies that 70.5 percent of the potential
land has already been brought under the major and medium irrigation projects, Similarly, 75,2
percent of the potential land to be irrigated through minor irrigation projects has already been
developed. In case of minor irrigation projects, groundwater lifling has been the primary source.
