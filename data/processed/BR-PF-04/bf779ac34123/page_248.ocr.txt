Bihar State Migrant Labourer Accident Grant Scheme : This scheme provides financial
assistance to the dependents of migrant workers in the event of accidents. If a migrant worker dies
due to an accident while working outside Bihar or abroad. the dependents of the deceased will
receive Rs, 2,00,000 within 180 days following the incident, The Government of Bihar has
amended the Bihar State Migrant Labour Accident Grant Scheme Rule, 2008 and has notified the
Bihar State Migrant Labour Accident Grant Scheme (Amendment) Rule, 2023, The compensation
amounts have been increased. The compensation for death resulting from an accident has risen
from Rs. 1,00,000 to Rs. 2.00,000. The compensation for permanent total disability has been raised
from Rs. 75, 000 to Rs. 1,00,000. In the case of permanent partial disability, the compensation has
increased to Rs, 50,000 from Rs, 37,500, In 2023-24, there were 213 deceased migrant workers,
and their dependents received a total of Rs. 2.32 crore in compensation (see also Table A 5.1),

Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board : According to PLFS,
2023-24, 18.2 percent of usually working persons were engaged in work in the construction sector
in Bihar, This sector was the second most significant sector, following agriculture, forestry, and
fishing in Bihar, The majority of construction workers were employed informally, with minimal
or no social security measures in place. To safeguard this substantial segment of the workforce,
the State government established the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare
Board (henceforth the Board). Initially, the Board focused on registering construction workers. In
2023-24, 405.01 thousand construction workers were registered with the Board. Over the past five
years (2019-20 to 2023-24), the cumulative number of registered construction workers has reached
1772.01 thousand. In 2023-24, the number of beneficiaries was 109.63 thousand. The total
sanctioned amount for that year was Rs. 694,80 crore, reflecting a 50 percent increase compared
to the funds allocated in 2022-23. The total amount of funds utilised was Rs. 622.70 crore, resulting
in a utilisation rate of 90 percent.

Between April and December 2023, the Board organised 6,041 camps at the block and panchayat
levels to promote the schemes implemented by the Board.
