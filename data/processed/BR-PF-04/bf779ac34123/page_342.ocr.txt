In recent years, the per capita consumption of energy in the state has also increased significantly,
increasing from 134 Kwh in 2012-13 to 363 Kwh in 2023-24. Financially, Bihar has made
substantial progress, with the aggregate technical and commercial (AT & C) losses declining from
45.41 percent in 2012-13 to 21.74 percent in 2023-24, surpassing targets fixed by the Ministry of
Power under the Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme (RDSS).

In the power generation segment, in line with the Central government's targets, the state is focusing
on increasing the share of renewable energy, Meanwhile, the transmission infrastructure is being
developed to cater to the additional generation capacity, especially in renewable energy. It is
planned to produce around | Gigawatt (GW) of renewable power in the coming years. The state is
implementing the Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Mission, wherein all government buildings will have grid-
connected rooftop solar plants. Bihar has one of the highest population densities in the country,
resulting in a scarcity of land in the state, Whatever land is available, it is either very fertile and
entirely under cultivation or waterlogged. There is a small area bordering Jharkhand, where solar
power generation is feasible. To tackle this issue, floating solar plants are being developed in the
state, While the government is looking at all the aspects of the value chain, the consumer is at the
centre of everything. There is a focus on enhancing consumer satisfaction with quality and round-
the-clock power supply.

The state’s commitment to providing sustainable power is evident from continuous infrastructural
development in the power transmission network. The number of grid substations increased from
45 in 2005 to 170 in October 2024, while the length of transmission lines expanded from 5000
circuit kilometres (ckm) in 2005 to 20393 ckm in 2024. These developments have laid a strong
foundation for providing reliable, quality and sustainable power to meet the growing demands of
the population and industries.

Recently, for its outstanding performance, the North Bihar Power Distribution Company
Limited (NBPDCL) received the prestigious Global CSR Award 2024 in the “CSR Needs of
the Community and Region of the Year’ category, organised by Global Energy and
Environment Foundation. Bihar State Power (Holding) Company Limited (BSPHCL) also
received the SKOCH Gold Award in the Power and Energy category for pioneering the
SAMAST project in the State Load Dispatch Centre (SLDC). Also, Bihar’s State Load Dispatch
Centre has gathered the 37 LDC Excellence Award 2024 for excellence and outstanding
contributions in ensuring the integrated operation of the power system in Bihar by adopting
new technologies.
