Under the ‘“Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission’, 1629 Solar Street Light
Systems (SSLS) have been installed at various locations in four districts (Gaya,
Rohtas, Kaimur and Saharsa). For this, the total expenditure is Rs. 1.66 crore.

Under the ‘Mukhyamantri Gramin Solar Street Light Yojana (MGSSLY)’, the
installation of Solar Street Lights is being done by the Panchayati Raj Department,
with technical support from BREDA. In every ward, 10 Solar Street Lights are to be
installed, totalling 11.73 lakh Solar Street Lights at identified existing electric poles.
Presently, a total of 470,000 Solar Street Lights have been installed in Panchayats
across the state.

The Solar Street Lights have the in-built facility of Remote Monitoring System
(RMS), through which the working status and different technical data from each light
can be monitored remotely on the dashboard.

A central monitoring system will be established at the headquarters of all the districts
for all Solar Street Lights.

Six Manufacturers of different components of Solar Street Lights (i.e. Luminary, PV
Module and Lithium battery) have been empaneled to set up their manufacturing units
in Bihar. Three manufacturing units of Luminary and Lithium batteries and one unit
of Solar PV modules have been established.
