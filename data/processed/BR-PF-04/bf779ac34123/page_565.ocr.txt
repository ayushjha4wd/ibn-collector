13.3 Sustainable Development Goals

The main agenda of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) is inclusiveness and improving
outcomes for all people within society. In particular, the agenda of SDG captures the need of
children, youth, persons with disabilities, refugees, migrants and many more. Monitoring a
country’s average level of progress towards the 17 SDGs, and the 169 targets that underpin them,
already poses significant data challenges. Going beyond the average to measure outcomes for
different population groups is still harder. Within the National Indicator Framework, 49 indicators
of SDG are directly related to children in the scope of survival, development, protection and
participation, As per the SDG India Index 2023-24 report, released in July, 2024, out of 113
indicators, 28 indicators across 7 SDGs are directly related to children. The goal-wise scores of
these in Bihar are presented in Chart 13.5. Out of 28 indicators, Bihar has scored better than the

national average on one-third (9 out of 28) indicators.
