In the public sector, a Health Sub-Centre represents the most remote and initial point of interaction
between the primary health care system and the community. A Sub-Centre engages with the
community at the grassroots level, delivering all primary health care services, According to
government regulations, a Sub-Centre should be established to serve a population of 5,000 in non-
tribal rural regions, and a PHC needs to be created for a population of 30,000 in similar areas.
Additionally, a CHC is required to function as a Referral Centre for every four to five PHCs,
catering to a population ranging from 80,000 to 120,000, In 2023, there are 35 district hospitals,
67 referral hospitals, 54 sub-divisional hospitals, 534 PHCs, 10399 Sub-Centres and 1399 APHCs;
the last three types add up to 12332 health centres. Thus, in 2024, for per 10 lakh of population,
there are about 122 health centres in the state (Table 12.4). The district-wise details are given in
Table A 12.5 (Appendix).
