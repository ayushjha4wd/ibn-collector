Support to Urban Street Vendors (SUSV)

Apart from identifying street vendors in urban areas through a socio-economic survey, the Support
to Urban Street Vendors (SUSV) programme aims to provide logistical and financial assistance to
street yendors, The activities undertaken by SUSY include helping vendors open savings accounts,
organising financial literacy camps and conducting various training programmes. Furthermore,

through SUSV, street vendors have also benefited from credit card access and insurance schemes.

To safeguard the interests of footpath vendors, the "Bihar State Footpath Vendors (Livelihood
Protection and Sale Regulation) Rules, 2017" and the "Bihar State Footpath Vendors (Livelihood
Protection and Sale Regulation) Scheme, 2017" have been enacted. Town Vending Committees
(TVCs) have been established in all municipal bodies. Vending zones have been approved in 17
municipal bodies to accommodate the surveyed shopkeepers. Currently, one vending zone is
operational in Bhagalpur, 16 in Patna, and one each in Buxar, Motihari, Darbhanga, and Bihiya,
with three zones in Sitamarhi.

The State government has been conducting a biometric socio-economic survey of street vendors
in 141 cities, which continued in 2023-24. As of 2023-24, the survey has been completed for
1,91,978 street vendors, and 1,66,436 vendors have received their ID cards. During this period,
4,512 savings accounts were opened, and 120 financial literacy camps were organised,
Additionally, 5,875 vendors have been trained through capacity-building initiatives. Furthermore,
8,321 street vendors have been linked to the insurance scheme.

The stated objectives of Prime Minister Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi) are:
(i) to facilitate working capital loans of up to Rs. 10,000; (ii) to incentivise regular repayment; and
(iii) to reward digital transactions. For the first loan, the total number of eligible applications was
1,97,130 (cumulatively), and loans were disbursed for 1,23,694 eligible applications. As of
September 2024, the number of second and third loans obtained by eligible applicants was 24,575
and 3,257, respectively.
