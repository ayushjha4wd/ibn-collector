The distribution of deposits collected and advances made by different bank groups in Bihar as of
September 30, 2024, is illustrated in Chart 11.9. Public Sector Banks hold the largest share of
deposits mobilised in the state, and the credit extended. As of this date, these banks collected
deposits totaling Rs. 3.96 lakh crore, which accounts for 75 per cent of the total deposits collected
by all banks in Bihar. However, they extended credit of Rs. 1.76 lakh crore, representing only 59
per cent of the total advances made by all banks in the state. Consequently, the credit-deposit (CD)
ratio for Public Sector Banks stands at 44.4 per cent, significantly lower than the average CD ratio
of 56.3 per cent for all banks in Bihar.
