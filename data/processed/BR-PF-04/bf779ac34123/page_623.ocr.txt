(vii) Pucca Protection and Anti-erosion Work near Sidhi Ghat : The administrative approval
for pucca protection and anti-erosion work near Sidhi Ghat on the right bank of the Ganga
channel under the Bakhtiyarpur block in the Patna district has been accorded for an estimated
cost of Rs. 5,606.00 lakh. After completion of this project, it will prevent the possibility of
erosion at this site during excessive flow of current in Ganga. It will provide additional
protection to the Bakhtiyarpur city from floods. Also, devotees will get facilities during
various festivals and religious rituals.
