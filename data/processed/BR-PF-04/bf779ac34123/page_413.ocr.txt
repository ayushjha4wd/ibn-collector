Construction of Shelter for Old-age People

The State government has launched the Mukhyamantri Vruddhajan Ashray Sthal Yojana, This
scheme aims to provide a healthy and dignified life for destitute, neglected, and helpless elderly
individuals in urban areas by offering shelter and other amenities. For this, in 2023-24, the State
government has allocated Rs. 19.68 crore to the municipal bodies across the state. The scheme
aims to offer comprehensive solutions to the challenges encountered by elderly citizens in need,
regardless of their economic status. This initiative ensures that they have access to essential
services such as healthcare, entertainment, yoga, skill development for livelihood, and
opportunities for participation in various activities.

(i) | Under this scheme, each district headquarters will operate a shelter for the elderly, with
100 beds divided into two units of 50 beds each.

(ii) A government building will be selected for the shelter. If no government building is
available, a rented facility will be chosen.

(iii) The shelter will operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Beneficiaries will be provided
with food that ensures an average intake of 1,700 calories and 50 grams of protein each
day, focusing on quality and variety. Additionally, the residents will receive clothing and
other daily essentials, which will be given within 24 hours of their arrival.

A total provision of Rs, 20.00 crore has been made under the scheme for the financial year
2024-25.

Mukhyamantri Samagra Shahari Vikas Yojana

With rapid urbanization in the state, the need for robust infrastructure development has become
critical. Although the 74th Constitutional Amendment empowered ULBs and facilitated some
progress, current efforts remain inadequate. To address this issue, the State government is
launching the Mukhyamantri Samagra Shahari Vikas Yojana in 2024-25. This initiative
emphasises on an integrated approach, prioritizing the construction and renovation of wide roads
equipped with drainage systems, development of parks, beautification of ghats, and improvements
of reservoirs. The goal is to achieve holistic and accelerated urban development throughout the
state. Priority will be given to municipal roads that connect highways and serve larger populations,
and drainage systems in waterlogged areas that link to the outfall channels.

Additional features will include tree planting, constructing parking spaces, footpaths, street
lighting, underground cabling, and installing dividers where feasible. The renovation of lakes,
ponds, parks, and ghats will also be prioritized.
