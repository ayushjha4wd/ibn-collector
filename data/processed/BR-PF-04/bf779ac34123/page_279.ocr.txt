Some Prestigious Undergoing Projects of BSRDCL

J.P. Ganga Path (Digha to Deedarganj): The stretch of JP Ganga Path from Digha to Kangan
Ghat (15.5 kms) is now operational and provides direct connectivity from Eastern Patna to Western
Patna and also connects two major bridges on the river Ganga. The movement between North
Bihar and South Bihar has become much easier for road users. Presently, 20 kms length out of
total 20.5 kms has been completed and the main alignment up to Deedarganj is likely to be
completed by March 2025.

6-Lane Bihar New Ganga Bridge; The project is being constructed with the assistance of ADB
at an estimated cost of Rs. 4988.40 crore. The ADB assistance is Rs. 3000 crore and the rest is
from the State government. This project has a total length of 19.76 kms, consisting of 9.760 kms
long six-lane extra-dosed bridge over the Ganga river and approximately 10 kms of approach
via duct and roads, tolling and service facilities. The project originates at the NH-30 at Kachchi
Dargah in Patna, passes through Raghopur Diara and terminates at NH-103 near Chaksikandar
(Bidupur) in Vaishali district. The project is expected to be completed by September 2025. It will
substantially reduce the travel time to Patna from the surrounding regions, in particular, north
Bihar.

Sipara — Ram Govind Singh Mahuli Halt Elevated/Grade Road: Under phase-I, the
construction of Elevated/Grade Road (6.7 kms) along the eastern side of Patna-Gaya railway line
(from Sipara to Ram Govind Singh Mahuli Halt, Patna) has been completed at the cost of Rs. 850.0
crore. Further under phase-II, the construction of 4-lane Elevated Road (2.1 kms) from Mithapur
to Sipara (along with 2-lane ROB at Sipara) and 4-lane at-grade road from Ram Govind Singh
Mahuli Halt to Punpun (Lakshman Jhula) (2.2 kms), total length of the project 4.3 km along the
eastern side of the railway line, Mithapur-Sipara-Mahuli-Punpun road is in progress. The cost of
the project is Rs. 437.15 crore.

Bakhtiyarpur — Tajpur Bridge: Construction of greenfield bridge across the Ganga river,
connecting Bakhtiyarpur bypass of NH-31 near village Karjan and Tajpur on NH-28 (bridge-5.52
kms and approach road 45,5 kms) is in progress at a total revised cost of Rs. 2875.20 crore. The
project is expected to completed by February, 2026,
