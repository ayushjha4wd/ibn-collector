I-GRAM (Integrated GIS-based Road Assets Management System) : This is a GIS-based
project which aims to digitise the data of various roads under the Road Construction Department
and display that on I-GRAM. Road alignment and details of Expressways, NH, SH and MDR roads
are available on 1-GRAM, which can be obtained on a GIS Map with different layers viz. State,
District, Habitations, Urban Local Bodies Location, Habitation Boundary, Railway, Airport,
Industrial Area, ete.

Output and Performance-based Road Assets Maintenance Contract (OPRMC) : It is a
Preventive Maintenance Policy, under which the responsibility of maintaining the Service Level
and International Roughness Index (IRI) of roads is given to the contractor for a particular time.
Under this policy, along with the road crust, other road furniture like shoulder drainage, vegetation
and signage, etc. are also maintained. Under this system, a total of 9064 kms of roads were
successfully maintained in the first phase (2013 to 2018) at the cost of Rs, 2579 crore. Further,
more than 13,000 kms of roads are being maintained at a cost of Rs. 6655 crore in the second phase
(2019 to 2026), The State government took a new step for real-time monitoring and reporting of
road maintenance work in July, 2022 through its Control and Command Centre. At present, the
maintenance work of a total of 1282 roads (9817 kms) is being monitored through this Centre at
the Headquarter level and 11 Superintending Engineers, 44 Executive Engineers, 116 Assistant
Engineers and 217 Junior Engineers are inspecting the task.

Contractor e-Registration ; It was introduced in 2016 for the re-registration of contractors and
provides them with a digital platform where they can easily apply for their registration and pay the
required fee. They can also download their registration certificate from the portal after their
application gets approved.

Online Management, Monitoring and Accounting System (OMMAS) : It is a web-based
system for monitoring of schemes related to Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY). This
facilitates planning, scheduling, monitoring, tracking and execution of the scheme. OMMAS
comprises master data, rural road plan, proposal, tendering, execution, quality monitoring, receipt
and payments and maintenance module. A citizen can track the day-to-day progress of the scheme.

Anurakshan App : Bihar Rural Roads Maintenance Policy, 2018 has been implemented for an
effective management of rural road assets. Using this mobile App, roads are inspected by field
officers, duly capturing and geo-tagging photographs of five components, namely — Carriageway,
Shoulder and Embankment, Cross Drainage including Culverts and Bridges, Road Signage, and
Vegetation. Based on the captured field data, quarterly inspection and payment are generated using
weightage percentage reduction for every component of the scoring matrix. Accordingly. quarterly
allotment is generated online through the MIS web service. Apart from Anurakshan App, the Rural
Works Department has also developed the Bihar Rural Road Routine Maintenance System
