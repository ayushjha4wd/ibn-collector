In Bihar, the Integrated Cluster Action Plan (ICAP) and Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) for most
clusters have been approved. Supplementary funding is provided through the Critical Gap Fund
(CGF), capped at 30 percent of project costs or Rs. 30 crore, with a 60:40 cost-sharing between
the Centre and State.

From 2020-21 to 2024-25 (up to September 2024), the number of proposed works surged from
579 to 2,623, with Rs. 52.3 crore spent under the CGF in 2024-25, Notable initiatives include
constructing science and computer labs, kitchen sheds, solar streetlights, and RO water filters. Till
September 2024, 827 projects were completed, reflecting significant progress in the mission
(Table 9.19).
