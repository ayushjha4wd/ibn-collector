The expenditure at various PRI levels serves as a crucial indicator of fund utilization and the extent

of work accomplished at each respective level. It reflects the effective utilization of allocated funds
and the scope of developmental activities.

1. Gram Panchayat: At the grassroots level, Gram Panchayats primarily focus their expenditure
on local infrastructure, drinking water supply, sanitation, and rural development programmes.
Between 2019-20 and 2023-24, expenditure at the Gram Panchayat level declined by 9.1
percent, However, from 2021-22 to 2023-24, it saw a significant increase of 54.0 percent
(Table 9.16).

2. Panchayat Samiti: This intermediate tier concentrates on block-level schemes such as
education, health, and agricultural support, ensuring effective coordination among multiple
Gram Panchayats, Expenditure at the Panchayat Samiti level witnessed a substantial increase
of 906.8 percent from 2019-20 to 2023-24 (Table 9.17).

3. Zila Parishad: At the district level, Zila Parishads manage larger projects such as road
connectivity, district-wide welfare initiatives, and monitoring resource allocation for
subordinate tiers. Expenditure at this level also increased significantly, by 342.8 percent from
2019-20 to 2023-24 (Table 9.18).

Efficient expenditure at each level is essential for achieving developmental goals and addressing
local needs effectively.
