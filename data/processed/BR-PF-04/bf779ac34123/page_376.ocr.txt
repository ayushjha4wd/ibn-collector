Alternate banking through Customer Service Points (CSPs) has become vital tool for providing
financial services to SHG and non-SHG members in rural areas. Under the 'One GP One BC’
strategy, the Banking Sakhi model has been expanded to promote digital transactions, aiming for
at least one CSP per panchayat. JEEVIKA collaborates with business correspondents from major
banks such as BOB, BOI, Canara Bank, CBI, CSC/Digipay, DBGB, FINO, ICICI, IDFC, PNB,
RBL, 881, Spice, and (181, It supports the identification, training, and technical assistance of Bank
Sakhis while offering financial aid to establish (१575, These IIBF-certified Bank Sakhis receive
initial handholding support to operate CSPs effectively. Presently, 5,878 Bank Sakhis are
managing CSPs across the region.

To promote goat rearing 5,792 community cadres have been developed as Pashu Sakhis to deliver
essential goat-rearing services to SHG and non-SHG households. These services include
vaccination, deworming, insemination, castration, azolla pit development, shed construction, and
feed preparation. The Pashu Sakhi model currently supports 5,90,827 households.

Under the Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Abhiyaan, the Government of Bihar has designated ponds for
maintenance and fishery activities by SHG members. Additionally, 86 Matsya Sakhis are engaged,
supporting 714 households through livestock interventions.

The Didi Ki Rasoi initiative by JEEVIKA promotes a community-based canteen model in Bihar,
operated entirely by women. This initiative ensures the supply of quality, hygienic food while
creating livelihood opportunities for rural communities. Initially serving patients, doctors, nurses,
and visitors, it has expanded significantly. Currently, 214 Didi Ki Rasoi units operate across the
state, Ninety-five Didi ki Rasoi are functional in divisional and subdivisional hospitals, medical
colleges, 88 units in SC/ST residential schools, and 31 units in banks, registrar offices, and the
Bihar Institute of Mental Health and Allied Sciences (BIMHAS).

JEEVIKA also seeks to improve the livelihoods of SHG members running kirana stores and
grocery shops by establishing a network of 100 stores and forming direct partnerships with market
companies. This initiative ensures SHG members have access to quality grocery items while
providing profit margins for SHG-run kirana stores and Grameen Bazaars. Grameen Bazaar also
serves as a platform to market the products made by SHG members.
