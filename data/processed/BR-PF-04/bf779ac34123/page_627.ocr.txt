New Initiatives for Managing Disasters

(i) Formation of Bihar State Disaster Management Service Cadre : Disaster management is
one of the top priorities of the State government. Similarly, disaster management at the local
level is very important for the state. Now, disaster management is not only relief and
rehabilitation, but also reconstruction and rapid restoration of infrastructure. Therefore, for
the successful conduct of activities, dedicated officers are needed at the district, division and
state levels. To tackle this need, a total of 57 posts have been created under the “Bihar State
Disaster Management Services’? cadre, in which 43 posts are for Assistant Disaster
Management Officer, 11 posts for Deputy Disaster Management Officer and 3 posts for
Disaster Management Officers,

(ii) Creation of Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) : SOPs have been formulated by the
Disaster Management Department to deal with different types of disasters. SOPs for disaster
management during flood, drought and drinking water crisis management have been
prepared. Apart from this, action is being taken to formulate SOP for earthquake disaster
management.
