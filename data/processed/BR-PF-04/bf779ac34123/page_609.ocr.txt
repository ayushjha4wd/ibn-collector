All the above components are being implemented in Bihar. For the protection and conservation of
wild animals in Kaimur, Bhimbandh, Rajgir, Udaipur, Rajauli (Nawada) Wildlife Sanctuaries and
Gogabeel Conservation and Community Reserve, developmental works and management through
scientific techniques are done in order to conserve and enhance the habitat and keep it suitable for
wildlife. Provision/construction of resources, facilities and infrastructure, community
development (eco-development), publicity, training and execution of other relevant works, etc. are
undertaken on a regular basis.

In the areas outside of the wildlife sanctuaries of the state, the works undertaken are - conservation
of the endangered Greater Adjutant bird species in the Gangetic riverine tract in Bhagalpur,
protection and conservation of Black Buck, Sloth Bear and Ungulates, mitigation of human-bear
conflicts, man-wildlife conflict mitigation and conservation of Peafowl in its habitat development
in Aran village (Saharsa district),

Under the Component Recovery Programme for saving critically endangered species and habitats,
implementation and execution of various activities for the conservation and promotion of Gangetic
Dolphins, Crocodiles and bio-diversity in the river basin areas and Gandak river of Gopalganj,
Bettiah districts, including the Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary area, in the eastern part
of the Ganga river in Bhagalpur district, the outer areas of the Dolphin sanctuary in the Ganga
river of Buxar, Bhojpur, Patna, Munger and Lakhisarai districts, Supaul, Saharsa, Kosi-Mahananda
of Araria district are undertaken.

Additionally, Wildlife Habitat Development works are being done inside the Protected Areas and
outside the same under the State Scheme — ‘Vanvapraniyo Ki Surkasha Sanrakshan and Vikas’.
