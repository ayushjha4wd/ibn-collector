Namami Gange Scheme

The scheme was sanctioned for 12 districts in 2021-22: however, the implementation began in
2022-23, and the scheme was approved for three years. The names of the districts for which the
scheme was sanctioned are: Buxar, Bhojpur, Patna, Lakhisarai, Munger, Bhagalpur, Katihar,
Samastipur, Vaishali, Saran, Begusarai, and Khagaria, In these 12 districts, 14,000 hectares of land
were covered. It involved 16,744 farmers distributed in 700 groups. The financial allocation for
this scheme was Rs. 3016.65 lakh, The project is in its third year of implementation.

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PK VY) was implemented in 11 districts, The selected districts
are: East Champaran, Muzaffarpur, Darbhanga, Siwan, Rohtas, Gaya, Jamui, Saharsa, Kishanganj,
Banka, and Araria. This scheme has been implemented in over 5,620 hectares, It includes 6,122
farmers distributed across 281 groups. The project is in its second year of implementation. This is
a centrally sponsored scheme, and the cost of the scheme is shared between the Central and State
governments in a ratio of 60:40. Farmers receive financial assistance in three annual installments
of Rs. 12,000, Rs. 10,000 and Rs. 9,000 respectively.
