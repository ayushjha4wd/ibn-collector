CSS Project for Tiger and Elephant: Project Tiger and Elephant have been merged as one
scheme as per the decision of the Expenditure Finance Committee which was further approved by
the Cabinet and named as CSS Project Tiger and Elephant (PT&E) from 2023-24 onwards.

This scheme is implemented in the tiger reserve areas declared by the National Tiger Conservation
Authority. Under Valmiki Tiger Reserve, various works for security, conservation and
management of reserve like anti-poaching and surveillance, development/construction of wireless
information system, establishment of patrolling camps/Anti-poaching camps/buildings/water
holes, procurement of night vision devices, uniforms for field personnel, purchase of binoculars,
employment of ex-servicemen and Home Guards, employment of local workforce for patrolling,
employment of anti-poaching squads, maintenance of existing water holes, employment of
workers for Nakas, research activities, purchase of field equipment, etc, are undertaken, some of
which are detailed below:-

Habitat improvement and management - development of grassland, cleaning of weeds, monitoring
of wild animals, construction of fire lines for fire protection, monitoring of wild animals by tiger
trackers, construction and maintenance of waterholes, etc.

Strengthening of infrastructure - upgradation of road network, provision of patrolling and rescue
vehicles, establishment of e-surveillance, housing for field personnel, hardware and software for
manpower training, etc.

Eco Development - Health camps for local villagers, distribution of solar lights, promotion of
tourism, welfare facilities in fringe villages, creating alternative livelihood opportunities for local
communities, including tribals, etc.

The goals of Project Elephant in the state are supporting research on ecology and management of
elephants, management of elephant rescue and rehabilitation centre inside Valmiki Tiger Reserve,
development of anti-poaching infrastructure, capacity building and rehabilitation of wild elephants
that have strayed from West Bengal, Jharkhand and Nepal into Bihar. Implementation of various
related programmes like control of human-elephant conflict has also been emphasised.

CSS-Integrated Development of Wildlife Habitat (IDWH)/ Wildlife Habitat Development:
Integrated Development of Wildlife Habitat (IDWH) is a centrally sponsored scheme. This scheme
has three components:

(1) Protection and conservation of wildlife and its habitat in Protected Areas.

(2) Protection and conservation of wildlife outside Protected Areas.

(3) Recovery programme for saving critically endangered species and habitats.
