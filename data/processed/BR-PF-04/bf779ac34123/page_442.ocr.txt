Although the rise of digital banking platforms has reduced the need for in-person visits to bank
branches, having a local branch remains essential for meeting the credit requirements of
individuals and businesses. Table 11.3 illustrates the distribution of the bank branch network
among Scheduled Commercial Banks in major states of India from 2020 to 2024. As of March 31,
2024, Bihar accounts for only 4.9 percent of the total bank branches in India, which is less than its
share of the national population. Additionally, the percentage of total bank branches in Bihar has
remained unchanged over the past five years. This lower share of bank branches indicates that
there are more people per branch in Bihar than in other states, underscoring the necessity of
expanding the banking network in the state.
