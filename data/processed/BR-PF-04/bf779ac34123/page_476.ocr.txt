Institutional Delivery

Maternal health is a core function of all public health services. According to the National Family
Health Survey (NFHS) data, the rate of institutional deliveries in Bihar rose nearly four-fold from
NFHS-3 (2005-06) to NFHS-5 (2019-21). Additionally, it's noteworthy that institutional deliveries
in rural regions also saw a four-fold increase, while urban institutional deliveries nearly doubled
within the span of 15 years. It is important to mention that this growth was relatively lower for
India. From NFHS-3 to NFHS-5, India experienced an increase in institutional deliveries by 49.9
percentage points, rising from 38.7 percent to 88,6 percent. In Bihar, this rise was 56.3 percentage
points, going from 19.9 percent in 2005-06 to 76.2 percent in 2019-21. These institutional delivery
rates reflect the strength of the state's healthcare system.

Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)

JSY is a demand promotion and conditional cash transfer scheme for promoting institutional
delivery. The scheme is a safe motherhood intervention under the National Health Mission (NHM)
launched in April, 2005 as a Centrally Sponsored Scheme. Reducing maternal and infant mortality
is one of the key goals of the NHM. JSY is a 100 percent centrally sponsored scheme that provides
cash assistance with delivery and post-delivery care. Under this scheme, the states are divided into
Low Performing States (LPS) and High Performing States (HPS) based on their performance in
institutional delivery rates. The Yojana has identified ASHA, as an effective link between the
