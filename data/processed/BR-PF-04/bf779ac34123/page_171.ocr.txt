Assistance to Farmers in Contingent Situation

Diesel Subsidy Scheme : Duc to erratic monsoons and drought-like situations, farmers are
provided a diesel subsidy to irrigate their crops. Assuming diesel consumption of 10 litres per
hectare of cultivated land, farmers receive diesel (at the rate of Rs. 75 per litre) for two irrigations
to protect their rice seedlings and three irrigations to save their standing crops in severe drought
conditions. In 2023-24, 6.3 lakh farmers benefited from the subsidy, and the total amount of the
subsidy was Rs. 102.93 crore, In 2024-25 (up to September 30, 2024), 1.85 lakh farmers received
the diesel subsidy, costing the state Rs. 34.93 crore (see Table 3.48).
