Residential schools ; The SC/ST Welfare Department runs 66 residential schools for SC students
and 25 for ST students with a capacity of 32.640 SC and 11,600 ST students. The State government
provides funds for the basic needs of the students like daily needs, study materials, food, clothing,
medicine, etc. The arrangement of food (including refreshments), supply of uniforms, cleaning of
school premises and laundry service for the students of all residential schools run under the
Department, during the period of accommodation, has been approved to be done through the
community organization supported by Jeevika,

The State government has decided to upgrade all residential schools to 10+2 level. Buildings of
10+2 residential schools with a capacity of 720 beds are being constructed. The new building will
have residential facilities for teachers and non-teaching staff, a library, playground, etc. There is
also a provision for maintenance and furnishings for five years in the model estimate.

School Upgradation : All residential schools are to be upgraded to 10+2 (720 seats) and run in
co-education mode with the development of infrastructure. As 10 percent of ST students and 10
percent of SC students are enrolled in residential schools, so residential schools are for both SC
and ST categories, Also, a provision of 40 percent reservation for Mushahar/Bhuiya community
and 25 percent reservation for children of persons engaged in unhygienic works are provided in
the enrolment in residential schools. A budget provision of Rs. 458.42 crore has been made for the
construction and furnishing of SC/ST residential schools and hostels in 2024-25.

Hostels : The SC and ST Welfare Department runs 118 hostels for SC/ST students. The hostels are
provided with cots, mattresses, RO water, etc. Old hostel buildings are being reconstructed in a
phased manner. At present, total 117 hostels are functioning in the state for the convenience of SC
and ST students and 47 new ones are being reconstructed.

Chief Minister Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe Civil Service Promotion Scheme :
Under this scheme, a one-time incentive of Rs. 50,000 is given to the candidates who pass the
BPSC civil services preliminary examination . At the same time, candidates passing the UPSC
civil services preliminary examination are given an incentive of Rs. 1.00 lakh for the preparation
of the next stages of their examination. Till recent, 144 UPSC and 4136 BPSC, total 4280
candidates have benefitted under this scheme.
