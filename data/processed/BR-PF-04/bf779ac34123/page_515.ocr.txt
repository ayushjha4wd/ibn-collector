OBC Hostel: Through this initiative, hostels are built for OBC students either in campus of
University/College or nearby, Every district has been covered under this scheme. Currently 23

hostels are being operated in 20 districts.

Jananayak Karpoori Thakur Hostel: Under this scheme, a 100 beded hostel for OBC Students
is to be constructed in each district. All the hostels have been constructed out of which currently

36 hostels are operational and the rest of the 2 hostels will be operated soon.

Pre-Examination Training Scheme: Under this scheme, BC and EBC students are being imparted
free coaching for the preparation of competitive examinations such as UPSC, BPSC, SSC, Railway,
Banking etc. Two batches consisting of 60 students are being operated for each session (6 months). The
annual income ceiling under the eligibility criteria has been raised from Rs 1,00 lakh to Rs 3,00
lakh. Total 38 centres have been sanctioned across the state. The Smart Classes have been

sanctioned for each centre at the rate of Rs. 2.5 lakh per centre.

Mukhyamatri Vayavsayik Pathyakram Margdarshan evam Utpreran Yojana : To help the
students of Backward Classes & Extremely Backward Classes in their preparation for admission
in professional courses, like management, law, etc. and for appearing in job-oriented competitions
and to increase the participation in higher educational institutions, a new scheme called
Mukhyamatri Vayavsayik Pathyakram Margdarshan evam Utpreran Yojana was launched in
2020-21. Under this scheme in 2020-21, centres at Chandragupta Institute of Management, Patna
and Chanakya National Law University, Patna have been established where free coaching for such
purposes is imparted. In 2022-23, such a centre was established at the Lalit Narayan Mishra
Institute of Economic Development and Social Change Patna. In 2022-23, centres in seven
universities were sanctioned for the preparation of NET/GATE/Ph. D/M. Phil and for appointment
to teaching posts in higher educational institutions. Till now, 10 centres have been sanctioned. The
candidate whose family income limit is not more than Rs, 3.00 lakh per annum is eligible to avail

the benefit of the scheme.

Jananayak Library and Digital Study Centre : It has been developed in all Jananayak Karpoori
Thakur Hostels, OBC Welfare Hostels and OBC Girls Residential +2 High Schools running in the

state to facilitate and ensure quality education through digital mode.
