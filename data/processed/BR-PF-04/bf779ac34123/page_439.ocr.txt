The area-wise distribution of Bank branches in Bihar from March 2021 to March 2024 is illustrated
in Chart 11.4. As of March 31, 2024, the state has 8,045 Bank branches. Notably, 46.9 percent of
these branches are located in rural areas, 31.7 percent are in semi-urban areas, and 21,4 percent
are in urban areas. This distribution pattern reveals that banks have primarily expanded their
presence in Bihar by opening new branches in semi-urban areas, followed by urban areas. The
number of Bank branches in semi-urban areas has increased from 2,327 in March 2021 to 2,553
in March 2024, representing a growth of 9.7 percent. Meanwhile, the number of Bank branches in
urban areas has risen 6.4 percent, from 1615 branches in March 2021 to 1719 branches in March
2024. The number of branches in rural area has increased by one percent during these years.
