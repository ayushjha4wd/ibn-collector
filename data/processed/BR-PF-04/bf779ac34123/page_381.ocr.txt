Pradhan Mantri Formalization of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME)
Scheme: Launched under the Aatmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan by the Ministry of Food
Processing Industries, the Pradhan Mantri Formalization of Micro Food Processing
Enterprises (PMFME) Scheme aims to enhance competitiveness and formalize the
unorganized food processing sector. With a budget of Rs.10,000 crore for 2020-25, it
supports agri-food processing groups, including FPOs, SHGs, and cooperatives. In 2023-24,
BRLPS met its seed capital target, benefiting 12,637 SHG members with Rs. 45.81 crore
disbursed through the State Nodal Agency.

One Stop Facility Centre (OSFC): The OSFC promotes entrepreneurship by offering a
single-window assistance model for growth-oriented enterprises (with a turnover of Rs. 6
19017), It provides access to finance (Rs, 50 lakh per block) for markets, mentorship,
branding, and packaging. This scheme has been implemented in 50 blocks across 12 districts.
The scheme has funded 7,298 enterprises with a total disbursement of Rs. 30.04 crore.
