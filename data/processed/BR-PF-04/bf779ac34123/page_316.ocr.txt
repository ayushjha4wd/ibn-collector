CIS HC ; It is functional in the Patna High Court and CIS 3.2 is functional in all 79 locations of
Bihar. The Patna High Court was the first to start this facility from October, 2022. The Buxar
Judgeship has been chosen as a pilot district for implementation of CIS 4.0. Migration of CIS 3.2
to CIS 4.0 is also ongoing and the same will be completed within a month in all the Judgeships of
Bihar.

e-Filing ; Almost all cases and documents are being e-filed in the Patna High Court, since 2020,
and since 2023 in the District Courts of Bihar. More than 4 lakh cases have been done on the
e-filing portal, including the Test and Live Server. The total number of Ld. Advocates registered
on the e-filing portal are more than 8000, Steps are being taken to encourage the lawyers to e-file

their cases.

Integrated Criminal Justice System (ICJS) ; With the aid of the ICJS platform, the metadata of
the First Information Reports (FIR) and Chargesheets can be accessed by courts. Currently, all the
District and Sub-Divisional courts of Bihar are consuming the metadata related to FIRs as well as
Chargesheets from Police Stations across the state. Till date, a total of 13.60 lakh FIRs and 2.45
lakh Chargesheets’ data have been made available on the CIS application.
