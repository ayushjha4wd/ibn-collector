At the national level, the primary sector contributed 16.6 percent to the GVA in 2023-24, while in
Bihar, it accounted for 19.9 percent. The secondary sector comprised 28.7 percent of the GVA at
the all-India level and 21.5 percent in Bihar for the same year, Bihar’s share of the secondary sector
in GVA increased by 1.4 percentage points from 2017-18 to 2023-24, at a higher rate compared to
0.1 percentage point increase at the national level. The tertiary sector contributed 58.6 percent to
Bihar’s GVA in 2023-24, more than the 54.6 percent contribution at the all-India level.
