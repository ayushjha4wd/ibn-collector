CHAPTER - IX
RURAL DEVELOPMENT

Abstract

Bihar’s progress is deeply tied to the social and economic advancement of its rural areas,
which house a significant portion of the state's population. In recent years, the State
government has launched numerous initiatives aimed at the holistic development of these
regions. Collaborative efforts from various departments have significantly improved the
livelihood opportunities for the ultra-poor and marginalized communities. JEEVIKA has been
a key driver in creating livelihoods for rural women. Similarly, the Mahatma Gandhi National
Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS) has provided vital employment
opportunities for the unskilled and manual labourers. Housing schemes like the Indira Awas
Yojana/Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Grameen (IAY/PMAY-G) and the distribution of
homestead land have been instrumental in providing shelter to the disadvantaged rural
populations. The Public Distribution System (PDS) has bolstered food security and improved
the nutritional well-being of rural residents. These diverse initiatives reflect the government's

commitment to uplifting rural communities and driving inclusive development.

Introduction

Rural development is crucial for inclusive growth in Bihar, where nearly 90 percent of the
population resides in rural areas. It is vital for the state's progress as agriculture, a cornerstone of
the rural economy, is the second-largest contributor to Bihar's Gross State Domestic Product
(GSDP) after the services sector. While rural development initially focused on agriculture and
allied sectors, it has evolved into a multi-dimensional approach encompassing economic, social,
and cultural aspects to improve the quality of life in rural areas. It now aims to empower rural
communities to set their priorities through democratic institutions, focusing on economic growth,
infrastructure, social justice, and human capacity development. Recognizing these needs, the State
government has introduced various initiatives to address poverty, improve livelihood options, and
ensure development.
