Rejuvenation of Dead Rivers : The Department has initiated a scheme to rejuvenate the
old Lakhandei river in the Sitamarhi district and the Ganga river in Bakhtiyarpur. The
scheme, which began on February 15, 2018, involves the inter-linking of old and new river
streams, construction of a 3-km long new channel, and desiltation work of the 18.27-km long
old stream of the Lakhandei river. The old Lakhandei river, which spans 170 kms., will be
available for irrigation in the blocks of Sonvarsha, Bathnaha, Sitamarhi and Runnisaidpur of
the Sitamarhi district by a channel length of 21.27 kms.
