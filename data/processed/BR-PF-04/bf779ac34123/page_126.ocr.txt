Agriculture and Allied Activities play a vital role in the state. as a large portion of the population
relies on these activities for livelihood. Acknowledging the importance of this sector, the State
government allocates significant fiscal resources to support individuals engaged in related
economic activities. Expenditure in this sector has increased from Rs. 4,066 crore in 2019-20 to
Rs. 5,171 crore in 2023-24, with the share of capital outlay at 2.7 percent in 2023-24, Meanwhile,
expenditure on the Industry and Minerals sector has increased from Rs. 813 crore in 2019-20 to
Rs. 2,872 crore in 2023-24, with capital outlay constituting 25.1 percent of this expenditure in
2023-24.
