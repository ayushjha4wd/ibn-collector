Bihar Aadhaar Authentication Framework (BAAF) : It is an umbrella framework that enables
all Aadhaar-based transactions in the state and helps in identification and authentication of
beneficiaries electronically in a safe and swift manner. It makes service delivery more efficient,
helps remove ghost beneficiaries, and reduces leakages. More than 57 crore authentications by 14
Sub-AUAs (State Government Department that use the facility of Aadhaar User Authentication)
have been carried out since 2018. Further, 2 additional Sub-AUAs are likely to be added soon.

Email Service : The DIT also provides free Email Services to all Departments, following the
National Email Policy, The department currently serves more than 1.14 lakh users, of whom
44,000 have been added in the last year.

Mobile Service Delivery Gateway (MSDG) : Bihar is the leading state in terms of push SMS
usage through MSDG. The DIT provides free of cost SMS services to all user departments and
Public Sector Undertakings. Around 16 crore SMSs are sent per month on an average in Bihar. To
increase multiple access channels, Unified Messaging Platforms are also being explored, including
WhatsApp and other channels.

Establishment of Blockchain as a Service Infrastructure : The IT Department has recently
approved the project which aims to secure key transactions, documents and certificates being
issued by various departments of the State government through immutable Blockchain-based
security.

Skilling in IT and 165 Domain : DIT is currently providing technical skills training throughout
the state for employability of youths. The government is providing 9 different courses through its
124 Skill Development Centres for the purpose. Till date, more than 56,010 candidates have
received training. A total of 2200 persons received training in the financial year 2024-25 and 1838
persons are currently under training.

C-DAC Centre in Patna ; In order to facilitate adyanced training and research in ICT within the
state, DIT has helped C-DAC to set up its Centre in Patna. C-DAC has four units — (i) Cyber
Security and Forensics, (ii) Al Centre of Excellence, (iii) loT and Embedded Lab, and (iv) Centre
for Advanced Data Analytics and Decisions. More than 100 students are currently receiving
training in Emerging Tech areas.
