rapid growth of the state's Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP). The regional pattern of
economic development within the state could be assessed through district-level data on the Gross
District Domestic Product (GDDP) and the Net District Domestic Product (NDDP). The district-

Appendix. Furthermore, the data on district-wise consumption of petroleum products (including
petrol, diesel, and LPG) is presented in the Appendix. Table 1.10 presents a list of the three most
prosperous and the three most impoverished districts in the state based on various indicators.

There is a significant variation in the per capita Gross District Domestic Product (GDDP) at
constant prices (2011-12), an indicator often used to proxy average per capita income across the
district, For 2022-23, Patna’s estimated per capita GDDP is Rs. 1,21,396, followed by Rs. 49,064
for Begusarai and Rs. 46,795 for Munger. In contrast, the estimated per capita GDDP for Sheohar
is Rs. 19,561, Rs. 21,931 for Sitamarhi and Rs. 22,204 for Araria. Similarly, there is considerable
variation in the per capita annual consumption of petroleum products across the state. In 2023-24,
the yearly petrol consumption per 1,000 persons ranged from 4.9 metric tonnes (MT) in Lakhisarai
to 17.7 MT in Patna. The diesel consumption per 1,000 persons was highest in Patna at 35.9 MT
and lowest in Sheohar at 8.9 MT. Additionally, Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) consumption was
also highest in Patna at 24.9 MT, while it was 7.5 MT in Araria for the year 2022-23. These patterns
indicate that Patna is the most prosperous district in the state, based on these indicators.
