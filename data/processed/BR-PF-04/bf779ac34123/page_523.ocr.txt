Civil Seva Protsahan Yojana : Incentive amounts of Rs. 50,000 and 1,00,000 is given to the
women candidates clearing the preliminary test of Civil Services Examination conducted by BPSC
and UPSC, respectively, for preparation of the Mains stage. Total 1615 women candidates of
BPSC and 102 of UPSC have availed the benefit and a total of Rs. 9.95 crore has been paid so far
to the beneficiaries under this scheme.

Service Field Training : Women and Child Development Corporation provides training to women
and adolescent girls with the aim of vocational training and capacity development to make them
skilled/self-reliant in different types of business. There is also a provision for placement and self-
employment after successful completion of the training. Under the service sector, women are also
provided training in housekeeping, beautician, computer and sales management by different
training institutes having specific experience and qualification in the work field, This training is
given free of cost to the selected trainees through the guidelines and listed institutions of the Bihar
Skill Development Mission.

Skill Training : Women and Child Development Corporation has started a combination of
traditional and modern-era courses like sewing machine operators which were prevalent choices
for women and the new courses that are essential for health and other services like General Duty
Assistant, Emergency Medical Technician, Electrician, Mason, Bar Bander, etc. WCDC, Social
Welfare Department has selected 37 job roles and is planning to add more courses like Agriculture
Drone Technician and Bakery courses to find employment opportunities and increase
employability, The overall status of Skill Training under the Mukhyamantri Nari Shakti Yojna
