Rural Self-Employment Training Institutes (RSETI)

The RSETIs impart skills among youth by conducting Entrepreneurship Development
Programmes (EDPs) for four different purposes, namely, Agricultural Programmes, Product
Programmes, Process Programmes and General Programmes. Apart from such EDPs, there were
skill upgradation and growth programmes, In Bihar, eight banks, including the Rural Development
and Self Employment Training Institute (RUDSET Institute, Hajipur) sponsor the RSETIs in all
the districts of Bihar; a list of the banks is provided in Appendix (Table A 5.4).

JEEVIKA is the nodal agency for providing support to RSETIs in the State. The number of training
programmes and the number of candidates trained have seen an increase over the years.
In 2023-24, 973 training programmes were organised, and 30,434 candidates were trained in such
training programmes. Total expenditure to run the training programmes was Rs. 10.91 crore in
2023-24 (Table 5.34). The employment opportunities for the trained candidates have been
consistently high, As in 2022-23 and 2023-24, 80 percent of the trained candidates received
employment. The sponsoring banks provide credit support to the trained candidates to start their
own enterprise. The gender composition of the trainees was also in favour of women as more
women participated in the training programmes compared to men.
