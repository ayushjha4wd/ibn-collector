9.2 Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS)

The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS) aims to
guarantee the right to work. The scheme annually ensures at least 100 days of unskilled wage
employment to rural households. MGNREGS has been instrumental in addressing seasonal
unemployment and providing social security, especially for marginalized communities, including
SC and ST communities. The success of any scheme relies on its effective implementation, and
the State government has ensured adherence to the operational guidelines and provisions of
MGNREGS for its proper execution.

The relevant information related to MGNREGS is provided in Table 9.7. It is clear from the data
that job cards issued to households have decreased from 171.6 lakh in 2019-20 to 136.0 lakh in
2023-24. The number of households provided with employment has increased from 33.7 lakh in
2019-20 to 48.2 lakh in 2023-24. Employment generated has also massively increased from 1416.2
lakh person-days in 2019-20 to 2205.3 lakh person-days in 2023-24, an increase of 55.7 percent.
The average number of days of employment per household (person-days) has fluctuated in the past
five years.

The number of completed works under MGNREGS has constantly improved from 4.6 lakh in
2019-20 to 13.0 lakh in 2021-22. However, the same has declined from 10.9 lakh in 2022-23 to
6.3 lakh in 2023-24. The fund utilization under the scheme and the number of accounts opened
have also fluctuated between 2019-20 and 2023-24. Fund utilization was highest in 2021-22 with
98.1 percent as per MIS and 96.5 percent in 2023-24. Similarly, 104.9 lakh accounts (the highest)
were opened under the MGNREGS in 2021-22 and 83,1 lakh (the lowest) in 2019-20. The number
