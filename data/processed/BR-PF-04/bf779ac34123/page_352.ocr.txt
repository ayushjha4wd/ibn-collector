Among the various renewable energy sources, Bihar has significant potential primarily in solar
energy, benefiting from abundant and consistent sunshine throughout the year, However, there are
two major obstacles — first, due to its fertile land, most agricultural fields are occupied with
growing crops throughout the year, and second, the high population density means that urban areas
also have limited open spaces. Only a small percentage of land is suitable for solar energy
generation.

The strategy of the Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Mission, launched in 2019, is to generate an additional
150-200 MW of solar power, with 50 MW already operational. The state has grand plans to
establish around | GW of renewable energy in the near future.

Solar Plants by Bihar Renewable Energy Development Agency (BREDA)

To encourage the adoption of renewable energy, the State government has established an
organization known as the Bihar Renewable Energy Development Agency (BREDA). Currently,
it is implementing the installation of Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar Power Plants, Floating Solar
Power Plants, Ground-Mounted Solar Power Plants, Off-Grid Hybrid Rooftop Solar Power Plants,
and Solar Street Lights through various initiatives aimed at promoting and spreading renewable
energy technology throughout Bihar, while increasing the proportion of renewable energy in the
energy mix and leading to a decrease in carbon emissions. This initiative will aid in global cfforts
to lower greenhouse gas emissions and promote a cleaner environment, Below are the activities
being carried out by BREDA:

(i) Grid-Connected Rooftop (GCRT) Solar Power Plant at Government Buildings under
Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Mission Phase-I; Under the ‘Mukhyamantri Navin Evam Navikarniya
Saur Adhisthapan Yojana’ and ‘Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Mission’, Grid-Connected Rooftop
Solar Power Plants are being installed by BREDA. These power plants have a capacity of
500 KWp. These plants will save electricity bill of the various State government offices and
promote the use of renewable energy in the state. The State government buildings covered
by the scheme are — Nek Samvad Bhawan, CM House, Patna High Court, Bihar Museum,
Buddha Smriti Park, Planetarium, IGIMS, various ITIs, BDO offices, Primary Health
Centers, Panchayat Sarkar Bhawan, Government High Schools, लए, Between 2018-19 and
2024-25, a total of 2589 Solar Power Plants have been installed, having a total capacity of
22 MW ata cost of Rs. 100 crore.

(ii) Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar Power Plants at Private Residential Buildings: Under
the ‘Mukhyamantri Navin Evam Navikarniya Saur Adhisthapan Yojana’, Grid-Connected
Rooftop Solar Power Plants have also been installed at private residential buildings by
BREDA. A total of 113 Solar Power Plants were installed at private residential buildings,
which together have 583 K Wp capacity, at the cost of Rs. 3.08 crore.
