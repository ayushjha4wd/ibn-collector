8.6 Operational and Financial Status of DISCOM

An overview of the financial status of the two DISCOMs is presented in Table 8.10. The generation
and purchase of power (net of central transmission loss) in Bihar increased from 32,071 MU in
2020-21 to 47,295 MU in 2023-24, implying an increase of 15,224 MU in three years, With
increase in power consumption, the revenue realization has also increased, The cost coverage was
88.1 percent in 2020-21, which increased to 95.1 percent in 2023-24, The DISCOM has improved
its financial and operational efficiency as shown in Table 8.10.
