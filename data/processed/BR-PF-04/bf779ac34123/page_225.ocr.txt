In 2023-24, the growth in the number of enterprises, of different scales, was significantly high. In
fact, the number of units set up in 2023-24 was more than double the previous year. Consequently,
the total investment had increased 3.5 times between 2022-23 and 2023-24. Micro enterprises have
attracted six times more investment in 2023-24 than that in 2022-23. On the employment
generation front, micro and small enterprises had created 66.0 percent of the total employment
created by enterprises in 2023-24. Between 2022-23 and 2023-24, the total employment generation
across enterprises increased by 2.6 times (see Table 4.30).

In 2023-24, the major share of investment came in four types of enterprises, namely, food
processing, general manufacturing, plastic and rubber, and Information Technology, Information
Technology-Enabled Services, Electronics System Design and Manufacturing (IT/ITES/ESDM).
The total investment in these four enterprises was 93.4 percent of the total investment in enterprises
in 2023-24. Furthermore, textile enterprises have shown tremendous potential to generate
large-scale employment. In 2023-24, 126 textile enterprises with an investment of Rs. 419.7 crore
could create 27.588 jobs. or, on an average, 219 jobs per textile unit. The next best job creator was
