Solar Plants by NBPDCL and SBPDCL
Residential Solar Rooftop Project

As per the guidelines issued by the Union Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) under
the Grid-Connected Rooftop System (GCRT) Phase-II scheme, the distribution companies are
designated as the nodal agencies for implementing Residential Solar Rooftop projects in the state.
The Ministry allocated 5 MW Rooftop Solar capacity for Bihar (3 MW for SBPDCL and 2 MW
for NBPDCL) in 2019-20. Additional 20 MW capacity has been sanctioned for the installation of
rooftop solar plants, which is under progress. Apart from the Central government subsidy, the State
government has also allowed an additional 25 percent subsidy for all loads for installation of Solar
Rooftop Plants that are grid-connected. Online applications were invited from interested
consumers through the web portal for its installation. Till September 2023, solar panels have been
installed on the premises of 1780 households with a capacity of 6630 KW and the works are still
in progress. District-wise installation of Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar Plants is given in Appendix
Table A 8.3. Additionally, solar rooftops have been installed and commissioned for more than
2252 consumers under SBPDCL (1640) & NBPDCL (612) under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijlee
Yojana.

Hydel Power

Bihar faces challenges in harnessing its substantial hydel power potential due to the shifting paths
of rivers and the flood-prone nature of North Bihar. The rivers in South Bihar do not provide
sufficient flow throughout the year and lack the necessary height for the effective use of hydel
power. As a result, the development of large hydroelectric power projects proves to be financially
unfeasible because of the area's geographical features. Nonetheless, mini and small hydroelectric
power plants are being developed at various suitable sites to tap into the hydel potential. The Bihar
State Hydroelectric Power Corporation (BSHPC) has been created to manage the growth of
hydroelectric power initiatives in the state. Currently, there are 13 operational mini and small hydel
projects in Bihar, boasting a combined capacity of 54.3 MW (Table 8.6). BSHPC is also in the
process of constructing 11 more mini hydel power projects, which together have a capacity of 9.3
MW (Table 8.7).
