5.5 Issuance of Passport

Better economic and educational opportunities outside India have always attracted the working
population of Bihar. Previously, the access to avail the opportunity to visit abroad was hindered
by the tedious and complicated process of obtaining passports for aspiring workers. In recent years,
the simplification of application procedures and time-bound police verification encourages
aspirant to apply for a passport, For instance, in the last five years, 1703.9 thousand people applied
for passports, and of them, 1683.1 thousand people have received the same (Table 5.35). The
Regional Passport Office, Patna, reported that for 84 percent of passport applicants, the police
verification was completed within 21 days in 2023. In 2019, the police verification was completed
