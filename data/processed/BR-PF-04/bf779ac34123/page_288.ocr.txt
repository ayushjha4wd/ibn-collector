Mukhya Mantri Prakhand Parivahan Yojana (MMPPY), 2024

MMPPY is a flagship programme of the Government of Bihar for providing a seamless, fast
and convenient mass transportation system to reach the unreachable location, improve lives,
and create livelihood in the remotest part of the State. With the development of strong road
network connectivity across the State, MMPPY makes a strong case to address connectivity
issues by providing subsidies on the purchase of buses. A subsidy of Rs. 5,00,000 for each bus
to purchase a maximum of 7 buses (2 members from SC/ST, 2 from EBC, |] from BC,1 member
from Minority and | from General Category) in each block, excluding the district headquarter
blocks, will be provided by the government to the various sections of society.

More than 6,000 applications have been received till September, 2024. A total of 100 buses
haye been purchased and the department has already provided a total subsidy of Rs. 50 crore.

6.9 Road Safety

Road safety is a multifaceted responsibility engaging various stakeholders. The state's commitment
towards ‘reduced fatalities’ led to the establishment of the Bihar Road Safety Council at the State
level and Road Safety Committees at the district level. The Council at the State level constitutes
officers from different departments like the Police, Road Department, Health Department,
Education Department, etc. Similarly, the Committees at the district level are constituted with
district-level officers from the same departments. The State government is committed to
minimizing road accidents and promoting road safety and security. This initiative involves officers
from different departments, fostering a coordinated approach. The strategy encompasses the 4 E's:
Enforcement, Engineering, Education, and Emergency care.

Motor Driving Training Schools: Motor Driving Training School Incentive Scheme has been
formulated by the department to be established 74 MDTS in the state, Subsidy of up to a maximum
of Rs. 20 lakh is being given to the beneficiaries in installments, These schools would also help in
strengthening the road safety, Construction of 31 such driving training schools has been completed.

Driving Testing Track: The State government has planned construction of driving testing tracks
in all 38 districts, along with plans for automation, Till date, testing tracks are functional in 23
districts, rest will be completed soon. Further, automation of 5 tracks is being done through a
collective effort of the Transport Department and Maruti Suzuki.
