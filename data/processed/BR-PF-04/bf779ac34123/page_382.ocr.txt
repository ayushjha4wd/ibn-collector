Start-Up Village Entrepreneurship Programme (SVEP): SVEP assists SHG members in
establishing small non-farm enterprises, offering financing through the Community
Enterprise Fund and business support services. In the third phase, 10 new blocks were added,
and 266 trained cadres provided ongoing enterprise development support. By September
2024, 4,861 business plans were approved under the programme.

Bag Cluster Unit in Muzaffarpur: The Bag Manufacturing Cluster in Bela, Muzaffarpur,
is a successful public-private partnership involving the Department of Industries, JEEVIKA,
and corporate partners. The cluster employs 42 JEEVIKA entrepreneurs, who have set up 24
machines, resulting in the production of bags worth Rs. 16.55 lakh, providing direct and
indirect employment to over 1,100 workers. Introducing Bartack machines has improved the
quality and durability of bags — 40 Bartack entrepreneurs are offering services, The
initiative has generated Rs. 86.27 lakh in revenue for micro-entreprencurs.

Art & Craft: JEEVIKA has mobilized over 4.100 artisans into producer groups across 20
districts, supporting them with finance, training, and marketing. In 2018, JEEVIKA
established the 'Shilpgram Mahila Producer Company Limited,’ which includes over 500
women artisans from Darbhanga, Madhubani, and Muzaffarpur. Shilpgram has generated
Rs. 7.78 crore in business in 2023-24, selling products through online market platforms.
JEEVIKA also promotes direct sales at various fairs and events across India.

Women-led Individual Financing for Enterprise Promotion

To encourage women’s entrepreneurship, JEEVIKA initiated a pilot project in 2023-24
to promote individual financing for women-led enterprises. This intervention is critical
for enabling SHG members to access the funds needed to expand their businesses and
improve their livelihoods. The initiative was piloted in Patna, Vaishali, and Nalanda and
extended to 70 blocks across 14 districts. By September 2024, 4846 enterprises had been
sanctioned loan by the banks.

Housekeeping and Laundry

JEEVIKA has signed two key Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) to broaden its
service offerings. The first, signed in 2023, with the SC/ST Welfare Department,
introduced cleanliness services in 87 SC/ST Residential Schools, benefiting around 700
households. The second, signed in 2024, with the Health Department, expanded these
services to Maternal and Child Health Centers, District Hospitals, and Sub-Divisional
Hospitals.

To support these initiatives, District Project Managers and Non-Farm Managers visited
the BIMHAS Centre for Excellence in Cleanliness and Laundry Services in Koilwar,
Operational since 2022, BIMHAS serves as a training and knowledge hub for these
services. Additionally, district teams have assessed health institutions and SC/ST
residential schools to ensure the effective rollout of cleanliness and laundry services.
