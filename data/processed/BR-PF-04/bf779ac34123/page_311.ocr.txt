Some of the important initiatives of the IT Department of the state are discussed below:

Bihar State Data Centre (BSDC 2.0) and DR (Disaster Recovery) : The BSDC facilitates the
State government to provide efficient, secure and reliable digital services from across its
departments to citizens. The BSDC 2.0 is now operational and is a Tier-II] compliant data centre
with 99.982 percent uptime and hosts 305 applications of the different departments. It is equipped
with 6200 cores capacity, 64000 RAM capacity and 2.6 petabyte storage capacity, and has multi-
layer security. It has the provision of a Disaster Recovery (DR) site. The objective of the DR-cum-
BCP (Business Continuity Programme) is to recover and restore data and operations when the
primary data centre becomes unavailable. Till September, 2024, a total of 150 applications hosted
at SDC have been provisioned with DR.

Bihar State Wide Area Network (BSWAN 2.0) : It is a network highway that provides
uninterrupted Voice, Video and Data connectivity services to the offices of the State government.
BSWAN 2.0 connects Government Offices (450), District Headquarters (38), and Block Offices
(528) through a high-speed network that supports efficient and transparent service delivery to
citizens, As of September, 2024, a total of 611 Points of Presence (PoP) have been established. It
is important to note here that continuing its thrust on network reach and accessibility, the State
government has approved BSWAN 3.0 to scale-up the network to the Panchayat level.
