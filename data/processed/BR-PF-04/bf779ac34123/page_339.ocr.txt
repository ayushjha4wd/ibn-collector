VanApp Mobile App for Plantation and Nursery Monitoring : Since its introduction in 2019,
the mobile App has been instrumental in enabling online integrated monitoring of plantation,
nursery, and soil moisture conservation (SMC) activities. Developed to assist the supervisory
officials, the App ensures real-time tracking and accountability for forestry and conservation
projects.

Bird Ringing Monitoring System (BRMS) Mobile App : The Bird Ringing Monitoring System
(BRMS) mobile App is a tool designed in 2023 to collect critical data on migratory birds, focusing
on their survival, productivity, and movement patterns. It plays a vital role in tracking bird
populations and informing conservation strategies, This App is a crucial step towards leveraging
technology for sustainable wildlife management and fostering a deeper understanding of avian
ecosystems.

Nursery Management Information System : The Nursery Management Information System
(NMIS) is a digital platform designed in 2023 to manage and monitor various aspects of nurseries,
including contact details, plant inventory, nursery area and other operational data, It ensures
streamlined management, transparency and better planning for afforestation and green initiatives.
The NMIS helps in maintaining a comprehensive database of nurseries and their assets, enabling
efficient resource allocation and improved monitoring. It supports data-driven decision-making
for effective management of plants and nursery operations. This system not only improves nursery
management but also strengthens afforestation programmes by ensuring efficient use of resources
and timely monitoring of plantation efforts.

Application for Issuing Non-Forest Land/Distance from Forest Area Certificates : This
application was designed in 2022 to streamline the process of issuing Non-Forest Land Certificates
and Distance from Forest Area Certificates. Developed under the ‘Ease of Doing Business*
initiative, it simplifies compliance with regulatory requirements for individuals and businesses,
ensuring transparency and efficiency in land-use permissions and forest area reporting.

Web Portal for Wood-Based Industry Registration ; In 2022, an application was developed to
facilitate the registration of individuals and enterprises in the wood-based industries sector seeking
subsidies under the Bihar Wood-Based Industries Promotion Policy, 2020. This initiative
streamlines the registration process and promotes investment and modernization within the sector,
This application is a step forward in fostering a sustainable business ecosystem, enhancing industry
standards, and generating employment in Bihar's wood-based industries.
