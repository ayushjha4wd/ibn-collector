The annual growth rate of the crops, livestock, forestry and logging, fishing, and aquaculture
sector, as presented in Table 3.1, indicates that crop sector has been the focal point of this growth.
According to provisional estimates for 2022-23, the crop sector recorded a growth rate of 6.7
percent. Additionally, the fishing and aquaculture sector has experienced a growth rate of 11.1
percent during the same period. Although the growth rate for fishing and aquaculture has been
consistently high over the years, it is important to note that this sector remains smaller. The growth
rate of the livestock sector has fluctuated over time. According to provisional estimates for
2022-23, this sector grew by 2.7 percent. Table 3.2 indicates that the agriculture, forestry, and
fishing sector contributes approximately one-fifth of the gross state value added (GSVA) annually.
For 2022-23, this sector accounted for 20.2 percent of the GSVA. The crop production contributed
6.4 percent of the GSVA. While fishing and aquaculture exhibited high growth rates over the
period, they accounted for only 1.9 percent of the GSVA produced in this sector.
