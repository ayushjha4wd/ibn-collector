Extension of JP Ganga Path and JP Ganga Path Samagra Udayan

Extension of JP Ganga Path from Digha to Koilwar is proposed to be constructed in the total
length of 35.5 kms, of which 17 kms road is an elevated structure of 6-lane configuration and
rest 18.5 kms is an embankment structure of 6-lane configuration. The tentative project cost is
Rs. 7577 crore. It will connect Sherpur-Dighawara Bridge that is a part of Patna Ring Road
being constructed by MoRTH. At Chhitnawan, the provision of 4-lane connectivity has been
made. Upon construction of this project, the movement of traffic from Western Bihar to
Eastern Bihar will become much easier and, the travel time will reduce significantly.

Extension of JP Ganga Path trom Deedarganj to Athamalgola via Bakhtiyarpur is proposed
to be constructed on an elevated structure of 6-lane configuration of 42.5 kms. The estimated
project cost is Rs. 15453 crore. This includes the provision of 6-lane connectivity with
Bakhtiyarpur-Tajpur Bridge, 4-lane connectivity at Fathua, Khusurupur and Salimpur. Upon
construction of this project, reaching Bakhtiyarpur and other parts of Bihar will become much
easier.

JP Ganga Path Samagra Udayan Project: JP Ganga Path is a 20.5 km long road along the
banks of the river Ganga, The first phase of JP Ganga Path was made operational in June 2022,
Post inauguration, it was witnessed that some sections of the JP Ganga path were being used
as tourist spot by people of Patna, specially during the evening hours. In view of the growing
popularity of the JP Ganga Path as a public attraction, there is a proposal of river front
development between Digha to Gandhi maidan along both sides of the JP Ganga path project
in an area of around 50 hectare, of which 90 percent area shall be developed as green belt and
10 percent as river front, parks for old-aged people and children, biodiversity park, food court,
cycle track/Pedestrian walkway, parking etc. along with the basic facilities. The tentative cost
of project is Rs. 785 crore.

6.5 Sulabh Samparkata under Saat Nishchay — 11

The Government of Bihar has adopted ‘Sulabh Samparkata’ as one of the seven resolves under
Saat Nishchay-I, A sum of Rs, 143 crore was sanctioned for 8 projects for construction of around
40 kms length of road stretches on different bypasses during 2021-22. A total of 12 projects were
sanctioned at an outlay of Rs. 413 crore in 2022-23 for construction of 52.7 kms road network on
different bypasses. However, during 2023-24, five projects were sanctioned at a cost of Rs.128.09
crore for construction of 30 kms length of bypasses (Appendix Table A 6.5). Once these bypasses
become operational, majority of the towns of the state will become congestion-free.
