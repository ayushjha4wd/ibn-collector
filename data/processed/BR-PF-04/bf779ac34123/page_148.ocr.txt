Fisheries

The Government of Bihar is actively promoting sustainable fish farming and aquaculture practices
by providing financial assistance in the form of subsidies to fishers, fish farmers, and
entrepreneurs. The aims are to increase fish production to meet local demand and to generate
surplus for export. Popular freshwater fish species in Bihar include Rohu, Catla, Mrigal, Silver
carp, Grass carp, Common carp, and various species of catfish. The Directorate of Fisheries has
implemented the following schemes.

Mukhyamantri Samekit Chaur Vikas Yojana : The objective of this scheme is to transform the
‘chaur’ areas of the State into aquaculture-based integrated farming systems using modern
technology. Under this scheme, financial assistance of 70 percent of the unit cost is offered to fish
farmers belonging to EBC, SC, and ST communities and 50 percent of the unit cost is provided to
farmers of other categories. In 2023-24, 549.6 hectares of chaur have been developed, with an
expenditure of Rs. 22.8 crore.
