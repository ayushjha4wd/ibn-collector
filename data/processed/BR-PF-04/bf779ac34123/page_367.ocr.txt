including dedicated feeders for agricultural water pump sets. Nevertheless, the DISCOMs
encountered challenges due to the limited work scope regarding feeder segregation as outlined in
DDUGIJY. To tackle this problem, the State government planned a new initiative called the
‘Mukhyamantri Krishi Vidyut Sambandh Yojana! (MKVSY) in August 2020 and allocated

connections released and the infrastructure created under MKVSY are detailed in Table 8.12.

Further, the State government in its fourth Agriculture Road Map (2023-2028) has formulated a
new scheme amounting to Rs. 2190.75 crore. for the creation of electrical infrastructure including
dedicated feeders for agriculture and providing around 4.80 lakh new agriculture connections
under the Mukhyamantri Krishi Vidyut Sambandh Yojana (MKVSY) Phase-2. In addition, the
work of solar electrification of the existing 1354 dedicated agriculture feeders is also covered in
the scheme. A subsidy is being provided by the State government to different categories of
consumers under the Mukhyamantri Vidyut Upbhokta Sahayata Yojana (MVUSY). At present,
electric power to agricultural consumers is being supplied at a highly subsidized rate of Rs. 0.55
per unit.
