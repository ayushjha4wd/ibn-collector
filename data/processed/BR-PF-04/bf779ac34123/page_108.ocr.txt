The tax revenue collected by the State government from various sources for the years 2019-20 to
2023-24, along with the budgeted estimate for 2024-25, is presented in Table 2.13. Table 2.14
shows the percentage share of the components of the state*s tax revenue from its own sources for
these years, including the budget estimates for 2024-25, The primary source of tax revenue for the
State government is the State Goods and Services Tax (SGST), which has made up over half of
the tax revenue from its own sources in the past five years. Its share has notably increased from
52.4 percent in 2019-20 to 57.2 percent in 2023-24. Other significant sources of tax revenue
include Taxes on Sales and Trade, which contributed 19.4 percent: Stamp and Registration Fees,
accounting for 13.1 percent; and Taxes on Vehicles, which made up 6.9 percent in 2023-24.
Additionally, there are two smaller components: land revenue, with a share of 1.2 percent, and
taxes and duties on electricity, contributing 1.8 percent.
