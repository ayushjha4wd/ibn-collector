Table 1.8 also presents the pattern of workforce participation across different sectors in Bihar.
More than half of the workforce in Bihar is involved in economic activities within the primary
sector, which includes agriculture, livestock, forestry, and fishing. From 2017-18 to 2023-24,
workforce participation in the primary sector has increased both in Bihar and at the all-India level.
However, the increase has been more pronounced in Bihar, where the share of this sector in total
workforce participation rose from 45.2 percent in 2017-18 to 54.2 percent in 2023-24. In contrast,
the all-India share increased from 44.6 percent to 46.3 percent during the same period. Meanwhile,
the contributions of the secondary and tertiary sectors to workforce participation have declined.
By 2023-24, the secondary sector accounted for 23.6 percent and the tertiary sector for 22.2 percent

of workforce participation in Bihar.
