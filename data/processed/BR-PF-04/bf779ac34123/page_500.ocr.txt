Capacity building

The department is committed to promoting knowledge transfer both between institutions and from
institutions to individuals. This initiative aims to share best practices, lessons learned, and
innovative ideas to prepare students in a better way for the demands of industry. Apart from these,
the department has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with I-Hub Divya Sampark,
IIT Roorkee, which aims to train 12,000 students and 3,000 faculty members in emerging
technologies such as Machine Learning and its applications, Data Science using Python, and loT
and Embedded Systems over three financial years. Additionally, a spoken tutorial project has been
launched in collaboration with ITT Bombay to provide free training in open-source software
(FOSS), including Linux, LaTeX, Java, Esim, Git, Open Modclica, Python, QCad, Scilab, and
Arduino.

In collaboration with the Bihar Skill Development Mission, the department has launched a skill
upgradation training program for final-year students in the Mechanical and Automobile
Engineering branches at the Government Polytechnics. This program is being conducted at the
Central Institute of Petrochemicals Engineering & Technology (CIPET) in Hajipur and the Tool
Room and Training Centre (TRTC) in Patna. The department has signed a Memorandum of
Understanding (MoU) with the National Institute of Electronics & Information Technology
(NIELIT), Patna to provide residential training program for students from government engineering
and polytechnic institutes in Bihar. Moreover, Faculty Development Programs have been
organized in collaboration with the National Institute of Technical Teachers’ Training and
Research (NITTTR) Bhopal, IIT Patna and NIT Patna to enhance faculty skills and expertise.

National Board of Accreditation (NBA)

The department actively facilitates accreditation programs for institutions following the standards
set by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA), Currently, Nalanda Engineering College
(Chandi) has received accreditation for its Computer Science and Engineering program, while MIT
Muzaffarpur has been accredited for its Civil Engineering program. Additionally, Government
Polytechnic, Patna has achieved accreditation for its Civil, Electronics, and Mechanical branches.

Promoting Start Up

To encourage the start-up culture and promote innovation and entrepreneurship among students
and faculty, start-up cells have been established in all government engineering colleges and
polytechnic institutes in line with the AICTE norms. Currently, 35 start-up cells have received
funding from the "Bihar Start-Up Fund Trust," and a total of 34 start-ups across 15 government
engineering colleges have been supported under the "Seed Grant Scheme" by the Department of
Industries.
