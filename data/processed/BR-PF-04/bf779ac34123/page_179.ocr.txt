Crop Residue Burning

The Agriculture Department has formed an inter-departmental working group to raise awareness
among farmers and the public about the harmful effects of burning crop residue in fields. To
prevent waste, the State government has provided a 40 percent subsidy to purchase machinery to
manage the crop residue. Demonstrations of the waste decomposer are conducted through 25
Krishi Vigyan Kendras under the Bihar Agricultural University. The decomposer is made available
for 3,000 acres of farmland.

These initiatives aim to promote sustainable farming and discourage crop residue burning. The
State government has also taken punitive action against farmers who burn crop residue. Since
2019, the Agriculture Department has blocked farmers burning crop residues from registering on
the DBT portal, disqualifying them from availing themselves of schemes. In 2023, 656 farmers
were penalized after wheat harvesting, and 2,398 farmers were penalized during paddy harvesting.
Furthermore, the list of offenders was shared with the Co-operative and Food and Consumer
Protection Departments to deny them benefits under rice procurement schemes.
