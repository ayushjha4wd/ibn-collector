Bihar State Housing Board

The Bihar State Housing Board is governed by the Bihar State Housing Board Act, 1982 and the
Bihar State Housing Board (Management and Disposal of Residential Real Estate) Rules, 1983.

The Housing Board has made significant progress in revenue collection over the past year, leading
to a substantial increase in its income. In 2021-22 and 2022-23, the total revenue receipts were
Rs. 49.15 crore and Rs. 51.41 crore, respectively. In 2023-24, revenue receipts reached Rs. 63.26
crore. For 2024-25, the revenue target is set at Rs. 73.80 crore. Furthermore, the Board contributed
Rs, 2.00 crore to the Chief Minister's Relief Fund in 2021-22 and Rs. 5.00 crore in 2022-23,

In line with the Affordable Housing Project (AHP), the construction of housing for the
Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) and the Lower Income Group (LIG) is mandatory, and
shelter fund fees are collected. The number of houses approved for EWS is 2,430 and that for LIG
is 855. Moreover, 192 building plans have been approved, and 52 No Objection Certificates
(NoCs) have been issued for petrol pump installations. The authority has collected Rs. 2.12 crore
in shelter fees and Rs, 4.15 crore in building fees.

Town and Country Planning Organisation (TCPO)

The State government has commenced the preparation of master plans for all district headquarters,
cities with populations exceeding one lakh, and other significant cities within the state. In this
context, planning areas have been designated, and Planning Area Authorities have been established
to develop GIS-based master plans for 44 cities.

Out of the 44 designated Planning Areas in the state, the GIS-based Master Plan for the Patna
Planning Area has been officially notified. It is currently being implemented by the Patna
Metropolitan Area Authority. The master plans for the remaining 25 notified Planning Areas are
in the process of development.

The State government has completed GIS-based mapping for 29 cities. Additionally, it will prepare
GIS-based maps for 26 municipal areas and conduct property surveys in those regions. Currently,
the preparation of GIS-based maps for 28 cities is underway. Simultaneously, GIS-based property
surveys have been conducted in 15 cities. The government is also getting 183 cities mapped.
Furthermore, the Department of Urban Development and Housing has designated 82 assistant town
planning supervisors to assist in the preparation of GIS-based mapping and the development of
GIS-based master plans.
