PLFS defines WPR as the proportion of persons employed among all the persons in the population.
Across all the selected states, WPR (in usual status) in rural areas was higher than that in urban
areas. The male and female WPRs in Bihar was lower than the male and female WPRs at the all-
India level. In both rural and urban areas in Bihar, female WPRs was much lower than male WPRs.

Both LFPR and WPR of women were low in most developing countries, indicating that most
women did not participate in economic activities due to the functioning of social and economic
institutions, especially in rural areas. In addition, the prevalent definition of work or economic
activity is biased against women’s work. The definition of work has limitations, in that it does not

recognise household chores and caregiving work that invariably fall on women.
