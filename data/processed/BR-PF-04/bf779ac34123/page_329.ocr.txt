processing time for payments by direct transfer to the bank account of the beneficiary/payee, thus
eliminating the need for frequent treasury visits. The claim processing time has been reduced from
30-45 days to just 3 days.

All government accounts are progressively being brought under the Treasury's control, resulting
in minimal idle cash, including the deposit of idle cash, to the tune of Rs. 6000 crore. Online budget
preparation and authorization of budget estimates have effectively reduced the budget preparation
time from 3-4 months to nearly | month, Also, it facilitates timely Budget Allotment as the budget
is available for allotment/sub- allotment on the very first day of a Financial Year. Online reporting
eliminates the need for maintenance of multiple physical registers i.e. allotment register,
expenditure register, payment register, etc, It also helps in identification of ghost
employees/pensioners due to the controls and checks introduced in the application. Audit Trail of
every action, including data created/modified/deleted, records or details of modification, note
sheets or remarks entered/modified, documents uploaded or removed, is maintained in the
application. Finally, it facilitates the online auto surrender of budgets at the end of a financial year.

The Finance Department is also gradually progressing to achieve a fully automated, workflow-
driven, and all-inclusive PFMS, and, therefore, the Department has already initiated actions for
stabilization and sustenance of the rolled-out CFMS application by CFMS 2.0, which shall broadly
cover the following additional functions: (1) Pensioners’ Digitization, (ii) Cash (Ways & Means)
& Debt Management, (iii) Revenue Management, (iv) Audit Management System, (v) Asset
Management System, (vi) Grievance Management, (vii) Business Intelligence/OLAP Tools, and
(viii) PFMS (SNA SPARSH) integration.
