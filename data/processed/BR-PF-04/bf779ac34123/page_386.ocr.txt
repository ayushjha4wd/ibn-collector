2021-22 to 45.4 percent in 2023-24. Jehanabad (65.1 percent), Sheohar (61.6 percent), and Saharsa

The share of households obtaining 100 days of employment increased from 0.1 percent in
2021-22 to 0,7 percent in 2023-24. Sheohar and Saharsa districts achieved the highest proportion
(1.8 percent) of households getting 100 days of employment. The total person-days of employment
generated in the state rose significantly from 954.3 lakh in 2021-22 to 2205.4 lakh in 2023-24,
showing an increase of 131.1 percent. Gaya (127.5 lakh) and East Champaran (114.7 lakh)
recorded the highest person-days of employment generated, while Nalanda, Samastipur, and
Saharsa each generated more than 90 lakh person-days of employment. The share of women in the
total employment generated exceeded 50 percent in most districts, with exceptions such as Patna,
Bhojpur, Buxar, Rohtas, Kaimur, Arwal, Aurangabad, Gopalganj, and Bhagalpur. Overall, the
share of women in the total employment generated improved from 53.4 percent in 2021-22 to 54.3
percent in 2023-24. The data indicates significant progress in several metrics under MGNREGS,
despite declines in some areas. Notable increases in employment demand and person-days of
employment generated underscore the programme's critical role in supporting rural livelihoods.

The data show that the implementation of MGNREGS is consistent across districts, with most
districts effectively utilizing the available funds. The overall fund utilization in the state from
2021-22 to 2023-24 exceeded 97.0 percent. Additionally, the availability of funds has shown an
improvement, increasing from Rs. 4,31,117.8 lakh in 2021-22 to Rs. 8,04,331.4 lakh in 2023-24,
reflecting a growth of 86.6 percent (Table A 9.3).
