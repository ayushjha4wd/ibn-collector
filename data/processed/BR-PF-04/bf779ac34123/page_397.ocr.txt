Panchayat Sarkar Bhawan

To provide efficient services (both online and offline) to rural citizens at their doorstep while
minimizing costs and delays, improving infrastructure at Panchayat Sarkar Bhawans (PSBs) is of
paramount importance. This is particularly crucial in Bihar, where Gram Panchayats have limited
revenue sources and require infrastructural support to deliver services that meet the expectations
of rural citizens. The installation of 10 kW rooftop solar plants on each PSB is proposed to promote
clean and green energy and reduce pollution. The surplus electricity generated will be fed directly
into the grid network, The credits earned from this excess electricity generation will help offset
future electricity bills, thereby contributing to Panchayats’ own source revenue. A total amount of
Rs. 1000.00 crore is being provided to the Local Area Engineering Organization (LAEO) from the
State Plan head in 2023-24 to construct PSBs. Further, for the implementation of this scheme, Rs.
636.00 crore has been made available from the State Plan head in 2024-25, Simultaneous efforts
are being made to construct PSBs and develop adequate infrastructure. As of September 2024,
1,474 PSBs have been completed, and 1,463 have been handed over to the respective Panchayats
(Table A 9.7).
