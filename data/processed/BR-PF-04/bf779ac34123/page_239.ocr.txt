enterprises. The proportion of self-employed female workers in Bihar was 17.1 percentage points
higher than the all-India average. In Bihar, 10.8 percent of female workers were engaged in casual
labour, and that was 4.1 percentage points more than the all-India average. The proportion of
female workers engaged in regular wage and salaried jobs was 5.5 percent in Bihar.
