The triennial average per capita Net State Domestic Product (NSDP) at constant prices (2011-12)
for select major states in India from 2011-12 to 2023-24 is presented in Table 1.6. It also includes
the per capita NSDP values for the years 2022-23 and 2023-24. For the year 2023-24, Bihar’s per
capita NSDP is estimated to be Rs. 32,174, which is 30.1 percent of the all-India average NSDP
for the state. Among the major states, the highest per capita NSDP is recorded for Karnataka at
Rs. 1,86,038, followed closely by Haryana at Rs. 1,85,490 and Telangana at Rs. 1,83,854. The per
capita NSDP of Bihar is estimated to have increased by 7.6 percent in 2023-24 over the previous

year, which places it among the fastest growing states in the country.
