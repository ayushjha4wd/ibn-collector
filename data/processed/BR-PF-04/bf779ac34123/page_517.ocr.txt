Some of the significant schemes of the Department of Minority Welfare are as follows:

Chief Minister Student Promotion Scheme (Mukhyamantri Vidyarthi Protsahan Yojana) ;
Under this programme, minority students who got first division in their matriculation examinations
receive Rs. 10,000, while Muslim girls who attain a first division in their intermediate
examinations are awarded Rs. 15,000 as motivation to pursue further education. Additionally,
minority students, who select Bengali as an optional subject in their matric exams and also secure
a first division in that subject, are eligible for an incentive of Rs. 10,000, Furthermore, students
who complete Fauqania (matric) and Maulvi (intermediate) with a first division under the Bihar
State Madarsa Education Board Examination will receive Rs, 10,000 and Rs. 15,000 respectively
as incentive amounts. For 2023-24, a budget of Rs. 91.10 crore has been set aside for 69,596
minority students.

Muslim Divorcee/Deserted Women Assistance Programme : The scheme was introduced in
2006-07 to provide financial assistance of Rs. 10,000 to Muslim women who are cither divorced
or deserted, In 2017-18, this amount was increased to Rs. 25,000. Since 2018-19, the beneficiaries
have been availing the facility through the e-welfare portal. In 2023-24, Rs. 1.08 crore has been
distributed among 430 Muslim women.

vocational education among minority students, there is a provision of providing free hostel
facilities in all district headquarters of the state. To date, 48 hostels have already been constructed
and 4 more are under construction. About 4885 students can reside in these hostels. An extra
