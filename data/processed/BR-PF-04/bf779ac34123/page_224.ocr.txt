Bihar Industrial Area Development Authority (BLADA)

As part of infrastructure development for industrialisation in Bihar, the Bihar Industrial Area
Development Authority (BIADA) has created nine clusters. By September 30, 2024, BIADA had
acquired and developed 7652.6 acres of land to make it suitable for industrial units. Of the total
land acquired, BIADA has already distributed 4520.8 acres among the industrial units.
