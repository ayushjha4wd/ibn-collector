2.6 Resource Management

This section provides an overview of the resources available in the accounts of the State
government, detailing various sources of revenue in recent years. The State government generates
income from both tax and non-tax sources, and it also receives funds through transfers and
grants-in-aid from the Central government.

Tax revenue includes funds collected from various taxes and duties imposed by the State
government, such as the State Goods and Services Tax (SGST) and the State's share of the divisible
pool of taxes and duties collected by the Central government, Non-tax revenues consist of the State
government's own non-tax income and grants from the Central government.

Since July 2017, the Goods and Services Tax (GST) system has replaced many traditionally levied
taxes. The GST framework has three components: State Goods and Services Tax (SGST), Central
Goods and Services Tax (CGST), and Integrated Goods and Services Tax (IGST). The GST
Council is responsible for determining the tax bases and rates for these components.
