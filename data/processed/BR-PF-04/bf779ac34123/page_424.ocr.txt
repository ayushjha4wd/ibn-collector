Clean Survey 2023

The Patna Municipal Corporation has secured first place in the Cleanest City category among cities
with populations exceeding one lakh in the state. Additionally, it has been awarded the highest
Water+ certification under the Open Defecation-Free category and has received a one-Star Rating
under the Garbage-Free City initiative. In 2022, Patna ranked 38th out of 45 cities in India with
populations over 10 lakh, However, in 2023, it improved to 77th place out of 446 cities nationwide.
Among the cities along the Ganges river, Patna ranked 12th, Barhaiya 14th, and Sultanganj 1 Sth.
The Supaul Municipal Council, which has a population of under one lakh, was recognized in the
Cleanest City category and received a one-Star Rating under the Garbage-Free City initiative.
Overall, 85 municipal bodies in Bihar have been certified ODF, 56 have received ODF
certification, and Patna is the only municipal body to have earned the Water+ certification.

Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT)

The AMRUT (Urban) 2.0 scheme, launched in 2021, aims to promote a self-reliant India. This
scheme focuses on providing a continuous drinking water supply to every household in all
municipal bodies across the state. Additionally, it emphasizes on water conservation, the revival
of water sources, the recycling and reuse of treated wastewater, rainwater harvesting, and
enhancing the aesthetics of cities by developing green spaces and well-organized open areas, such
as parks. Sewerage and septage connections will also be provided in the older AMRUT cities of
the state. The total project fund for AMRUT 2.0 is Rs. 2628.00 crore. The funding criteria are
based on the size of the city population. The State government’s contributions to AMRUT 2.0 are
as follows: for cities with populations exceeding 10 lakh, the State government will cover 75
percent of the project cost. For cities with populations between | lakh and 10 lakh, two-thirds of
the project cost will be borne by the State government. For cities with population below one lakh,
the State government will provide 50 percent of the project costs. Under AMRUT Mission 2.0,
master plans can be developed for 50 cities in the state with populations ranging from 50,000 to
1,00,000. Currently, planning areas for 16 cities within this category have been designated, and
efforts are underway to make the Planning Area Authorities operational.

Under the AMRUT scheme, 36 water supply projects have been approved across 21 municipal
bodies, with an estimated cost of Rs, 2260.06 crore, Of these, 20 projects have been completed.
To date, Rs. 2117.0 crore has been spent on implementing these water supply schemes.
