Krishonnati Yojana

The Krishonnati Yojana is a centrally sponsored scheme with nine components. These are:
National Food Security and Nutrition Mission (NFSNM), National Mission on Edible Oil-Oil
Seeds (NMEO-OS), National Mission on Edible Oil-Oil Palm (NMEO-OP), Mission for
Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH), Digital Agriculture Mission, Mission Organic
Value Chain Development for the North Eastern Region (MOVCDNER), Sub-Mission on
Agricultural Extension (SMAE), Integrated Scheme for Agriculture Marketing-National
Agriculture Market (ISAM-eNAM), and Integrated Scheme for Agricultural Marketing-Others
(ISAM-Others). Under the Krishonnati Yojana, the National Food Security Mission and the
National Mission on Edible Oil-Oil Seeds (NMEO-OS) are discussed here. The stated objectives
of the Missions are: to increase production of rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals (maize and barley),
multi-cereal, and oilseeds through area expansion and productivity enhancement in a sustainable
manner in the identified districts of the country. It aims to restore soil fertility at the individual
farm level, and to restore confidence among the farmers by improving farm-level economy.
Furthermore, it aims to enhance post-harvest value addition at farm gate for better price realization

for farmers through efficient market linkages.

The Missions identify less productive districts in the country and intervene to increase the
productivity of the selected crops. Under this scheme, the districts across the states have been

identified to improve the productivity of selected crops like rice, wheat, and oilseeds.

The National Mission on Edible Oil-Oil Seeds (NMEO-OS) is implemented in Bihar, where a
subsidy is provided for different interventions such as crop demonstration, seed distribution,
Integrated Nutrient Management (INM), Integrated Pest Management (IPM), and farm equipment,
In Bihar, under these two missions, 15 districts have been selected for rice, 10 for wheat, 38 for
pulses, 11 for coarse cereals, seven each for nutri cereals, TRFA-pulses, TRFA-oilseeds, and jute,

5 for sugarcane, and 10 for oilseeds.
