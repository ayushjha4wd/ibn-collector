Bihar Rajya Pul Nirman Nigam Limited (BRPNNL)

The BRPNNL is another profit-making State government’s Corporation constructing roads and
bridges having an estimated cost of more than Rs. 25.00 lakh. During the period from 2012-13 to
2023-24, the Nigam has constructed 1112 bridges in the state and recorded a work execution of
Rs. 16,761 crore. It earned an amount of Rs. 545.07 crore as a net profit during the same period.
Table 6.6 presents an overview of the working condition of the Corporation during the last 12
years. The work executing (work turnover) of the Corporation was Rs. 1567.15 crore in 2023-24,
with a gross profit of Rs. 47.74 crore and a net profit of Rs. 18.65 crore. In addition, the Nigam
has contributed Rs. 205.11 crore to the State government as a dividend and also donated
Rs, 205.00 crore to the Chief Minister’s Relief Fund. Apart from this, the Corporation has
undertaken work worth Rs, 54.43 crore under its CSR initiative. During 2023-24, 132 new projects
costing Rs. 2523.58 crore and for 2024-25 (up to 30" September, 2024) 12 new projects worth Rs.
267.30 crore have been allotted to the Corporation.
