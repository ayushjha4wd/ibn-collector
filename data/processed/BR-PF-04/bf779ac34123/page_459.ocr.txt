A comparison of sector-wise non-performing assets (NPA) of banks in Bihar between March 2023
and March 2024 is illustrated in Chart 11.14, The NPA for outstanding loans to priority sectors in
Bihar has decreased, dropping from 15.0 percent in March 2023 to 12.2 percent in March 2024.
Specifically, the NPA for agricultural loans has fallen from 21.5 percent to 18.1 percent during the
same period, In the micro, small and medium enterprises (MSME) sector, the NPA was relatively
lower at 7.8 percent in March 2024, a decrease of 2.1 percentage points compared to the previous

year,

Conversely, although still low, the NPA in non-priority sectors increased slightly from 1.1 percent
in March 2023 to 1.2 percent in March 2024. Overall, the NPA as a percentage of total outstanding
advances by banks in Bihar has declined from 9.3 percent in March 2023 to 7.5 percent in March
2024.
