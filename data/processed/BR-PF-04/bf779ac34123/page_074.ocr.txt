Chapter-IV provides a comprehensive overview of the enterprises sector in the state. It highlights
the policy initiatives of the State government in recent years to promote industrial growth, tourism,
and entrepreneurial activities. The State government has made significant strides in generating
employment and enhancing the skill levels of its working-age population, Chapter-V focuses on
various aspects of workforce engagement within the state. It details government initiatives aimed

at skill development for young individuals and efforts to enhance their employment opportunities.

The State government has consistently prioritised improving essential infrastructure to support
economic activities and improve the overall quality of life for its citizens. This commitment is
evident in several initiatives to upgrade physical and digital infrastructure across the state. In
Chapter-V1, the Economic Survey delves into the current status and recent advancements in
physical infrastructure in the state. This includes improvements in transportation networks such as
roads, bridges, and public transit systems that facilitate ease of movement for people and goods.
Chapter-VII focuses on digital infrastructure, emphasising the importance of using technological
advancements in improving service delivery across the state. Access to electricity is essential for
modern life and economic development. State government initiatives have significantly improved
the energy sector. Chapter-VIII outlines the sector's institutional structure, details on power
demand and supply, the transmission network, the financial status of distribution companies, and
government schemes aimed at benefiting citizens.

A significant portion of the population in the state resides in rural areas, making it essential to
improve their standard of living for the overall socio-economic development of the state. Chapter-
IX outlines various government initiatives aimed at rural development. At the same time, the
growing urbanization in the state necessitates enhanced policy efforts toward sustainable urban
development. Chapter-X examines the current state of urbanization and documents the
government's various policy initiatives for the development of the urban sector, Additionally, credit
from formal financial institutions is vital for the growth of economic activities in the state, To
maintain steady economic growth, there a need of greater access to credit from banks and other
financial institutions. Chapter-X1 discusses the current status and recent developments in banking

infrastructure and credit facilitation provided by the financial institutions.
