Palna Ghar : Women are increasingly getting involved in employment and services by knowing
the importance of contribution to the family income. To support women to carry on their jobs, a
créche facility has been started to care for infants and young children aged 5 years or less while
their parents are at work. Creche provides a conducive environment for the all-round development
of children, provides group care and a positive environment for their personality development.
Presently, in Patna, 3 créches are running in ; (1) Jail Department — Secretariat Patna, (2) Ground
Floor — Vishveshwaraiya Bhawan — Patna, and (3) Vikas Bhawan. Under the Chief Minister Nari
Shakti Yojana, 100 créches have been sanctioned. Funds are being transferred for the construction
of one créche each in the headquarters of all 38 districts. Till now, total 62 Palna Ghars have been
established and the operation of all 100 créches will start soon in all 38 districts.

Gender Resource Centre : Gender Resource Centre has been established by the Women and
Child Development Corporation for training, study and research, etc. on gender-related topics as
well as to provide technical support in the preparation of the gender budget. Gender Resource
Center mainly carries out the work of research, analysis, sensitization of all stakeholders towards
gender mainstreaming.

Beti Bachao Beti Padhao : This scheme is an initiative to prevent and increase the declining child-
sex ratio and encourage girls education, On the occasion of the International Girl Child Day 2023,
a state-level program was organized by the Women and Child Development Corporation (WCDC)
in Bihar towards increasing the importance of the girl child. All 38 districts have also organised
two-day programs such as cycle rally. coffee with the girls students, etc. at district, block and
panchayat levels. In the last two months, WCDC organized street plays, tree plantation and speech
and painting competitions in schools to honour the immunized girls and their parents.

Menstrual Hygiene Management Roadmap : The Menstrual Hygiene Management Roadmap of
Bihar was prepared by the WCDC in June 2022. Under this roadmap, a state task force will be
formed under the chairmanship of the development commissioner and a district-level task force
under the chairmanship of the district officer. In this, the role of education, health, social welfare,
and rural development departments will be prominent. Under the Menstrual Hygiene Management
Roadmap, awareness is being spread through media along with radio campaigns at the state level
to adopt the right measures and practices on menstrual hygiene management.
