Initiatives of Science, Technology and Technical Education

Indira Gandhi Planetarium

The Indira Gandhi Planetarium, also known as the Patna Planetarium, is located in Patna’s
Indira Gandhi Science Complex. It is equipped with world-class technical features and has a
seating capacity of 200 persons. Taramandal is now operational and opened for public after a
major revamp and installation of the advanced projection system.

Darbhanga Planetarium

The Darbhanga Planetarium is Bihar's second planetarium and science museum, also known
as Taramandal-cum-knowledge and Science Museum. The planetarium is located in the
Kadirabad Polytechnic Ground of the Darbhanga city, and has been designed by the US
architectural firm Chelsea West Architects, The planetarium features three distinctive shapes:
elliptical, spherical, and dome-shaped. Planetarium and Science Museum, Darbhanga is now
operational and opened to the public.

Regional Science Centre, Gaya

The Regional Science Centre in Gaya is located in Bodh Gaya and has been developed by the
National Council of Science Museums (NCSM). The objective is to promote science not only
in Gaya, but also in the nearby districts. The Science Centre includes Fun Science, Science and
Technology Heritage, a Children's Gallery, a Science Park, and an Auditorium. Additionally,
an Innovation Hub, with a multi-dimensional laboratory with various equipment, has also been
constructed.
