Mukhyamantri Talab Matsyki Vikash Yojana: The aim of this scheme is to maximise the
sustainable utilisation of ponds, tanks, and other water resources to increase fish seed production,
as well as overall fish production and productivity. The components of the scheme include advance
input supply scheme for fish farming in ponds, advance fish seed production, installation of
tubewells and pumpsets, and renovation and development of finfish hatcheries. Under this scheme,
fish farmers from EBC, SC, ST communities are provided with financial assistance covering 70
percent of the unit cost, while other communities receive 50 percent of the unit cost. The details
of physical and financial achievements are presented in Table 3.18,

government spent Rs. 6.13 crore for the construction of ponds on 77.27 acres of land.
