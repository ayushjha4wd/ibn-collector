6.12 Air Transportation

Growth in air transport sector shows the economic activities and trade in the regions, The Patna
airport is one of the emerging airports in the country, The contribution of air transport in economy
has been increasing over the years in Bihar. Chart 6,10 provides a trend of growth recorded in
airways (sub-sector of GSVA) of Bihar during 2011-12 to 2023-24, The size of air transport in
GSVA jumped by nine times from Rs. 31 crore in 2011-12 to Rs, 282 crore in 2023-24. This
sector has recorded an annual growth of 21 percent during 2023-24, compared to the

previous year.
