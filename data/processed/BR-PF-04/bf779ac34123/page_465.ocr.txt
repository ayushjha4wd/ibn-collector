CHAPTER - XII
HUMAN DEVELOPMENT

=

Human Development emphasizes on people and their capabilities to assess development. It
covers not only the economic growth and sharing, but also all kinds of human needs. In the
contemporary era, human development is the greater need of the hour, There are six basic
pillars of human development, namely, equity, sustainability, productivity, empowerment,
welfare and security. All these have a profound impact on the quality of life. Therefore, to
achieve these goals, the State government has allocated 36.4 percent of its total budget in
2023-24 to the human development-related sectors. Between 2005-06 and 2023-24, the
expenditure on the social sector has increased 13 times. Additionally, between 2015-16 and
2023-24, the per capita expenditure on social services grew at the rate of 9.4 percent and the
Per Capita Development Expenditure grew at the rate of 6.5 percent annually. A sector-wise
analysis shows indicators like institutional delivery, IMR, immunization, etc. have recorded a
substantial improvement in the health sector, Similarly, stable enrolment and steady fall in the
drop-out rate at primary, upper primary and secondary levels of education also reflect the
strengthening of education, a significant pillar of human development. Access to safe drinking
water and sanitation are among the eminent goals of human development, which is a major
concern for the State government. Along with these agendas, the well-being of SCs, STs,
minorities and women are also given high priority in the development policy in Bihar. The
recent progress in human development parameters in Bihar is the result of the productive

implementation of various programmes by the Government,

Over the last decade, the government has accelerated its efforts toward inclusiveness by ensuring
optimum expenditure on social infrastructure to promote human development. It is critical at this
juncture to focus on public investments in human capital and strengthen the delivery mechanisms
of government interventions to ensure transparency and accountability, With Bihar possessing a
demographic edge, enhancing educational quality, equipping the youth with skills, reducing
disease burden, uplifting marginalized groups and individuals with disabilities, and integrating
women into various sectors will aid in harnessing the potential of a prosperous economy in the
