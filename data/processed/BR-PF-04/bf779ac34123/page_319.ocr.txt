e-Sakshya Platform

The e-Sakshya platform is suite of e-Sakshya mobile application for recording of process,
Sakshya locker for storage and bundle of APIs for authentication and data exchange. e-Sakshya
mobile App is intended to record the process for three purposes:

(i) Scene of Crime Videography — Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita 173;
(ii) Search and Seizure Videography — Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita 105; and,
(iii) Search by Police Officer Videography Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita 185.

e-challan : Violation of traffic rules is captured by camera and after entry of vehicle number or
Driving Licence, e-challan is issued after selection of offence. These cameras capture photographs
and videos of traffic violations and are linked to the Vahan and Sarathi Portal of the National
Informatics Centre (NIC). Hand-held Devices (HHD) have been given to the police personnel to
issue e-challan against traffic violations in real time. With the help of hand-held devices, the traffic
police collect fines from violators in real time through debit cards, credit cards, and UPI. It
introduces transparency in issuing challan on the spot and facilitates online payments. A sum of
Rs. 120 crore has been collected through e-challans to 19 lakh traffic violators till September,
2024.

iRAD & eDAR:: Integrated Road Accident Database- Electronic Detail Accident Report (iRAD-
eDAR) project provides an integrated digital platform for all stakeholders and collects real-time
accident data in the entire country to analyse the causes of crashes and helps in devising safety
interventions to reduce accidents and fatalities. The State government rolled out this platform in
Bihar in January 2022 and 32,725 accident cases have been registered so far and 2877 cases
proceeded to e-DAR, It covers 40 police district administrations, 1237 police stations, and 3084
field office users. It integrates all the divisions and subdivisions of road agencies right from NH to
Village Road. Also, 38 District Transport Offices and more than 76 MVIs are registered in the
system for motor vehicle inspection, More than 146 hospitals and 2975 patients are registered in
iRAD. A total of 14,394 training sessions have been organized throughout the state for 39,560
participants.
