11.7 Other Development Funds

In addition to the above-mentioned investments that involve credit from banks and other financial
institutions, the state also receives funding from the National Housing Bank (NHB) and the Small
Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI). These funds are allocated for development
projects through the Urban Infrastructure Development Fund (UIDF) and the SIDBI Cluster
Development Fund (SCDF). The loans are regulated by Article 293(3) of the Constitution of India
and can only be accessed within the state's borrowing limits for the financial year. Oversight of
these loans falls under the Finance Department of the Government of Bihar.

Urban Infrastructure Development Fund (UIDF)
In 2023-24, the Government of India launched the Urban Infrastructure Development Fund (UIDF)

to enhance infrastructure, including roads, bridges, water supply, drainage, sewerage, and solid
waste management in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities (populations between 50,000 and 999,999 based on
the 2011 Census). Eligible activities for funding support include Urban Water Supply and
Sanitation, Urban Connectivity, and Urban Development. However, the fund does not cover
administrative expenses, housing, electricity, telecommunications, urban transport, health,
educational institutions, and maintenance costs.
