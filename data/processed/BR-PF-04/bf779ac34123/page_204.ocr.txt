4.2 Informal Enterprises in Bihar

The unincorporated sector contributes immensely to the growth and employment of the Indian
economy, including Bihar. The size of the unincorporated sector in Bihar is significantly large, An
important data source of the unincorporated sector is the Annual Survey of Unincorporated Sector
Enterprises (ASUSE), 2022-23. The basic features of this sector are the following: (a) easy entry
for new enterprises, (b) family ownership of enterprises, (c) reliance on indigenous resources, (d)
small-scale operations and low productivity, (e) labour-intensive technology, etc. For the survey
in 2022-23, 16,497 (8,568 in rural and 7,929 in urban) first-stage units (FSUs) had been selected
at an all-India level. Out of these, 16,382 FSUs (8,495 in rural and 7,887 in urban) have been
surveyed, The total number of establishments surveyed was 4,58,938 (2,58,296 in rural areas and
2,00,642 in urban areas). ASUSE 2022-23 provides an estimate of 6.50 crore establishments
categorised as the unincorporated non-agricultural sector for the period October 2022 — September
2023 for different broad activity categories (BAC). Out of these 6.50 crore establishments, 3.56

crore belong to the rural areas and 2.94 crore to the urban areas,

ASUSE categorises establishments into two; own account establishments (OAE), and hired worker
establishments (HWE). OAE is defined as an establishment that is run without any hired workers
employed on a regular basis (the major part of the period during which the operations of an
establishment are carried out in a reference period), HWE is defined as an establishment that
employs at least one hired worker on a regular basis. Paid or unpaid apprentices, paid household
member/servant/resident workers in an establishment are considered as hired workers. Out of 6.50
crore establishments, 5.53 crore establishments were OAEs, and 97 lakh establishments have been
estimated as HWE. In rural India, the estimated number of OAEs was 3.28 crore, and the estimated
number of HWEs was 0.28 crore. In urban India, the estimated numbers of OAEs and HWEs were

2.25 crore and 0.69 crore, respectively.

Out of 6.5 crore unincorporated non-agricultural establishments, 5.7 percent were located in Bihar
(see Table 4.7). Further disaggregation suggests that, out of 3.56 crore establishments in rural
India, the number of establishments in Bihar was 28.09 lakh (or 7.9 percent) in 2022-23. Out of
these 28.09 lakh establishments in rural Bihar, 25.67 lakh were OAEs, and 2.4 lakh were HWEs.
After Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal, Bihar has the largest number of establishments in rural areas.
The number of establishments in urban Bihar was 8.9 lakh. Out of 8.9 lakh establishments in urban
Bihar, 7.0 lakh were OAEs, and 1.9 lakh were HWEs.
