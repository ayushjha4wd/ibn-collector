1.3 Per Capita GSDP

The data on the per capita Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) in Bihar and the per capita Gross
Domestic Product (GDP) in India, both at current and constant (2011-12) prices, is presented in
Table 1.5. Chart 1.3 illustrates the per capita GSDP in Bihar at these price levels. In Bihar, the per
capita GSDP at current prices has risen from Rs. 23,525 in 2011-12 to Rs, 66,828 in 2023-24, At
constant (2011-12) prices, the per capita GSDP has increased from Rs. 23,525 in 2011-12 to
Rs. 36,333 in 2023-24. The per capita GSDP in Bihar is estimated to have increased by 12.8 percent
at current prices and by 7.6 percent at constant (2011-12) prices compared to the previous year.
For India, the estimated per capita GDP at current prices is Rs. 1,84,205, while at constant
(2011-12) prices, it is Rs, 1,06,744 for the year 2023-24.

Between 2011-12 and 2023-24, the per capita Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) at current
prices in Bihar grew at an average annual rate of 9.1 percent. In contrast, the per capita GSDP at
constant prices experienced an average annual growth rate of 3.7 percent. Following the economic
contraction caused by the Covid-19 pandemic in 2020-21, Bihar's per capita GSDP at current
prices demonstrated consistent double-digit growth rates. It increased by 12.4 percent in 2021-22,
13.6 percent in 2022-23, and 12.8 percent in 2023-24. The per capita GSDP at constant (2011-12)

prices in Bihar grew by 8.2 percent in 2022-23 and 7.6 percent in 2023-24.
