5.3 Employment Generated by the State Government and Other Organisations

The Government of Bihar has been creating a substantial number of employment opportunities in
the public sector and related services. The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC), the Bihar
Technical Services Commission (BTSC), the Bihar Police Sub-Ordinate Services Commission,
the Central Selection Board of Constables, and the Bihar State Electronic Development
Corporation (BSEDC) are responsible for recruiting qualified candidates for various positions

within the State government departments.

Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) : The commission selects officers for various
departments of the Government of Bihar and submits recommendations for appointments. After
receiving requisitions from various departments, advertisements are published for various posts.
Based on the applications received from eligible candidates in response to these advertisements,
competitive examinations are conducted to select suitable candidates. In 2023-24, advertisements
were published for 4,29,015 posts. As of September 30, 2024, 2,00,592 applications have been
received for 3420 advertised posts.
