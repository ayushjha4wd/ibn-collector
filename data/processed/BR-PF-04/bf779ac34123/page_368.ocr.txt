8.9 Citizen Friendly Services

Bihar DISCOMs have consistently prioritized delivering online services to their customers to

enhance satisfaction. Below are some key services offered to consumers:

Bill through SMS on Missed Call: Through SMS, the consumer can now get information about
the current due payment and due date against the electricity connection, by just giving a missed
call. Additionally, the Uniform Resource Locator (URL) is also being provided by the system for
downloading the latest electricity bill. This service has significantly decreased the number of
customer visits to the electricity office and has minimized paper usage by providing e-duplicate
energy bills via a PDF link sent through SMS.

Suvidha Mobile App: The DISCOMs have launched a mobile app called SUVIDHA to provide
various services to consumers. These services encompass applying for new service connections,
paying bills online, downloading bills, checking recent payment transactions, registering
complaints, reporting electricity theft, changing load, and updating personal information such as
name, address, and mobile number. Consumers can also monitor the status of their service requests
through the SUVIDHA App. Additionally, all supply divisions have established SUVIDHA
counters, These counters serve as a single-window interface between service providers and

consumers,

Social Media: To create an enabling environment, DISCOMs have established a dedicated cell to
answer issues or concerns raised in social media. They have created official accounts on Facebook,
Twitter and YouTube to promote various schemes through these platforms to keep the consumers

updated on any policy issues.

Grievance Redressal: Consumers can lodge complaints or grievances by calling the toll-free
number 1912. Additionally, an Online Grievance Page is accessible on the company’s website,
allowing the complainant to file complaints or grievances directly. They can also track the progress

of the actions taken regarding their complaints or grievances on the portal.

Self-Generation of Electricity Bill: A new facility has been added the 'Suvidha’ App’, through
which consumers can generate and print their electricity bill by just sending a self-meter reading.
The bills are generated by the system without manual intervention. This facility is available to the
consumers whose regular bill is not served/ generated within 35 days from the previous bill.
