Motivational/Promotional activities
Certificate of Merit

Government Engineering Colleges and Polytechnic Institutes have been organizing annual events
on Independence Day to recognize the achievements of their meritorious students. Additionally,
the department also hosts an annual ceremony on September 1510 award the top performers from
each branch in these institutions.

Science Day

The department celebrates Science Day every year to recognize students’ talents, promote interest
in understanding and learning, address challenges in science, and promote scientific thinking. Each
year, a C.V. Raman Talent Search competition in science is organized for students in grades 6 to
12. An award ceremony is held at both district and state levels, where students who achieve first,
second, and third place are honoured.

Math Day

The department celebrates Math Day annually to recognize students’ talents, promote interest in
understanding and learning, address challenges related to mathematics, and promote logical
thinking. Each year, the Srinivasa Ramanujan Talent Search competition is held for students in
grades 6 to 12. Award ceremonies are held at both district and state levels, honouring students who
achieve first, second, and third place.

Other Initiatives

Bihar Student Credit Card Yojana (BSCCY) : The Bihar Student Credit Card Yojana is a
transformative initiative designed to enhance educational accessibility and equity in Bihar. It
directly contributes to improving the Gross Enrolment Raio in higher education and aligns with
the state's broader socio-economic development goals. The scheme was launched on October
2,2016 with an objective to provide financial support to students for pursuing higher education,
thereby reducing economic barriers to learning. BSCCY is associated with the “Aarthik Hal,
Yuvaon ko Bal” which comes under the Saat Nishchay Programme of the Bihar government.
Under this, a loan amount upto Rs. 4 lakh is given for the courses in different Higher education
(Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Professional courses), This also includes Polytechnic courses
and Industrial Training Institute (ITI) programmes. The per annum rate of interest for this loan is
4 percent for boys and 1 percent for girls, disabled, and transgender students respectively. Under
this scheme, in the initial phase, the loans were disbursed through banks but since April 1,2018,
loans are disbursed by the Bihar State Education Financial Corporation Limited (BSEFCL) for
streamlined and efficient processing. The scheme emphasizes gender equity and supports
marginalized groups like differently-abled and transgender students with significantly lower
interest rates, This scheme eases the financial burden on families and supports students to focus
on their studies without economic worries. The scheme also provides support for ITI and
Polytechnic courses which aligns with the state's vision to enhance vocational skills and
