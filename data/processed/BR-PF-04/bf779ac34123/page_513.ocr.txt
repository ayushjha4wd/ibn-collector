Welfare of Backward and Extremely Backward Classes

The Backward Classes are those castes/communities that are notified as such by the State or
Central government, In Bihar, there are 131 backward castes and they account for about 64 percent
of its population. In 2007-08, the State government had constituted the Backward Class and
Extremely Backward Class Welfare Department for the welfare and overall development of these
communities. The outline of financial progress from 2018-19 to 2024-25 for the welfare of
backward and extremely backward population is presented in Table 12.28. In the last seven years,
the expenditure for the welfare of backward classes has increased at the rate of 7.3 percent

annually. Also, the expenditure under state scheme increased at the rate of 7.1 percent annually.
