Mahadalit Welfare

The State government has launched various specialized initiatives based on the findings of the
State Mahadalit Commission, aimed at helping the most marginalized segments of the scheduled
castes. The Commission noted that out of the 22 scheduled castes in Bihar, 21 experience severe
deprivation concerning their economic, social, cultural, and political conditions. The individuals
from these castes have not reaped significant benefits from the affirmative action designed for the
SC population. Consequently, the Bihar Mahadalit Vikas Mission was conceived to fulfill the
fundamental needs of all Mahadalit communities. This Mission is officially registered under the
Societies Registration Act of 1860. The State government's specific focus on the welfare of
Mahadalits encompasses:

Vikas Mitra : The Vikas Mitras have been appointed for assisting in proper implementation of
programmes in panchayats and wards. To promote empowerment of women, 50 percent of the
posts of Vikas Mitra are reserved for women. Presently, out of the total sanctioned posts of 9820
Vikas Mitras, 9648 are working in the state and they are selected from the Mahadalit community
itself.

Dashrath Manjhi Kaushal Vikas Yojana : The scheme has been started with an objective to
improve the employability of the members of the Mahadalit Community through skill development
and training in different trades. The scheme is being executed by the Department of Labour
