14.8 Plastic Waste Management

The State government has notified a complete ban on the manufacture, import, store, transport,
sale and use of plastic carry bags (irrespective of their size and thickness) in Bihar since 2018 in
all ULBs and Gram Panchayats, The State government has not prohibited carry bags made from
compostable plastic materials, According to the amended Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016,
the thickness requirements do not apply to carry bags made of compostable and biodegradable
plastics. Carry bags produced from compostable plastic must adhere to the Indian Standard IS
17088:2021,

Furthermore, on August 12, 2021, the Union Ministry of Environment, Forest, and Climate Change
(MoEF&CC) issued a notification aimed at regulating single-use plastics, while the State
government's Department of Environment, Forest, and Climate Change also put out a similar
notice. According to these notifications, the production, import, storage, distribution, sale, and use
of certain single-use plastic items, including polystyrene and expanded polystyrene, are prohibited
since July 1, 2022:

(a) Ear buds with plastic sticks, plastic sticks for balloons, plastic flags, candy sticks, ice-cream
sticks, polystyrene (Thermocol) for decoration,

(b) Plates, cups, glasses, cutlery such as forks, spoons, knives, straw, trays, wrapping or packing
films around sweet boxes, invitation cards, and cigarette packets, plastic, or PVC banners
less than 100 micron and stirrers.

(c) The above provisions are not applicable to the commodities made of compostable plastic and
bio-degradable plastic as per the MoEF&CC notification of July 6, 2022.
