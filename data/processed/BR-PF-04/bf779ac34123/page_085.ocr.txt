CHAPTER - II
STATE FINANCES

Abstract

The State government's budget has significantly increased over the years, and this increase
has been effectively utilised to improve the infrastructure necessary for economic growth in
Bihar. Furthermore, the government has allocated more funds to achieve various
developmental goals, resulting in notable improvements across indicators. At the same time,
the government has managed its finances prudently, ensuring that the rise in expenditure
remains fiscally sustainable. Additionally, its fiscal management has been flexible enough to
avoid vulnerabilities. In 2023-24, the total expenditure of the State government was
Rs. 2,52,082 crore, which comprised a revenue expenditure of Rs. 1,90,514 crore and a capital
expenditure of Rs. 61,568 crore. The receipts in the revenue account were Rs. 1,93,347 crore,
resulting in a surplus of Rs. 2,833 crore. The receipts in the capital account for the State
government amounted to Rs. 60,313 crore in 2023-24. Within the total expenditure for
2023-24, Scheme expenditure accounted for Rs. 1,01,835 crore, while Establishment and
Committed expenditures totalled Rs. 1,50,247 crore. During this period, the state's total
borrowing was Rs. 60,218 crore, with a public debt repayment of Rs. 22,979 crore. Interest
payments made by the State government in 2023-24 were Rs. 17,606 crore, and the outstanding
debt stood at Rs. 3,32,741 crore. Overall, the state's distribution of receipts and expenditures
in 2023-24 reflects prudent fiscal management, highlighting its commitment to long-term
economic growth.
