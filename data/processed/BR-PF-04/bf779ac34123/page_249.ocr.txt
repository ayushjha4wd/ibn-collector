Eradication of Child Labour : The existence of child labour poses a significant threat to any
civilised society. In accordance with the Child and Adolescent Labor (Prohibition and Regulation)
Act, 1986, the Bihar State Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Rules, 2024
have been implemented, Furthermore, the Bihar State Child Labor Commission (Amendment) Act,
2024 was enacted to amend the Bihar State Child Labor Commission Act, 1996.

The State government established the Bihar State Child Labour Commission in 1999. The
Commission's mandate includes addressing child labour issues, facilitating rehabilitation, and
advising the government on related matters. Recently, the State government has reorganised the
Commission to enhance its effectiveness. District-wise number of rescued and rehabilitated child
labour under the Chief Minister’s Relief Fund is presented in Table A 5.2,

The Commission has collaborated with NGOs across all districts of Bihar to organise awareness
campaigns at various locations and on multiple occasions. Furthermore, the Commission has
established task forces in different districts to rescue child labour. These task forces hold regular
meetings at the district, block, and panchayat levels.

Following the Hon'ble Supreme Court’s directive, the offending employers are required to pay a
fine of Rs. 20,000 as compensation. This amount is deposited into the Child Labour Rehabilitation
and Welfare Fund. Upon rescue, each child receives Rs. 3,000 for clothing and one month’s supply
of food, Additionally, Rs. 25,000 is allocated to each freed child from the Chief Minister's Relief
Fund.
