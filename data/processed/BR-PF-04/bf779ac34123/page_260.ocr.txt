Sankalp Yojana: Sankalp Yojana is a centrally sponsored scheme, and is funded by the World
Bank. It has introduced innovative training programmes in Bihar, including tyre fitting, street
vending, and e-cart driving for economically disadvantaged women under Patna Municipal
Corporation, So far, 1.301 candidates have been trained under Sankalp, with 1,181 candidates
receiving the completion certificates. Under the Sankalp Yojana, a Migration Counselling and
Registration Centre (MCRC) is being established for migrant workers of Bihar. An RFP has been
published to select an agency to set up MCRC centres across ten districts in Bihar (Patna, Purnea,
Bhagalpur, Madhubani, Siwan, Darbhanga, Muzaffarpur, Samastipur, Sitamarhi, and Gaya).
Additionally, centres will be established at ten key locations outside Bihar (like Mumbai, Delhi,
Guwahati, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Surat, Pune, Ludhiana, and Jaipur) to offer migrant

workers vital guidance, information, and counselling to face workplace challenges.

Other Achievements

The innovative flagship programmes, like KYP and RTD scheme, have been recognised with the
national-level SKOCH Award in 2023-24. NITI Aayog has also recognised the Kushal Yuva

Programme.

At the 2023 World Skills Competition (considered the Olympics of skill competitions),
participants from Bihar won 13 medals, including 4 gold, 2 silver, and 5 bronze. In 2023-24,
BSDM has organised state-level competitions with the National Skill Development Corporation
(NSDC).

In future, BSDM plans to organise roadshows, creating a command-and-control centre,
implementing KYP in high schools, conducting 3-day training sessions for KYP trainers, and
launching divisional-level reviews and KYP dialogues. New skill centres will be opened in
subdivisions where it was non-existent previously. Under BS-CFA, a "Mega Skill Centre” in Patna

will be established using the hub-and-spoke model.

different departments in the State (Table 5.30). BSDM and the Department of Rural Development
have received Rs. 799.03 crore, translating to 89 percent of the total allocation. BSDM alone
received a budget allocation of Rs. 621.0 crore in 2023-24 for the skilling programmes. The
finances for the skilling programme came from three sources, viz., the state plan, the state plan
