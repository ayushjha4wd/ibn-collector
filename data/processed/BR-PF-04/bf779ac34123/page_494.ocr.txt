Higher Education

The higher education sector in Bihar has witnessed a tremendous expansion in terms of the number
of universities, university-level institutions and colleges over the years. The importance of higher
education in contributing towards the development of human potential is even more significant for
states like Bihar. The progress of higher education in any state is assessed in terms of its
institutional capacity and streams, The educational institutional capacity is measured by the
number of educational institutions — universities and colleges, number of teachers and number of
students seeking higher education, Streams may be of several natures — general, vocational,

professional or technical education. Chart 12.13 portrays the status of higher education in Bihar.
