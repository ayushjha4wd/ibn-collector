Flood Forecasting Models : Precise and reliable forecasting is important for flood warning, flood
control planning, and rehabilitation. After the establishment of MMC, the flood forecasting is
being done for major north Bihar rivers in terms of water level and discharge at various locations
inside Bihar. These flood forecasting models use Rainfall data (observed as well as forecast),
observed water level and discharge at various locations, cross-section of the river, evaporation data
and other hydrological data as input. These operational flood forecasting models in Bihar continue
to be developed, based on the availability of quality input.

River Behaviour Analysis : Characteristics of the rivers are largely determined by the
geomorphological characteristics of boundary materials ic. bank and bed materials, its terrain
slope, topography, geology, physiography, tectonics and seismicity. An in-depth understanding of
the morphology and behaviour of the river is thus a pre-requisite for a scientific and rational
approach to address different problems of the river, and for proper planning and designing of water
resources projects. For river behaviour analysis, time-series satellite images and historical maps
are great sources to understand the planform changes of a river. The River Behaviour Analysis
tool for conducting a morphological study of the Kosi river system was developed in 2017. This
has been helpful in planning river training works and anti-erosion control schemes aimed at
sustaining flood protection since then. Presently, a morphological study of the Gandak River is
ongoing for development of the river behaviour analysis tool for this river,
