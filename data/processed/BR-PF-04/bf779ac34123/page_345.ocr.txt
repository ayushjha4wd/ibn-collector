North Bihar Power Distribution Company Limited (NBPDCL) and South Bihar Power
Distribution Company Limited (SBPDCL)

These two companies undertake the activities of distribution of electricity to all consumers, trading
of electricity, and implementation of rural electrification schemes — under Deen Dayal Upadhyay
Gram Jyoti Yojana (erstwhile Rajiv Gandhi Grameen Vidyutikaran Yojana), Special Plan
(Backward Region Grant Fund - BRGF), Integrated Power Devolvement Scheme, State Plan and
schemes funded by Asian Development Bank (ADB), the last one being an Externally-aided
Project (EAP), The introduction of open access in distribution as per the Electricity Act, 2003 and
the directions of the Regulator is also the responsibility of these two companies. They also tender,
finalise and execute the Power Purchase Agreements (PPA) and other agreements for sale or
purchase of electricity.

The fund allocation under the State Plan for BSPHCL and its subsidiary companies, Bihar
Renewable Energy Development Agency (BREDA) and Bihar State Hydroelectric Power
Corporation (BSHPC) in 2023-24 was Rs. 1586.52 crore. Due to a new step taken by the State
government, which relates to Har-Khet-Tak-Sinchai-Ka-Paani (Saat Nishchay-2), a sum of
Rs. 300.00 crore is being allocated every year from 2021-22 for the scheme. The breakup of the
