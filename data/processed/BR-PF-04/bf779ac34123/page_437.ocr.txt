The data indicates a significant adoption of digital infrastructure-based banking services in Bihar
over the past five years. The number of mobile banking users in the state rose from 48.0 lakhs on
March 31, 2020 to 162.9 lakhs on March 31, 2024, reflecting an increase of 239.3 percent. Internet
Banking users grew by 168.9 percent during the same period, from 57.0 lakhs to 153.3 lakhs.
Additionally, the number of ATM cards issued and the number of POS machines operated by
banks in Bihar increased by 41.9 percent and 45.9 percent, respectively. These trends demonstrate
a growing reliance on technology-based banking services in the state compared to the earlier
practice of visiting Bank branches or ATM centers,
