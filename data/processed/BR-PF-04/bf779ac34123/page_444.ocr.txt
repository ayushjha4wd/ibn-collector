A summary of the loans provided by MFI lenders in Bihar and across India from 2022 to 2024 is
presented in Table 11.5. Chart 11.8 illustrates the changes in the number of NBFC-MFI and NGO-
MFI lenders during these years. In Bihar, the number of NBFC-MFI lenders rose from 42 in March
2022 to 64 in March 2024. Meanwhile, at the national level, the number of NBFC-MFI lenders
increased from 64 to 90. Additionally, the number of NGO-MFI lenders in Bihar grew from 4 in
March 2022 to 11 in March 2024, At the all-India level, the number of NGO-MF! lenders also saw

significant growth, rising from 21 to 45 during the same period.
