Bihar shares an international border with Nepal and the people of both Bihar and Nepal travel very
frequently to each other’s territory for economic, social, cultural, and religious reasons. In this
background, through a mutual agreement, BSRTC has provided two buses on Bodh Gaya — Patna
~ Kathmandu and four Buses on Patna-Janakpur routes as daily transport services.

In order to provide safe, clean and comfortable services to the passengers and to strengthen the
public transportation in the state, currently 581 buses are plying in rural and urban areas through
the Corporation. In addition, 300 buses are running under the Public-Private Partnership (PPP)
mode. In light of the increasing population of Patna, 127 buses are provided for easy and speedy
travel in Patna and nearby regions. BSRTC has taken a number of measures for the benefit of its
passengers.

The BSRTC has further embarked upon the journey of adopting state-of-the-art technologies in
the domain of online ticketing, GPS-based vehicle location tracking systems, Automatic fare
collection system (AFCS), Bus Ticketing Machines and Common Mobility Cards. All the City
buses have been equipped with the ETIMs and other necessary equipment for the same and the
passengers are availing of the Prepaid cards, passes and online services, providing a positive
impact on travelling experience of the commuters.

As a recent initiative, the Transport Department has surveyed Bus Stands across Bihar. It has been
decided that the BSRTC bus stands will be used as model bus stands to be developed across the
state for better citizen-centric services. The Department has taken this step to ensure proper
accessibility, conducive ambience, smooth and digital ticketing system, food court, Public
Announcement System and amenities for commuters at the bus stands under the jurisdiction, The
BSRTC has received Rs. 12.26 crore for the upgradation and renovation of facilities at 14 Bus
Stands in 2023-24, A comprehensive plan is being chalked out to cover all the depots in the state.

6.11 Railway Network

Railway plays a pivotal role in the transportation of passengers and freights in India. A total of
4509 kms of railway tracks existed in Bihar till March, 2024, which constituted 3.4 percent of the
total tracks in the country. It contributes a notable proportion of transportation of goods as well as
passengers. It is an economic means of transportation. Chart 6.8 illustrates contribution of railways
sector in GSVA during 2011-12 to 2023-24 in Bihar. The railways transport in GSVA of Bihar
shows moderate growth, with fluctuating trends, and recorded a decline in growth during 2016-17
and 2020-21. However, this sub-sector of economy grew by almost three times during these 13
years, from Rs. 2751 crore in 2011-12 to Rs. 7522 crore in 2023-24.
