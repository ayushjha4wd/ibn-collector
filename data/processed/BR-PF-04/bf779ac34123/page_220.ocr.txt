A new initiative has been taken by COMFED for the all-round development of farmers,
especially, milk producers in November, 2023, COMFED has helped in the constitution of 10
Farmers Producer Organisations (FPOs) in five milk unions. Generally, FPOs are formed and
managed by farmers, which gives them bargaining power to determine the prices of the produce
owing to their control over a large quantity of the produce. To form FPO and making it
economically viable and self-reliant, milk unions of COMFED have been selected as Cluster
Based Business Organisation (CBBO) in collaboration with the National Dairy Development
Board (NDDB), Anand. Two types of FPOs have been constituted: Fodder Plus FPOs and
Honey Production FPOs. Out of 10 FPOs constituted, seven were Fodder Plus FPOs and three
were Honey Production FPOs,

4.4 Non-Agro Based Industries
Textile Industry

Under the purview of the Bihar Industrial Investment Promotion Policy 2016, the Bihar Industrial
Investment Promotion Policy (Textile and Leather Policy) 2022 has been notified. This policy
aims to create a value chain from fibre to fashion, promote micro and small units, and incentivise
the value addition of khadi, silk, handloom, and powerloom. Furthermore, the policy seeks to
incentivise the large units in the hosiery, garments, and textile processing segments. In Bihar, 14
districts produce specialised handloom products (shown in Table 4.23).

The state produces three types of silk: mulberry, tasar, and castor/Eri, As shown in Table 4.24, the
total mulberry cocoon production was 10,4 tonnes, yielding |.2 tonnes of raw silk in 2023-24. The
production of tasar and castor/Eri raw silk was 43.9 tonnes and 15.2 tonnes, respectively. Between
2022-23 and 2023-24, the production of raw silk from tasar and castor/Eri has increased by 18
percent and 15 percent, respectively. In 2023-24, the budget allocation was Rs. 430.0 lakh,
reflecting a 32.3 percent growth over a one-year period. The entire budget allocation was utilised
in that financial year.
