Under the Integrated Horticulture Development Scheme, farmers cultivating yam, turmeric, and

organic inputs. The programme is being implemented in 12 districts in Bihar. Training. seminars,
and workshops are organised to enhance farmers’ knowledge and skills in cultivation of crops. In

Flower Production

Commercial production of flowers has taken place in some parts of Bihar, The most prominent
flowers are marigold, rose, and jasmine. In 2023-24, 1.350 thousand hectares of land were
cultivated with marigold, and the total marigold production was 12.8 thousand tonnes. Marigold
production constituted 96.0 percent of the total flower production.

To promote collectivization and improve the marketing facilities for the horticulture crops. the
Horticulture Cluster Development Scheme has been launched for guava, Indian gooseberry,
lemon, wood apple, papaya, dragon fruit, strawberry, lemongrass, and marigold flower. Each
cluster will cover a minimum area of 25 acres in a revenue village for cultivation of identified
horticulture crops. The farmers are offered a subsidy of Rs. 2 lakh per acre for strawberry and
