The geographical complexion of Bihar, its boundary, its land, its water bodies, the climate and,
above all, its location makes it the Home State of Hazards. Almost all the districts are prone to
most of the major hazards: earthquake, floods, cyclone, drought and fire. Sometimes two of the
major hazards visit different parts of the state during the same period. This constitutes the multi -
hazard profile of the state in the true sense of the term. The severity of disaster losses depends on
a number of factors, including the type of disaster, its magnitude, and the vulnerability of the
affected population. The lives lost and injured casualties in the state due to various natural disasters
and accidents are presented in Table 14.25. In 2023-24, the total deaths were 2107, of which, the
highest number of deaths occurred due to drowning (1560), followed by lightning (242) and fire
(120). Also, 151 persons died of snakebite in the state during the same year. In addition to flooding,
the state frequently experiences thunderstorms, particularly in the south-central and south-eastern
regions. Appendix (Table A 14.7) presents the district-wise incidence of thunderstorm-related
deaths.
