The Fourth Agricultural Roadmap (2023-28) reports that the Forest Survey of India estimated the
state's total green area to be 13,896 sq km, representing 14.75 percent of its land area in 2019,
based on high-resolution satellite imagery (LISS-IV), This figure encompasses both notified forest
areas and non-forest tree cover.

Trend and Pattern of Forest Density

In order to create a sustainable environment, the State government took several initiatives to
increase areas under forest cover as well as tree cover. Between 2011 and 2023, open forest grew
by 527 sq km followed by very dense forest area, which has increased by 156 sq km (Table 14.3).
Open forest constitutes more than half of the forest, while moderately dense forest contributes 44
percent (Chart 14.7). The distribution of district-wise forest cover is shown in Appendix Table
