State Government Initiative

Sudha Milk : In addition to the breakfast, children receiving pre-school education through
Anganwadi Centres between 3 and 6 years of age are provided Sudha Milk Powder two days in a
week (Tuesday and Thursday). Twelve Grams of Sudha Milk Powder is mixed with 100 ml of
water and given to the children.

Egg : In addition to the breakfast, Hen’s Egg is being given to the children of 3-6 years of age
receiving pre-school education through Anganwadi Centres and those children who do not eat Egg
are provided Roasted Peanuts two days a week (Wednesday and Friday).
