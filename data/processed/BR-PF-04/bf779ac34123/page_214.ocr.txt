4.3 Agro-based Industries
Sugar Industry

Besides sugar production, ethanol production as biofuel and power generation has increased the
role of sugar mills in producing energy for the state. The National Policy on Biofuels, 2018 allowed
the production of ethanol using sugarcane juice, In 2023-24, 677.20 lakh quintals of sugarcane was
crushed in nine sugar mills of Bihar. At the recovery rate of 10.16 percent (with BH), the total
sugar production was 68,77 lakh quintals (see Table 4.15). Over the years, the recovery rate has
increased; hence, the productivity of sugarcane has increased. In 2023-24, the distillery capacity
and power generation of sugar mills have been 530 KLPD (kilo litre per day) and 88.5 MW (mega
watt), respectively (see Table 4.16), The increased production-related activities in sugar mills have
also increased the number of days of operation, which implies that more number of days of
employment were generated in the sugar mills (see Table 4.17).

Based on the National Policy on Biofuels, 2018, the Government of Bihar has notified the Bihar
Biofuels Production Promotion Policy, 2023, The stated objectives of the policy are, first, to
promote production of Ethanol and Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG)/bio-CNG from all feedstocks
permitted under the National Policy on Biofuels, 2018, and by the National Biofuel Coordination
Committee. The second objective is to facilitate and financially incentivise eligible investment in
the sphere of Ethanol and Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG) manufacturing. Third, this policy also aims
to increase the income of farmers for producing feedstock or raw material, and generate
employment at the manufacturing units and related services.
