CHAPTER - I
BIHAR ECONOMY : AN OVERVIEW

Abstract

Bihar has witnessed significant economic growth over the last two decades, resulting in notable
improvements in several socio-economic indicators. The State government's initiatives during
this period have contributed to advancements in education, health, and other social indicators,
along with a general increase in the per capita income. According to the latest available
estimates, the Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) of Bihar for the year 2023-24 is estimated
to be Rs. 8,54,429 crore at current prices and Rs. 4,64,540 crore at constant (2011-12) prices.
The GSDP in 2023-24 is estimated to have increased by 14.5 percent at current prices and 9.2
percent at constant prices, respectively, compared to the previous year. The per capita GSDP
in Bihar at current and constant (2011-12) prices is estimated to have increased by 12.8 percent
to Rs, 66,828 and hy 7.6 percent to Rs. 36,333, respectively, in 2023-24 over the previous year.
The share of the tertiary sector in the Gross State Value Added (GSVA) at constant (2011-12)
prices is estimated to be 58.6 percent, followed hy 21.5 percent from the secondary sector and
19.9 percent from the primary sector. The Gross Fixed Capital Formation (GFCF) at current
prices in the state is estimated to be Rs. 38,986 crore in 2023-24, which is 4.6 percent of the
GSDP. The analysis of economic activities in the state, as outlined in the Bihar Economic

Survey 2024-25, presents an encouraging picture for further development in the state.
