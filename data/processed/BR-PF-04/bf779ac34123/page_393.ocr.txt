The Food Corporation of India (FCI) is crucial in ensuring food security by consistently supplying
food grains to the state governments. It procures wheat and rice from farmers at Minimum Support
Prices (MSP), ensuring fair prices and stabilizing incomes. FCI allocates food grains to PDS and
welfare schemes, ensuring efficient transportation and timely distribution. It stabilizes market
prices during shortages and acts as a buffer stock provider during emergencies, thereby supporting
the nation’s food security,

To combat malnutrition across the country, the Government of India has incorporated Fortified
Rice into the PDS and welfare schemes such as the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS)
and the Mid-Day Meal Scheme. Fortified Rice Kermels (FRK), enriched with essential nutrients
like iron, folic acid, and vitamin 3-12, are blended with regular rice in a 1:100 ratio during milling.
This initiative aims to ensure the delivery of vital nutrients to beneficiaries, thereby improving
overall health outcomes.
