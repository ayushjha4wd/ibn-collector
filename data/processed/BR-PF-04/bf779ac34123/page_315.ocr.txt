Traffic Signals Integration with Surveillance and CCTV Operations (TISCO) : In order to
implement CCTV-based surveillance systems in 9 cities in Bihar - Begusarai, Bettiah, Chhapra,
Darbhanga, Dehri, Gaya, Munger, Purnea and Saharsa, the TISCO project aims to create a safe,
secure and empowering environment for citizens in public places, to enable them to pursue all
opportunities without any threat of gender-based violence and/or harassment and also implement
the intelligent and adaptive traffic control system. The State government has taken the initiative to
implement TISCO in 9 cities of Bihar to ensure the safety and security of citizens, determine crime,
and facilitate a real-time traffic detection system 10 avoid congestion, wastage of fuel, ete.

availed the services being offered under the project. Phase-I of the project was initiated in 2009,
