Value Added Tax Management Information System (VATMIS) : The VATMIS is a web-based
application software, which provides an integrated computerized solution to the Commercial
Taxes Department for administration of various taxes, like Value Added Tax, Central Sales Tax,
Luxury Tax, Electricity Duty, Advertisement Tax, Entertainment Tax and Professional Tax.

GST Single Sign-On ; GSTSSO is an integrated platform designed to streamline the access and
management of various GST-related applications. Key platforms hosted on GSTSSO include GST
Prime, Comprehensive e-Waybill Analytics, e-Waybill MIS, and e-Invoice Analytics, each of

which plays a crucial role in the tax administration system.

GAIN Portal (GST Analytics and Intelligence Network) : Earlier known as BIFA Portal, it has
been developed by BIFA Unit-GSTN for the use of DGGI, CGST and SGST officials. It envisages
detection of tax evasion by its ability to display complex networks of suppliers and do an analysis
of connected transactions. The focus areas of this tool are primarily Gap Analysis, Network
Analysis, Anomaly Detection, Identification of Inconsistent Behaviour, Revenue Analysis and
Predictive Modelling.

e-Stamping : This provides e-stamping facility to the citizens through a secure electronic way to
generate e-Stamp certificates. The Stamp certificate so generated will have unique identification
number and security features that will eliminate the counterfeits. As a part of Citizen Services and
to have a digital strategy to ensure a fast and hassle-free payment process, the departmental in-
house ‘e-Stamping’ is required in the new e-Registration Solution which, in turn, makes handling
this mandatory procedure convenient, every time a party executes a deed/contract.

e-Registration

The e-Registration facility for land transactions has been gradually rolled out to the public from
July 29, 2024, which is now available in all 137 offices in the State, This initiative allows the
public to apply for land registration from their homes and can automatically calculate the value
of their land, Through this new software, users will receive real-time updates on their
application status, land category information, and applicable fees thereof. The system also
incorporates e-K YC (Aadhaar Authentication) for the concerned parties and is integrated with
the Land Revenue and Reforms Department's platform. For individuals in land transactions,
the registration process requires only one visit to the office for getting their photographs,
fingerprints, and signing the agreement.
