4.9 Mines and Geology

One of the formidable sources of revenue for the State government has been minerals. In 2023-24,
the State government set a target for revenue collection of Rs. 3,300.00 crore, which was 10 percent
more than the revenue target of 2022-23. In 2023-24, the amount of revenue earned from all
sources of minerals was Rs. 3,114.79 crore, which was 14 percent more than the revenue collection
of the previous year.

in the absence of major minerals, Bihar’s primary source of revenue generation has been minor
minerals. Among minor minerals, the most important one is sand mining. The construction
activities in Bihar have caused the demand for construction materials, including sand, to rise
rapidly. In 2023-24, of the total revenue collected from mining (both major and minor), sand
mining alone contributed 50.0 percent. Furthermore, over the years, the revenue collection has
increased significantly. Between 2022-23 and 2023-24, revenue collection from sand mining
increased from Rs. 1,384.47 crore to Rs. 1,558.16 crore, registering a growth of 12.5 percent.
Similarly, the revenue collection from the Public Works Department has also increased
significantly in the past five years. Between 2022-23 and 2023-24, the revenue collection of the
Public Works Department rose by 30 percent (see Table 4.36).

In recent years, the State government has gained control over 12 major mineral blocks from the
Ministry of Mines. Since nine out of 12 mineral blocks are of strategic importance, the Government
of India will carry out the auction process. Meanwhile, the State government will auction three
mineral blocks (two blocks of iron ores in Jamui district and one block of limestone in Rohtas
district), In 2023-24, the total revenue collection from major minerals was Rs. 8.32 crore.
