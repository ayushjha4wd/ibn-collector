Jalasaya Matsyki Vikas Yojana : The Bihar State Reservoir Fisheries Policy 2022 was approved
by the state government. Under this policy, the Animal and Fisheries Resource Department has
granted ‘Fisheries Rights’ in all the reservoirs of the State to increase fish production. To achieve
this goal, the Jalasaya Matsyki Vikas Yojana has been sanctioned, The components of the scheme
include the stocking of fish fingerlings in reservoirs, the installation of cages in reservoirs, and
installation of pens in reservoirs. Farmers are offered financial assistance covering 70 percent of
the unit cost. In 2022-23 and 2023-24, the state government has spent Rs. 2.98 crore for various
components of the scheme.
