Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)

Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) provide essential banking services to underserved populations,
particularly in rural areas of Bihar. These regions often need more adequate access to the banking
facilities offered by Scheduled Commercial Banks and other financial institutions. RRBs play a
crucial role in supporting the economic activities of small and marginal farmers, artisans, and other
economically weaker sections of society in these areas. Bihar has two Regional Rural Banks:
Dakshin Bihar Gramin Bank and Uttar Bihar Gramin Bank. These banks offer various services,
including savings accounts, loans, and financial literacy programs, which contribute to
empowering the local population.

As of September 2024, the distribution of branches for Dakshin Bihar Gramin Bank and Uttar
Bihar Gramin Bank across the state is illustrated in Chart 11.7. Out of a total of 2,105 branches of
RRBs in Bihar, 1,078 branches (51.2 percent) belong to Dakshin Bihar Gramin Bank, while 1,027
branches (48.8 percent) belong to Uttar Bihar Gramin Bank. Among these two RRBs, Dakshin
Bihar Gramin Bank has a more extensive network of branches in rural areas, with 813 branches
compared to the 637 branches of Uttar Bihar Gramin Bank. In contrast, Uttar Bihar Gramin Bank
has a larger presence in semi-urban areas, with 346 branches, while Dakshin Bihar Gramin Bank
has 204 branches in these regions.
