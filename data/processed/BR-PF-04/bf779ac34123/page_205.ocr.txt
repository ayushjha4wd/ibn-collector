ASUSE collects information on a varicty of ownership of the establishments. The surveyed types
of ownership are proprietary (male/female/transgender), partnerships with members of the same
household, partnerships between members not all from the same household, self-help groups
(SHGs), societies/trusts/clubs/associations/bodies of individuals/membership organisations,
co-operatives, and others. Table 4.8 suggests that the majority of unincorporated non-agricultural
establishments were proprietary. At the all-India level, 94.7 percent of establishments were
proprietary, followed by SHG at 3.7 percent. Only 0.07 percent of establishments fall into the
ownership category of partnerships with members from the same or a different household. In the
case of proprietary establishments across all the states in India, male members held most of the
property rights of the establishments. In Bihar, 99.3 percent of the establishments were proprietary.
Of all the proprietor ownerships, only 14.5 percent of the proprietors were female.
