Jaivik Corridor Yojana

The second phase of the Jaivik Corridor Yojana was implemented in the same 13 districts as the
first phase. In this phase, 20,000 acres of land were distributed among the interested farmers who
were not part of the first phase of the Jaivik Corridor Yojana. The objectives of the Yojana evolved
to ensure sustainable agriculture, maintain soil quality, minimise the use of chemical fertilisers,
and create employment opportunities. Following a cluster-based approach, 129 FPOs or FPCs
were formed for organic farming. A comprehensive package of assistance is being extended to the
farmers to promote organic farming. The focus of the scheme is to provide infrastructural support
to farmers in terms of the Common Facility Centres (CFCs) and insulated vans as logistic support,
along with marketing assistance in the form of logistics and also organise three buyer-seller
mectings per year at the district level. The Jaivik Corridor Yojana has nearly achieved its physical
target in 2023-24. The State government has allocated Rs. 3,192.62 lakh for 2023-24, and
acquiring the C1 certification is in progress. As this is a continuous process, the work will continue
in 2024-25 to achieve its desired goal.
