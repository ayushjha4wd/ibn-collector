In 1989, the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child (UNCRC) adopted the most
widely approved human rights which defines the fundamental human rights of children along the
core principles that together cover their civil, political, social, economic and cultural rights. To
meet the needs of children, the first major comprehensive initiative taken by the Central
Government was the adoption of the National Policy for Children (NPC) in 1974. Another
initiative was the framing of the National Policy for Children 2013, which defined a child as a
person between 0 21018 years of age. For this stage of life, the Policy underlines the need for a
long-term, sustainable, multispectral, and inclusive approach. Thereafter, many reforms were
adopted to promote child development. One of those was to prepare separate child budgets. In
Bihar, this practice of child budgeting started in 2013-14.

13.1 Demography

India ranks as the second most populous nation globally, with 39 percent of its populations falling
within the 0-18 age group. according to the 2011 Census. In Bihar, 48 percent of the population is
in this age group. The state contributes to 11 percent of India’s overall child population. Bihar has
4.98 crore child population, of which 4.47 crore (89.9 percent) live in rural areas and 0.50 crore
(10.1 percent) in urban areas. As per gender details, 2.35 crore are female and 2.62 crore are male
in the age bracket of 0-18 years. The distributions of the child population across different age-
groups are — 0-6 years (38.4 percent), 7-14 years (45.4 percent) and 15-18 years (16.2 percent)
