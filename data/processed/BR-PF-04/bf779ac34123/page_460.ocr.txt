11.6 National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD)

The Nationa! Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) plays an essential role in
promoting the development of agriculture, agro-industries, and rural crafts. It provides credit by
refinancing lending institutions in rural areas, which helps stimulate growth in village and cottage
industries, handicrafts, and various rural crafts. In addition, NABARD offers low-cost loans to
state governments and their enterprises for infrastructure development projects. These projects
include irrigation, soil conservation, watershed management, drinking water supply, cold storage
chains, and other rural infrastructure developments. Moreover, NABARD manages the Rural
Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF), established to address the shortfall in priority sector
lending by commercial banks and other eligible financial institutions,

A summary of sector-wise refinancing by NABARD in Bihar from 2019-20 to 2023-24 is
presented in Table 11.19. The total financial support provided by NABARD is categorized into
three main areas: crop loans, investment credit refinances, and sanctions for credit support to the
Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF), the Warehouse Infrastructure Fund (WIF), and
the Food Processing Fund (FPF), The total financial support from NABARD_ increased
significantly, from Rs. 2.992 crores in 2019-20 to Rs. 6,596 crores in 2023-24. For 2023-24, the
crop loan disbursement amounted to Rs, 2,300 crores, while investment credit refinances reached
Rs. 2,362 crores. Additionally, support through RIDF disbursement was Rs. 1,934 crores in
2023-24.
