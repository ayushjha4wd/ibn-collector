The State government allocates a significant portion of its fiscal resources to water supply,
sanitation, housing, and urban development. Expenditure on these heads has increased from
Rs. 11,949 crore in 2019-20 to Rs. 18,908 crore in 2023-24. The salary component in these sectors
remains lower compared to other categories of social services expenditure by the State
government. In 2023-24. it accounted for only 1.4 percent of the total revenue expenditure of
Rs. 17,228 crore, Additionally, the share of capital outlay in the total expenditure for these areas
was 8.9 percent in 2023-24.

Expenditure on Economic Services

The State government prioritises expenditure in several key Economic Services sectors, which
include Agriculture and Allied Activities, Irrigation and Flood Control, Energy and Power,
Industry and Minerals, and Transport. The Energy and Power sector is vital for providing reliable
electricity and supporting sustainable energy initiatives. The Industry and Minerals sector
promotes economic development through industrial growth and responsible mineral resource
extraction. The Transport sector is essential for enhancing connectivity and facilitating the
movement of goods and people. In particular, the government's support for Agriculture and Allied
Activities focuses on supporting farmers and boosting agricultural productivity. Irrigation and
Flood Control initiatives are designed to effectively manage water resources, preventing flooding

In the fiscal year 2023-24, the total expenditure on Economic Services reached Rs. 23,788 crore,
reflecting a 16.9 percent increase from the previous year. Before this, there was a marked rise of
25.8 percent in 2022-23 and 23.2 percent in the year before that. Within Economic Services, the
highest share of expenditure was allocated to Energy and Power, followed by the Transport sector.
These two sectors are essential components of economic activities; consequently, the State
government has allocated significant fiscal resources. In 2023-24, the State government spent
Rs. 15,979 crore on Energy and Power and Rs, 13,935 crore on the Transport sector. Notably, the
share of capital outlay in the total expenditure for the Transport sector has also increased from 37.8
percent in 2019-20 to 54.5 percent in 2023-24,

The State faces recurring challenges from floods, primarily caused by rivers originating in the
neighbouring countries. To assist residents in mitigating these challenges, the State government
allocates a substantial portion of its fiscal resources. In 2023-24, expenditure on irrigation and
flood control increased by 87.1 percent over the previous fiscal year, totalling
Rs. 7,382 crore. Of that amount, Rs. 5,876 crore was as capital outlay. The share of capital outlay
in this sector has consistently risen over time, increasing from 38.9 percent in 2019-20 to 79.6
percent in 2023-24.
