7.3 Promotion of Economic Development

E-governance provides opportunities to investors through a single window system, e-auction,
e-bidding, and e-clearance. However, the government monitors and evaluates its infrastructure
projects through PMIS, provides transparent bidding and public procurement; digitalise land
records; provides transparent financial support to farmers, supports entrepreneurs and labourers;
facilitates ease of doing business; etc. E-governance programmes in the state provide a platform
to connect all stakeholders (i.e. government, houscholds, bankers, businesses houses) and help
them interact in a better way to improve the economy.

Physical Infrastructure

Project Management Information System (PMIS) ; In order to monitor real-time progress of the
ongoing projects, infrastructure sectors like roads and bridges, building construction, power
infrastructure, etc. utilises the PMIS. It includes a project’s scope management, cost management,
time management, quality management, communications management and its online monitoring.
Online project monitoring helps to supervise the project's execution progress as planned. In case
of an issuc in the project plan, PMIS helps to review the requirements to make sure that the project
has met its goals and objectives. It also organizes the information collected over the course of the
project for performance review and productivity analysis. PMIS now provides exact real-time
location of road projects of any scheme on Google Maps. PMIS open dashboard is now available
and all information related to road statistics can be seen here. Also, physical and financial

information with graphical view of road projects are available on this dashboard.
