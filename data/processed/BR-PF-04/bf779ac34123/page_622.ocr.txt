Construction of Sidhi Ghat and Channel in Old Uttarwahini Dhar at Ajgaibinath
Temple : The administrative approval for construction of sidhi ghat and channel in old
Uttarwahini Dhar at right bank of Ganga river at Ajgaibinath temple in Sultanganj in
Bhagalpur district has been accorded for an estimated cost of Rs 164.57 crore. After
completion of the project, it will prevent possibility of erosion at this site during excessive
flow of current in Ganga. Also, Devotees will get facilities during various festivals and

religious rituals.

Construction of Right Sikrahana Embankment ; The Sikrahana right embankment,
spanning 56.22 kms., will be constructed in West Champaran and East Champaran, covering
25.87 kms, and 30.34 kms. respectively. The estimated cost is Rs. 239.63 crore, and will
benefit an area of 69,606 hectares. The construction will benefit around 2,35,000 people in
Chanpatia, Bettiah, Majhaulia block of West Champaran, and Sugauli, Banjaria and Motihari

block of East Champaran.

Bagmati-Shantidhar-Burhi Gandak link project in Samastipur District: The Bagmati-
Shantidhar-Burhi Gandak link project in Samastipur district has been approved for an
estimated cost of Rs. 12,096.11 lakh. The main goal is to divert excess flood water from the
Bagmati river to the Burhi Gandak river, thereby creating an irrigation potential of 0.39 lakh

hectares.

Construction of New Embankment on Left and Right bank of Sugarway river (New
and Old): The administrative approval for the construction of a new embankment on the left
and right bank of the Sugarway river has been granted, costing Rs. 7,308.53 lakh, The scheme
will protect a population of 0,35 lakh in Jhanjharpur block in Madhubani district from floods

and provide transportation facilities, benefiting the entire Madhubani district.

Bagmati-Budhi Gandak river-linking project (Belwa-Minapur link channel) : The
Bagmati river in the Sheohar district is being connected to the Budhi Gandak river in
Minapur to divert excess flood discharge. The project, approved for Rs, 13,088.57 lakh, will
benefit over 30 lakh people in three districts: Sheohar, Muzaffarpur, and East Champaran. It
will increase irrigation potential and improve the socio-economic conditions of people in
these areas, The irrigation facility will be available to 1,35,000 hectares in four districts such
as Sheohar, Muzaffarpur, East Champaran, and Samastipur, providing flood control in
Sheohar and Muzaffarpur. Other benefits include sustainable water management, improved
agriculture, employment opportunities, green cover, food security, economic development,

command area development, and improved market facilities.
