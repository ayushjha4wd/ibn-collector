Under the Watershed Development Component of the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana 2.0,
the State government has initiated various activities. Some of the major activities undertaken in
2023-24 include the construction of 60 concrete check dams, 55 farm ponds, 232 water harvest
tanks (WHTs), 162 ahar renovations, 285 community irrigation wells, 35 community ponds, 11
pond renovations, 69 Earthen Check Dams (ECDs), 325 Saad barrier dams, levelling of 20.10
hectares of cultivable land, creation of plantation on 114 hectares. Additionally, 47,480 production
system works, and 1,603 livelihood projects have been completed. The State government has spent
Rs. 10,287,71 lakh to complete these works.

State-sponsored Schemes for Irrigation

Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Abhiyan : Under the Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Abhiyan, 2,377 schemes were
approved by 2023-24, of which 2,182 have been completed, restoring 2,22,639 hectares of
irrigation capacity and 992 lakh cubic meters of water storage, The State government has spent
Rs. 269.66 crore in 2023-24. In 2024-25, 138 schemes worth Rs. 252.10 crore has been approved,
which are expected to restore 25,830 hectares of irrigation capacity. As a result, a progressive
increase in the groundwater level has been observed in majority of the blocks.

Har Khet Tak Sinchai ka Pani: Under the ‘Har Khet Tak Sinchai ka Pani’ programme of the Saat
Nishchay-2, the Minor Water Resources Department has been given the responsibility for
renovating Ahar-Payne with area greater than one acre and constructing or renovating weirs or
check dams (above 15 meters in length) to provide irrigation facilities in command areas up to
2,000 hectares. By 2023-24, administrative approval was granted for 981 schemes, of which 707
have been completed, restoring irrigation facilities in 1,02,957 hectares of land by spending Rs.
308.73 crore. In 2024-25, approval has been granted for 1,528 schemes worth Rs. 2339.10 crore,
which are expected to restore irrigation facilities in 1,87,194 hectares of land.
