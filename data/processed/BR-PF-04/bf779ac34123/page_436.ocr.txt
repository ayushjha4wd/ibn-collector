11.1 Banking Infrastructure

The banks in Bihar have introduced innovative practices that have resulted in an improved access
to their services, Customer services are provided through both physical and digital infrastructure.
While the bank branches, ATMs and Customer Service Points (CSP) continue to deliver services
through the physical presence of these institutions, the role of technology-based platforms like
UPI, Mobile Banking, and Internet Banking has increased manifold. The low-cost extension of

select banking services through the CSPs has increased manifold in the state.

The state's banking landscape is being driven primarily by a substantial rise in the number of CSPs
or Bank Mitras rather than by the establishment of traditional full-service bank branches, Between
March 31, 2020 and March 31, 2024, the number of CSPs in Bihar surged impressively from
20,994 to 57,589, marking an astounding growth rate of 174.3 percent over these five years (as
illustrated in Chart 11.1), In stark contrast, the increase in full-service bank branches has been
relatively modest, with their numbers climbing just 6.0 percent during the same timeframe -
shifting from 7589 branches to 8045. These developments highlight a significant shift in how
banking services are delivered, with CSPs as the primary means of expanding access to financial

services across the state.
