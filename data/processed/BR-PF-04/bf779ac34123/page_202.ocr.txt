The structural ratios in the context of Bihar and at the all-India level, such as fixed capital per
factory, fixed capital per person engaged, workers per factory, persons engaged per factory, net
value added per factory, and net value added per person engaged, are discussed below. These
factors indicate the performance of the industries in Bihar compared to the all-India levels. In terms
of industrial production growth, Bihar has performed satisfactorily over the last few years. The
fixed capital per factory has increased significantly from Rs. 607.1 lakh in 2021-22 to Rs. 966.1
lakh in 2022-23, registering a growth rate of 59.1 percent within one year. The fixed capital per
factory in Bihar, compared to the all-India average, has risen from 40.7 percent in 2021-22 to 59.4
percent in 2022-23. A similar pattern was also observed for all other structural ratios. For two
specific ratios, fixed capital per person engaged and net value added per person engaged, Bihar
has outperformed the all-India levels.
