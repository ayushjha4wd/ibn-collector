Farm Machinery

Farm mechanisation enhances both crop production, productivity and reduces crop duration. At
the same time, it also reduces human drudgery in agricultural operations, Table 3.29 suggests that
farmers purchased pump-sets for irrigation and threshers availing subsidy scheme in 2023-24. A
large number of manually operated tools or implements were purchased in 2023-24.

The State government has approved Rs, 11,900.00 lakh for agricultural mechanisation in 2023-24.
As per the provision, a 40 to 80 percent subsidy on 108 types of agricultural equipment is offered
to farmers. To manage crop residue, 33 percent of the total fund was set aside to subsidise
implements like different kinds of seeders, and straw reapers. Small and marginal farmers are
eligible for an 80 percent subsidy (up to Rs. 800) to purchase small tools like hoes and spades. To
encourage the agricultural machinery manufacturing in the state, a provision has been made in the
Agricultural Mechanization State Scheme 2023-24. Under this scheme, a 10 percent additional
subsidy is provided to farmers on the purchase of agricultural machinery manufactured by the
