Forest Fire

Forest fires require a holistic approach for mitigation and control and shape the conservation and
management of forest ecosystems. As per the India State of Forest Report, persistent hotter and
drier weather due to climate change and other human factors such as land conversion for
agriculture are the main drivers of increase in forest fires. The forest fires are a major threat to
forest ecosystems and human communities around the world. The annual trend of forest fire is
fluctuating. The highest incidents (771) have been recorded during 2023-24 (Table 14.5). Nearly
half of these incidences happened in VTR (Chart 14.10), These can have consequences for
biodiversity, soil erosion and economic damage. Fortunately, half of the Bihar's forests are
categorized as less fire-prone (Chart 14.10). The State government is keen to create awareness
among the public about fire safety and minimizing instances of crop residue-burning across the
