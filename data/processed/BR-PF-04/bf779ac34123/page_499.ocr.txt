Establishment of Centres of Excellence (CoE)

Centres of Excellence have been established at government polytechnic institutes in collaboration
with IIT Patna. Currently, there are 11 hubs and 33 spokes, focused on emerging fields such as
drone technology, electric vehicles, artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things, 3D printing,
robotics, and industrial automation. In the second phase, 33 additional hubs will be set up with the
support of ITT Patna. This phase will introduce two new branches — Transformer Manufacturing
and Optical Fiber (including 5G) — alongside the six branches established in the first phase. These
centres are expected to become operational before March 2025. Additionally, Centres of
Excellence in welding technology have been established at New Government Polytechnic (NGP)
Patna, and in solar technology at GP Vaishali and Siwan, with the support from WEB-Tec

Corporation and Snyder Technology, respectively.

Placement

A Placement Committee has been established at the department level, and a dedicated placement
cell at each institution to facilitate job placements and encourage interaction with industry partners.
The department also actively promotes entrepreneurship and supports students pursuing higher
studies, In the last three academic sessions, a total of 17822 students have successfully secured
placements in reputable industries, 7872 from government engineering colleges and 9950 from
government polytechnic institutes (Chart 12.15), These quality placements greatly improve our
students’ employability,
