Deputy Chief Minister-
cum-Finance Minister

Bihar

Bihar continues to step forward on the path of economic
prosperity with great achievements. Our commitment is to lead
every citizen of the state in the journey of 'Development with
Justice’. The growth rate of Bihar's economy is 9.2 percent in the
year 2023-24, which places Bihar at third place among all the
states of the country.

In the recent years, due to combined efforts of the state and
the central governments, there has been a significant increase in
the size of the state budget, especially on development
expenditure. As a result of the prudent economic and fiscal policy
implementation of the state government, remarkable changes
have been recorded in the socio-economic sector of the state.

The state is setting new records in terms of development in
road, rail and air transports including other infrastructure. By
using e-governance in the state, the services for the people is
being made easy, accessible and transparent.

The life of farmers has become prosperous due to the
strengthening of agriculture and rural economy. Along with the
focus on human development, child development and_ the
participation of women in the development process, the state
government is also vigilant about climate change and is taking
measures to improve it.

These aspects have been described in depth in the Bihar
Economie Survey, 2024-25. I am confident that this document
will serve as a valuable resource for all stakeholders interested in
the sustainable development and progress of the state.

(Samrat Choudhary)
