Indo-Nepal Border Road Project : Under this project, a 2-lane road of 554.00 kms length is
being constructed in Bihar and starts at Gobarhtya (Madanpur) on the border of Uttar Pradesh and
runs parallel to the India-Nepal international border, passing through 7 districts (West Champaran,
East Champaran, Sitamarhi, Madhubani, Supaul, Araria and Kishanganj) before reaching its end
point at Galgaliya in Bihar at the border of West Bengal. It will provide connectivity to the border
post on the sensitive border between India and Nepal. At the same time, unwanted and illegal
activities have to be controlled by effectively patrolling the border. This project is important on
strategic, economic and social dimensions. At present, out of 552.00 kms length, 492.00 kms
earthwork and 405,00 kms bituminous work has been completed. Along with this, 125 High Level
Bridges and 752 culverts have also been completed. This project is targeted to be completed by
December 2025.

6.4 Bihar State Road Development Corporation Limited (BSRDCL)

The BSRDCL is an ISO 9001: 2008 certified corporation of the State government and is
operational since 2009. The Corporation endeavors to build roads and also earn profit since its
beginning. It took projects of ADB, JICA, PPP, EPC and CRF. Table 6.5 presents the financial
performance of the Corporation during the last five years. For development of society and needy
people, the Corporation has contributed Rs. 7.5 crore during 2023-24 in Chief Minister Relief
Fund. Under its CSR initiative in 2023-24, a sum of Rs. 1.28 crore has been contributed by the
Corporation.
