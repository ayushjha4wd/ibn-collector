Biofertilizers and Biopesticides

In Bihar, biofertilizers and biopesticides are promoted through various schemes such as the Jaivik
Corridor Plan, the Traditional Agricultural Development Scheme under Namami Gange, and the
Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana. In 2023-24, the State government has allocated Rs. 2253.4
lakh to achieve a physical target of 19,594.9 acres under the Jaivik Corridor Plan. Furthermore,
through the Traditional Agriculture Development Scheme under Namami Gange. 16,744 farmers
received benefits in 2023-24. The State government has spent Rs. 2,301.7 lakh to achieve a
physical target of 35,000 acres.

Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY) (DPR-based Scheme)

One of the important components of the RK VY Cafeteria scheme is the RK VY Detailed Project
Report (DPR). Under the RKVY DPR-based scheme, the state government has spent Rs, 167.69
lakh for rice and Rs. 193.71 lakh for wheat in 2023-24. Furthermore, the total expenditure on
composite or hybrid maize amounted to Rs. 135.50 lakh during the same period. The scheme
addresses various aspects of crop production. For instance, in rice cultivation, emphasis is placed
on different rice planting methods, distribution of quality seeds, and techniques for nutrient
