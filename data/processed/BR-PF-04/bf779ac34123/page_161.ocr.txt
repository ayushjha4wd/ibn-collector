The State government has invested heavily in creating irrigation infrastructure in Bihar. Table 3.40
indicates that expenditures on irrigation have been increasing over the years. Between 2019-20
and 2023-24, the expenditure on irrigation was the highest in 2021-22, at Rs. 2670.5 crore. In
2023-24, the total expenditure on irrigation was Rs. 1674.2 crore, registering an increase of 9.7
percent over 2022-23. Of the total expenditure on irrigation, around 90 percent was capital
expenditure, Numerous projects have been undertaken in Bihar over the years to increase the
command area of irrigation and ensure the continuous availability of water during the crop seasons.
