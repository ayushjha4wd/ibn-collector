Agriculture and Allied Sectors

Agriculture Single Window : The primary goal of this project is to address inefficiencies and
transparency issues in the previous system, creating a single transparent window for
communication and information exchange. The system incorporates a four-layer verification
process involving the Panchayat, Block, District, and Headquarters levels. This structure ensures
thorough validation and reduces the average processing time from 90 to 15 days. Farmers can
submit requests online or through 45,000+ VLE centres/cybercafés, reducing the need for physical
visits and paperwork. Payments are directly credited to Aadhaar-linked bank accounts, reducing
issues related to payment retums by 90 percent. Automated SMS alerts keep beneficiaries
informed of their request status and updates throughout the process, improving transparency and
communication. A comprehensive dashboard and MIS facilitate real-time monitoring and
reporting of agricultural schemes, including procurement, seed testing, and weather updates. A
digitized database of two crore registered farmers is maintained, allowing for better management
and evaluation of agricultural schemes.
