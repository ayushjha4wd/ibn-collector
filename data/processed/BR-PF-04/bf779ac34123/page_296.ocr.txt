6.13 Building Construction

The State government has been continuously strengthening the governance, administration,
economy and culture of the state through building administrative, social, historical, cultural and
sports infrastructures in the state. The trend of annual expenditure of the Building Construction
Department (BCD) is given in Chart 6.12. As the data shows, the scheme expenditure by BCD has
recorded an increase of more than 332 times, while committed and establishment expenditure went
up by 4 times only during the last one and a half decade (2008-09 to 2024-25 BE), The scheme
expenditure has been gradually surpassing the committed expenditure since 2011-12. The State
government has budgeted an amount of Rs. 4008 crore under the scheme expenditure head and a
sum of Rs. 1005 crore under the committed and establishment expenditure head during 2024-25
BE. Further, the composition of work assigned by different departments of the State government
during 2024-25 BE is shown in Chart 6.13. These investments create direct and indirect
employment opportunities and also provide an opportunity to grow economy at a higher rate.
