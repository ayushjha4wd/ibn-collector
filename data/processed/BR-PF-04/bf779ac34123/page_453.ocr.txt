The distribution of deposits, credit, and credit-deposit (CD) ratios in Bihar at the end of 2023-2024
is detailed in Table 11.14. The figures are as of March 31, 2024. It is observed that banks with a
limited presence in terms of deposits or credit tend to have a relatively higher CD ratio. For
instance, the CD ratio for Small Finance Banks (SFBs) reached 438.3 percent at the end of
2023-2024. Similarly, Cooperative Banks and Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) reported CD ratios
of 78.2 percent and 58.2 percent, respectively, higher than the 54.1 percent CD ratio for
Commercial Banks. Additionally, there is significant variation in the CD ratios across different
areas within the state. For both Commercial Banks and RRBs, the CD ratios are higher in rural
areas than in Semi-urban and Urban areas.
