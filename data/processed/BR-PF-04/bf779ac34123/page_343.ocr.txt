Bihar Government has continuously carried out Krishi Roadmaps with a focus on development of
electrical infrastructure for the agriculture sector through Mukhyamantri Krishi Vidyut Sambandh
Yojana (MK VSY) and under two phases has provided 2.37 lakh agriculture connections to the
farmers, With this, total number of agricultural (farming) consumers in the state has reached 5.42
lakhs. Power supply to the agriculture sector is also being made at a very subsidized rate which is
now Rs. 0.55/kWh (92%). This has brought down the cost of agricultural products and is enhancing
direct benefits for the farmers.

The transmission sector has aided the extensive growth and advancement of electrical
infrastructure in the distribution sector by building numerous new grid sub-stations and
transmission lines. So, the state has been able to substantially increase the number of electricity
consumers, as well as, meet the highest peak demand of 8005 MW in 2024-25 (upto September).
The increase in Supply Hours and availability of Quality Power has increased the per capita
consumption of power.

Ensuring consumer satisfaction continues to be the top priority for the state. The use of TT in
Revenue Management has allowed the department to serve a large consumer base effectively. The
SUVIDHA App was created to offer online services to consumers. In a progressive move, the
Government of Bihar opted to implement Smart Meters for all consumers on a prepaid basis. This
initiative was a first for the country, and following positive outcomes, plans are underway to extend
it to the rural areas as well.

8.2 Institutional Structure of Power Sector

The Bihar State Electricity Board (BSEB) was originally constituted in April 1958 under Section
5 of the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948. It was mandated to manage generation, transmission,
distribution, and other electricity-related activities in Bihar. Under the new Bihar State Electricity
Reforms Transfer Scheme 2012, the BSEB was unbundled into five companies with effect from
November 01, 2012 —

(i) Bihar State Power (Holding) Company Limited (BSPHCL),

(ii) Bihar State Power Generation Company Limited (BSPGCL),

(iii) Bihar State Power Transmission Company Limited (BSPTCL),

(iv) North Bihar Power Distribution Company Limited (NBPDCL) and

(v) South Bihar Power Distribution Company Limited (SBPDCL).
