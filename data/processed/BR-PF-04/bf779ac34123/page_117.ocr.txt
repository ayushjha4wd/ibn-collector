2.7 Expenditure Management

The consolidated account of the State government is organised into two main categories: revenue
accounts and capital accounts. Expenditures from these accounts are classified as revenue
expenditure and capital expenditure. Furthermore, expenditures are organised into three primary
functional categories: General Services, Social Services, and Economic Services. In addition to
these categories, other types of expenditures include Capital Outlay, Repayment of Loans, and
