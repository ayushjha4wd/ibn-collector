Under PMAY-G, the Central and the State governments share the cost of housing in a 60:40 ratio.
Each beneficiary receives financial assistance of Rs.1.20 lakh in plains and Rs.1,30 lakh in hilly
or difficult areas (North-Eastern Region States and Hill States of Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand,
Union Territories of Jammu & Kashmir, and Ladakh), The scheme also promotes convergence
with other government programmes to provide facilities such as toilets, electricity, and clean
cooking fuel,

from backward classes, ensures their right to own land and empowers them to construct their
homes, fostering dignity and socio-economic stability. Table 9.9 presents the budget allocation
