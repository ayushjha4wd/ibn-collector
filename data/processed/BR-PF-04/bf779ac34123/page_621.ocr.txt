Construction of New Embankment from Hasanpur-Baniya to Saguni in length 8.33 km
with protection work : The construction of a new embankment from Hasanpur-Baniya to
Saguni, covering 8.33 kms. and requiring protection work, has been approved at an estimated
cost of Rs. 5,998.27 lakh. The embankment will be built on the river side between the
Madhopur Zamindari Bandh and Saran embankment, providing protection to Panapur and
Taraiya blocks in the Saran District and adding an additional layer of safety during river
discharges.

River Linking of Gandak-Akali Nala (Chhari)-Gandaki-Mahi-Ganga : The Gandak-
Akali Nala (Chhari)-Gandaki-Mahi-Ganga river linking project has been approved for an
estimated cost of Rs. 6,989.79 lakh. The project aims to divert logged water through drainage
channels to a final outfall in the Ganga river, which can be used for irrigation and agricultural
purposes during Kharif and Rabi seasons. The project will span 170 kms., benefiting a
population of around 7 lakh and an area of 0.60 lakh hectares by providing proper drainage

for excess flood water.

Restoration of Saran Embankment from km 80.00 to km 120.28 with Provision of
Bituminous Road on the Top of the Embankment: It has been approved at an estimated
cost of Rs. 12411.00 lakh. The project aims to strengthen the Saran embankment in a length
of 40.28 km and construction of bituminous road on top of embankment in the above reach.
The strengthening of embankment will provide safety from recurring floods of River Gandak.
Also the construction of bituminous road on top of embankment will facilitate better
inspection of the embankment along with providing better rural connectivity in the Barauli,
