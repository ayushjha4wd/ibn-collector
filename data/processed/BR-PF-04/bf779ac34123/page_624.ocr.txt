Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Abhiyan (JJHA): This flagship programme of the State government
was launched on October 26, 2019 with the objective of conserving water and restoring the
irrigation potential of traditional schemes. Under this, total 2377 schemes have been
approved till 2023-24, out of which 2182 have been completed. The completed schemes have
created 2,22,639 hectares of irrigation capacity and 992 lakh cubic meters of water storage
capacity. An amount of Rs. 269.66 crore has been spent on these schemes in the financial
year 2023-24,

Installation of ‘Telemetry Systems: For monitoring ground water, 504 telemetry systems
(Automatic Water Level Recorders) have been installed in each block and district
headquarters. The groundwater level data obtained from these systems over different periods

are shared with various government and related non-government institutions.

climate-related disasters have risen significantly. In 2023-24, floods affected 3.16 lakh persons of
