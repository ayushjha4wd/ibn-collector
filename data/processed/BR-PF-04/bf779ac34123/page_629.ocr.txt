Disaster Risk Reduction: In coordination with the Bihar State Disaster Management
Authority, JEEVIKA organized a three-day residential training on Module-1 of Disaster Risk
Management and Mitigation for Master Trainers. A total of 800 Master Trainers were trained
in disaster risk management. After training, these Master Trainers train community members,
project staff, and Community-Based Organizations (CBOs) in their respective blocks and
districts. Also, 800 JEEVIKA trainers have completed Module-2 training in seven batches
across all 38 districts.
