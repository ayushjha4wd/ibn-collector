Bihar State Power (Holding) Company Limited (BSPHCL)

This company has been primarily an investment company. Now, it ts vested with the assets, interest
in property, rights and liabilities of the erstwhile Bihar State Electricity Board (BSEB). It co-
ordinates the activities of other companies, handles disputes and provides all necessary support to
them. It owns shares of the newly incorporated and reorganized four companics —

(i) Bihar State Power Generation Company Limited,

(ii) Bihar State Power Transmission Company Limited,

(iii) South Bihar Power Distribution Company Limited and

(iv) North Bihar Power Distribution Company Limited.

Bihar State Power Generation Company Limited (BSPGCL)

This company is responsible for the generation and supply of electricity and carrying on the
business of purchasing, producing, trading or otherwise dealing in electric power and coordinating,
aiding and advising on the activities of other companies and concerns including subsidiaries
engaged in the generation, distribution or trading of power on all matters concerning the operation
and maintenance of electricity generating station. BSPGCL is also responsible for acquiring,
establishing, constructing, operating, managing, buying, selling, maintaining, renovating,
moderising and using electric generating stations, projects associated lines and all things
connected thereto for generation and distribution of power including power sub-stations.
