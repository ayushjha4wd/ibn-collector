Bihar State Khadi and Village Industries Board (KVIB)

The Bihar State Khadi and Village Industries (KVIB) plays the role of a facilitator for the
operations of the handloom sector. According to Table 4.26, in 2023-24, the KVIB has provided
Rs. 436.13 lakh as the working capital and spent Rs. 191.94 lakh to distribute charkhas to the
handloom operators. In 2024-25 (up to September 30, 2024), it has distributed 650 charkhas and
150 looms along with Rs. 270.0 lakh as the working capital, which was provided as loans to the
loom operators. The K VIB has also recovered the loans. It has recovered Rs. 418.58 lakh from the
loom operators in 2023-24 and Rs, 221.53 lakh in 2024-25 (up to September 30, 2024). Evidently,
loan recovery has increased over the last two years.

In addition to distributing charkhas and looms, the K VIB has provided rebates to Khadi institutions
and committees. In 2023-24, the K VIB granted rebates to 53 institutions or committees, amounting
to Rs, 292.45 lakh. In 2024-25 (up to September 30, 2024), the rebate amounted to Rs. 290.83 lakh
and was provided to 52 institutions or committees.

4.5 Industrial Promotion in Bihar

The State government has identified priority sectors based on an assessment of the sectoral
advantages the state enjoys under the Bihar Industrial Investment Promotion Policy 2016.
Moreover, the State government intends to leverage these advantages to maximise industrial
growth and employment creation, The State government has identified the following priority
sectors of Bihar: food processing, tourism, smal! machine manufacturing, IT, 1168, electrical and
electronic hardware manufacturing, textiles, plastics, and rubber, renewable energy, healthcare,
leather, and technical education. In recent years, the State government has also prepared long-term
