e-Parivesh Portal : e-Parivesh (Pro-Active and Responsive facilitation by Interactive and
Virtuous Environmental Single-window Hub) portal is an initiative by the Ministry of
Environment, Forest, and Climate Change (MoEF&CC) to streamline the environmental clearance
processes. It serves as a single-window platform for submitting, processing, and monitoring
applications related to environmental, forest, wildlife, and coastal regulation zone (CRZ)
clearances.

e-Ticketing System for Ecotourism : The e-Ticketing System for ecotourism sites like Valmiki
Tiger Reserve, Patna Zoo, Rajgir Zoo Safari, and Rajgir Nature Safari is a digital platform designed
to facilitate online ticket bookings for visitors. It aims to enhance the visitor experience, improve
operational efficiency, and support eco-friendly tourism practices.

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) in Forest Department : Customer Relationship
Management (CRM) in the forest department started in 2021 and involves using structured
processes and data analytics to manage and enhance interactions with the public. By analyzing
large datasets, CRM helps the department improve engagement, address public concerns, and
ensure efficient service delivery related to forest conservation, afforestation programmes, and
environmental awareness.
