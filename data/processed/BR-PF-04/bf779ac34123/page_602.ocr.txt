Hariyali Mission: Under the Hariyali mission, large-scale plantation is being undertaken to
increase the green cover of the state. Under the Agro-forestry Scheme, a total of 20.77 lakh
plants have been planted with the help of farmers. With the help of JEEViKA SHGs, 494
units of nurseries have been distributed among the farmers and JEEViKA Didis. Total 494
nurseries’ Kisan Nurseries have been established and about 98.80 lakh plants have been
raised in them. Under the Mukhyamantri Niji Paudhshala (Poplar) Yojana, total 3.2 lakh ETP
stump plants have been planted by farmers in 32 nurseries in 2023-24.

Bamboo Mission : Under this project, bamboo is planted on government land/private land
and nurseries are developed. The bamboo plants are distributed as per farmers’ demand.
Under National Bamboo Mission Scheme, for 2024-25, proposal worth Rs. 62.50 lakh has
been submitted to the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.

Agro Forestry- Poplar (ETP) Scheme: Under the Agro-forestry scheme, the plantation of
poplar species has been mainly initiated as a short-duration plant. It has been operational
since 2012-13. Poplar saplings are made available to the entrepreneurs’ farmers/
beneficiaries from the local nurseries of the Department of Environment, Forest, and Climate
Change at a price of Rs. 10 per sapling during December-January, An incentive amount is
also given to the farmers for taking care of the plants. After three years, there is a provision
to pay an incentive amount of Rs. 60 per plant and a reserve price of
Rs. 10, if the survival rate is 50 percent or more.
