Solar Water Pumps: Solar Water Pump is an application of photovoltaic technology that
uses solar power to run irrigation pump sets, replacing diesel-operated pumps. Under the
‘Mukhyamantri Navin Evam Navikarniya Saur Pump Yojana’, 2771 Solar Water Pumps
have been installed at the cost of Rs. 77.16 crore.

50 MW Grid-Connected Ground-Mounted Solar Power Plant: A Grid-Connected
Ground-Mounted Solar Power Plant is an electricity-generating solar Photovoltaic (PV) plant
that is connected to the utility grid. A grid-connected PV system consists of solar panels,
central inverters and Inverter Duty Transformer (IDT) and other grid-connecting equipment.
To fulfil the Renewable Purchase Obligation (RPO) of the State government and to
contribute towards the ambitious target of the Central government of generating 500 GW of
Renewable Power by 2030, the 50 MW Ground-Mounted Grid-Connected Solar Power Plant
was installed and commissioned by M/s Avaada Clean Sustainable Energy in May, 2024.
The generated power from the plant is purchased by BSPHCL at the rate of 3.11 paise/unit
through a PPA effective for 25 years.

Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar Power Plant at Government Buildings under Jal-
Jeevan-Hariyali Mission Phase-II: Under ‘Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Mission’ Phase-Il, Grid-
Connected Rooftop Solar Power Plants are being installed by BREDA, with system capacity
of 2 to 500 KWp on government buildings under different departments. A work order for the
installation of 108.23 MW Rooftop Solar Power Plant at 13072 government buildings has
been issued. The work started in 2022-23 and at present 69 MW Solar Power plants have
been installed at 7232 buildings. The total project cost is Rs. 522 crores.

Hybrid (Off-Grid) Rooftop Solar Power Plant at Government Buildings: Installation of
Hybrid (Off-Grid) Rooftop Solar Power Plants has been done by BREDA to provide battery
backup during grid failure or in emergency at various State government offices, such as
BSEIDC (Bihar State Educational Infrastructure Development Corporation) buildings in the
Gaya, Kishanganj and Muzaffarpur districts. At present, the total installed capacity of such
Solar Power Plants is 409 KWp. The cost for this project is Rs. 3.96 crore.
