Technical Education

Science and technology have always been vital components of our social and behavioural culture,
supported through various forms of technical education. To better reflect its mission and activities,
the department was renamed as Department of Science, Technology, and Technical Education
(DSTTE) in June 2023. It aims to deliver quality education that meets the standards established by
the All-India Council of Technical Education (AICTE). In alignment with the state's seven
resolves, the department is dedicated to offer high-level technical education to the students of Bihar
at a minimal or no cost.

Institutional Arrangements

To meet the needs related to science, technology, and technical education, the Department of
Science, Technology, and Technical Education (DSTTE) has established various institutions.
Bihar Engineering University (BEU) was established in 2022, and colleges and institutions
providing education in Engineering and Technology in the state have been affiliated with this
university since March 2023. Additionally, the Bihar Council on Science and Technology (BCST)
was created in 1984, while the State Board of Technical Education (SBTE) was established in
1955, BCST focuses on actively promoting science and technology throughout Bihar, whereas
SBTE is responsible for the administration, evaluation, and certification of students in government
polytechnic institutes. To facilitate decentralized planning and grant greater autonomy to
government engineering colleges and polytechnic institutes, the Bihar government has recently
approved the formation of an ‘Institute Development Society’ for all institutions under the Societies
Registration Act of 1860, Furthermore, a consultancy policy has also been developed to adopt a
self-sustaining ecosystem within these institutions.

Infrastructure

As part of the government's ‘Seven Resolves’ program and the Yuva Up mission initiative, known
as ‘Awsar Badhe Aage Padhe,' one engineering college and at least one polytechnic institute have
been established in each district of Bihar. Currently, 38 government engineering colleges and 46
government polytechnics are operational across the 38 districts (Appendix Table A 12.25 and A
12.26), These institutions feature modern infrastructure, including administrative and academic
blocks, separate hostels for boys and girls, and faculty residences. They have high-quality facilities
such as well-equipped classrooms, laboratories, workshops, libraries, auditoriums, and smart
classrooms, all designed to provide quality technical education. To meet the growing needs of
institutes, students, and faculty, the second phase of construction is currently underway at various
engineering colleges and polytechnic institutes. The department has also implemented measures
to ensure a 24/7 power supply in all institutions through dedicated feeders and establishing Wi-Fi-
enabled campuses.
