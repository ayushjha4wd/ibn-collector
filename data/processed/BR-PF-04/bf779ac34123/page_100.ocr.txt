2.4 Cash Management and Guarantees

The overall cash balance of the State government in a fiscal year consists of the following
components: deposits with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), investments held in a cash balance
investment account, cash held by departmental officers, permanent advances for contingent
expenditure with departmental officers, and investments of earmarked funds.

As of the end of the fiscal year 2023-24, the total cash balance and investments of the State

government in Bihar amounted to Rs. 36,982.2 crore. A significant portion of this, Rs. 27,488.8

crore, was available as the General Cash balance. This included Deposits with the RBI totalling
