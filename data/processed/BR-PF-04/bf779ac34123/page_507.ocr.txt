Construction of block-level outdoor stadium under Mukhyamantri Khel Vikas Yojana :
Under the scheme, in order to develop sports infrastructure, the administrative approval has been
provided for construction of 334 outdoor stadiums at the block level in various districts. Out of
these, construction of 229 stadiums has been completed and 77 are under construction. The process
is underway to start the construction of the remaining 57 stadiums.

Construction of world-class sports infrastructure in divisional headquarters: The consent has
been given for the construction of sports infrastructure in all divisional headquarters, keeping in
view the population and popular sports at the local level. Apart from playing grounds for all
popular sports, it will have housing and other facilities for players, trainers, team managers and
technical officials, Under this plan, proposals have been sought from the district officials of all
divisional headquarters,

Construction of State Sports Academy-cum-International Cricket Stadium ; The construction
work of State Sports Academy-cum-International Cricket Stadium is under progress in Rajgir, on
90 acres of land and at a total cost of Rs. 740 crore. The target is to complete it by June 2025.
Recently, the Bihar Sports University and State Sports Academy, Rajgir were formally
inaugurated, For the first time in Bihar, the international-level Asian Hockey Champions Trophy
(Women) 2024 was organized, in which 6 leading Asian countries participated.

Construction of Sports Bhawan-cum-Gymnasium Building as Regional Office of Sports
Department : In 2023-24, the construction of sports building-cum-gymnasium was completed in
25 districts at a cost of Rs. 165.25 crore (Rs. 6.61 crore per district) and the construction work is
underway in the remaining 13.

Khelo India Scheme ; Under the Khelo India Scheme of the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports,
Government of India, modern sports infrastructures such as synthetic athletic track, hockey
astroturf, multi-purpose indoor hall, swimming pool, football ground, etc. are to be constructed in
the states. So far, approval has been given for the construction of 10 such infrastructure. For this,
the Bihar State Sports Authority has received a total of Rs. 20,50 crore from the Government of
India. Apart from this, a request has been made to the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports seeking
approval for the construction of 35 sports infrastructures. At present, total four schemes are being
operated under this: Khelo India Centre of Accreditation, Khelo India State Level Centre of
Excellence, Khelo India Small Level Centre, and Creation of sports infrastructure.
