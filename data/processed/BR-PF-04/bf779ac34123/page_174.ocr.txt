Bihar Skill Development Mission (BSDM) ; Recognising the lack of skills among the rural youth
in agriculture, skill training is provided to fill this gap. Efficient farming, value addition, storage,
and marketing increases income for skilled workers. There are two main components of the
training programme conducted in skill development, that is Domain Skilling and Recognition of
Prior Learning (RPL). The programme aims to increase the quality of skills and develop
entrepreneurial knowledge with the following objectives. First, to develop skilled technical and
professional qualifications and capacity by providing skill training to young men and women in
agriculture and agriculture-related activities. Second, to provide gainful employment to youth and
women after skill training in agricultural commercial establishments, meeting their demands and
enabling quality materials to be manufactured in these establishments. Third, to organise skill
training for the maximum number of young men and women by granting funds to skill

development centres. Lastly, to refine the existing skills through the RPL course.
