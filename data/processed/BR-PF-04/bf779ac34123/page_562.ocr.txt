Sex Ratio at Birth (SRB)

Preference for a male child is a deep-rooted cultural phenomenon in many states of India, including
Bihar, which results in discriminatory and harmful practices against women and girls, So, to
understand the status of girls, the analysis of the Sex Ratio at Birth (SRB) becomes very important.
Gender-biased sex selection is measured through the Child Sex Ratio (CSR) and SRB, The
differences in SRB is also influenced by several other factors such as under-registration of girls,
differential infant and age misreporting. Therefore, the imbalance in SRB cannot be entirely
attributed to the practice of gender-biased sex selection. SRB is defined as the number of girls born
for every 1000 boys. As per the latest figures of the Sample Registration System, SRB for the state
as a whole has gone down by 13 points to 895 in 2018-20 from 908 in 2014-16. The SRB of Bihar
is 894 in rural areas and 897 in urban in 2018-20. Chart 13.2 depicts the variation in SRB in rural
and urban areas in the consecutive years, The district-wise details of the sex ratio in different age
groups in Bihar are given in Appendix (Table A13.2).

Child Sex Ratio (CSR)

The Sex Ratio is an important indicator of the status of women, both for children and adults. The
Census 2011 data reveals that the child sex ratio is higher than the overall sex ratio in Bihar. The
sex ratio in the age group 0 -6 years is 935 females per 1000 males, 923 for 0-14 years, and 897
for 0-18 years. For all ages. the ratio is 918. Among the districts, Kishanganj (971), Katihar (961)
and Gaya (960) have relatively better child sex ratio in the age group of 0-6 years, in comparison
to Vaishali (904), Patna (909) and Muzaffarpur (915) which all have a highly adverse ratio. The
child sex ratios among the Scheduled Castes (962) and Scheduled Tribes (969) are better than the
state average of 935. The district-wise figures in Appendix (Table A13,2) indicate that the child
sex ratio for 0-18 years, as per the 2011 census, was above the state average in 20 districts. Out of
all districts, the highest sex ratio is recorded in — Gopalganj (959), Kishanganj (958) and Siwan
