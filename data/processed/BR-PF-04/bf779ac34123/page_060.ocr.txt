As per the latest available quick estimates at the time of preparing this report, the Gross State
Domestic Product (GSDP) of Bihar for the year 2023-24 is estimated to be Rs. 8,54,429 crore at
current prices and Rs. 4,64,540 crore at constant (2011-12) prices, Between 2011-12 and 2023-24,
the GSDP at current prices experienced an average annual growth rate of 10.9 percent. When
adjusted for inflation, the GSDP at constant prices has increased at an average annual rate of 5.4
percent. The Net State Domestic Product (NSDP) at current and constant (2011-12) prices has
grown at average annual rates of 10.7 percent and 5.0 percent, respectively. The year-on-year
percentage growth in the State’s GSDP at both current and constant (2011-12) prices, along with
the fitted linear trend line from 2012-13 to 2023-24, is depicted in Chart 1.1. Following a V-shaped
recovery in 2021-22, the State's economy has continued to expand at a double-digit growth rate.
