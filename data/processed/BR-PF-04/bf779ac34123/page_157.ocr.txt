Power Use

The use of energy in agriculture has increased over the years. As Table 3.30 suggests, in the total
power supply, the share of power consumption in agriculture in the total power consumption has
increased from 4.3 percent in 2019-20 to 17.6 percent in 2023-24. Considering the rise in power
consumption in agriculture, the State government has installed dedicated feeders to ensure an
uninterrupted power supply under the Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gram Jyoti Yojana (DDUGJY),.
Under this scheme, 291 power substations and 1,354 dedicated agricultural feeders have been

constructed,

The State government, under the Mukhyamantri Krishi Vidyut Sambandh Yojana (MKVSY), has
provided electricity subsidies for the agricultural sector. Due to the increase in electricity
connections for agriculture, the amount of subsidies has also risen tremendously, from Rs. 486.93
crore in 2019-20 to Rs, 3856.8 crore in 2023-24. Between 2019-20 and 2023-24, the amount

disbursed for electricity subsidies has increased eightfold (see Table 3.32).
