Approval has been granted for the construction of 69 new shelters for homeless individuals across
various municipal bodies. Of these, 54 are currently operational, and five are under construction.

Tenders for the remaining 10 shelters have been issued.

The State government has allocated Rs. 46.45 crore to municipal bodies for the repair and
operationalization of existing night shelters and the construction of new ones. Renovation work
has already been completed in 54 night shelters, which are now operational. Additionally, in urban
areas, the operation of these shelters has been integrated with the Area-Level Organization (ALO)

for women to ensure better management and support.

National Mission for Clean Ganga

In Bihar, solid waste management, sewerage networks, interception and diversion systems,
drainage (including STP), riverfront development, and crematoria were funded under the National
Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG). Among these projects, only the ones related to interception
and diversion drainage (including STP) received Rs. 139.49 crore in 2023-24 and Rs. 356.99 crore
in 2024-25 (up to September 2024), However, work on the other projects in 2023-24 was funded
through allocations made in the previous years. The total expenditure for 2023-24 amounted to Rs.
473.77 crore. Specifically, the expenditures for the sewerage network and the interception and
diversion drainage (including STP) work were Rs. 279.95 crore and Rs. 190.74 crore. respectively.
In 2024-25 (up to September 2024), the total expenditure was Rs. 188.90 crore.
