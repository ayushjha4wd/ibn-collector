1.2 Structure of the Bihar Economy

The size of economic activity in a state during a specific period can be estimated by measuring the
total value added from those economic activities. The Gross State Value Added (GSVA) reflects
the overall yalue added to the state economy, calculated by subtracting the intermediate costs
incurred in producing output from the total value of that output. Table 1.4 presents the sectoral
composition of GSVA at constant prices (2011-12) in Bihar for the years 2019-20 to 2023-24, The
figures for 2023-24 are quick estimates, while those for 2022-23 are provisional estimates.

The share of primary, secondary and tertiary sectors in the GSVA at constant (2011-12) prices in
Bihar for 2019-20 and 2023-24 is shown in Chart 1,2. The share of the tertiary sector is estimated
to have declined from 61 percent of the Gross State Value Added (GSVA) in 2019-20 to 59 percent
in 2023-24, Despite this decrease, it remains the most significant contributor to value addition
through economic activities in the state. Conversely, the share of the secondary sector is estimated
to have increased from 19 percent to 21 percent during the same period. Meanwhile, the share of
the primary sector in the state's GSVA is estimated to remain at 20 percent in 2023-24, unchanged
from its share in 2019-20.
