Drinking Water Supply: Har Ghar Nal Ka Jal (HGNJ)

In order to achieve affordable and sustainable water supply in rural/urban areas, the State
government rolled out the Mukhyamantri Peyjal Nishchaya Yojana “Har Ghar Nal Ka Jal’
programme in September 2016. The scheme intends to provide 70 litres per capita per day (LPCD)
of potable drinking water to approximately two crore households in the state through state agencies

with the concerted cooperation of the people of every village and locality in Bihar.

The Mukhyamantri Peyjal Nishchay Yojana is one of the vows of Saat Nishchay, The Yojana is
meant for both rural and urban areas. In the rural areas, three of the schemes are- Mukhyamantri
Gramin Peyjal Nishchay Yojana (Quality Affected Areas), Mukhyamantri Gramin Peyjal
Nishchay Yojana (Non-quality Area) and Mukhyamantri Gramin Peyjal Nishchay Yojana. The
first one covers those areas where the quality of groundwater is contaminated due to excess
presence of arsenic, fluoride or iron, and the second covers non-quality affected areas of those
Gram Panchayats where piped water supply schemes were commissioned before the launching of
the scheme. The third scheme is related to the Panchayati Raj Department (PRD), where the Ward
Implementation and Management Committee (WIMC) of Gram Panchayats has been designated
as the executive agency to plan and execute the construction work of piped water supply scheme.
All schemes executed by the PRD under “Har Ghar Nal ka Jal” have now been transferred to the
Public Health Engineering Department (PHED) for operation and maintenance. Accordingly, the
schemes executed in 58003 Wards were transferred to the PHED between June 2023 and October
2023 for smooth operation and maintenance. The Mukhyamantri Shahri Peyjal Nishchay Yojana

is implemented in urban areas by Nagar Nikay.
