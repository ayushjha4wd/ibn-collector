8.8 Power Sector Schemes
Central Government Schemes

To extend the electricity supply coverage throughout the state, the Central government recently
initiated several programmes in Bihar. Six of them have already been completed, which are —

(i) Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gram Jyoti Yojana (DDUGJY),

(ii) Rural Electrification-Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gram Jyoti Yojana (RE-DDUGJY); erstwhile
Rajiv Gandhi Grameen Vidyutikaran Yojana (RGGVY) during 2007-12 (Phase Il) and
2012-17,

(iii) Restructured Accelerated Power Development and Reforms Program (R-APDRP), for
strengthening of system with IT implementation,

(iv) Integrated Power Development Scheme (IPDS),
(v) Real-Time Data Acquisition System (RT-DAS) &
(vi) Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijlee Har Ghar Yojana (SAUBHAGYA).
