The financial position of the State government over the past five years is summarised in Table 2.1.

Out of the total expenditure of Rs. 2,52,082 crore in 2023-24, the revenue expenditure was
Rs. 1,90,514 crore and Rs. 61,568 crore was the capital expenditure. The expenditure in revenue
and capital accounts has increased by 3.6 percent and 28.5 percent, respectively, in 2023-24 over

2022-23.

The receipt in the revenue account was Rs. 1,93,347 crore in 2023-24, 12.0 percent higher than
the previous year. As a result, there was a surplus of Rs. 2,833 crore in the revenue account in
2023-24. This surplus was used as the capital expenditure. Receipt in the capital account of the
State government was Rs. 60,313 crore in 2023-24, 24.8 percent higher than that in 2022-23. The
pattern of receipt and expenditure in the revenue and capital accounts of the State government in
recent five years, from 2019-20 to 2023-24, is illustrated in Chart 2.3. As a result of the efforts to
increase capital expenditure, the gap between receipts and expenditures in the capital account has
decreased over the years. Similarly, the difference between receipts and expenditures in the
reyenue account has not only reduced, but there has also been a surplus in 2023-24 which could

be utilised in the capital account.
