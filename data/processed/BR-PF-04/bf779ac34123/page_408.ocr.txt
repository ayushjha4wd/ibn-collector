CHAPTER - X
URBAN DEVELOPMENT

Abstract

Bihar, one of India's most populous states, is undergoing rapid urbanization, although its
urbanization rate remains at 11.3 percent (Census 2011), which is significantly lower than the
national average of 31.2 percent. To tackle urban challenges and improve infrastructure, the
State government has substantially increased the budget by 227.3 percent. Key initiatives
include projects under Atmanirbhar Bihar Saat Nishchay-2, such as storm water drainage
systems, Mokshdham construction, shelters for the elderly, and the Mukhyamantri Shahri
Peyajal Nishchay Yojana. The State government initiated the Mukhyamantri Samagra Shahari
Vikas Yojana in 2024-25, Additionally, the Patna Master Plan 2031 — covering an area of
1,167 square kilometers and encompassing seven urban areas — aims to improve urban
planning and infrastructure. Other major projects include the expansion of the Metro Rail
system in four key cities of the state. The Smart City Mission has been allocated Rs. 640 crore
for 2024-25. The Central government programmes such as DAY-NULM (Deendayal Antyodaya
Yojana - National Urban Livelihoods Mission), and the National Mission for Clean Ganga are
being implemented. Furthermore, Bihar is committed to achieving the objectives of the Swachh
Bharat Mission Urban 2.0 and AMRUT 2.0 to improve urban sanitation, cleanliness, and
infrastructure. These comprehensive efforts underscore Bihar's dedication to fostering
sustainable and inclusive urban development.

Introduction

In recent years, several initiatives of the State government have given a strong impetus to the
urbanisation process in Bihar. The first step in this process was the major reorganisation of
jurisdictions of Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), followed by the adoption of multiple developmental
activities started in the reorganised ULBs. Along with different Centre-sponsored schemes of
urban development, the State government’s Saat Nishchay-2 has envisaged many schemes to
create amenities and infrastructure for urban dwellers. In this chapter, a detailed account of the

recent developments related to urbanisation is presented.

Urban development is critical for fostering economic growth, improving quality of life, and
alleviating poverty. However, due to geographic and economic factors, the level of urbanization

varies significantly across different districts of Bihar.
