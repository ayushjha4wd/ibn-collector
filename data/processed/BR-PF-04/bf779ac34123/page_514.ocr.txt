The targeted efforts implemented by the State government to address the welfare needs of the

backward and highly backward classes are outlined below:

Mukhyamantri Pichhada Varg evam Atyant Pichhada Varg Pre-Matric Chhatravritti
Yojana: Previously, all the OBC students studying in class I to X were covered under the Centrally
Sponsored OBC Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme. Under the new guideline of PM YASASVI-Pre-
Matric Scholarship to OBC Students, only the students of IX and X are to be covered. So, in order
to benefit all the students studying in class | to X like before, a new scheme called Mukhyamantri
Pichhada Varg evam Atyant Pichhada Varg Pre-Matric Chhatravritti Yojana has been launched
from the year 2022-23 under state scheme. Under the scheme, scholarship is being provided at the

following rate.

Mukhyamantri Pichhada Varg evam Atyant Pichhada Varg Praveshikottar Chhatravritti
Yojana-2023: The rate of scholarship has been reduced under the Centrally Sponsored Scheme of
providing Post-Matric Scholarship to OBC students. So, to provide the scholarship in such cases,
especially to those pursuing technical courses, a new state scheme called Mukhyamantri Pichhada
Varg evam Atyant Pichhada Varg Praveshikottar Chhatravritti Yojna has been launched from 2022-
23, under which scholarship amount of upto Rs. 4 lakh is being awarded.
