14.5 Wetland Conservation and Development in Bihar

Bihar is located at the centre of the Gangetic Plain. Several rivers traverse through the state, many
of them originating in Nepal. These rivers in the past have changed their courses several times,
leaving natural depressions that are later fed by water from rainfall and river overflows. They often
form oxbow lakes, riverine wetlands, cut-off meanders, manns and chaurs. Besides these natural
wetlands, there are several man-made reservoirs/wetlands that are ecologically significant. As per
the National Wetland Atlas (NWA) 2010, there are 4416 wetlands in Bihar, each with an area of
at least 2.25 hectares. In addition to that, about 17,582 smaller wetlands (with area of less than 2.25
