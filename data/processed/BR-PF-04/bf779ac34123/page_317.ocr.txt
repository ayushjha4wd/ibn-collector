NSTEP : The facility of NSTEP was started in District Courts in 2020 that enables real-time
tracking of serving notices and summons by courts. A total of 340 smartphones for Process
Servers/ Bailiffs have been procured and a total of 6,73,183 NSTEP Summons have been
consumed. As of November, 2024, a total of 2,33,185 NSTEP Summons had been delivered in

various Judgeships of Bihar.

Multi-Level e-Sewa Kendra : Patna High Court has 4-Tier e-Sewa Kendras. All District Courts,
Sub-Divisional Courts and courts in Panchayats can also access this facility. For taking the e-court
initiatives to the end user at the panchayat level, the High Court has embarked upon the task. It has
started offering e-Sewa Kendra facilities in panchayats by enabling the existing panchayat
bhawans to cater the facility, Till date, 41 e-Sewa Kendras have been established (one in Patna
High Court, 37 in District courts, one in Sub-divisional Court and two in Panchayats).

CCTV-based Surveillance System : In order to provide overall safety and security at important
places, the Department of Home has installed CCTV cameras to monitor the activities and prevent
unlawful activities. It covers Secretariat Buildings in Patna such as Old Secretariat, Adhiveshan
Bhawan, Sinchai Bhawan, Vikas Bhawan, Soochna Bhawan, Vishveshvaraiya Bhawan and
Niyojan Bhawan. 1 also covers Vidhan Sabha (old and new Annexe Buildings). Apart from the
Secretariat and Vidhan Sabha, the Department has also covered all Police Stations and all Court
campuses (from the Subdivisional Courts to the High Court) with the 24/7 CCTV surveillance
project. The system captures the details of vehicles entering and exiting the building premises,
identifies any suspicious activity and alerts the control-cum-monitoring rooms using state-of-the-
art technology and video analytics.

e-Prisons : It is an integrated system application software for computerising all the activities
related to prison and prisoner management in real time. It has covered the entire lifecycle of the
prisoners in all 59 prisons of Bihar since 2016. Legacy data of prisoners have also been captured
in e-Prisons application since 2005. There are several modules in the application for serving the
purpose at various levels of the government. New laws — (i) Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, (ii)
Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, and (iii) Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam have been
incorporated in to the system. Till now, details of total 28.23 lakh prisoners haye been captured.

e-Mulakat System : This system is available at all prisons of Bihar and provides citizen-centric
services to the relatives of inmates. This includes registration for visiting an inmate and inmates’
video conference-based Mulakat with their relatives. The portal also consists of dashboards
showing statistical data of various prisons in the district/country. Grievances with respect to their
wards inside the prison can also be submitted by people through this portal, which are then
forwarded to the respective prison for action. These services are being provided to the
relatives/advocates/doctors of the prisoners. Till now, a total of 23.28 lakh visits of prisoners’
relatives/friends have been completed at prisons of Bihar.
