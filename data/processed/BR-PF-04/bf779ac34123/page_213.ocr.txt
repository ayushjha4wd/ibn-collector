first, the industrial sector in Bihar has been small, but has shown a progress in terms of scale and
productivity in recent years. To attract more investment in Bihar, the State government has devised
an overall industrial promotion policy as well as sector-specific policies. Moreover, the State
government has created an institutional structure to ensure rapid industrialisation in Bihar. Second,
as shown by ASUSE, 2022-23, the unincorporated non-agricultural sector in Bihar was large but
represented a low scale of production and productivity. To harness this vast sector, the State
government has adopted various entrepreneurship and skill development programmes. The state’s
