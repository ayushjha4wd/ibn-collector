The total Annual Credit Plan (ACP) target set by various banks in Bihar increased by 28.9 percent,
rising from Rs. 2.0 lakh crore in 2022-23 to Rs. 2.6 lakh crore in 2023-24. The achievement of the
ACP target was 96.3 percent in 2023-24, which is lower than the 108 percent achievement of the

target in the previous year. In 2023-24, the total allocation of the ACP to the Priority Sector was

Rs. 2.0 lakh crore, accounting for 77.2 percent of the overall ACP target. In comparison, the

allocation to this sector was 75.5 percent in 2022-23.

Among the priority sectors, Agriculture received the largest share of the ACP target at Rs, 0.94

lakh crore, followed by Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) with Rs. 0.89 lakh crore in

2023-24. Notably, the achievement rate for non-priority sectors in 2023-24 was significantly

higher at 122.0 percent, compared to 88.8 percent for the priority sector. Within the priority sectors,

the achievement rates were 88.8 percent for Agriculture and 86.0 percent for Small and Medium

Enterprises.
