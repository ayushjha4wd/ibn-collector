Provisioning of Uniform

To promote equity in AWCs, the department is facilitating the provision of uniforms for children,
the cost of which is Rs.400 per beneficiary, This amount has been deposited into the accounts of
the parents of the children, enabling them to purchase the uniforms independently.

Local monitoring and supervision committee

To ensure the effective management of early childhood education across various levels within the
state, the local monitoring and supervision committee at the block level comprises the following
members: the president from the Child Development Project Office, a lady supervisor serving as
coordinator on a rotational basis, the Block Development Officer, the in-charge medical officer,
the Block Education Officer, and two members from the Civil Society Organization at the block
level. This committee is tasked with overseeing activities conducted at the block level, holding
monthly review meetings to identify challenges and necessary corrective actions, and

communicating with higher authorities to facilitate required interventions.

Community Engagement

The Poshan Bhi Padhai Bhi initiative aims to transform Anganwadi Centres (AWCs) into dynamic
learning hubs. To achieve this, the department has adopted a community-based approach,
recognizing the community as a vital stakeholder in the pre-school education programme.
Engaging community members is essential for gaining a deeper understanding of both children
and their families. During the early developmental years, it is crucial for parents to comprehend
and support the activities occurring within the AWC, while Anganwadi Workers (AWWs) must
also be aware of the child's home environment. Parents are regularly updated on their child's
progress. Consequently, the state has established guidelines for observing a monthly ECCE day in
the centres, in accordance with the directives from the Ministry of Women and Child Development.
Acomprehensive 12-month plan detailing theme-based activity and the roles and responsibilities
of stakeholders has been disseminated to all districts. It is recommended that the ECCE day session
lasts a minimum of two hours. This plan is synchronized with the academic calendar, from April
to March, to facilitate a smooth transition for children entering primary school.
