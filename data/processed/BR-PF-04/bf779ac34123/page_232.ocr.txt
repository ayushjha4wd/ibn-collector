Major Tourism Development Works

In 2023-24, the State government has approved Rs. 494.71 crore to implement various projects
across the state. The three most prominent projects in terms of the amount of money spent are as
follows:

First, the estimated cost of the Gaya Ji Dharamshala in the Gaya district was Rs. 120.16 crore.
Second, the estimated cost for developing Punaura Dham in Sitamarhi was Rs. 72.47 crore.

Third, the estimated cost for the construction of the pathway-cum-shed building structure and the
alternate approach path for the Vishnupad Temple and the bus depot in the Gaya district was
Rs. 61.97 crore.

In 2024-25, the State government has allocated Rs. 204.99 crore to carry out important projects.
The three most prominent projects, in terms of cost, are as follows:

First, the estimated cost for the construction of a budget hotel on the premises of Hotel Janaki
Vihar in Sitamarhi is Rs. 29.87 crore.

Second, the estimated cost for the construction of the main building for the service plaza and motel
in Supaul is Rs. 29.54 crore.

Third. the estimated cost for the construction of the office building of the Bihar Tourism

Development Corporation in the Patna district is Rs. 28.87 crore.
