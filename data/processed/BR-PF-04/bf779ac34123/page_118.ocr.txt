The total expenditure by the State government has risen by 1,7 times over the five years from
2019-20 to 2023-24. It rose from Rs. 1,46,097 crore in 2019-20 to Rs. 2,52,082 crore in 2023-24.
The growth rates for the past three years were 8.7 percent in 2023-24, 20.1 percent in 2022-23,
and 16.6 percent in 2021-22. During this period, expenditure on social services accounted for the
largest share of the total expenditure, representing 33.0 percent in 2023-24, This was followed by
general services at 23.5 percent and economic services at 19.1 percent. The expenditure on
Economic Services and General Services increased by 21.4 percent and 5.7 percent, respectively,
in 2023-24, Chart 2.13 compares the percentage composition of government expenditure across
various categories for the years 2019-20 and 2023-24. The allocation of expenditure by the State
government has changed during this period. However, the largest portion of this expenditure
continues to be directed towards Social Services, followed by allocations for General Services and
Economic Services.

The capital outlay has steadily increased over the last five years, rising from 8.4 percent of total
expenditure in 2019-20 to 14.5 percent in 2023-24. There has also been a notable rise in the portion
of expenditure allocated for the repayment of public debt, increasing from 4.9 percent in 2019-20
to 9,1 percent in 2023-24, The amount spent on repayment of public debt rose by 64.1 percent in
2022-23 and 60.1 percent in 2023-24. Loans and advances extended by the State government
accounted for 0.8 percent of the total expenditure in 2023-24.
