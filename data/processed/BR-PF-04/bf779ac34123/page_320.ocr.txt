Child Labour Tracking System (CLTS) : It is a web-based tracking system launched in 2016
which facilitates the monitoring of rescued child labours and their rehabilitation. After the release
of the child labour, a sum of Rs. 3000 is provided as immediate assistance by the Labour Resources
Department. According to the order of Hon*ble Supreme Court, a sum of Rs. 5000 by the State
Government and Rs. 20,000 from the accused employer is deposited in District Rehabilitation-
cum-welfare fund. In addition to this after entry on CLTS portal, for each eligible rescued child
labour an amount of Rs. 25,000 from the CM Relief Fund is provided as F.D / annuity. A total of
2912 child labours have received assistance of Rs. 25,000 per child labour from CMRF through
this annuity scheme. The total number of entry of child labours in CLTS is 10,039 (as on 31-10-
2024). Dhawa Dal App has been launched to monitor the activities of Dhawa Dals constituted in
every district for rescue of child labours.

e-Office : The e-Office aims to support governance using more effective and transparent inter and
intra-government processes. This system involves all stages of working in a file, including the
diary noting of inward receipts, creation of files, movement of receipts and files and finally, the
archival of records. With this system, the movement of receipts and files becomes seamless and
there is more transparency in the system since each and every action taken on a file is recorded
electronically. This simplifies decision making, as all the required information is available at a
single point. It envisions a paperless office, with increased transparency, efficiency and
accountability of the organization. E-Office has been functioning smoothly across the departments,
selected districts and subsidiaries across the state since 2015,
