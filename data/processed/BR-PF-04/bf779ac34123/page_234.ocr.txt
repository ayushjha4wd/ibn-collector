LABOUR, EMPLOYMENT AND SKILL

The economic prosperity of a nation largely depends on the skill level of its workforce and its
ability to cater to industry-specific labour demands. To. align with the growing needs of the
economy, the labour force needs to acquire diverse and specialized skills, Recognising the
importance of a skilled workforce, the State government has taken proactive steps to address
the increasing demands for skilled labour by implementing various skill development
programmes, These programmes aim to equip the workforce with necessary competencies. In
addition to such initiatives, the government has also introduced welfare schemes, targeting
both organized and unorganized sector workers to ensure their well-being. Regular
recruitment drives are conducted to create quality employment opportunities within the state,
Furthermore, in a significant step towards women’s empowerment and increase their
participation in the workforce, the state has implemented extensive women's reservation
policies across employment levels. This chapter highlights the government's concerted efforts
to eradicate child labour and the implementation of schemes aimed at their rehabilitation and
reintegration into mainstream. Through these initiatives, the State government strives to
ensure holistic development and inclusive growth by addressing the needs of its diverse labour

force.

Recognising labour as a critical aspect of economic progress, the government of Bihar has taken a
multitude of initiatives to harness its labour resources. The State government has devised a range
of policies and programmes that would benefit every section of the labour force, On the one hand,
the State government has created jobs in the public sector to bolster the government service
delivery system, In recent years, a substantial number of employment opportunities have been
created in different government departments. Commissions and institutions. such as the Bihar
Public Service Commission, recruit suitable candidates for government departments. On the other
hand, the State government has adopted various policies to provide timely social security benefits
to almost all sections of the large workforce engaged in the informal sector. Generally, workers in
the informal sector receive low remuneration and are mostly deprived of any social security
benefit. The State government has taken the responsibility of providing different kinds of benefits
