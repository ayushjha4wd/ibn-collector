Committed expenditure: Committed expenditure of a state typically includes expenditure on payment of
salaries, pension, and interest. A larger proportion of the budget allocated for committed expenditure items
limits the state’s flexibility to decide on other expenditure priorities, such as capital outlay. In 2026-27, Bihar is
estimated to spend Rs 1,33,494 crore on committed expenditure items, which is 47% of its estimated revenue
receipts. This comprises spending on salaries (26% of revenue receipts), pension (12%), and interest payments
(9%). In 2024-25, committed expenditure constituted 39% of revenue receipts. Expenditure on salaries in
2026-27 is estimated to increase at an annualised rate of 38% over 2024-25. Expenditure on salaries by the
Education Department is estimated to increase from Rs 14,561 crore in 2024-25 to Rs 36,658 crore in 2026-27.
