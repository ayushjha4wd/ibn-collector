OFFICE MEMORANDUM
Subject: Constitution of the High Level Expert Group on Agriculture Exports
The Fifteenth Finance Commission has been constituted by the President vide
notification no. S.O. 3755(E) dated 27th November, 2017 (copy enclosed). A copy of Terms of
Reference (ToR) dated 29.11.2019 of the Fifteenth Finance Commission (FC-XV) is also
enclosed. The Commission has decided to constitute a High Level Expert Group on Agriculture
Exports in pursuance of its ToR (para 7) i.e to recommend measurable performance incentives
for States to encourage agri exports as well as to promote crops to enable high import
substitution. The composition of the group is given below:
(i) Shri Sanjiv Puri, Chairman and Managing Director, ITC – Chairman
(ii) Ms. Radha Singh, Former Agriculture Secretary - Member
(iii) Representative of Ministry of Food Processing Industries - Member
(iv) Chairman, Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export
Development Authority (APEDA), Ministry of Commerce and Industry - Member
(v) Shri Suresh Narayanan, CMD, NESTLE India - Member
(vi) Mr. Jai Shroff, CEO-UPL Limited – Member
(vii) Shri Sanjay Sacheti, Country Head India-Olam Agro India Ltd - Member
(viii) Dr. Sachin Chaturvedi, Director General, RIS - Member
2. The Committee is free to seek assistance of any other institution or entity as considered
appropriate for completion of the work. It is requested that the Committee may submit
its recommendations within three months for further consideration of the Commission.
3. The Terms of Reference of the Group is Annexed.
