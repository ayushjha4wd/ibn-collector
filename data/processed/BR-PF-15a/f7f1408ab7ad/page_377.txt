Conservation of forest and wildlife management
61. The memorandum from Maharashtra highlighted the needs for conservation of forests
and wildlife and increasing the green cover in the State. The State is rich in biodiversity and is
home to six national parks, forty-nine sanctuaries and six conservation reserves and six tiger
reserves. We recommend Rs. 500 crore for this purpose
Development of Revas Reddy Coastal Highway
62. The Government of Maharashtra has sought support for construction of roads parallel to
Konkan's coastline. The scenic Mumbai-Goa coastal marine highway of 540 km, that is being
hailed as Gateway to Konkan, is expected to give a huge boost to the Konkan economy, especially
tourism and fisheries. We recommend an amount of Rs. 1,250 crore for this purpose.
