IT Return Data and National Accounts—Some indications
It is useful to compare the income reported in income tax returns and the incomes estimated from
the National Accounts. The two should approximate each other with appropriate adjustments.
Because these two datasets approach incomes from different angles, perfect matching is not
possible. The table below attempts a broad correspondence between the two datasets.
