About Us

The Finance, Government of Bihar is responsible for management of finances of the State Government. It is concerned with all economic and financial matters affecting the State as a whole including mobilization and allocation of resources for infrastructural development, social welfare, human development and administrative purposes. The major functions and activities of the Finance are budgeting and preparation of the Annual Financial Statement, monitoring of expenditure, administration of Treasuries and internal audit of Government Departments.

Vision

To facilitate development and inclusive growth for Bihar through prudent and progressive financial management.
